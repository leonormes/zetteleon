> *test double* is an object that looks and behaves like its release-intended counterpart but is actually a simplified version that reduces the complexity and facilitates testing. This term was introduced by Gerard Meszaros in his book, *xUnit Test Patterns: Refactoring Test Code* (Addison-Wesley, 2007). The name itself comes from the notion of a stunt double in movies.
