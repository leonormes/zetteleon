[[BookFusion/Books/User Story Mapping Discover the Whole Story, Build the Right Product-3531505|User Story Mapping: Discover the Whole Story, Build the Right Product]]

**Date Created**: 2024-01-29 12:38:13 UTC
> It’s that longer-term stuff that happens as a consequence of good outcomes that’s I’ll label *impact*. Outcomes are often something you can observe right away after delivery. But impact takes longer. 

[!note]
> We care about the impact our product has on the users ability to achieve their goals. But it is hard to measure, or takes a long time. And may not be clear to the user.  Do we know what the impact is? We say what the app does. But so what if it does that? How does that make a difference.
