**Date Created**: 2025-04-06 07:54:17 UTC
> **Mind Movie**: The fantasy your mind plays in its head about how things should be. This fantasy might be about how nice your life would be if you had a meditation habit, or ate healthily, or got fit with Crossfit. The fantasy might be about how disciplined or productive or mindful you should be. It might be about how considerate other people should be to you, or how they should ideally behave, or how great your life should be. These fantasies aren’t true, but your mind believes them anyway, because it really wants them to be true. It feels like they should be real. Unfortunately, these Mind Movies get in our way of appreciating life and making healthy changes, and they create struggle for us. 

[!note]
> this is clearly what happens to me all the time. as a kid I spent hours fantasising about all kinds of greatness. I have always done it. if I had a wish type thoughts.
