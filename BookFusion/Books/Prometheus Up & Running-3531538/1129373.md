[[BookFusion/Books/Prometheus Up & Running-3531538|Prometheus: Up & Running]]

**Date Created**: 2023-09-22 22:41:50 UTC
> In the Linux kernel, enhanced Berkeley Packet Filters (eBPF) allow detailed profiling of kernel events from filesystem operations to network oddities. These provide access to a level of insight that was not generally available previously. eBPF comes with other advantages, such as portability and safety. We’d recommend reading Brendan Gregg’s writings on the subject.
