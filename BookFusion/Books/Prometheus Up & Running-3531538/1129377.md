> Application logs Not all logs are about requests; some are about the process itself. Startup messages, background maintenance tasks, and other process-level log lines are typical. These logs are often read directly by a human, so you should try to avoid having more than a few per minute in normal operations.
> 
>  Debug logs Debug logs tend to be very detailed and thus expensive to create and store. They are often only used in very narrow debugging situations, and are trending toward profiling due to their data volume. Reliability and retention requirements tend to be low, and debug logs may not even leave the machine they are generated on.
