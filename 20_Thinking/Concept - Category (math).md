A mathematical structure that allows for mapping relationships between different maths subjects. 
We can reduce things to their essentials and remove all the irrelevant properties to work out more complex details. When things are reduced they often share similarities across different areas. Category theory is this property of nature formalised. 
The simplified versions of things are categories. 
The relationships between objects then reveal general patterns.
