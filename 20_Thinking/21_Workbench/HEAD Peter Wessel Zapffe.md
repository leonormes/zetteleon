The video you shared explores the philosophy of Peter Wessel Zapffe, a Norwegian philosopher who argued that human suffering is a direct result of our "overdeveloped consciousness." Unlike other animals, humans possess a level of awareness that allows us to contemplate our own mortality, the vastness of the universe, and the inevitability of pain—a burden Zapffe believed we were never meant to carry.

According to Zapffe's essay, _The Last Messiah_, we protect ourselves from this "cosmic panic" using four psychological defense mechanisms \[

\]:

### 1\. Isolation

This involves the deliberate or unconscious dismissal of disturbing thoughts and feelings from our minds. We "block out" the ongoing tragedies of life to function.

- Example: Professionals (like doctors) who focus strictly on technical tasks to avoid being overwhelmed by the suffering they witness daily \[
	[10:27](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jH0rxb2_KWs&t=627)
	\].

### 2\. Anchoring

Zapffe describes this as the "happiest" of the defenses. We anchor ourselves to fixed values, institutions, or structures to create a sense of order in a chaotic universe.

- Example: Relying on the safety of childhood homes, religious beliefs ("God is watching over us"), or societal norms to feel secure \[
	[11:29](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jH0rxb2_KWs&t=689)
	\].

### 3\. Distraction

This is the most common modern tactic. We keep our minds in constant motion to avoid facing our own "endurable condition."

- Example: The endless loop of entertainment, smartphones, career ambitions, and consumption. Zapffe compares this to a flying machine that must stay in motion to avoid crashing \[
	[12:53](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jH0rxb2_KWs&t=773)
	\].

### 4\. Sublimation

The rarest of the four, sublimation involves looking directly at the suffering and transforming it into something else, typically art.

- Example: Turning personal tragedy into music, painting, or writing. The video creator notes that both Zapffe's essay and the video itself are forms of sublimation \[
	[14:16](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jH0rxb2_KWs&t=856)
	\].

### The Radical Conclusion

Zapffe concluded that because these defenses are only temporary escapes, the only true "solution" to human suffering is for humanity to cease reproducing—to "be infertile and let the earth be silent after you" \[
