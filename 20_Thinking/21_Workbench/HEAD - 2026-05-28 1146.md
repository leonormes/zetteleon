Found the issue. Here's what the data shows:

`pod` is structured metadata, not a stream label. The stream labels for `{cluster="testing"}` are: `cluster`, `container`, `namespace`, `node`, `job`, `service_name`, etc.—`pod` is absent from that list. However, `pod` does exist as structured metadata (e.g., `compliance-snapshotter-67cbbdbbbf-78mmz`, `dev-mongodb-b17ef-0`)—it just can't be used inside `{}` selectors.

This is typically caused by the Grafana Alloy / k8s-monitoring Helm chart being configured to send `pod` as structured metadata rather than a stream label (intentional to reduce label cardinality in Loki).

How to debug and fix:

- Confirm it's metadata: `{cluster="testing"} | pod!= ""` works (as you can see above), but `{cluster="testing", pod="…"}` won't
- Check your Alloy config for a `loki.process` or `discovery.relabel` stage—look for `pod` being set via `loki.write` structured metadata rather than a label
- In `grafana/k8s-monitoring` Helm chart, the `logs.pod_metadata` or `structured_metadata` config controls this—to promote `pod` to a stream label, move it from `structured_metadata` to a relabel rule that keeps it as a label
- Be aware: adding `pod` as a stream label significantly increases cardinality—consider filtering by `namespace` + `container` first

In the meantime, filter by pod using: `{cluster="testing"} | pod="your-pod-name"`
