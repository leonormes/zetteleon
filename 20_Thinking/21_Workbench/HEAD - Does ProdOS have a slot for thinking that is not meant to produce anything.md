## The Question

ProdOS is telic all the way down. Next actions must be physical and visible. HEAD notes must state a closing condition or they are flagged as rotting. Captures must be routed. Thinking must eventually converge into an SoT. The architecture's central assumption is that anything without an exit will fill up and degrade — which is stated outright in [[SoT - HEAD Note Contract (The Workbench)]] §6.

[[Intellectual Activity Belongs to the Atelic Sphere]] asserts that some of the most valuable thinking has no exit *by design*. So: is there anywhere in this system that such thinking is allowed to live without accruing a processing debt, and should there be?

## Why It Matters

If the answer is no and that is a defect, then the system is quietly taxing the activity I built it to support — every unconverged thought showing up as an open loop rather than as a completed act. If the answer is no and that is correct, then I should stop expecting the vault to validate contemplation and accept that it is a work system with a bounded remit. Either answer changes how I read my own backlog. The wrong answer to this makes the vault a source of low-grade guilt about thinking, which is the exact failure mode I am trying to counter.

## What I Currently Think

Leaning towards: the absence is real but the fix is not a new folder. Adding an "atelic zone" would create exactly the ungoverned space the HEAD contract was written against, and I would end up with a third inbox. The likelier correct answer is that ProdOS's remit is *work*, and the mistake is mine — reading an absence of representation as a judgement. But I am not confident, because [[SoT - Processing IS the Work]] already blurs the line by treating the cognitive engagement as the value rather than the note.

## What Would Settle It

Go through one month of `01_journals/` and count the entries where thinking happened but no artefact resulted. If those entries were recorded at all, the system already has a slot and I have not noticed it. If they are systematically absent — if the journal only records outputs — then the system is invisible to that activity and the question becomes a real design decision rather than a misreading.
