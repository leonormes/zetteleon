Based on _Agile Estimating and Planning_, here are the specific steps to facilitate a Planning Poker session:

1. **Setup:** Provide every estimator (developers, testers, analysts) with a deck of cards representing your estimation scale (e.g., 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, etc.). The Product Owner attends to answer questions but does **not** vote.
2. **Read the Story:** A moderator (often the Product Owner) reads the description of the user story or feature.
3. **Clarify:** The team asks the Product Owner questions to understand the requirements and risks. The goal is to understand the "what," not necessarily design the entire "how" right then.
4. **Private Estimation:** Each estimator selects a card representing their estimate and places it face down. Keeping it private prevents participants from anchoring their estimate to someone else's.
5. **Reveal:** Everyone turns their cards over simultaneously.
6. **Discuss Differences:** If the estimates vary significantly, ask the outliers (the highest and lowest estimators) to explain their reasoning. They often share important information the rest of the group missed (e.g., "This requires a new database table" or "We can reuse existing code here").
7. **Re-Estimate:** After the discussion, the team votes again. Repeat this cycle until the estimates converge on a consensus number.
