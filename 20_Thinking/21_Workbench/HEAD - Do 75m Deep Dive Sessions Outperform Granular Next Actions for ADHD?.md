## Context

- [[Deep Dive Sessions for ADHD (Adapted GTD Next Actions)]] hypothesises that hiding Todoist Next Actions and blocking two 75-minute project slots per day lowers list-management friction and sustains flow.
- In practice, no 1-week daily log was ever completed, while `[[Protocol - Daily Plan (Todoist Timeboxing)]]` now schedules `15m`, `30m`, or `60m` Todoist execution boxes directly on the runway.

## What Would Settle It

- **Pass:** Initiated >= 3 blocks without stalling and reported lower end-of-day overwhelm than granular-task days → extract as `claim`/`evidence` and update `[[Deep Dive Sessions for ADHD (Adapted GTD Next Actions)]]`.
