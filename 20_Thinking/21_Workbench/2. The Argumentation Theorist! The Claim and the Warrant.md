## 2\. The Argumentation Theorist: The Claim and the Warrant

Stephen Toulmin explicitly rejects the sterile, mathematical view of the formal logicians. He argues that propositions in the wild are not just true/false abstractions; they are living components of an argument.

- The Rule: Toulmin renames propositions as Claims. A claim cannot stand alone; its validity depends on the _Data_ provided and the unstated _Warrant_ that bridges the data to the claim _(Toulmin, The Uses of Argument, Ch. 3)_.
- The Lens: _Warrant_ and _Assumption Set_. Toulmin teaches us to look at a proposition not just for its truth value, but for the buried rules (warrants) that allow the author to assert it.
