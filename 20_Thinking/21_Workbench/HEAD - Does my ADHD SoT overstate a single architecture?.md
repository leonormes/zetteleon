## The Question

[[SoT - ADHD Neurology & Core Concepts]] §1.1 presents a single architecture table. [[ADHD is a Heterogeneous Condition with Unique Symptom Patterns]] and [[ADHD Task Initiation is Not Universally the Hardest Symptom Due to Individual Variation]] both argue the presentation varies enough that one spec sheet misleads. Separately, §1.5's 'Glitchy Switch' framing competes with [[ADHD Task-Paralysis Reflects DMN-FPN Maturational Lag, Not Willpower Failure]] — dysregulation or developmental lag?

## Why It Matters

This SoT is the foundation the whole ADHD cluster rests on, and I read it as a description of *me*. If it is really a description of a modal case, I may have adopted protocols aimed at someone else's presentation.

## What I Currently Think

The table is a useful default and I should keep it — but it does not currently say it is a default, and an unhedged spec sheet reads as universal. The heterogeneity note's own weakness is that its load-bearing statistic (91.8%, 116,220 combinations) is precise, unattributed, and therefore uncheckable, so it is not strong enough to demote the table on its own.

## What Would Settle It

Add one hedging sentence to §1.1 stating the table is the modal presentation rather than a universal spec. Separately, find the source for the 91.8% figure or mark [[ADHD is a Heterogeneous Condition with Unique Symptom Patterns]] `conformant: false` — an uncheckable statistic cannot be allowed to be load-bearing.
