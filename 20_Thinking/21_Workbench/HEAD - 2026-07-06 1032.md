## Important limitation

This GitOps change updates **Fitfile workloads** (ffcloud init env, Grafana Alloy `noProxy` via `proxy.noProxy`). It does **not** update **AKS cluster-level `http-proxy-config`**, which is what injects `NO_PROXY` into `azure-aks-backup` extension pods.

So from these logs we can say:

- ArgoCD picked up the commit and synced successfully
- No problems caused by the deployment
- We **cannot** confirm the backup extension fix from logs alone

For FTFL-762 acceptance you still need:

1. **Action 1 (cluster):** `az aks update --http-proxy-config` with `.blob.core.windows.net` in `noProxy`
2. **Action 2 (networking):** DNS conditional forwarder for `privatelink.blob.core.windows.net` → `168.63.129.16`
3. **Validation:** `nslookup aksffuksgp01cuhbackup.blob.core.windows.net` → `10.250.16.84`, then reinstall `azure-aks-backup`
