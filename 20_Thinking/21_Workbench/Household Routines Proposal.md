## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

I withdrew from tidy-up to win an argument about systems, and I own that first. The way forward is one small joint change, agreed before it is enforced, chosen by both of us, and tested for two weeks. The calm routine should be the default of the environment, not a rule one of us polices.
