## 2\. The Recursive "but what about the meta-level?" Move

You did this repeatedly:

- Validated JTB → asked about IOED → then asked whether IOED is a claim or concept → then asked how to represent typed links → then asked about granularity → then asked about end-state
- Each question went _up a level_ from the previous one.

This is characteristic ADHD cognition: difficulty stopping at the level of analysis that answers the immediate question. The brain keeps asking "yes but what is the _real_ question underneath?" This is a genuine strength (it is why you spotted that Gemini was using mathematical vocabulary loosely) _and_ a source of paralysis (you cannot ground until you have hit the meta-ceiling).

Relevant quote from your brain dump: _"I don't have an end game… that is endless. Each node off I could do the same thing."_

This is the recursion experienced from the inside. The infinite regress is not a philosophical problem you stumbled on—it is the shape of your attention pattern made visible.
