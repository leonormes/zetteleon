### 7. Conversation Protocol

- Choose a time that is not bedtime and not straight after a row.
- Open with section 1, then ask "what do you think is going wrong?" and restate her answer before mine.
- One topic at a time. If it drifts: "let's park that, it's written down, back to X"–never name the fallacy aloud.
- If either of us is heated, stop and resume next day.
- Vault scripts for the moment (the rule stands: never name the fallacy aloud):
  - Her counter-grievance or "you do it too": agenda parking with a fixed slot straight after. [[Gish Gallop of Grievances]]—supports: gives the script.
  - "But you said…": acknowledge once, name the present topic, restate the boundary. [[Gish Gallop of Grievances]]—supports.
  - "There is no solution": [[The No Solution Deflection in Difficult Conversations]]—supports: reads it as "no solution I am willing to implement", so ask which one she would.
  - Restating her answer before mine: [[Active Listening De-escalates Conflict]]—supports; [[Habit 5 - Seek First to Understand, Then to Be Understood]]—supports.
  - Stating the proposal as data, impact, proposal, check: [[SoT - The Data-Impact-Proposal-Check Framework]]—supports.
  - Intent against impact, and her pain not deleting mine or mine hers: [[SoT - Framework for Healthy Communication]]—supports.
  - Matching her frequency (practical, emotional, identity) before offering fixes: [[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]]—supports, section 4.1.
