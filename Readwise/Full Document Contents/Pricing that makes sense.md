#### Forged for Efficiency

Everything you need to manage your repositories, simplified.

Visualize your commit history with a beautiful, interactive graph. Merge branches simply by dragging one node onto another.

Integrated Gemini AI (Bring Your Own Key) to auto-generate commit messages, explain diffs, and analyze code changes.

Blazing fast performance with a tiny memory footprint. Get things done quickly and efficiently.

The power of VS Code's editor for diffing files. Syntax highlighting, side-by-side comparison, and image diffs.

#### Pricing that Makes Sense

ArezGit is free for public projects. Purchase a license only if you need to manage private repositories.
