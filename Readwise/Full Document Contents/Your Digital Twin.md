#### AI that links apps, reveals insights, and cuts busywork

* "Littlebird made onboarding to new projects painless. I could see all the context without asking a single question."

between coding tasks without losing my flow. It intelligently surfaces  
context from Slack, code, and docs, making it effortless to pick up right  
where I left off on any project.
