#### Get expert guidance on the forces reshaping data, analytics, and AI

The data and analytics space has never been hotter. Under the pressure of rising executive expectations, emerging regulatory demands, and a rapidly evolving data ecosystem landscape, your annual outlook is brimming with opportunity—and risk.

To help you stay ahead, three of the industry’s top data and AI strategists distilled this year’s most critical trends, offering an insider’s perspective on what’s changing, why it matters, and most importantly, what you can do about it.

**With their expert guidance, you’ll be able to:**

* Identify innovation hotspots—from MCP and agent orchestration to semantic layers
* Sidestep hazards around AI regulation, literacy, interoperability, and trust
* Lead with confidence using evidence-based strategies and real-world examples

Don’t wait for an innovation mandate or fall into a fading AI fad. Get the insights you need to build an adaptable data strategy and lead with confidence through data and analytics’ most dynamic era.
