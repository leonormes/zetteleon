##### Scan Performance RTX

This Scan Performance RTX features the 8GB NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 graphics card based on the Blackwell architecture, featuring GDDR7 memory, 4th generation RT cores, 5th generation Tensor cores and support for DLSS 4.

We have paired this powerful GPU with the 6C/12T AMD Ryzen 5 9600X CPU. The system also includes 16GB of Corsair Vengeance 6000MHz DDR5, an ASUS TUF GAMING A620M-PLUS WIFI motherboard and a 1TB Kioxia EXCERIA PRO M.2 PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD for lightning quick loading times and file transfer and storage.

This gaming PC comes with Windows 11 Home pre-installed and a 2-year warranty.
