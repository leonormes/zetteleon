### Suggested Reading Order

Builds from mathematical foundations → algorithm → applied cryptography → protocol deployment.

1. Numbers: A Very Short Introduction—Gentle introduction to primes, modular arithmetic, and number-theoretic foundations. (~1 hour)
2. The Music of the Primes—Fall in love with primes. Du Sautoy's narrative covers the Riemann Hypothesis, Euler, Fermat, Gauss, and why primes are the "atoms" of number theory—and the bedrock of RSA. (~8 hours)
3. Understanding Cryptography—First rigorous treatment. Paar & Pelzl build RSA from the ground up: Euclid's algorithm, Euler's theorem, Fermat's little theorem, key generation, encryption, signing, and padding. (~15 hours)
4. Serious Cryptography—RSA as it's actually used. Aumasson covers key generation pitfalls, OAEP padding, timing attacks, and real-world vulnerabilities. (~12 hours)
5. Real-World Cryptography—RSA in production. Wong covers RSA in TLS, certificate chains, RSA-PSS, and how modern systems deploy (and sometimes misdeploy) public-key crypto. (~10 hours)
6. Bulletproof TLS and PKI—RSA at the protocol level. Ristić covers TLS cipher suites, RSA key exchange, certificate validation, and PKI infrastructure. (~20 hours)
7. Algebra-Driven Design—Abstract structures. Maguire's treatment of groups, rings, and fields provides the formal algebraic framework underlying RSA's modular arithmetic. (~15 hours)

### Open Questions

Gaps in the current library for a complete RSA research track:

- Integer Factorisation—No dedicated text on the factoring algorithms (quadratic sieve, number field sieve) that threaten RSA at scale. _Recommended:_ Crandall & Pomerance, "Prime Numbers: A Computational Perspective".
- Quantum Computing & Shor's Algorithm—No coverage of how quantum computers break RSA. _Recommended:_ Nielsen & Chuang, Chapter 5; or Bernstein & Lange, "Post-Quantum Cryptography".
- Side-Channel Attacks—No book dedicated to timing attacks, power analysis, and fault injection against RSA implementations. _Recommended:_ Mangard, Oswald & Popp, "Power Analysis Attacks".
- Post-Quantum Alternatives—No coverage of lattice-based, hash-based, or code-based crypto designed to replace RSA. _Recommended:_ Bernstein, Buchmann & Dahmen, "Post-Quantum Cryptography".
- Formal Verification of Crypto—No text on machine-checked proofs of RSA correctness or security. _Recommended:_ Barthe et al., "Formal Verification of Cryptographic Implementations".
- Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC) as RSA successor—Only superficially covered in Real-World Cryptography and Serious Cryptography. A dedicated ECC text would be valuable.
