A fundamental insight across multiple domains: symbols, representations, and models are distinct from the concepts, realities, or territories they attempt to capture. This separation is crucial for deep understanding and avoiding conceptual confusion.

## Foundational Principle

[[The Map is Not the Territory]] - The core principle that any representation is necessarily incomplete and simplified compared to what it represents. %%[synthesizes:: [[The Map is Not the Territory]]]%%

## Cross-Domain Connections

These examples all demonstrate the same fundamental pattern:

1. Symbol/Representation (numeral, word, model) ≠ Concept/Reality (number, meaning, territory)
2. Conflation leads to limitations - Mistaking the representation for the reality constrains understanding
3. Separation enables mastery - Recognizing the distinction allows for deeper, more flexible thinking
