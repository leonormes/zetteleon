## MOC - AI as Thought Partner

> [!abstract] Executive Summary
> This MOC outlines the shift from using AI as a "Problem Solver" to using it as an **Amplifier** for human intent. As execution becomes commoditized, our value migrates to defining the problems, providing the strategic intent, and curating the context within which the AI operates.

---

### 1. The Core Philosophy (The "Why")

- **[[SoT - The Inspiration Economy (Agentic Frameworks)]]**: Defines the shift from the **Capital Economy** (where efficiency wins) to the **Inspiration Economy** (where intent wins).
- **[[SoT - AI-Resilient Task Taxonomy (Human 3.0)]]**: Defines your role in this new economy as the **Problem Definer** and **Strategic Narrator**. Your job is to ask the right questions, not to find the answers.

---

### 2. The Architecture (The "How")

- **[[SoT - The Telos Method]]**: The primary framework for defining the **Policy Layer**. It provides the "Chain of Explainability" that ensures every AI action maps back to your core mission.
- **[[SoT - The Inspiration Economy (Agentic Frameworks)]]**: Outlines the **SPQA Architecture** (State, Policy, Question, Action) which governs the human-AI interaction.
    - **You Provide:** The Policy (Intent) and the Question (Trigger).
    - **The AI Provides:** The Action (Execution).

---

### 3. The Human's Role (The "What")

- **Context Engineering**: The primary skill. You must curate the `State` (your Obsidian vault) to ensure the AI has high-quality context to work with.

---

### 4. The AI's Role (The "Amplifier")

- **Executor**: Offloads the "Capital Economy" tasks (e.g., refactoring code, summarizing documents, creating boilerplate).
- **State Manager**: Uses frameworks like LangGraph to maintain memory and state during complex, multi-step tasks.
- **Thought Partner**: In the **A-C-T Loop**, the AI acts as a sparring partner to help define the **Minimum Viable Action (MVA)**.

---

### 5. Key Protocols & Integrations

- **[[SoT - PRODOS The Unified Runtime]]**: The A-C-T Loop (Action, Container, Thought) is the primary "compute engine" where human-AI collaboration happens.
- **[[SoT - Material Symbols]]**: We use external symbols (notes, diagrams) to "scaffold" our thinking, allowing the AI to interact with stable "thought objects" instead of ephemeral ideas.
- **The Augmentation Stack**: The combination of **Obsidian** (State/Policy), **Todoist** (Action), and an **LLM** (Executor) forms your Personal AI Infrastructure (PAI).
