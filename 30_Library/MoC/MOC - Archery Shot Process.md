## Overview

This Map of Content (MOC) provides a structured, step-by-step breakdown of the archery shot process. The process is divided into two major phases: Technique Structure (setting up the shot) and Shot Execution (the dynamic release).

---

## Phase 1: Technique Structure

Focuses on establishing a solid foundation, setting the hook/grip, raising the bow, and drawing to a consistent anchor point.

- [[Archery Shot Process - Nocking]]—Securing the arrow on the string and rest.
- [[Archery Shot Process - Stance]]—Setting a stable foundation and foot positioning.
- [[Archery Shot Process - Posture]]—Engaging the core and aligning the torso/head.
- [[Archery Shot Process - Setup]]—Setting the hook on the string and the grip on the riser.
- [[Archery Shot Process - Raise]]—Raising the bow and setting the front shoulder.
- [[Archery Shot Process - Pre-draw]]—Initial rotation and shoulder alignment checks.
- [[Archery Shot Process - Loading]]—Drawing with back tension and aligning muscles.
- [[Archery Shot Process - Anchor]]—Finding the final physical reference point at full draw.

## Phase 2: Shot Execution

Focuses on expansion, extending the bow, triggering the release, and follow-through.

- [[Archery Shot Process - Expansion]]—Continuing dynamic tension and the push-pull action.
- [[Archery Shot Process - Release]]—Triggering an involuntary release of the string.
- [[Archery Shot Process - Follow-through]]—Maintaining physical tension and structure post-shot.
