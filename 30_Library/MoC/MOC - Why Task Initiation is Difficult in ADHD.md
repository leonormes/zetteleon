## MOC - Why Task Initiation is Difficult in ADHD

This Source of Truth summarizes the neurological, psychological, and behavioral factors that make task initiation (Activation) a core impairment for individuals with ADHD.

### 1. The Neurological Basis of Activation Failure

Task initiation is not a matter of willpower; it is a "neurologically expensive" process that fails in ADHD due to convergent deficits across multiple brain systems.

#### A. Dopamine & Reward Circuitry

- Dopamine Dysregulation: [[Dopamine reward pathway dysfunction causes ADHD task initiation difficulty|Low dopamine in the reward centers]] prevents the "anticipatory dopamine" required to start a task.
- Receptor Deficiency: [[Reduced Dopamine D2 D3 Receptors Impair Motivation in ADHD|Reduced D2/D3 receptors]] impair the brain's ability to perceive the value of starting.
- The "Interest-Based" Nervous System: The [[The ADHD nervous system is primarily interest-driven|ADHD nervous system]] requires high novelty, urgency, or personal meaning to trigger enough dopamine for activation.

#### B. Executive Control & Energy

- Catecholamine Insufficiency: [[Prefrontal Cortex Catecholamine Insufficiency Impairs Executive Control in ADHD|Insufficient norepinephrine and dopamine in the PFC]] weakens the brain's executive control center.
- Neuroenergetic Deficits: [[The Neuroenergetic Deficit Theory Posits Chronic Energy Insufficiency in ADHD Brains|Chronic insufficiency of neural resources]] makes the "activation cost" of starting a task feel physically exhausting.
- [[Executive Function Challenges are Central to ADHD|Executive Dysfunction]]: Impairments in planning, prioritizing, and organizing create "Analysis Paralysis."

#### C. Network & Error Processing

- The Glitchy Switch: [[The Glitchy Switch - TPN and DMN Dysregulation in ADHD|Dysregulation between the Task-Positive Network (TPN) and Default Mode Network (DMN)]] makes it difficult to transition from mind-wandering to goal-directed action.
- Impaired Error-Processing: [[ADHD is Associated with Reduced Error-Related Negativity (ERN) and Error Positivity (Pe)|Reduced automatic error-processing (ERN/Pe)]] means the brain is less sensitive to the consequences of _not_ starting.

---

### 2. Psychological & Metacognitive Barriers

- Emotional Dysregulation: [[MOC - Emotional Dysregulation|Emotional barriers]] like anxiety, shame, and overwhelm trigger the "freeze" response.
- Task Paralysis: [[ADHD Paralysis is the Inability to Start a Task Until it Becomes an Emergency|Paralysis]] occurs when the cognitive load of choosing "how" to start outweighs the reward.
- Time Blindness: [[Time Agnosia Impairs Planning for Developers with ADHD|Difficulty perceiving the passage of time]] makes tasks feel perpetually "far away" until they become emergencies.
- Working Memory: [[Working Memory Limitations in ADHD]] make it difficult to hold the first three steps of a task in mind long enough to execute the first one.

---

### 3. The Phases of Task Execution

Task initiation is only Phase 1. ADHD impacts the entire lifecycle:

1. Activation (The Start): The highest hurdle (discussed here).
2. Focus (The Middle): Maintaining engagement.
3. Effort (The Grind): Pushing through non-novel segments.
4. Transition (The End): Stopping or switching tasks.
See: [[MOC - The Phases of Task Execution and the Impact of ADHD]]

---

### 4. Behavioural Interventions (The Antidote)

[[Behavioral Interventions for ADHD Compensate for Neurobiological Deficits|Effective interventions]] focus on lowering the "Activation Cost" through external structures.

- Micro-Stepping: [[Micro-Stepping Reduces Cognitive Load for Task Initiation|Breaking tasks into atomic, binary steps]].
- External Cues: Using [[Environmental and Physical Hacks for ADHD Focus|external scaffolding]] (timers, body doubling) to bypass internal motivation.
- Behavioural Momentum: [[Chaining Starter Tasks Creates a Momentum Ramp|Starting with a "low-cost" win]] to build a momentum ramp.
- Rapid Feedback: [[Rapid Feedback Loops are Essential for ADHD Motivation|Immediate rewards for starting]] provide the dopamine the brain lacks.
