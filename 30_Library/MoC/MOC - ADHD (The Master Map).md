## Overview: The ADHD Operating System

ADHD is not a "deficit of attention," but a Resource Management Failure. It is the biological inability to self-regulate focus, arousal, and effort based on abstract "Importance." This hub serves as the central entry point for understanding the ADHD brain and the external scaffolding required to thrive.
