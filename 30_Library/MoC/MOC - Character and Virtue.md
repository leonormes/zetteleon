## Purpose

The entry point for everything in this vault about what kind of person to be, and how that question decomposes into things you can actually name, notice and practise.

The map has a spine: an ancient philosophical question (_what is a good character?_), a modern empirical taxonomy that operationalises it (_VIA_), a mechanism that explains how it is acquired (_habituation through values-aligned action_), and a counterweight that stops the whole thing curdling into self-blame (_capacity is not character_). Read it in that order—each layer is unintelligible without the one above it.

---

## 1. Foundations—Virtue Ethics

Before the taxonomy, the question the taxonomy is answering.

- [[The Concept of a Character Ideal]]—the aspirational guide at the centre of virtue ethics; Aristotle's golden mean as the desirable middle between defect and excess (courage between cowardice and recklessness).
- [[Aristotle Distinguished Between Episteme, Techne, and Phronesis]]—the tripartite split of knowledge. Virtue lives in _praxis_, not _theoria_: it cannot be held purely as theory.
- [[Phronesis (Practical Wisdom) is Knowledge Gained Through Action and Experience]]—practical wisdom as the bridge between knowing the good and doing it. The keystone claim of this whole map.
- [[Akrasia is Acting Against One's Better Judgment]]—the failure mode phronesis is defined against: knowing what is good and failing to do it. Holton's refinement (akrasia vs. weakness of will) matters here, because the two have different remedies.

> The load-bearing asymmetry: virtue is not a belief you hold, it is a disposition you have built. Everything below is downstream of that.

## 2. The VIA Framework—Six Virtues, Twenty-Four Strengths

The VIA (Values in Action) Classification is positive psychology's attempt to give virtue ethics an empirical, cross-cultural vocabulary. It identifies 24 measurable character strengths grouped under six virtues found in most moral traditions. Its value here is actionability: "be a better person" is not a next action; "practise judgment by examining the opposing case first" is.

### 2.1 [[VIA Virtue of Wisdom|Wisdom]]

- [[Creativity is Thinking of Novel and Productive Ways to Do Things|Creativity]]
- [[Curiosity is Taking an Interest in Experience for its Own Sake|Curiosity]]
- [[Judgment is Thinking Things Through and Examining All Sides|Judgment]]
- [[Love of Learning is Mastering New Skills and Knowledge|Love of Learning]]
- [[Perspective is Providing Wise Counsel to Others|Perspective]]

### 2.2 [[VIA Virtue of Courage|Courage]]

## 3. Character as Constructed, Not Discovered

The bridge from taxonomy to mechanism. If character were a fixed trait you _have_, a strengths list would be a personality test. It is not—it is a curriculum.

- [[SoT - Values and Eudaimonia]]—the anchor SoT. Eudaimonic well-being arises from acting in alignment with chosen values, not from pursuing pleasure. Distinguishes the phasic hedonic spike from the tonic eudaimonic hum, and gives the neurobiology.
- [[The Self is Constructed Through a Commitment to Chosen Values]]—the self is an act of construction, not a pre-existing entity to be found.
- [[Integrity Means Acting in Accordance With Chosen Values]]—integrity as a _structural_ description (a bridge has integrity when it holds under load), not a moral verdict.
- [[Values Serve as Navigational Compass in Absence of Absolute Truth]]—why chosen values remain workable without metaphysical certainty.
- [[Aligning Actions with Core Values Makes Them Feel More Natural]]—the felt payoff of alignment.
- [[Humans Retain Freedom to Choose Their Response to Any Situation Regardless of Circumstances]]—the enabling condition for any of this being possible at all.

## 4. From Virtue to Practice—Habituation

Aristotle's claim that virtue is acquired by habituation, cashed out in this vault's habit and discipline material.

- [[MOC - How to Build Discipline]]—the practice-side map. Enter here for the mechanics.
- [[Discipline and Values]]—discipline is conscious alignment with values, not self-denial. The hinge note joining §3 to §4.
- [[Disciplined Choices are Votes for Your Future Self]]—the per-decision unit of character formation.
- [[Success is the Result of Disciplined Repetition]]—repetition, not talent, as the mechanism.
- [[Discipline Is a Property of External Structure More Than of Will]]—environment over willpower. Critical for the §6 counterweight: if discipline is largely a property of the situation a person is standing in, then reading its absence as a character defect is a category error. (Merged 2026-08-01 with the former `Systems and Environment are the Backbone of Discipline`.)
- [[Habit Change Must Align With Personal Values Not External Expectations]]—habit changes that skip §3 fail.
- [[SoT - Habit Formation Framework]]—the underlying formation model.
- [[Protecting Attention is the First Rule of Discipline]]—the modern precondition.
- [[MOC - The 7 Habits - The Maturity Continuum]]—an alternative, principle-centred route from dependence through independence to interdependence. A rival curriculum to VIA, not a subset of it.

## 6. The Counterweight—When Character Talk Goes Wrong

The most important section for practical use. A virtue vocabulary is a precision instrument for describing behaviour and a blunt one for explaining it. Misapplied, it converts capacity limits into moral failings and fuels shame spirals. These notes are the brake.

- [[Reframe Habit Setbacks as Capacity Limits Not Character Flaws]]—the canonical statement. Failure to sustain drastic change is a predictable capacity outcome, not laziness.
- [[ADHD Task-Paralysis Reflects DMN-FPN Maturational Lag, Not Willpower Failure]]—the neurological instance.
- [[Hedonic Adaptation to Achieved Goals Is a Biological Certainty, Not a Character Failing]]—the affective instance.
- [[Binary Person-Judgement Is a Cognitive Default, Not a Character Flaw]]—the meta-instance: even the _tendency to judge character in binary terms_ is a cognitive default rather than a defect.
- [[Misinterpretation as Character Flaws]]—the interpersonal instance: attributing others' behaviour to character rather than situation.
- [[SoT - Bonhoeffer's Theory of Functional Stupidity]]—the sociological counterweight: stupidity as a structural/sociological condition rather than an individual vice. Note this SoT _contradicts_ the Binary Person-Judgement claim in the live graph—a real, unresolved tension, not a filing error.
- [[MOC - Shame]] and [[SoT - ADHD Self-Compassion & Strengths]]—the failure mode this section exists to prevent, and the repair.

> Operating rule derived from this section: use virtue language descriptively and prospectively ("what would perseverance look like here?"), never diagnostically and retrospectively ("I lack perseverance").

## 7. Adjacent Traditions

Other answers to the same question, kept separate rather than assimilated into VIA.

- [[SoT - Logotherapy and the Will to Meaning]]—Frankl. Meaning as the primary motivation; [[Meaning Is Discovered Through Creative Experiential and Attitudinal Values]] and [[Tragic Optimism Is Hope and Meaning That Acknowledges Rather Than Denies the Tragic Triad]].
- [[SoT - Miyamoto Musashi's Philosophy on Mastering Emotions]]—_heijō-shin_, ordinary mind. Emotional mastery as constancy, not suppression.
- [[SoT - Wabi-Sabi]] and [[Taru o shiru is the practice of contentment with what one has]]—Japanese aesthetics of imperfection and sufficiency; a direct counter to perfectionist readings of a character ideal.
- [[SoT - The Philosophy of the Absurd (Camus)]]—the rival position: meaning is constructed in defiance, not discovered.
- [[SoT - Peter Singer's Famine, Affluence, and Morality]]—the consequentialist rival to virtue ethics. Asks what you must _do_, not what you must _be_.
- [[Evil Attacks the Shared Moral Community Itself Not Merely a Norm Within It]]—the far pole of the character axis.
- [[Parable of the Bitter Spring]]—a narrative treatment of character formation.
- [[SoT - Personal Agency and Transformation]] and [[SoT - Intentional Living]]—the agency substrate.

## Tensions & Gaps

- Two parallel strength vocabularies. [[Perseverance is Finishing What One Starts Despite Obstacles]] / [[Perseverance is Finishing What You Start Despite Difficulty]] and [[Self-Regulation is Disciplining One's Feelings and Appetites]] / [[Self-Regulation is Managing Your Emotions and Actions]] are near-duplicate pairs—the formal version and the child-facing version of the same strength. Merging them would break §5's readability; leaving them splits backlinks. Unresolved. The cheapest fix is aliasing rather than merging.
- VIA is a taxonomy, not a theory. It tells you what strengths exist and that they cluster; it does not explain how one is acquired or why the six virtues are the right cut. §1 (Aristotle) and §4 (habituation) are doing the explanatory work VIA does not do. Do not mistake the list's tidiness for a causal model.
- The empirical status of the six-virtue structure is weaker than the map implies. The claim of cross-cultural universality is contested and the factor structure has not replicated cleanly. This map treats VIA as a _useful vocabulary_, not a validated ontology—consistent with the Utility-over-Truth axiom.
- §6 contradicts §2 in tone if not in content. §2 supplies a vocabulary for naming what a person lacks; §6 says never use it that way. Both are correct, but the resolution is a discipline (prospective, not diagnostic) rather than a fact. The live `contradicts` edge between [[SoT - Bonhoeffer's Theory of Functional Stupidity]] and [[Binary Person-Judgement Is a Cognitive Default, Not a Character Flaw]] is the sharpest instance and remains open.
- Missing sibling note. §5 has three of four branches split out; "Thinking & Creating Skills (Your Clever Brain)" is still only a heading inside [[A Concept Map for a Flourishing Human]].
- Under-connected strengths. Several of the 24 are one-line stubs with no inbound links from real use (spirituality, humor, love, appreciation of beauty). They are placeholders in a taxonomy rather than live nodes; the map does not yet distinguish the two.
- No archery note yet. Recurve archery is the obvious live testbed for perseverance, self-regulation and zest ([[MOC - Archery Shot Process]] exists but carries no character edges). An unexploited link.
