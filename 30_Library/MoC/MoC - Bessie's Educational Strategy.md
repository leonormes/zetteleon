## MoC - Bessie's Educational Strategy

> **The Core Mission:** To reframe school not as a "prison sentence" but as a "Skill-Building Workshop," focusing on the _process_ of learning rather than the _outcome_ of grades. This strategy is tailored for a highly creative, verbal, and "big picture" thinker with ADHD and Dyslexia.

### 1. The Core Philosophy: "Opportunity, Not Obligation"

We shift the narrative from "You have to do this" to "This is a tool for your future self."

- **The Workshop Model:** School is a place to acquire tools (resilience, communication, logic) that she can use for her own passions (film, art, horses).
- **Deconstructing Comparison:** Actively fighting the "Judge" by focusing on **Generative Knowledge** (creating) rather than **Applicative Knowledge** (following rules).
    - _Mantra:_ "I am in competition only with who I was yesterday."

### 2. Mathematical Strategy: "The Art of Explanation"

Addressing the "Maths Brain" myth and the disconnect between school calculation and real mathematics.

- **Generative Maths:** Using activities (like the Handshake Puzzle or Wolf Pack Algebra) where she _invents_ the formula rather than just applying it.
- **The Toolbox Analogy:** Skills (fractions, algebra) are just tools in a carpenter's box. The goal is to build something beautiful (a proof, a pattern, a solution).
- **[[SoT - The Philosophy of Mathematics Education]]:** The theoretical backing for this approach (Lockhart's Lament).

### 3. Practical Protocols (The "How")

#### A. The "Now" Motivation (ADHD Friendly)

- **Gamification:** Using timers, "Quest Logs," and "XP" for mistakes.
- **Tangibility:** "Done Boards" and "Skill Trees" to make abstract progress visible.
- **Teachback:** Leveraging her verbal strength by having her "teach" concepts back to you.

#### B. The "Two-Second Rule" (Communication)

- To combat interruption and "timing blindness" in conversations.
- **Protocol:** Wait two seconds after the other person stops speaking before responding.

### 4. Subject-Specific Maps

#### Mathematics (KS3)

- **Concept Map:** Visualizing the 6 branches (Number, Algebra, Geometry, etc.) in Excalidraw.
- **RAG Rating:** Colour-coding confidence (Red/Amber/Green) to track growth, not failure.
- **Key Concepts:**
    - **Scaling:** Multiplication is stretching/shrinking (The Photocopier Model).
    - **Balance:** Algebra is a set of scales; the `=` sign means "same value as," not "the answer is."

#### Science

- **Focus:** Observation and reaction (e.g., "The Stone in Water").
- **Action:** Safe home experiments (Vinegar + Bicarbonate) to demystify abstract concepts.

### 5. Strategic Prompts (The "AI Tutor")

- **"The Translator":** A permanent prompt instruction for AI to act as her "Educational Strategist," translating standard tasks into Bessie-friendly formats (visual, chunked, interest-led).
