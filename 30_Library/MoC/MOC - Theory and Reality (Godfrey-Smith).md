## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

The guiding question, from [[Reading Plan — Theory and Reality (Godfrey-Smith)]]: how can science be a fallible, historically-changing, socially-organised activity and still deliver knowledge of a mind-independent world? Godfrey-Smith's book walks that question through a sequence of positions, each fixing a problem in the last and creating a new one: empiricism → Popper → Kuhn → the sociological challenge → naturalism → realism → explanation, truth and models.

> [!note] How this hub came to be
> The reading-plan project asked for one durable note per chapter, earned only when it clears a bar (changes a belief, recurs, connects two areas, is a live disagreement, or supports future writing), plus this MOC as the book hub. The chapter-by-chapter Reading Log in that project was never filled in, but the atomic notes it asked for were captured anyway—around forty of them, well past what a single pass through the book usually leaves behind. This MOC is the hub that was always planned, built now to organise what already exists rather than to log a fresh read.

## The Fourteen Chapters

Real chapter titles, confirmed from the book itself (Calibre id 1611, EPUB) rather than guessed—this resolves the "Correction: Edition Mismatch" item the reading-plan project left open.

### 1–3. Introduction, Empiricism, Evidence and Induction

- [[Classical Empiricism Treats Experience as the Sole Source of Knowledge]]
- [[Classical Rationalism Grounds Knowledge in Reason, Not the Senses]]—_the position empiricism answers._
- [[Descartes Requires Infallible "Clear and Distinct" Ideas for Rigorous Knowledge]]
- [[Logical Positivism Restricts Science's Aim to Predicting Sensory Patterns]]
- [[Logical Positivism Treats Unobservable Entities as Merely Linguistic Prediction Tools]]
- [[The Problem of Induction Means Past Regularity Cannot Logically Guarantee Future Regularity]]—_the crack empiricism could not close on its own._
- Three theories of justification the book sets out before turning to Popper: [[Internalism Requires Conscious First-Person Access to Justification]], [[Nozick's Tracking Theory Grounds Knowledge in Truth-Tracking Across Counterfactuals]], [[Goldman's Reliabilism Grounds Knowledge in a Reliable Belief-Forming Mechanism]]

### 4. Popper: Conjecture and Refutation

- [[Falsifiability Distinguishes Science from Dogma]]—_the demarcation criterion itself._
- [[Falsifiability Is Necessary But Not Sufficient for a Claim to Be Scientific]]—_qualifies it: measurement and design quality still matter._
- [[Duhem-Quine Holism Complicates Simple Falsification]]—_a falsifying result can always be pinned on a background assumption instead of the theory._
- [[Godfrey-Smith's Confirmation Objection to Strict Falsificationism]]—_Godfrey-Smith's own objection: if theories can never be confirmed, nothing justifies relying on them at all._

### 5–6. Kuhn's Revolution, Theories and Frameworks

- [[Kuhn's Four-Stage Model of Paradigm Collapse]]
- [[Kuhn's Incommensurability Thesis Denies Simple Cross-Paradigm Progress]]
- [[A Paradigm Only Collapses When a Viable Replacement Exists]]
- [[Paradigm Shifts Complete Through Generational Turnover, Not Persuasion]]
- [[Lakatos's Research Programmes Replace Kuhn's Single Paradigm With Competing Cores]]—_fixes Kuhn's single-paradigm rigidity: competing research programmes, not one paradigm at a time._
- [[Laudan Distinguishes Rationally Pursuing a Theory From Accepting It as True]]—_refines Lakatos further: pursuing a theory is not the same as accepting it._
- [[Galileo's Heliocentrism Is Feyerabend's Counterexample to Strict Empiricist Rules]]
- [[Feyerabend's Epistemological Anarchism Denies Any Fixed Scientific Method]]—_the most radical response to Kuhn: no fixed method at all._

### 7–8. The Challenge from Sociology of Science, Science Is Political

- [[Latour Treats Scientific Facts as Socially Constructed Through Networks of Power]]
- [[Standpoint Epistemology Claims Marginalised Groups See Facts the Privileged Cannot]]
- [[Longino's Feminist Empiricism Locates Objectivity in Diverse Community, Not Individual Detachment]]
- [[Merton's Reward System Drives Scientific Norms]]
- [[Science's Division of Labour Lets Individual Scientists Be Dogmatic]]—_Godfrey-Smith's answer to the sociological challenge: individuals can be dogmatic because the community, not the individual, is the unit that stays open-minded._
- [[Science's Adversarial Incentive Structure Rewards Overturning Established Theories]]
- [[Trusting a Scientific Authority Feels Psychologically Identical to Trusting a False One]]—_the epistemology-of-testimony problem the sociological challenge sharpens: delegated trust feels the same whether or not it is warranted._
- [[The Public Paper Trail of Science Makes Its Self-Correction Verifiable Without Firsthand Observation]]
- [[Reproducibility and Replicability Are Distinct Standards of Scientific Reliability]]
