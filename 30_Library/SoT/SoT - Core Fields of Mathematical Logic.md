## Core Fields of Mathematical Logic

Modern logic is a major branch of mathematics, subdivided into several key disciplines that form the foundation of the field.

### 1. Set Theory

Set theory is the "lingua franca" of modern mathematics.

- **Role:** Concepts like functions, numbers, and geometric spaces are defined in terms of sets.
- **Foundation:** The ZFC axioms (Zermelo-Fraenkel with Choice) provide the standard foundation, expressed in first-order logic.
- **Syntax:** Fundamental proposition: $x \in A$ (element $x$ is in set $A$).

### 2. Proof Theory

Proof theory treats mathematical proofs as formal objects of study.

- **Focus:** It analyses the structure of proofs, provability strength, and computational content.
- **Essence:** The "mathematics of mathematical logic."

### 3. Model Theory

Model theory studies the relationship between formal languages (syntax) and the mathematical structures (semantics) that "model" them.

- **Inquiry:** It asks what structures satisfy a given set of axioms (e.g., what structures satisfy the axioms of a Group or Field).
