## 1. Definitive Statement

> [!definition] Definition
> The **Dopamine Menu** (Dopamenu) is a pre-validated list of stimulating activities designed to supply the **Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)** with the neurotransmitters required for task initiation and regulation.
>
> It prevents the "Doom Scroll" loop by offering "Good Dopamine" (Active/Restorative) alternatives to "Cheap Dopamine" (Passive/Draining) when the brain signals boredom or fatigue.

---

## 2. The Menu

### 🥗 Appetizers (Quick Hits)
**Cost:** < 5 minutes
**Goal:** Instant state change to prime the PFC for Phase A (Action).
-   **Physical:** 20 Jumping Jacks / Push-ups.
-   **Sensory:** Splash cold water on face.
-   **Audio:** Blast *one* high-energy song (The "Hype Track").
-   **Order:** Tidy *one* visible surface (The "Desk Clear").
-   **Consumption:** Eat a piece of fruit / Drink a glass of water.

### 🥩 Main Courses (Deep Sustenance)
**Cost:** 30+ minutes
**Goal:** High-interest activities that induce Flow state and deep satisfaction.
-   **Creation:** Coding a "Spike" (Proof of Concept).
-   **Learning:** Watching a technical deep-dive (at 1.5x speed).
-   **Physical:** Lifting weights / Long walk.
-   **Social:** Meaningful conversation with a friend.
-   **Hobby:** [Insert Specific Hobby Here].

### 🍚 Sides (Accompaniments)
**Cost:** Concurrent
**Goal:** Background stimulation to occupy the Default Mode Network (DMN) so the Task Positive Network (TPN) can focus (Tangential Immersion).
-   **Audio:** White Noise / Brown Noise / Lo-Fi Beats.
-   **Visual:** "Study with Me" videos.
-   **Social:** Body Doubling (Flown, Focusmate).
-   **Fidget:** Fidget spinner / Stress ball.

### 🍩 Desserts (Treats)
**Cost:** Post-Task Only
**Goal:** Reward for completing a "Main Course" or "Action Block."
-   **Digital:** Social Media scroll (Time-boxed: 10 mins).
-   **Entertainment:** One episode of a show.
-   **Food:** A specific treat.

### ⚠️ The "Do Not Eat" List (Cheap Dopamine)
**Prohibited during "Work Hours"** as they deplete dopamine sensitivity without restoring energy.
-   Infinite Scroll (Twitter/Reddit/Shorts).
-   News Feeds.
-   Checking Email/Stats compulsively.

## 3. Usage Protocol

1.  **The Signal:** When you feel "Bored," "Stuck," or "Tired."
2.  **The Pause:** Stop. Do not open a new tab.
3.  **The Selection:** Pick **one** item from the *Appetizers* list.
4.  **The Execution:** Do it immediately.
5.  **The Result:** Attempt the **120-Second Commitment** on your task.
