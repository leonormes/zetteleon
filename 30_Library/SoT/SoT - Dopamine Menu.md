## 1. Goal: Intentional Stimulation

To prevent the ADHD brain from defaulting to "cheap dopamine" (infinite scrolling), we use a pre-compiled, categorized menu of stimulation activities to reduce decision fatigue.

---

## 2. The Menu

### 🥗 Appetizers (Quick Hits / Ignition)

**Cost:** < 5 minutes
**Goal:** Instant state change to lower the activation energy for work.
- **Physical:** 20 Jumping Jacks, cold water splash, 60-second pace.
- **Sensory:** Blast one "Hype Track," use a grip trainer.
- **Order:** Clear one visible surface (The "Desk Clear").

### 🥩 Main Courses (Deep Flow / Sustain)

**Cost:** 30+ minutes
**Goal:** High-interest activities that induce deep satisfaction.
- **Creation:** Coding a "Spike" (PoC), writing in a flow state.
- **Learning:** Technical deep-dives, complex problem-solving.
- **Physical:** Lifting weights, interval training.

### 🍚 Sides (Concurrent Stimulation / Tangential Immersion)

**Cost:** Simultaneous with work
**Goal:** Occupy the Default Mode Network (DMN) so the Task Positive Network (TPN) can focus.
- **Audio:** Brown noise, Lo-Fi, specialized video game soundtracks.
- **Visual:** "Study with Me" videos, biophilic views (nature/fractals).
- **Social:** Virtual Body Doubling (Focusmate, muted cameras).
- **Fidget:** Squeezing putty, mechanical keyboard tactile feedback.

### 🧘 Intermezzos (Boring Breaks)

**Cost:** 5-10 minutes (Between task blocks)
**Goal:** Metabolic reset without baseline spikes.
- **Action:** Staring at the ceiling, focused breathing, stretching, walking without a phone.
- **Rule:** **No Input.** Use this to let the TPN rest so you can return to flow without resistance.

### 🍩 Desserts (Post-Task Rewards)

**Cost:** Post-work only
**Goal:** High-dopamine rewards for completing an action block.
- **Digital:** Time-boxed social media scroll (10 mins).
- **Entertainment:** Single episode of a series.

---

## 3. The INCUP Fuel Injectors

When a task fails to ignite, reframe it using the **INCUP** filter to alter its "Dopamine Signature":

- **I**nterest: Connect to a special interest.
- **N**ovelty: Change environment or tool.
- **C**hallenge: Gamify (Time Trial mode).
- **U**rgency: Create artificial deadlines.
- **P**assion: Reframe the "Why" (Who does this help?).

---

## 4. Spite-Driven Development (Dark Fuel)

When "Self-Care" fails, use the **Adversarial Protocol**.

- **Mechanism:** Reframe the task as **Rebellion**.
- **Action:** Tag tasks with `#spite` or `#prove_them_wrong`.
- **Logic:** Activates the "Fight" response (Noradrenaline)Constructively, bypassing the paralyzing "Fear" of executive dysfunction.

---

## 5. Usage Protocol

1. **The Signal:** Feeling "Bored," "Stuck," or "Tired."
2. **The selection:** Pick one **Appetizer**.
3. **The Execution:** Do it immediately to achieve "Ignition."
4. **The Commit:** Attempt the **120-Second Commitment** (Two-Minute Rule) on the main task.
5. **The Guardrail:** **No Dopamine Stacking.** Do not combine loud music with pre-workout and intense focus; this ensures your baseline doesn't crash post-session.
