## 1. Definitive Statement

The **Hinge** is a posterior chain-dominant movement pattern characterised by maximal hip flexion and minimal knee flexion. It is the primary mechanism for picking up loads from the floor and developing explosive power.

## 2. Core Mechanics

- **Primary Joints:** Hips (Flexion/Extension).
- **Secondary Joints:** Knees (Minimal flexion), Ankles (Stability).
- **Key Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Erector Spinae.
- **Biomechanical Goal:** Maintain a neutral spine while shifting the centre of mass backward.

## 3. Stability & Stillness (Isometrics)

To build the foundation for the Hinge, focus on:

- **Superman**: Prone hold lifting chest and legs. Counteracts 'inactive' muscles from sitting and protects the spine.
- **Glute Bridge**: Supine hold with hips raised. Corrects postural imbalances by creating a rigid posterior chain.
- **Hinge Hold**: Maintaining the "bottom" position of a hinge with a flat back and active hamstrings.

## 4. Exercises (Collection)

- **Deadlift**: The definitive bilateral hinge pattern.
- **Good Mornings**: Focused hamstring loading via the hinge mechanism.
