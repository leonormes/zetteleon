## 1. Definitive Statement

> [!definition] Emotional Regulation in ADHD
> ADHD is not just a cognitive disorder; it is an **Emotional Regulation disorder**. The brain’s inability to dampen emotional signals leads to "Emotional Flooding," where feelings of shame or rejection become as intense as physical pain.

---

## 2. The Shame-Procrastination Cycle

A self-reinforcing feedback loop where failure leads to shame, which leads to avoidance.

1. **EF Failure:** You miss a deadline or forget a task.
2. **Shame Development:** You feel "broken" or "lazy" (Character-based judgment).
3. **Anticipatory Anxiety:** Your brain predicts the "Pain" of the next task and the "Wall of Awful" associated with it.
4. **Avoidance (Mood Repair):** To escape the pain, you flee to low-effort dopamine (scrolling).
5. **Performance Failure:** The avoidance causes further failure.
6. **Identity Reinforcement:** The loop "proves" you are incompetent, deepening the shame for the next round.

---

## 3. Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD)

RSD is a neurological hypersensitivity where perceived or actual rejection is processed by the brain as a **Physical Threat**.

- **The Emotional Switch:** The brain fails to filter signals of social disapproval.
- **The Protective Response:**
    - **Avoidance:** Refusing to share work or take risks.
    - **People Pleasing:** Sacrificing authenticity to ensure "Acceptability."
    - **Hypervigilance:** Scanning for flaws pre-emptively to "beat others to the punch."

---

## 4. The Internal World: The Only Safe Haven

For those with RSD, the **Internal World** is the only space where ideas are safe from the "Crucible of Critique."

- **The Peril of Sharing:** Moving an idea from "Internal" to "External" is high-stakes. The creator perceives the potential for criticism as a trigger for unbearable emotional pain.
- **The Defense:** Private externalization (writing for yourself only) is a necessary bridge before public sharing.

---

## 5. Strategies for Interruption

Breaking these cycles requires moving from "Shame Mode" to "Debugging Mode."

### 5.1 "Capacity, Not Character"

Reframe failures as **Hardware Limitations** (Empty RAM/Battery) rather than moral failings. You aren't lazy; you are out of resources.

### 5.2 The "Safe Mode" Protocol

When trapped in a shame spiral:

1. **Collapse the List:** Delete everything except **one** single priority.
2. **Self-Compassion:** Acknowledge the glitch. "My brain is in a loop."
3. **External Anchor:** Use Body Doubling to get back into the "Motion State" without having to "Decide" to start.

### 5.3 Defusion (ACT)

Shift from "I am bored/stupid" to "I am having the thought that I am bored/stupid." This creates space between the emotion and the self.
