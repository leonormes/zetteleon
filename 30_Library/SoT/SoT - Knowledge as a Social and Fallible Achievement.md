## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Method alone does not explain why we should trust what science produces, because scientists are people and people are poor at tracking truth. The vault records two kinds of answer. One says the social side undermines objectivity, and the other says the social structure is what produces it. It then asks how someone who cannot check the evidence personally can tell a genuine consensus from groupthink.

## 1. The Social Challenge and the Replies

| Question | Claim | Counter-claim | What moved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Are scientific facts constructed? | [[Latour Treats Scientific Facts as Socially Constructed Through Networks of Power]]: empirical truth is a resource used in debates to gain status. | [[Science's Division of Labour Lets Individual Scientists Be Dogmatic]]: the community divides the labour, so individuals can be dogmatic while rivals do the critical testing. | The unit of objectivity shifted from the individual to the community. |
| Whose standpoint counts? | [[Standpoint Epistemology Claims Marginalised Groups See Facts the Privileged Cannot]]: situatedness gives some groups access to facts others miss. | [[Longino's Feminist Empiricism Locates Objectivity in Diverse Community, Not Individual Detachment]]: objectivity comes from a diverse community checking each other's biases. | Diversity became a mechanism of error correction, not only a matter of fairness. |
| Does the system protect the status quo? | (the assumption the note attacks) | [[Science's Adversarial Incentive Structure Rewards Overturning Established Theories]]: researchers are rewarded for tearing down established ideas. | A consensus is evidence of surviving many attempts to break it. |
| Are individuals reliable? | [[Human Cognition Evolved for Social Survival, Not for Tracking Objective Truth]]: a felt sense of being right is not evidence of accuracy. | [[Science Is Trustworthy Because Its Structure Corrects for Bias, Not Because Scientists Are Less Biased]]: the structure builds correction in, so no one person has to be unbiased. | Trust moves from people to process. |

## 2. Telling Genuine Consensus From Groupthink

- [[Contested Edges Signal a Healthy Scientific Consensus]]—_the core is settled and the boundary is still argued over._
- [[Consilience Signals Genuine Scientific Consensus]]—_several independent fields converge, where groupthink relies on one circular method._
- [[The Professional Treatment of Dissent Signals Whether a Consensus Is Genuine]]—_dissenters with verified flaws are rewarded, not excommunicated._
- [[Data Transparency Over Authority Distinguishes Genuine Consensus from Groupthink]]—_challenge is met with open data and repeatable method._
- [[Advance Predictive Risk Distinguishes Legitimate Consensus from Post-Hoc Rationalisation]]—_predictions are stated before observation, not fitted afterwards._
- [[Selective Citation of Successful Predictions Creates a Hindsight-Bias Illusion of Predictive Skill]]—_a caution: a record of memorable hits is not a track record._

## 3. Trust Without Checking

- [[Trusting a Scientific Authority Feels Psychologically Identical to Trusting a False One]]—_the testimony problem: delegated trust feels the same from the inside whether or not it is warranted._
- [[The Public Paper Trail of Science Makes Its Self-Correction Verifiable Without Firsthand Observation]]—_the correction leaves a public trail you can inspect without being in the lab._
- [[Open Knowledge Systems Prevail Over Closed Systems]]—_systems that expose claims to demonstration and refutation are more durable._
- [[Alchemy Protected Truth Through Obscurity]] and [[Mathematics Established Knowledge Through Open Demonstration]]—_the 17th-century contrast behind that claim._
- [[Preferring Science Is Itself a Falsifiable, Evidence-Based Belief, Not a Dogmatic One]]—_the preference for science is held on the same terms as any other belief._
- [[Q — Why Trust Science When My Brain Runs on the Same Bias-Prone Machinery as Everyone Who Believes Something False]]—_the open question this whole cluster is answering._

## 4. Working Knowledge

- Judge a consensus by its structure, not by the confidence of its members: look for contested edges, open data, rewarded dissent and predictions made in advance. (from section 2)
- Delegated trust is unavoidable, so the useful question is whether the process leaves a public trail you could check. (inferred from section 3)

## 5. Tensions & Gaps

- Latour's claim and the structural defence are both recorded, but no note says whether the division-of-labour reply fully answers the claim that truth is a status resource. The existing map calls it Godfrey-Smith's answer, so the relationship is held as a reply and not as a contradiction.
- Standpoint epistemology and Longino's view are both feminist epistemology, and no note compares them.
- The Godfrey-Smith map links a note on Merton's reward system that does not exist, so the norms-of-science claim has no atomic note.
