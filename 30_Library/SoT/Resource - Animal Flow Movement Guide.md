## 1. Definitive Statement

> Animal Flow is a ground-based movement practice that combines elements of gymnastics, yoga, and breakdancing to improve mobility, stability, and "reflexive strength" (moving as a unit). It is a "Practice," not a "Workout."

## 2. Technical Reference (The ABCs)

### I. The Base Positions (Static Stability)

_Check these points if you feel unstable._

| Position | Key Cue | Common Fault |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| Beast | "Knees 1 inch off floor." | Hips too high (like Bear). |
| Crab | "Shoulders down and back." | Shrugging shoulders to ears. |
| Ape | "Heels down, chest up." | Rounding the back. |
| Loaded Beast | "Load the spring." | Knees touching the floor. |

### II. The Transitions (Dynamic Movement)

A. Underswitch (The Connector)

- Concept: Rotating _under_ yourself to switch between Beast and Crab.
- Cue: "Lift opposite hand and foot. Push the gas pedal. Travel under the window."

B. Side Kickthrough (The Rotator)

- Concept: Lateral kick from Beast.
- Cue: "Drop the heel flat. Kick the leg straight. Pull the elbow back (Archer)."

C. Scorpion Reach (The Extender)

- Concept: A circular reach from Loaded Beast.
- Cue: "Drive knee to opposite wrist. Circle leg up and over. Drop head between arms."

## 3. Full Session Transcripts (Deep Dive)

### Video 1: The System (Base, Mobilise, Flow)

_Source: [YouTube Link](https://youtube.com/watch?v=CkFqQD-sMXk)_

Flow Sequence:

1. Set Crab
2. Left Leg Underswitch (to Beast)
3. Right Leg Side Kickthrough
4. Right Leg Underswitch (return to Crab)
5. Set Loaded Beast
6. Right Leg Front Step
7. Return to Loaded Beast
8. Set Beast -> Underswitch to Finish.

### Video 2: Maintenance Protocol

_Source: [YouTube Link](https://youtu.be/pJHCgMrAg38)_

- Intent: Reconnection, not fatigue.
- Frequency: 2x per week.
- Drills: Beast Hold, Kick Through, Deep Squat Flow, Bear/Lizard Crawl, 90/90 Hip Switch.

### Video 3: The 5 Foundational Crawls

_Source: [YouTube Link](https://youtu.be/jKVk9lK9kCk)_

1. Cat Crawl: Opposite hand/foot (reflexive).
2. Bear Crawl: Hips high, legs straight (posterior chain).
3. Ape Traverse: Lateral movement (side body).
4. Frog: Forward pushing strength (anterior chain).
5. Crab Walk: Reverse pushing strength (triceps/posterior).
