## 1. Goal: Bypassing the "Brainbound" Bottleneck

Task initiation fails when we rely on internal willpower to move from "Importance" (Abstract) to "Action" (Physical). This protocol offloads the cost of starting to external scaffolding.

---

## 2. Phase I: Situated Scaffolding (Space)

### A. The Cockpit Strategy (Spatial Offloading)

Do not hide your tasks in a digital tab.

- **Action:** Use a physical **Analog Dashboard** (Whiteboard/Post-its).
- **Mechanism:** Moving a physical object engages the parietal cortex, reducing prefrontal load.
- **Constraint:** Only **one** item in the active visual field at a time to mimic single-threaded focus.

### B. Contextual Stations (Cues of Identity)

Condition your brain to associate specific locations with specific modes.

- **Action:** Define a **"Start Zone"** (e.g., a specific chair or standing desk corner) strictly for initiation.
- **Rule:** Never relax or doom-scroll here. If you disengage, physically leave the space.

### C. Biophilic Reset (Soft Fascination)

- **Action:** Position your desk to gaze at a plant or a window with a natural view.
- **Mechanism:** Nature's fractals provide **"Soft Fascination,"** which restores attention resources without effort.

---

## 3. Phase II: Embodied Scaffolding (Kinetic)

### A. Micro-Movements for Arousal

The ADHD brain is often "under-aroused" and needs stimulation to engage.

- **Action:** When stuck at the "Wall of Awful," **do not sit still**.
- **Protocol:** Pace the room, use a wobble board, or squeeze a grip trainer.
- **Mechanism:** Movement increases circulating dopamine/norepinephrine, "cranking the engine" of the prefrontal cortex.

### B. Gestural Thinking

- **Action:** Stand up and explain the first step of the task out loud to an empty room, using exaggerated hand gestures.
- **Mechanism:** Offloads the structure of the task from your brain to your motor system.

---

## 4. Phase III: Distributed Scaffolding (Social)

### A. Virtual Body Doubling

- **Action:** Use Focusmate or a video call with a peer (mics muted, cameras on).
- **Mechanism:** The **"Hive Switch"** creates a field of accountability that overrides internal dopamine deficits.

### B. Cognitive Apprenticeship (Mimicry)

- **Action:** Watch a 5-minute "Study With Me" video.
- **Mechanism:** Primes neural pathways for work through imitation, bypassing the need for internal activation energy.

---

## 5. The Algorithm: The Launch Sequence

When resistance is high, execute this sequence:

1. **Select:** Pick one physical Post-it (The Goal). Stick it on your monitor.
2. **Kinetic:** Stand up. Pace for 60 seconds while verbalizing the first action (e.g., "I will open the file").
3. **Context:** Sit in your **Start Zone** or trigger a **Body Doubling** session.
4. **Execute:** You have now offloaded the initiation cost. Begin.
