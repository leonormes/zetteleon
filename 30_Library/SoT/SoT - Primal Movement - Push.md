## 1. Definitive Statement

The **Push** pattern involves moving a load away from the body or the body away from a surface. It encompasses both horizontal (e.g., push-up) and vertical (e.g., overhead press) planes.

## 2. Core Mechanics

- **Primary Joints:** Shoulders (Flexion/Abduction), Elbows (Extension).
- **Key Muscles:** Pectorals, Triceps, Anterior Deltoids, Serratus Anterior.
- **Biomechanical Goal:** Maintain shoulder "packing" (stability) while generating force through the upper limb.

## 3. Stability & Stillness (Isometrics)

To build the foundation for the Push, focus on:

- **Long Lever Plank**: Elbows positioned further forward than a standard plank to increase core demand and protect the lower back.
- **Wrist Conditioning**: Backhand weight shifts and static holds to prepare the \"Hardware\" for hand-balancing and load bearing.
- **Plank**: Foundation for transverse abdominis strength.

## 4. Exercises (Collection)

- **Push-ups**: A staple for the chest, triceps, and serratus anterior.
- **Dips**: Primary builder for the chest and anterior shoulders.
- **Bent-Arm Bear Walk**: Hands-and-feet locomotion with bent elbows to force rotator cuff stabilisation.
- **Spider-Man Push-Up**: Bringing a knee to the elbow while lowering to integrate rotation and hip mobility.
- **The Beast (Animal Flow)**: Fundamental prone quadrupedal hold and movement.
