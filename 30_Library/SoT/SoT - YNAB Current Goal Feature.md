## YNAB Current Goal Feature

> [!abstract] The Mechanism
> The **Current Goal** feature leverages the **Mere Exposure Effect** by placing high-priority financial targets at the top of the YNAB Home Tab. This "top of mind" visibility interrupts impulsive spending loops.

### 1. Implementation Logic

To activate this feature, a category must meet specific criteria:

- **Target Type:** Must use a **Custom Target** (non-repeating).
- **Visibility:** Once pinned, it displays progress bars, target dates, and remaining amounts prominently on the mobile dashboard.

### 2. Functional Behaviors

When setting the custom target, users choose between two logic models:

| Mode | Logic | Best For |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| **Set Aside** | Allows spending _from_ the category without lowering the "Goal Progress" percentage. | **Multi-stage costs** (e.g., Holidays: booking flights, then hotel, then car). |
| **Have a Balance of** | Strict accountability. Spending money from the category _reduces_ the progress bar. | **One-time purchases** (e.g., Buying a car, Engagement Ring). |

### 3. Strategic Mental Models

#### A. The Test Drive

- **Scenario:** Considering a new recurring cost (e.g., a pet or a car payment).
- **Action:** Set a Current Goal to save that exact amount for 6 months.
- **Result:** proves affordability before committing to the contract.

#### B. The Cushion

- **Scenario:** Building an emergency fund or "Income Replacement" fund.
- **Action:** Visualizes the slow, dollar-by-dollar growth of security.

#### C. The Trade-off (Opportunity Cost)

- **Scenario:** Preventing impulse buys.
- **Action:** The goal acts as a psychological barrier. "Do I want these slippers more than I want the [Engagement Ring]?"
