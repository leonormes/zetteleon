## 1. Definitive Statement

The **Lunge** is a single-leg dominant pattern that bridges the gap between static bilateral stances and dynamic gait. It requires significant balance, unilateral stability, and pelvic control.

## 2. Core Mechanics

- **Primary Joints:** Knees, Hips (Asymmetric flexion/extension).
- **Key Muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes, Hamstrings, Hip Stabilisers (Glute Medius).
- **Biomechanical Goal:** Control the descent while preventing knee valgus (caving) and maintaining pelvic level.

## 3. Stability & Stillness (Isometrics)

To build the foundation for the Lunge, focus on:

- **Reverse Lunge Iso Hold**: Maintaining the split stance with the back knee hovering to isolate single-leg stability and correct imbalances.
- **Split Squat Hold**: Holding the position with the back knee hovering just above the floor.
- **Single-Leg Stand**: Developing the \"root\" strength required for unilateral loading.

## 4. Exercises (Collection)

- **Shrimp Squat**: A single-leg squat where the non-working knee touches the floor behind you. Excellent for exposing balance deficits.
- **Traveling Lunge**: Dynamic progression of the split stance pattern.
- **Note on Imbalances**: Lunges are the primary tool for identifying and correcting strength/mobility discrepancies between legs (e.g., glute activation issues).
