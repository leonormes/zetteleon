## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

To discuss "Purpose," we must first define the ontological status of the universe. The Taxonomy of Cosmic Purpose classifies four possible states of reality based on the relationship between Potentiality (Capacity for purpose) and Actuality (Existence of purpose).

---

## The Four Universes Framework

### 1. Latent Purpose (Potentiality without Actuality)

- Definition: The universe contains the raw ingredients and laws necessary for purpose to emerge, but no agent has yet actualized it.
- Analogy: A fertile field waiting for a farmer.
- Worldview: Materialist / Secular Humanist. Before life, the universe was a silent machine. Purpose is a late-stage emergent property created by conscious beings (us).

### 2. Created Purpose (Potentiality with Actuality)

- Definition: An agent has utilized the universe's potential to establish a specific purpose.
- Analogy: A house built by an architect.
- Worldview:
    - Theistic: A Creator (God) designed the universe for a reason (e.g., Glorification, Love). Purpose existed _before_ the universe in the mind of God.
    - Existentialist (Sartre): Humans (the agents) create purpose in a universe that allows it. "Existence precedes Essence."

### 3. Absolute Purposelessness (No Potentiality, No Actuality)

- Definition: The universe is fundamentally incapable of having a purpose. "Why?" is a category error.
- Analogy: Asking for the "personality" of a rock.
- Worldview: Nihilism / Absurdism.
    - Camus: The "Absurd" is the conflict between the human search for meaning and the "unreasonable silence" of this universe.
    - _See: [[SoT - The Philosophy of the Absurd (Camus)]]_

### 4. Inherent Purpose (Actuality without Potentiality)

- Definition: Purpose is not created; it is a fundamental, objective feature of reality, like gravity or mathematics. It simply _is_.
- Analogy: Mathematics. We do not "create" primes; we discover them.
- Worldview:
    - Platonism: The universe strives towards the eternal "Form of the Good."
    - Stoicism: The _Logos_ (Divine Reason) guides the cosmos. Purpose is the rational unfolding of this principle.
    - Pantheism (Spinoza): God _is_ Nature. The purpose is the perfect, logical self-expression of the universe.
    - _See: [[Numbers as Abstract Objects (Platonism)]]_

## Application: The "Pre-Life" Question

"Did purpose exist before life?"

| Framework | Answer | Reason |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| Latent | No | Purpose requires a mind; minds evolved later. |
| Created | Yes | It existed in the mind of the Creator. |
| Absolute | No | It never existed and never will. |
| Inherent | Yes | It is woven into the fabric of spacetime/logic itself. |
