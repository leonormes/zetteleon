## 1. Definitive Statement

The **Pull** pattern involves moving a load toward the body or the body toward a surface. It is essential for posterior chain health and countering the postural effects of prolonged sitting.

## 2. Core Mechanics

- **Primary Joints:** Shoulders (Extension/Adduction), Elbows (Flexion).
- **Key Muscles:** Latissimus Dorsi, Rhomboids, Trapezius, Biceps.
- **Biomechanical Goal:** Initiate movement through scapular retraction before elbow flexion to ensure back-dominant loading.

## 3. Stability & Stillness (Isometrics)

To build the foundation for the Pull, focus on:

- **Isometric Row**: Holding the peak contraction of a row to strengthen the upper back and prevent rounded shoulders.
- **Hanging (The Foundation)**: See [[SoT - Hanging Mechanics and Progressions]] for detailed mechanics.
    - **Passive Hang:** For spinal decompression and lat mobility.
    - **Active Hang:** For scapular control and shoulder stability.
    - **Hollow Hang:** For core-to-upper-body integration.
- **Row Hold:** Holding the peak contraction of a row to build rhomboid endurance.

## 4. Exercises (Collection)

- **Pull-ups / Chin-ups**: The \"King of upper body exercises.\" Essential for lats, arms, and forearms. Requires [[SoT - Hanging Mechanics and Progressions|Hanging]] as a foundation.
- **The Dead Hang**: Foundational for vertical pulling and spinal health.
- **The Crab (Animal Flow)**: Supine quadrupedal pattern that strengthens the posterior chain.
- **Scapular Pulls**: Vertical pull activation to build foundation for active hanging and pull-up initiation.
