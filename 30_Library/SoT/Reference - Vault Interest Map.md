## Reference - Vault Interest Map

Derived from an LLM analysis of ~2,650 notes (1,075 atomic zettels, ~200 SoT/Protocol notes, 100+ MoCs, project notes) on 2026-06-10. Ranked by note volume.

### Core Interests

1. ADHD & executive function—the dominant theme (~90+ zettels, a dozen MoCs). Task initiation, hyperfixation-burnout cycles, dopamine, body doubling, routines, the knowing-doing gap. Leon studies his own cognition as an engineering problem.
2. Cloud infrastructure & Kubernetes—networking (CNI, kube-proxy, etcd), AKS/EKS, container primitives built from raw Linux namespaces/cgroups, Terraform/GitOps, Vault secrets, Grafana observability. Both professional (FitFile, NHS/OMOP) and hands-on hobby labs.
3. Knowledge management itself (PKM)—Zettelkasten theory, ProdOS, "processing is the work", proposition-centred notes. A strong meta-interest: notes about taking notes.
4. Productivity systems & behavioural design—GTD, habits, timeboxing, choice architecture, momentum ramps, protocols for everything. Closely fused with the ADHD theme.
5. AI & agentic engineering—LLM agents, context engineering, agent skill architecture, model routing (Hermes/OpenRouter), AI's impact on software engineering.
6. Philosophy & epistemology—what knowledge _is_ (Aristotle, Korzybski, Camus, Schopenhauer, Singer), models vs reality, thought vs language, meaning, ethics, the absurd.
7. Mathematics foundations—what maths _is_, set theory, type theory, Curry-Howard, prime numbers/RSA, order theory. First-principles curiosity rather than computation.
8. Software design philosophy—data-centric design, type-driven development, Rust (ownership, type mechanics), CUE, formal methods, "Simple Made Easy", pragmatism vs rigour.
9. Family, relationships & parenting—Bessie's GCSEs and SEN support, relationship maintenance, communication, family dynamics.
10. Physical health & movement—aerobic base training, primal movement, tendon resilience, isometrics, metabolic protocols.
11. Psychology & social dynamics—cognitive biases, shame, status, social stratification, transactional analysis, self-perception.
12. Lighter threads—cyberpunk, hip hop origins, fables/storytelling, photography, wabi-sabi, physics (relativity, time).

### The Unifying Pattern

A pull towards systems and first principles—whether the system is a Kubernetes cluster, a brain, mathematics, or a family, nearly every note asks "what is the underlying mechanism?"
