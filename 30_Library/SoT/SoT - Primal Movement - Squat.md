## 1. Definitive Statement

The **Squat** is a knee-dominant movement pattern involving simultaneous flexion of the hips, knees, and ankles. It is the fundamental pattern for lowering the centre of mass and vertical propulsion.

## 2. Core Mechanics

- **Primary Joints:** Knees, Hips, Ankles.
- **Key Muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes, Adductors, Core.
- **Biomechanical Goal:** Maintain an upright torso and "active" feet while descending between the hips.

## 3. Stability & Stillness (Isometrics)

To build the foundation for the Squat, focus on:

- **End-Range Squat Hold**: Holding the active bottom position for 10+ seconds to build control where the body is weakest.
- **Wall/Invisible Squat**: 90° knee flexion hold. Concentrates force on the thighs and knee stabilisers.
- **Ape Hold / Deep Squat Hold**: Staying active in the bottom position to open the hips.

## 4. Exercises (Collection)

- **Chair Squats**: Beginner regression to ensure safe mechanics and hip depth.
- **Duck Walks**: Walking while maintaining a deep squat to build end-range stability.
- **Gorilla Walks**: Wide stance lateral movement with knuckle support to target the frontal plane.
- **Bunny Hops**: Bouncing in a low squat to develop ankle elasticity.
- **The Ape (Animal Flow)**: Deep squat pattern used for lateral locomotion and hip opening.
