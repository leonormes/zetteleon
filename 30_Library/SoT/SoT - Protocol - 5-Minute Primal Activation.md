## ⚡ The 5-Minute Primal Flow

Perform each movement for 60 seconds. Transition immediately to the next to maintain momentum (MTTFA).

### 0:00 - 1:00 | Foundation: Wrist Prep & Static Beast

- **Pillars:** [[SoT - Primal Movement - Push|Push]], [[SoT - Primal Movement - Gait|Gait]] (Activation)
- **Action:** Circular wrist rolls for 20s, then hold **Static Beast** (knees hovering 1 inch off floor) for 40s.
- **Goal:** "Bring the system online" and stabilise the shoulders.

### 1:00 - 2:00 | Lower Body: Lateral Ape

- **Pillars:** [[SoT - Primal Movement - Squat|Squat]], [[SoT - Primal Movement - Hinge|Hinge]]
- **Action:** Deep squat position. Reach hands to the side, shift weight, and hop feet across.
- **Goal:** Open hips and decompress the lower back.

### 2:00 - 3:00 | Posture: Traveling Crab

- **Pillars:** [[SoT - Primal Movement - Pull|Pull]], [[SoT - Primal Movement - Gait|Gait]]
- **Action:** Supine position (chest up). Move forward and backward using opposite hand/foot.
- **Goal:** Counter "Tech Neck" and activate the posterior chain.

### 3:00 - 4:00 | Core & Flow: Side Kickthroughs

- **Pillars:** [[SoT - Primal Movement - Twist|Twist]]
- **Action:** From Beast, pivot on one hand/foot and kick the opposite leg through the midline.
- **Goal:** Rotational balance and midline stabilisation.

### 4:00 - 5:00 | Strength: Bent-Arm Bear Walk

- **Pillars:** [[SoT - Primal Movement - Push|Push]], [[SoT - Primal Movement - Gait|Gait]]
- **Action:** High-hip crawl with elbows bent at 90 degrees.
- **Goal:** Final shoulder burnout and rotator cuff resilience.
