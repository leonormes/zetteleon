## 1. Definitive Statement

**Intentionality** is the fundamental shift from living on "autopilot" (model-free learning) to acting with deliberate purpose (model-based learning). In Japanese, the kanji for intention combines **"Heart"** and **"Direction,"** suggesting that true intent is the alignment of one's path with core values.

## 2. Intention vs. Autopilot

- **Autopilot (Habit):** Reactive, patterned, and efficient. Useful for routine but indifferent to long-term value.
- **Intentionality:** A conscious override of automaticity. It requires the "Pause"—creating space between a stimulus and a response to insert a chosen action.

## 3. The Structural Pillar: Essentialism

Intentional living is tied to **Essentialism**—the disciplined pursuit of less.

- **Ownership of Time:** Moving from "I don't have time" to "I choose how to spend my hours."
- **Boredom as a Tool:** Embracing stillness to create the space necessary for deep work and creativity.

## 4. Operational Protocols

- **[[The Sacred No - Intentionality as Habit Intervention|The Sacred No]]:** Cultivating the ability to say no to distractions to protect the "Sacred Yes" of prioritised work.
- **[[The Third Urge Rule - Resilient Persistence|The Third Urge Rule]]:** A protocol for building resilience without rigidity during difficult tasks.
- **Habit Stacking:** Attaching new intentions to established anchors to reduce decision fatigue.
