# SoT - Behavioral Protocols

## 1. Protocol: Change (Map-Build-Dismantle)

*Goal: Engineering new behaviors using CBT principles.*

### Step 1: Map (Architecture Disclosure)
Make the unconscious visible. Write down the loop:
*   **Cue:** Trigger (Time/Feeling).
*   **Routine:** Action (Scrolling).
*   **Reward:** Benefit (Numbing).

### Step 2: Build (Design)
Design for the "Real Self," not the "Ideal Self."
*   **Habit Stacking:** "After I [Brush Teeth], I will [Meditate 1 min]."
*   **Friction Engineering:**
    *   *Add Friction:* Put phone in a timed lockbox.
    *   *Remove Friction:* Sleep in gym clothes.

### Step 3: Dismantle (Replacement Logic)
You cannot delete a habit; you can only replace the **Routine**.
*   *Rule:* Keep the Cue and Reward the same.
*   *Swap:* Cue (Stress) -> Routine (Doomscroll) -> Reward (Calm) BECOMES Cue (Stress) -> Routine (Box Breathing) -> Reward (Calm).

## 2. Protocol: Sustain (The Honeyman Method)

*Goal: Managing Energy Fluctuations (ADHD Resilience).*

To prevent "All-or-Nothing" collapse, define 3 tiers of execution.

### Tier 1: The Ideal (High Energy)
*   *Scope:* The "Pinterest Perfect" routine. Expansion work.
*   *Context:* 100% Battery. Rare.

### Tier 2: The Baseline (Normal)
*   *Scope:* Core maintenance. The standard day.
*   *Context:* 60-80% Battery. Sustainable.

### Tier 3: The Minimum (Survival)
*   *Scope:* The **MVA** (Minimum Viable Action) to keep the lights on.
*   *Rule:* Doing Tier 3 counts as **100% Success**. No guilt.
*   *Example:* Instead of "1h Gym", do "5 Pushups." Continuity > Intensity.

### The Morning Handshake
Check your battery daily. Declare your Tier. Adjust the Todoist view accordingly (e.g., in Tier 3, hide everything except P1 tasks).
