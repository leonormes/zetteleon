## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Once a method is agreed, a second question remains: what is science for? One side says the aim is prediction and empirical adequacy, with talk of unobservable entities as a convenient tool. The other says the aim is explanation of a real structure. The vault holds both sides, and the disagreement shows up again in a milder form when it asks whether a model that is known to be wrong can still be true enough.

## 1. Prediction Against Explanation

| Question | Claim | Counter-claim | What moved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Are unobservables real or tools? | [[Logical Positivism Treats Unobservable Entities as Merely Linguistic Prediction Tools]]: terms for electrons or genes are devices for predicting what we observe. | [[Deutsch Rejects Instrumentalism Because Prediction Is a Means, Not the Purpose, of Science]]: prediction is a means, the purpose is explaining reality. | The aim of science became a stated question rather than an assumption. |
| Does science need to claim truth about the hidden? | [[Constructive Empiricism Targets Empirical Adequacy, Not Truth About Unobservables]]: be empirically adequate, stay agnostic about hidden structure. | [[Evidence - Deutsch Says Theories Are Explanations Not Merely Predictions]]: theories are explanations, not merely predictions. | A middle position appeared that lets a scientist use a theory without believing its entities. |
| What marks a good theory? | [[Predictive Power Verifies a Theory via Its Outputs, Not Its Proofs]]: working technology verifies a theory without the observer needing the mathematics. | [[A Good Explanation Is Hard to Vary]]: a good explanation has details that play a functional role and cannot be swapped freely. | Reliability of output and quality of explanation are offered as separate tests. |
| How far does pursuing commit you? | [[Constructive Empiricism Separates Accepting a Theory from Believing Its Unobservables]]: accept and use without believing. | [[Laudan Distinguishes Rationally Pursuing a Theory From Accepting It as True]]: a scientist can pursue a theory without accepting it as true. | Commitment is split into pursuing, accepting and believing. |

## 2. Choosing Between Rival Explanations

- [[Underdetermination Means Multiple Theories Can Fit the Same Evidence]]—_fit to the data alone cannot decide._
- [[Occam's Razor Favours Fewer Assumptions Among Equally Explanatory Theories, Not Simplicity Itself]]—_a tie-breaker between theories that explain equally well._
- [[Abductive Reasoning Infers the Best Explanation for a Set of Observations]]—_inference to the best explanation, never guaranteed._
- [[Deutsch Rejects Empiricism for Conjecture-Led Theory Formation]]—_theories start as conjectures and experience chooses between them._
- [[Deutsch and Godfrey-Smith Converge on Rational Reliance From Different Grounds]]—_both justify relying on a well-tested theory, from different premises._
- [[Salmon Argued That Rejecting Induction Leaves Popper Without a Rational Basis for Prediction]]—_added 2026-10-23: the objection that Deutsch's hard-to-vary reply is set against, in the chat's account; the chat doubts the reply escapes the assumption that the world is regular._

## 3. The Same Split Applied to Models

- [[Instrumentalism vs Realism in Understanding]]—_the distinction restated for personal mental models._
- [[Effective Theory]]—_a model can be accurate within a domain while wrong at a deeper level._
- [[Pragmatic Truth Focuses on Utility Over Absolute Correctness]]—_Newtonian mechanics is true enough to build bridges._
- [[Radical Nominalism Treats Language as an Inherited, Provisional Tool, Not a Direct Conduit to Objective Reality]]—_language is a provisional tool, not a window on reality._
- [[SoT - Reality, Models, and the Limits of Accuracy]]—_the general statement that models are simplifications._

## 4. Working Knowledge

- When a source says a theory is "just a model", ask whether it means a tool for prediction or a claim about structure, because the two readings lead to different standards. (inferred)
- Prediction can verify that a theory works. Explanation is what lets you know why, and what to change when it fails. (inferred from the Deutsch notes)

## 5. Tensions & Gaps

- The two constructive empiricism notes are near duplicates, and no edge says which is primary.
- The vault does not say whether constructive empiricism counts as a form of instrumentalism or a separate position.
- No note supplies the strongest realist argument or an anti-realist reply to Deutsch, so the explanation side is stated by one author.
- Pragmatic truth and the truth requirement in [[SoT - What Knowledge Is]] are the same disagreement in a different setting, but no note connects them.
