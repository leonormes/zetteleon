## SoT - Cognitive Engineering Protocols

> [!definition] Definition
> Cognitive Engineering is the deliberate use of external tools (pen, paper, syntax) to regulate the nervous system and optimize Executive Function. It treats "Writing" not as an artistic output, but as a System Maintenance Tool (Defrag/Clear Cache).

---

### 1. Core Theory: Neuro-Synchronization

The brain struggles to process high-load emotion because it remains trapped in the "hot" loops of the Amygdala (Fear) and Mid-Cingulate Cortex (Pain).

- The Glitch: Unprocessed inputs consume Working Memory (RAM), causing "Brain Fog."
- The Fix: Writing forces the brain to translate abstract feeling into linear language (Syntax). This activates the Prefrontal Cortex (PFC), dampening the Amygdala and freeing up RAM.

---

### 2. Module A: The Brain Dump (Clear RAM)

_Goal: Immediate reduction of Cognitive Load. Use when overwhelmed._

#### Protocol

1. Isolate: Choose _one_ capture point (Digital Note or Physical Paper).
2. Dump (Capture Phase): Write down _everything_—tasks, worries, ideas, sensory annoyances.
    - Rule: Do not process. Do not organize. Do not filter. Just vomit.
3. Sort (Clarify Phase): _Only_ after the dump is complete:
    - Actionable: Move to Todoist.
    - Reference: Move to Obsidian.
    - Trash: Delete.

#### Module A.1: LLM-Assisted Sorting

For high-velocity capture or executive function fatigue, use the Action-First GTD method to offload the "Sorting" phase to an LLM.

- rel:: [[Protocol - Action-First GTD (LLM Chief of Staff)]]

#### The 10-Item Limit

If the dump is too big, list only the top 10 loudest items. Clear those first.
