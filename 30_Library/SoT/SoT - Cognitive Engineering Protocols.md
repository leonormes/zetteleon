# SoT - Cognitive Engineering Protocols

> [!definition] Definition
> **Cognitive Engineering** is the deliberate use of external tools (pen, paper, syntax) to regulate the nervous system and optimize Executive Function. It treats "Writing" not as an artistic output, but as a **System Maintenance Tool** (Defrag/Clear Cache).

---

## 1. Core Theory: Neuro-Synchronization

The brain struggles to process high-load emotion because it remains trapped in the "hot" loops of the Amygdala (Fear) and Mid-Cingulate Cortex (Pain).

* **The Glitch:** Unprocessed inputs consume Working Memory (RAM), causing "Brain Fog."
* **The Fix:** Writing forces the brain to translate abstract feeling into linear language (Syntax). This activates the **Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)**, dampening the Amygdala and freeing up RAM.

---

## 2. Module A: The Brain Dump (Clear RAM)

*Goal: Immediate reduction of Cognitive Load. Use when overwhelmed.*

### Protocol

1. **Isolate:** Choose *one* capture point (Digital Note or Physical Paper).
2. **Dump (Capture Phase):** Write down *everything*—tasks, worries, ideas, sensory annoyances.
    * **Rule:** **Do not process.** Do not organize. Do not filter. Just vomit.
3. **Sort (Clarify Phase):** *Only* after the dump is complete:
    * **Actionable:** Move to Todoist.
    * **Reference:** Move to Obsidian.
    * **Trash:** Delete.

### The 10-Item Limit

If the dump is too big, list only the **top 10** loudest items. Clear those first.

---

## 3. Module B: Expressive Writing (Emotional Repair)

*Goal: Emotional regulation and "Loop Completion" for lingering stress.*

### Protocol

1. **Trigger:** High emotional load, rumination, or "stuck" thoughts.
2. **Execute:** Write continuously for **15 minutes**.
    * **Constraint:** Do not stop typing/writing. Grammar does not matter. Truth is the only metric.
3. **The Burn:** Destroy the output (if analog) or delete it (if digital). This signals to the brain that the "threat" has been processed and discarded.

---

## 4. Module C: The Thinking Snapshot (Context Capture)

*Goal: Capturing the "Machinery" of a thought, not just the conclusion.*

### Protocol

When an insight strikes, do not write a textbook. Capture the **Metadata of the Thought**:

1. **Why-Now:** What triggered this specific thought? (e.g., "Bug in Auth Service").
2. **The Hunch:** What is the hypothesis?
3. **The Tension:** What contradicts this?
4. **Future-Hook:** "Future Me needs this when X happens."

---

## 5. The Toolkit (Analog Latency)

**Why Analog?**
Handwriting is physically slower than thinking. This **Cognitive Latency** forces the brain to slow down its processing loop, preventing the "Transcription Trap" (typing without thinking).
* **Theta/Alpha Waves:** Handwriting triggers brain states associated with deep learning.
* **Protocol:** Use pen and paper for *Stage 1 (Generation)* and *Emotional Repair*. Use Digital for *Storage*.
