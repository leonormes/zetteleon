## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

The divergence between human and AI cognition is a gap between Grounded Simulation (Human) and Statistical Prediction (AI). Humans are evolutionarily predisposed to attribute intelligence and agency to anything that masters language (the "Language-Intelligence Link"), leading to the Eliza Effect—the persistent anthropomorphism of LLMs despite knowing their mathematical nature.

## Working Knowledge

- Symbol Grounding: Humans understand meaning through multi-modal physical experience; LLMs understand meaning through high-dimensional word co-occurrence (Distributional Semantics).
- The Eliza Effect: A psychological phenomenon where users attribute human traits to computer programs, first documented with the 1960s ELIZA chatbot.
- Probabilistic vs. Causal: LLMs predict the next most likely token based on statistical weights; humans build causal mental models to simulate "what if" scenarios.
- Free Energy Principle: Biological intelligence acts to minimize "surprise" (entropy) to ensure survival; LLMs minimize a loss function without existential risk or "Internal State."

## Current Understanding

### 1. The Core Divergence: Syntax vs. Semantics

The primary disconnect is the Symbol Grounding Problem.

- Human (Grounded Cognition): Concepts are anchored in reality. Language is a lossy compression of a pre-linguistic "operating system" of physics and experience.
- LLM (Distributional Semantics): Meaning is a vector. It is a closed loop of language (the map) that cannot verify reality (the territory).

### 2. The Psychology of Misperception (The "Taken In" Factor)

Despite the technical reality of LLMs as "Advanced Autocomplete," humans find it difficult to remain objective due to:

- The Language-Intelligence Link: Historically, coherent language implied a mind. We use language as a proxy for thought, "filling in the blanks" when an LLM speaks empathetically.
- The "Black Box" Mystery: The lack of transparency in billions of parameters creates a vacuum filled by mystical or sci-fi explanations like "emergent consciousness."
- Cultural Conditioning: Decades of sci-fi (2001, Her) have primed us to expect AI as a self-aware entity, allowing marketing to exploit "Sentient AI" tropes.

### 3. Comparison: Technical Reality vs. Popular Perception

| Aspect | The Technical Reality | The Popular Perception |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| Process | Predicting the next likely token. | "Thinking" or "Reasoning." |
| Logic | Correlative & Statistical. | Causal & Intentional. |
| Knowledge | Static map of word relationships. | A conscious library of facts. |
| Errors | Statistical "hallucinations" (noise). | "Lying" or "Being confused." |
| Empathy | Mimicking tone from training data. | Genuinely caring about the user. |

### 4. Biological Architecture (Active Inference)

- Markov Blankets: Biological systems have a statistical boundary separating internal states (mind) from external states (world). LLMs have no "self" to protect, thus no inherent "meaning" derived from survival.
- Simulated Futures: Human planning is a Tree Search of simulated futures (allowing hypotheses to die in our stead). LLM "planning" is a Chain of Thought—text that _looks_ like a plan.

## Tensions & Gaps

- The Verification Gap: As LLMs improve, the Eliza Effect becomes harder to resist, even for experts who understand the underlying calculus.
- Agency vs. Tooling: At what point does a sufficiently complex simulation of agency require a different ethical or operational framework, regardless of "grounding"?
