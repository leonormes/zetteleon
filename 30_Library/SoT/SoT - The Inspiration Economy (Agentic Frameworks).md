## SoT - The Inspiration Economy (Agentic Frameworks)

> [!definition] Definition
> **Inspiration Economy (IE):** An economic logic based on "Capacity vs. Demand," where value comes from human *Intent* and *Problem Framing*.
> **Capital Economy (CE):** The old logic of "Supply vs. Demand," where value comes from *Execution Efficiency* (now commoditized by AI).

### 1. Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

As AI automates the **Execution Layer** (doing the work), human value migrates to the **Orchestration Layer** (defining the work). Success requires building a **Personal AI Infrastructure (PAI)** that converts a single human from a "Task Executor" (Human 2.0) to an "Idea Originator" (Human 3.0).

---

### 2. The SPQA Architecture

The theoretical model for "Understanding-based" software.

| Component | Role | Human vs. AI |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| **State** | **Context.** The totality of user data/history. | *Managed by Framework (Context Engineering).* |
| **Policy** | **Intent.** The mission, guardrails, and strategy. | **The Human Role (Problem Definer).** |
| **Question** | **Trigger.** The specific instruction or framing. | **The Human Role.** |
| **Action** | **Execution.** The output generated by the system. | *The AI Role (Agentic Labor).* |

---

### 3. The Augmentation Stack (Infrastructure)

To function as Human 3.0, one needs a technical stack to manage the "Swarm" of agents.

#### A. The Orchestration Layer

- **n8n (Glue):** Handles deterministic, linear integrations (SaaS glue).
- **LangGraph (Reasoning):** Handles complex, stateful workflows with loops and memory (Agentic logic).

#### B. Context Engineering

The primary management discipline of the IE.

- **Compaction:** Summarizing history to prevent "Context Rot."
- **Compiled View:** Treating context not as a raw log but as a structured, high-signal view derived from event streams (Google ADK model).

### 4. Framework Specialization

| Framework | Function |
|:--- |:--- |
