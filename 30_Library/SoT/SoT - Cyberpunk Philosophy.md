## 1. Socio-Economic Critique: Hyper-Neoliberalism

Cyberpunk serves as a diagnostic tool for late-stage capitalism, specifically **Neoliberalism** carried to its logical extreme.

### 1.1 Corporate Sovereignty

* **The State's Demise:** Governments are impotent or vestigial. Real power resides in Mega-Corporations (Zaibatsus).
* **Privatization of Everything:** Security (PMCs), healthcare (Trauma Team), and even law are commodities.
* **The "Slow Cancellation of the Future" (Mark Fisher):** A cultural stagnation where the future is just a recycled pastiche of 20th-century aesthetics because capitalism prioritizes efficiency and repetition over novelty.

### 1.2 The Gig Economy & Precarity

* **Barbaric Hedonism:** In the absence of meaning or social safety nets, life revolves around "spontaneous," insecure labor and the pursuit of synthetic pleasure (drugs, braindances) to numb the pain of systemic poverty.

## 2. Transhumanism & Identity

The genre explores the **Software of the Mind** and the fragility of "Humanity."

### 2.1 The Ship of Theseus

* **Cybernetics:** As the body is replaced by machines, at what point does one cease to be human?
* **The Empathy Metric:** Philip K. Dick proposed that **Empathy**, not intelligence, is the sole differentiator between human and machine.
    * *Case Study:* [[SoT - Cyberpunk Case Study - Blade Runner#2.2 Diagnostic Systems|The Voight-Kampff Paradox]]—Replicants fail due to lack of socialization, not lack of capacity.

### 2.2 Cognitive Commodification

* **Neural Interfaces:** The mind becomes a writable surface. Memories can be implanted, skills downloaded, and consciousness hacked.
* **Predatory Transhumanism:** Technology is not a liberation but a **capitalist venture**. Immortality and enhancement are luxury goods; the poor are harvested for parts.

## 3. Simulation Theory & Gnosticism

### 3.1 The Desert of the Real

* **Hyperreality (Baudrillard):** The map has replaced the territory. Society interacts with simulations (media, branding, the Matrix) rather than reality itself.
* **Gnostic Rebellion:** The "Red Pill" archetype represents the painful pursuit of **Truth** over the blissful ignorance of the simulation/control system.
