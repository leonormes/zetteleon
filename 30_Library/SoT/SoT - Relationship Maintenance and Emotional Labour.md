## 1. Definitive Statement

Relationship maintenance is the ongoing cognitive and emotional effort required to monitor, protect, and repair the relational interface. It is a shared responsibility that cannot be delegated to a single partner without compromising the integrity of the partnership.

## 2. The Labour of Repair

Repair is a distinct phase of communication that occurs _after_ a conflict. It involves:

- Reflection: Thinking about the issue between conversations.
- Initiation: Proactively bringing the topic back up to check on progress.
- Calibration: Proposing tangible changes to prevent recurrence.

## 3. Asymmetric Maintenance Patterns

A relationship becomes unstable when the labour of maintenance is asymmetric.

- The Sole Carrier: One partner researches solutions, follows up, and initiates all "difficult" talks.
- The Passive Responder: One partner only engages when forced, treats concerns as "your problem," and relies on "forgetting" to end the conversation.

## 4. The Diagnostic for Investment

The "Acid Test" for relational investment is Follow-up Frequency.

- Does the partner remember and revisit concerns that were raised?
- Does the partner initiate "repair" without being prompted?
- Logic: If a partner can remember details about their own interests/stress but "forgets" relationship concerns, they are categorising those concerns as "not my department."

## 5. Summary

A healthy relationship requires Collaborative Systems Design. It moves beyond scoring points to designing shared protocols (e.g., monthly check-ins with explicit commitments) that ensure both partners feel heard and respected.
