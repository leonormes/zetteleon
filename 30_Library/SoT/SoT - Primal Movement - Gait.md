## 1. Definitive Statement

**Gait** is the complex coordination of all other primal patterns into locomotion (walking, running, crawling). It is the ultimate expression of human movement and neuromuscular control.

## 2. Core Mechanics

- **Primary Mechanism:** The Cross-Crawl Pattern (Opposite arm and leg moving in unison).
- **Key Components:** Balance, momentum management, and energy recycling through elastic tissues.
- **Biomechanical Goal:** Efficiently transfer the centre of mass through space with minimal energy expenditure.

## 3. Stability & Stillness (Isometrics)

To build the foundation for Gait, focus on:

- **Static Crawling Holds:** (e.g., Static Beast or Crab) to build the requisite shoulder and core strength for quadrupedal movement.
- **Single-Leg Balance:** Vital for the "stance phase" of walking and running.

## 4. Exercises (Collection)

- **Traveling Beast**: Forward and backward prone quadrupedal movement.
- **Traveling Crab**: Supine locomotion to challenge posterior coordination.
- **Lateral Ape**: Side-to-side squat-based movement.
- **Bear Crawl**: High-hip locomotion for shoulder and hamstring loading.
