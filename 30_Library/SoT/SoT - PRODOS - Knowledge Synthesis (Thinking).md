# SoT - PRODOS - Knowledge Synthesis (Thinking)

> [!definition] The Core Axiom
> **Writing IS Thinking.**
> You do not write to record what you already know. You write to find out what you think. Writing acts as a "mirror," forcing vague neural patterns into linear, testable syntax.

---

## 1. The 3-Stage Architecture

Thinking is a production line. Do not mix the stages.

### Stage 1: Generation (The "Barf" Draft)

*Goal: Overcome the Inner Critic. Quantity > Quality.*
* **Protocol:** **"Brain Vomit."** Stream-of-consciousness dump.
* **Constraint:** No editing. No fact-checking. No backspacing.
* **Tool:** Analog Paper or a blank digital file (`HEAD` note).
* **Output:** Messy, raw, vital energy.

### Stage 2: Refinement (The Logic Engine)

*Goal: Structure and Logic. Clarity > Cleverness.*
* **Protocol:** **"The Strauss Method."**
    1. **Edit for Self:** Does this make sense to me?
    2. **Edit for Audience:** Is the language unambiguous?
    3. **Edit for Critics:** Where are the logical holes?
* **Constraint:** **The Confusion Constraint.** A reader may disagree, but they must never be confused.
* **Tool:** Digital Editor (Obsidian).

### Stage 3: Integration (The Wisdom Layer)

*Goal: Behavior Change. Wisdom > Knowledge.*
* **Protocol:** **"The Application Test."**
    * How does this change my behavior *today*?
    * If the answer is "It doesn't," the note is intellectual entertainment. Delete or Archive it.

---

## 2. Note Types (Thinking Scaffolds)

Stop treating all notes as "Information." Categorize them by their cognitive role:

| Type | Definition | Structure |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| **Model Note** | A framework for prediction. | "Analogy", "When it works", "Where it breaks". |
| **Move Note** | A specific tactical pattern. | "When in situation S, do move M, to get outcome O." |
| **Delta Note** | A record of learning. | "Previous Belief" vs "New Belief" + Evidence. |

---

## 3. The Unified Writing Process (How to Think)

### 3.1 The "Two Crappy Pages" Rule

Success is not "writing something good." Success is "writing two pages of anything." Lower the bar until you cannot fail.

### 3.2 The Dictum of Omission

"When in doubt, take it out."

* If a sentence does not advance the argument, it is friction.
* **Active Voice:** Use "The user clicked the button" (Active) over "The button was clicked by the user" (Passive).

---

## 4. Operational Integration (HEAD -> SoT)

1. **Spark:** Create `HEAD` note. Use **Stage 1 (Generation)**.
2. **Think:** Use **Stage 2 (Refinement)** to structure the model.
3. **Merge:** Use **Stage 3 (Integration)** to update the `SoT` note.
4. **Archive:** Delete the `HEAD` note.
