> [!definition] Definition
> **AI-Resilient Tasks:** Roles that leverage intrinsic human capacities (Intent, Empathy, Narrative) which AI cannot replicate.
> **The Shift:** From "Problem Solver" (finding answers) to "Problem Definer" (framing questions).

## 1. Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

As AI commoditizes execution (The Capital Economy), human value migrates to the edges: **Defining the Goal** (Telos) and **Connecting with Others** (Empathy). The middle layer—processing information—is dead.

---

## 2. The Four Resilient Roles

### A. The Problem Definer (Chief Question Officer)

*The Architect of Intent.*
- **Function:** AI excels at solving defined problems. The human value is in *framing* the right problem.
- **Key Skill:** **Context Engineering**—curating the information environment to guide the AI.
- **Telos Connection:** Aligns with the "Mission" layer of [[SoT - The Telos Method]].

### B. The Empathy Catalyst

*The Bridge of Trust.*
- **Function:** While AI simulates sentiment, it cannot build trust. Healing, community building, and leadership require shared human experience.
- **Key Skill:** **Emotional Resonance.**

### C. The Strategic Narrator

*The Visionary.*
- **Function:** AI provides probabilistic forecasts. Humans provide "Leaps of Logic" and craft the **Narrative** that mobilizes people.
- **Key Skill:** **Fusion Strategy**—blending AI data with geopolitical/market intuition.

### D. The Craft Master

*The Artisan.*
- **Function:** High-touch physical craftsmanship. The value lies in the "imperfection" and the story of the human hand.

## 3. The Economic Logic

| Economy                  | Logic               | Human Role            | AI Role                  |
|:----------------------- |:------------------ |:-------------------- |:----------------------- |
| **Capital (Legacy)**     | Supply vs. Demand   | Executor (Efficiency) | Competitor (Replacement) |
| **Inspiration (Future)** | Capacity vs. Demand | Creator (Intent)      | Partner (Amplifier)      |

---

## 4. ProdOS Application

- **Role Definition:** You are the **Problem Definer**. The AI (ProdOS) is the **Executor**.
- **Task Delegation:** Use **Agentic Frameworks** (n8n/LangGraph) to offload the "Capital Economy" tasks (Data entry, research aggregation).
- **Focus:** Dedicate "Deep Work" blocks solely to **Strategic Narrative** and **Problem Framing**.
