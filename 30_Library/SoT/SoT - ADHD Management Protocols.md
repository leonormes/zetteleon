## 1. Tactical Principle: Systemic Accommodation

We do not "fix" the brain; we build External Scaffolding that accounts for its failures. The goal is to offload the "Manager" (Executive Function) to the environment.

[depends_on:: [[ADHD Task Initiation Difficulty is a Neurological Issue Not Laziness]], strength=5, confidence=high] [depends_on:: [[Externalize Memory Aggressively (cognitive offloading)]], confidence=high] [extends:: [[ADHD Brain Wiring vs. Classic Productivity Systems]], confidence=high] [supports:: [[Productivity Systems Are Scaffolding Not the Engine of Progress]], confidence=medium]

_Rests on: task-initiation difficulty being neurological rather than moral, and on aggressive cognitive offloading. Specialises the general claim that ADHD wiring defeats classic productivity systems._

## 2. Environmental Design: Systematized Calm

The core principle: "Make good choices easy and bad choices hard."

[implements:: [[Reducing friction is a key strategy for building ADHD-friendly habits]], strength=5, confidence=high] [depends_on:: [[Habits are Automatic Behaviors Triggered by Environmental Cues]], confidence=high] [depends_on:: [[Making a behavior easy increases the likelihood it will become a habit]], confidence=high] [supports:: [[ADHD-Friendly Cleaning Strategies Focus on Cues and Transitions]], confidence=medium]

### 2.1 Spatial Zoning

- Active Zones (Hot): Items used daily must be visible and at waist-height. If it's in a drawer, it doesn't exist.
- Passive Zones (Cold): Low-frequency items go in opaque, high-shelf storage.
- The "Work Only" Zone: A specific desk or corner that anchors the "Work Mode" identity. Never relax here.

### 2.2 Visual Triage (The 3-Box Rule)

To prevent decision paralysis when decluttering:

1. Exit: Bin/Recycle.
2. Buffer: The "Maybe" box. Seal it. If unopened in 6 months, bin it.
3. Retain: Find a permanent home now.

## 3. Initiation Tactics: Breaking Inertia

[depends_on:: [[ADHD Causes Task Initiation Deficits Due to Dopamine Hyposensitivity]], strength=5, confidence=high] [depends_on:: [[Limbic Friction is the Activation Energy for Habits]], confidence=high] [depends_on:: [[ADHD Paralysis is the Inability to Start a Task Until it Becomes an Emergency]], confidence=medium]

_The whole section rests on a declared axiom: initiation deficits are dopaminergic, so every tactic below is an activation-energy reduction rather than a motivation appeal._

### 3.1 The Starter Task (Micro-Victories)

A Starter Task is an action so small it requires zero cognitive effort.

- The Rule: Must be < 5 minutes and purely physical (e.g., "Put on running shoes," "Clear desk corner").
- The Goal: Overcome inertia. Once the "motion state" is entered, momentum takes over.
- Realises [[Micro-Stepping Reduces Cognitive Load for Task Initiation]]. [implements:: [[Micro-Stepping Reduces Cognitive Load for Task Initiation]], strength=5, confidence=high]

### 3.2 The 120-Second Commitment

If a task feels daunting, commit to working for exactly 2 minutes. You are authorized to stop after 120 seconds.

[implements:: [[If a Next Action Takes Less Than Two Minutes Do It Immediately Rather Than Track It]], confidence=medium]

### 3.3 Body Doubling (The Hive Switch)

Leverage the "Observer Effect." Working in the presence of someone else (Focusmate, Co-working) triggers a "Hive Switch" that overrides internal resistance.

[implements:: [[Body Doubling for ADHD Focus]], confidence=high]

_Note: the live body-doubling note is framed as a personal experiment in "body doubling without the body" (self-recording). This section describes the classic co-presence form; the two are variants of the same Observer Effect mechanism, not the same protocol._

### 3.4 The Breadcrumb Method (Manual Feedback Loop)

Used as a "starter motor" for the brain when paralyzed by large tasks or low interest.

- Mechanism:
	- Quanta: Quantify work into arbitrary 5-to-10-minute intervals.
	- Visual Scaffolding: Pre-generate a physical visual representation (e.g., boxes in a notebook) for these intervals.
	- Check-and-Reset: Complete one interval, physically tick the box to trigger a minor dopamine response, and immediately proceed to the next micro-goal.
- Constraint: This is a high-overhead tactic. Use it to lower activation energy; once a flow state is achieved, abandon the manual tracking to focus on qualitative output.

[implements:: [[Rapid Feedback Loops are Essential for ADHD Motivation]], strength=5, confidence=high] [supports:: [[Externalizing Progress Makes it Tangible and Motivational]], confidence=high]

## 4. The Dopamine Menu (Fueling)

To prevent defaulting to "Junk Dopamine" (scrolling), select from a pre-compiled menu:

| Course | Duration | Example |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| Appetizers | < 5m | 20 Jumping Jacks, cold water splash, blast one "Hype Track." |
| Mains | 30m+ | Coding a prototype, intense workout, deep-dive learning. |
| Sides | Concurrent | Brown noise, Lo-fi music, fidget toys, "Study with Me" videos. |
| Intermezzos | 5-10m | Staring at the ceiling, breathing, walking without a phone. |
| Desserts | Post-work | Time-boxed gaming or social media. |

[depends_on:: [[Dopamine Regulates Motivation (Wanting) Separately from Pleasure (Liking)]], strength=5, confidence=high] [depends_on:: [[Dopamine Neurons Encode Reward Prediction Error, Not Pleasure]], confidence=medium] [implements:: [[Dopamine-Aware Planning Aligns Tasks with the Brain's Reward System]], confidence=high] [supports:: [[Designed Task Switching Leverages Dopamine Boosts for ADHD Motivation]], confidence=medium]

_The menu only works if wanting and liking are separable—otherwise "junk dopamine" would simply be the most enjoyable option and there would be nothing to substitute. See Tensions for what the menu does not account for._

## 5. The Four-Step Organization Framework

1. Brain Dump (Externalize): Immediately move all mental load to a physical/digital capture tool.
2. Hierarchical Planning: Yearly -> Monthly -> Weekly -> Daily (Limit to 3 Main Priorities).
3. External Cues: Use "Cognitive Prosthetics" (sticky notes, phone alerts).
4. Performance Tracking: Use habit trackers for "Objective Data."

[depends_on:: [[ADHD Working Memory Deficits Create a Compulsive Re-Planning Loop]], strength=5, confidence=high] [depends_on:: [[Writing Acts as an External Working Memory]], confidence=high] [implements:: [[A Next Action Must Be the Absolute Next Physical Visible Activity Required to Move a Situation Forward]], confidence=high] [supports:: [[For ADHD The Clarification Process Externalizes Decision-Making and Builds System Trust]], confidence=high]

_Step 1 exists specifically to break the compulsive re-planning loop named in the axiom: unwritten plans get re-derived from scratch every time working memory drops them._

## 6. Context-Specific: The Developer's Toolkit

### 6.1 Digital Scaffolding (The External Brain)

- IDE: Use linters and snippets to reduce Working Memory load.
- Task Management: Use rigid ticket breakdown (ProdOS/Todoist) to externalize "Next Actions."

[implements:: [[Digital Tools Help Externalize Memory and Structure for ADHD Developers]], strength=5, confidence=high]

### 6.2 Managing the "Wall of Code"

- Initiation: Coding requires massive context loading. Use Analog Anchors (Stack Tracing on paper) to load context before touching the keyboard.
- Time Blindness: Use physical Time Timers to visualize passing time during sprints.

[depends_on:: [[Hyperfixation Creates Time Blindness in ADHD]], confidence=high] [supports:: [[Spatial Arrangement Externalises Relational Structure to Reduce Working Memory Load]], confidence=medium]

### 6.3 Mitigating RSD in Code Reviews

- Pair Programming: Provides instant feedback and shared cognition, bypassing RSD spirals.
- Reframing: Code reviews are a critique of the _artifact_, not the _architect_.

[depends_on:: [[Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD)]], strength=5, confidence=high] [implements:: [[Collaborative Programming Mitigates RSD and Focus Issues in ADHD]], confidence=high]

## 7. Resilience & Tenacity Protocols

### 7.1 AMCC Training (The 15% Push)

To strengthen the "Tenacity Muscle" and break the "Novice High" quitting cycle:

- The Protocol: When you hit the "Wall of Boredom" or the impulse to stop arises, push for exactly 15% more (e.g., read one more page, write 5 more lines of code).
- The Objective: The goal is not the work itself, but the "Rep" of defiance. You are training the brain to ignore the signal to quit.

[supports:: [[The Quit on the Third Urge Rule]], strength=5, confidence=high]

_Companion protocol: [[The Quit on the Third Urge Rule]] declares this note as its `upstream` but was never linked back from here. Both build the same buffer between impulse and action—the 15% Push sets the size of the buffer, the Third Urge Rule sets its count._

### 7.2 Boredom Intolerance Training

Modern "Supernormal Stimuli" (social media) raise the stimulation baseline, making deep work feel agonizing.

- Stimulus Control: Intentionally remove background noise (phones, non-functional music) to force the brain to shift from consumption to creation.
- Micro-Boredom: Practice 5-minute "Intermezzos" (staring at a wall) to reset dopamine sensitivity.

[contradicts:: [[ADHD Systems Fail When They Become Monotonous]], confidence=medium]

_This section asks you to build tolerance for monotony; that atom argues monotony is precisely what kills ADHD systems. See Tensions._

### 7.3 External Scaffolding for Grit

Internal willpower is a finite resource. Supplement it with external structures:

- Body Doubling: (See Section 3.3).
- Time Visualization: Use analog clocks/timers to make the "invisible" passage of time tangible.
- Gamification: Attach artificial "Challenges" (INCUP) to boring maintenance tasks to trick the dopamine system into engaging.

[supports:: [[Minimum Viable Experiment (MVE)]], confidence=medium]

## Tensions

Recorded as prose where the conflict is real but a typed edge would overstate it.

- Monotony: train it or design it out? §7.2 prescribes deliberate boredom to reset the dopamine baseline. [[ADHD Systems Fail When They Become Monotonous]] argues that routine maintenance stops delivering dopamine and the system gets abandoned—and [[Designed Task Switching Leverages Dopamine Boosts for ADHD Motivation]] sides with engineered novelty. These may be reconcilable (tolerance for boredom _inside_ a work block vs. novelty _across_ a system's lifecycle), but the SoT does not currently say so. This is the only `contradicts` edge emitted from this note.
- The Dopamine Menu's dessert problem. §4 places gaming and social media at the end as a post-work reward. [[The Dopaminergic Mirage — Anticipation Migrates to the Earliest Predictive Cue]] predicts that once the dessert is reliably anticipated, the dopaminergic peak migrates _forward_ to the earliest cue predicting it—so the reward stops terminating the work session and starts driving it. No edge emitted: the mirage qualifies the menu's design assumption rather than negating the protocol.
