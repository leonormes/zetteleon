Cravings are not resource requests but **Microbial Hijacking** or **Low-Fidelity Proxies** for energy. Stability requires "Front-Loading" protein to suppress the "Ghrelin Spike" before it occurs.

# 3. Signal Fidelity Framework

The body's sensors vary in accuracy. The "Architect" must distinguish between High-Fidelity signals and Low-Fidelity proxies.

- **High Fidelity (Trust):** Salt, Thirst, Protein.
- **Low Fidelity (Reject):** "I need Toast" (Proxy for "Low Energy"), "I need Sugar" (Microbial Hijack).

# 4. The Mechanisms

## 4.1 The "Toast" Short-Circuit

The brain prefers **High-Velocity Glucose** (white bread) during perceived energy crises because it offers the fastest resolution to the "Low Battery" signal. Fruit fails this check because it has high volume (stretch) but low caloric density (payload), creating a "Partial Success" signal that does not extinguish the alarm.

## 4.2 Microbial Puppetry

Gut bacteria (Firmicutes) that thrive on sugar can influence neurochemistry via the Vagus Nerve. This creates "Cravings" that feel like instinct but are actually external signal interference.

**The Fix:** "Starve" the malware (no sugar) and "Provision" the firmware (Probiotics/Inulin) to re-weight the population towards _Akkermansia_ and _Bifidobacteria_.

# 5. Tensions & Gaps

- **Cognitive Dissonance:** The Logic Layer knows plants are superior, but the Legacy OS (Brainstem) executes the "Toast Protocol" for speed.
- **Latency Mismatch:** Plants require digestion time (computation); refined carbs offer immediate energy (pre-compiled).
