> **Core Philosophy: "** **Systemic Accommodation.**"

## Step 1: Brain Dumping (Externalising Thought)

- **The Problem:** Holding 10,000 simultaneous thoughts creates cognitive overload and paralysis.
- **The Mechanism:** [[SoT - Brain Dumping for Cognitive Offloading|Externalizing Thought]]. Unload the mental RAM into a physical or digital space to reduce load.
- **Implementation:**
    - Use a dedicated "Brain Unloaded" notebook or phone app.
    - Capture random tasks and lingering thoughts *immediately* before they cause overwhelm.
    - *ProdOS Link:* This maps directly to **Phase 1: Capture** in the [[SoT - Action Management Framework#The 4-Phase ProdOS Workflow|ProdOS Workflow]].

## Step 2: Hierarchical Planning (Top-Down Structure)

- **The Problem:** Disconnection between high-level goals and daily actions.
- **The Mechanism:** A layered approach to transition from macro to micro.
- **Implementation:**
    1. **Yearly (Wall Calendar):** Family-wide visibility for birthdays and major events.
    2. **Monthly (Habit Focus):** Focus on habit formation and high-level budgeting (offload tracking to automated sheets).
    3. **Weekly (Synthesis):** Synthesize the "Brain Dump" and "Monthly Goals" into actionable weekly priorities.
    4. **Daily (Execution):** Limit to **three main priorities** to prevent burnout. Include hourly scheduling.
    - *ProdOS Link:* This mirrors the **Horizon** planning in GTD and the temporal structures in [[SoT - Temporal Management (Blocking and Boxing)]].

## Step 3: External Cues (Reminders)

- **The Problem:** Working memory deficits lead to forgetting tasks the moment they leave focus ("Out of sight, out of mind").
- **The Mechanism:** [[SoT - The Extended Mind|Cognitive Prosthetics]]. Compensate for memory gaps using visual and auditory cues.
- **Implementation:**
    - **Phone Notifications:** Set *immediate* reminders for tasks.
    - **Sticky Notes:** Use physical visual cues in the environment.
    - *ProdOS Link:* See [[SoT - ADHD Environmental Design]] for creating a "Designer Environment" that prompts action.

## Step 4: Performance Tracking (Audit & Feedback)

- **The Problem:** "Time Blindness" and subjective assumptions about consistency (e.g., "I never do this" vs. "I did it 3 times").
- **The Mechanism:** **Data over Assumption.** Use objective data to verify reality.
- **Implementation:**
    - **Habit Trackers:** Monitor progress visually (e.g., reading habits).
    - **Time Audits:** Identify "time sinks" like social media usage.
    - *ProdOS Link:* This aligns with the **Hansei** (Reflection) loop and [[SoT - Identity-Based Habit Formation]].

## Core Mental Model

The system's logic is based on **systemic accommodation**: rather than trying to fix the "ADHD brain," you build external systems that account for lack of discipline and poor working memory. The creator emphasises that the habit of planning must precede the purchase of premium tools.
