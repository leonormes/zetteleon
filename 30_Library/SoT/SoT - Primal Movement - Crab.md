## 1. Definitive Statement

> [!definition] Definition
> The **Crab** (Supine Quadrupedal) is a foundational primal movement form where the body is supported face-up on hands and feet. It is primarily used for **Posterior Chain Activation** and **Chest Opening**.

## 2. Mechanical Setup: Hand Positioning

Hand orientation is the most common point of confusion. It should be selected based on **Shoulder Mobility** and **Wrist Resilience**.

### A. Standard: Fingers Toward Feet (Forward)
- **Mechanism:** Induces **Shoulder External Rotation**, which stabilizes the joint and opens the chest.
- **Loading:** High demand on wrist extension.
- **Best For:** Maximum chest stretch and standard athletic conditioning.

### B. Modification: Fingers Away From Feet (Backward/Outward)
- **Mechanism:** Reduces the required angle of wrist extension.
- **Loading:** Lower demand on the carpal tunnel/forearm flexors.
- **Best For:** Individuals with wrist impingement, limited mobility, or tight chest muscles that cause internal rotation.

## 3. Technical Cues (The "Software")

Regardless of hand direction, the structural integrity relies on:

- **Scapular Retraction & Depression:** Pull shoulder blades together and "down" (toward back pockets). Avoid shrugging into the ears.
- **Grounding:** Press through the entire palm and knuckles to distribute weight.
- **Vertical Alignment:** In the full "Tabletop" position, hands must be under shoulders and feet under knees.
- **Active Hips:** Drive through the heels to engage glutes and hamstrings.

## 4. Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

> [!check] The Rule
> **Point your fingers where it feels stable.** 
> Toward the feet is "Standard" for chest opening; away/outward is a valid "Safety Hack" for tight wrists. Prioritize **Scapular Depression** to keep the shoulders safe.

## 5. Integration with Animal Flow

The Crab is the posterior base for the **Underswitch** algorithm.
- **Anterior to Posterior:** Beast -> Underswitch -> **Crab**.
- **Focus:** Transitioning into a stable Crab requires immediate scapular retraction to prevent the "collapsed chest" failure mode.
