## 1. Definitive Statement

The **Twist** (or Rotation) pattern involves movement around the vertical axis of the body. It is critical for power transfer between the lower and upper body and for spinal health.

## 2. Core Mechanics

- **Primary Joints:** Thoracic Spine, Hips. (The Lumbar spine should remain relatively stable).
- **Key Muscles:** Obliques, Multifidus, Hip Rotators.
- **Biomechanical Goal:** Dissociate the shoulders from the hips to generate torque or maintain stability during offset loading.

## 3. Stability & Stillness (Isometrics)

To build the foundation for the Twist, focus on:

- **End-Range Rotation Hold**: Maintaining the maximum active twist position for 10 seconds to strengthen the spine's outer limits.
- **Pallof Press Hold**: Resisting rotation to build "anti-rotational" core strength.

## 4. Exercises (Collection)

- **Side Kickthrough (Animal Flow)**: Pivot on one hand and foot to kick the opposite leg through, crossing the midline.
- **Spider-Man Push-Up (Rotation)**: Using the knee-to-elbow transition to train anti-rotational stability.
