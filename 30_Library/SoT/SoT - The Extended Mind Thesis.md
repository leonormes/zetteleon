## 1. Definitive Statement

The **Extended Mind Thesis (EMT)** posits that the machinery of the human mind is not confined to the biological brain or skin-bag. Cognitive processing "leaks" into the environment, incorporating bodily actions and external artefacts (like Obsidian) into the active circuits of thought.

## 2. Core Frameworks

- **[[The Parity Principle - Functional Equivalence in Cognition|The Parity Principle]]:** If an external process performs a function we would call "cognitive" if it were in the head, it *is* part of the cognitive system.
- **Active Externalism:** External features play a crucial, causal role in the "here-and-now" of thinking.
- **Hypothesis of Cognitive Impartiality:** The brain is a "locus of recruitment" that uses whatever resource (neural, bodily, or digital) is most cost-effective.

## 3. Mechanisms of Extension

- **[[Epistemic Actions - Thinking via Doing|Epistemic Actions]]:** Physical actions taken specifically to aid computation (e.g., shuffling tiles in Scrabble).
- **[[Transparent Equipment - The Phenomenology of Tool Integration|Transparent Equipment]]:** Tools so reliable they become part of the body schema (looking *through* the pen to the writing).
- **Material Symbols:** Language and labels act as "cognitive scaffolding" to reduce environmental complexity.

## 4. Relation to ProdOS

ProdOS is an operational implementation of EMT. By offloading memory to **SoT Notes** and processing to the **Workbench**, the user "supersizes" their cognitive capacity, treating the vault as externalised working memory.
