> Open threads: [[HEAD - Where is the line between trusting my gut and emotional reasoning?]]

## 1. Working Knowledge (Stable Foundation)

ADHD Emotional Reasoning is a cognitive distortion where temporary emotional states are interpreted as objective facts about reality. For the ADHD brain, this is not just a logical error but a neurological event; the intensity of [[MOC - Emotional Dysregulation]] and [[Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD)]] makes the emotion feel "louder" and thus more "true," while executive function deficits impair the ability to pause and objectively evaluate the feeling.

## 2. Current Understanding (Coherent Narrative)

Individuals with ADHD are uniquely susceptible to emotional reasoning due to a "triple threat" mechanism:

1. Intensity: Neurological differences create more intense emotional experiences. A feeling of anxiety isn't just a whisper; it's a shout, making it harder to ignore as a data point.
2. Executive Delay: The "pause button" (inhibition) in the ADHD brain is underpowered. This makes it difficult to step back from an intense emotion to question its validity before reacting.
3. RSD Amplification: [[Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD)]] specifically amplifies negative emotions related to social standing or performance, turning a minor worry about a social interaction into a perceived factual certainty of failure or rejection.

The core narrative is that for the ADHD mind, Emotion = Reality until proven otherwise, whereas the neurotypical model might be Emotion = Data to be evaluated.

## 4. Understanding Layers (Progressive Abstraction)

- Layer 1 (The Distortion): "I feel anxious, therefore I _am_ in danger." / "I feel like a failure, therefore I _am_ a failure."
- Layer 2 (The Mechanism): Executive dysfunction (poor inhibition) prevents the necessary cognitive pause to evaluate the emotion against evidence.
- Layer 3 (The Amplifier): High emotional lability and RSD increase the "signal strength" of the emotion, overriding logical processing centers.

## 5. Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

- Definition: Believing your emotions are facts (e.g., "I feel bad, so the situation is bad").
- ADHD Multiplier: Made worse by intense feelings (dysregulation) and poor impulse control (executive dysfunction).
- Key Identifier: The inability to separate "how I feel" from "what is happening."
- Immediate Action: Pausing to ask: "Is this a fact, or just a feeling?"

## 6. Battle Testing and Decay Signals

- Core Claim: ADHD brains are neurologically predisposed to emotional reasoning due to deficits in inhibition and emotional regulation.
- Current Status: Developing. Requires more connection to specific coping mechanisms (like [[Morita Therapy - Applications for Anxiety, Perfectionism, and ADHD]]).
- Decay Markers: Watch for research distinguishing "intuition" from "emotional reasoning" in neurodivergent brains.

## 7. Tensions, Gaps, and Cross-SoT Coherence

- Tension: [[Your emotions are real but they are your responsibility]]. How do we validate the _experience_ of the emotion (it is real) without validating the _content_ (it is a fact)?
- Confidence Gap: At what point does "trusting your gut" become "emotional reasoning"? This boundary is blurrier in ADHD.
- Cross-SoT Conflicts: None currently identified, but must align with [[MOC - Emotional Dysregulation]] SoT to ensure definitions of "regulation" match.
