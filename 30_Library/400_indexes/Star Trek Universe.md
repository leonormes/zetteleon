### 🗺️ Chronological Watchlist

- **Star Trek: Enterprise** (2151–2161)
	- _Logic:_ Humanity’s first Warp 5 vessel. Establishments of first contact protocols and the coalition that becomes the Federation.

#### Era 2: The Expansion Era (23rd Century)

- **Star Trek: Discovery (Seasons 1–2)** (2256–2258)
- **Star Trek: Strange New Worlds** (2259–Present)
- **Star Trek: The Original Series (TOS)** (2265–2269)
- **Star Trek: The Animated Series (TAS)** (2269–2270)
- **TOS Films (Movies 1–6)** (2273–2293)
	- _Note:_ Ends with _The Undiscovered Country_, marking the transition to peace with the Klingon Empire.

#### Era 3: The "Lost Era" (Late 23rd–Early 24th Century)

- **Star Trek: Section 31** (Movie, set circa 2330s–2340s)
	- _Logic:_ Bridges the gap between the TOS movie era and the TNG era.

- **Star Trek: Deep Space Nine (DS9)** (2369–2375)

- **Star Trek: Lower Decks** (2380–Present)
- **Star Trek: Prodigy** (2383–Present)
- **Star Trek: Picard** (2399–2401)
	- _Logic:_ High-concept density regarding artificial intelligence (Synths) and the Borg endgame.

#### Era 5: The Distant Future (32nd Century)

- **Star Trek: Discovery (Seasons 3–5)** (3188–3191)
- **Star Trek: Starfleet Academy** (32nd Century–_Commencing January 2026_)

### 🏗️ Timeline Branches

The timeline bifurcates at specific "Nexus Points" where external interference rewrites the causal chain.

#### Branch A: The Kelvin Timeline (Parallel 23rd Century)

- **The Divergence:** 2233.
- **The Cause:** The Romulan ship _Narada_ travels back from 2387 and destroys the _USS Kelvin_.
- **Impact:** A tech-accelerated reality where Vulcan is destroyed and James T. Kirk’s trajectory is altered.
- **Media:** _Star Trek (2009)_, _Star Trek Into Darkness_, _Star Trek Beyond_.

#### Branch B: The Mirror Universe (Parallel Constant)

- **The Logic:** A dark reflection of the Prime Timeline (Terran Empire vs. United Federation of Planets).
- **Interaction:** Not a single branch point, but a parallel dimension accessed via transporter accidents or "interphasic" rifts (e.g., in _TOS_, _DS9_, _ENT_, and _DIS_).

### ⏳ Temporal Mechanics: Logic of the Forks

#### The Borg Incursion (2063)

- **Type:** Closed Loop.
- **Source:** _Star Trek: First Contact_.
- **Mechanism:** Chronometric particles.
- **Outcome:** The Federation is founded _because_ the Enterprise-E intervened. Without the intervention, the Prime Timeline collapses.

#### The Kelvin Divergence (2233)

- **Type:** Parallel Branch.
- **Source:** _Star Trek (2009)_.
- **Mechanism:** Red Matter Singularity.
- **Outcome:** Creation of the [[Kelvin Timeline]]. The Prime Timeline remains intact (see: _Star Trek Picard_), but a secondary reality is formed where the Romulan Empire's future interference rewrites the 23rd century.

### 🖖 First Contact Index

#### 🪐 Vulcan

- **Date:** April 5, 2063
- **Vessel:** T'Plana-Hath
- **Significance:** Initiated the "Golden Age" of humanity. Ended poverty and war on Earth.

#### ⚔️ Klingon Empire

- **Date:** 2151 (Klaang incident)
- **Status:** Former adversaries, now strategic allies (post-2293).
- **Key Event:** [[The Khitomer Accords]] - The peace treaty that ended the Federation-Klingon War.

#### 🦅 Romulan Star Empire

- **Date:** Mid-22nd Century (War era) / 2266 (Visual contact)
- **Status:** Isolationist / Hostile.
- **Lore Pivot:** A schism from Vulcan logic thousands of years ago.

#### 🔘 The Borg

- **Date:** 2365 (System J-25)
- **Note:** While _First Contact_ (movie) shows them in 2063, the official 24th-century contact was orchestrated by Q to warn Picard.
- **Logic:** Collective intelligence vs. Individualism.
