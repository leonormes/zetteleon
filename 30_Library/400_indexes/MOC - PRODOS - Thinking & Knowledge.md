## 1. The Core Philosophy: The Factory Mindset

ProdOS redefines Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) from "Storing Facts" to "Manufacturing Insights."

- **The Manifesto:** [[SoT - PRODOS (System Architecture)]] - The master blueprint defining the move from "Museum" (Storage) to "Factory" (Compute).
- **The Foundation:** [[SoT - The Extended Mind]] - Why the system exists: to serve as a cognitive prosthesis for the ADHD brain, extending memory and processing power.
- **The Evolution:** [[SoT - Evolutionary Note System]] - How the system grows organically rather than through rigid pre-planning.

## 2. The Engine: How We Think

The "Thinking" process is distinct from "Knowing." It is the active, volatile simulation of reality.

- **The Process:** [[SoT - PRODOS - Knowledge Synthesis (Thinking)]] - The core protocol for "Thinking as Simulation" and the "Spark to Synthesis" pipeline.
- **The Method:** [[SoT - The Unified Writing to Think Process]] - How to write to find out what you think, rather than writing to record what you know.
- **The Mindset:** [[SoT - Processing IS the Work]] - Reframing "admin" as "creation." Processing notes *is* the act of knowledge building.
- **The Artifact:** [[SoT - PRODOS - The Tri-State Router]] - How to exit a thinking session (Action, Knowledge, or Wait).

## 3. The Structure: Where It Lives

We avoid "Filing" in favor of "Tagging" and "Linking" within a simplified 6-component architecture.

- **The Map:** [[SoT - PRODOS - Structure & Storage]] - The PARA-like structure (`00_Inbox`, `10_Actions`, `20_Thinking`, `30_Library`).
- **The Workbench:** [[SoT - PRODOS (System Architecture)#A. HEAD Notes (The Workbench)|HEAD Notes]] - The ephemeral workspace for active thought.
- **The Canon:** [[SoT - PRODOS (System Architecture)#B. LIB/SoT Notes (The Canon)|SoT Notes]] - The durable, trusted Source of Truth.

## 4. Cognitive & Psychological Drivers

The system is designed specifically to counteract ADHD executive dysfunction and cognitive biases.

- **The Why:** [[SoT - ADHD and Motivation]] - Understanding the dopamine-seeking brain.
- **The Blind Spot:** [[SoT - Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IoED)]] - Why we *think* we understand until we try to write it down.
- **The Scaffolding:** [[SoT - ADHD Environmental Design]] - Shaping the digital environment to reduce friction.
- **The Deep Dive:** [[SoT - Cognitive Ambiguity and Deep Thinking]] - Managing the discomfort of the "Groan Zone" in complex problem solving.
