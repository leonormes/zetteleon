## 1. Design & Architecture

- [ ] A Pattern Language - _Christopher Alexander_ (Rec: Brian Eno)
    - _Context:_ An architectural framework focusing on "habitat" and what makes spaces welcoming. A key text for systems thinking.

## 2. History & Intellectual Evolution

- [ ] Printing and the Mind of Man (1963) - _British Museum Catalogue_ (Rec: Brian Eno)
    - _Context:_ Primary source for understanding the ideas that constructed Western culture over the last 500 years.
- [ ] Naples '44 - _Norman Lewis_ (Rec: Brian Eno)
    - _Context:_ A British intelligence officer's diary in post-war Naples. Deep observation of a world that felt like "another planet."

## 3. Periodicals

- [ ] London Review of Books (Subscription) (Rec: Brian Eno)
    - _Context:_ Cited as the best intellectual reading in the English language.
