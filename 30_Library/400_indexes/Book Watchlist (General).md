## 1. Design & Architecture

- [ ] **A Pattern Language** - *Christopher Alexander* (Rec: Brian Eno)
    - *Context:* An architectural framework focusing on "habitat" and what makes spaces welcoming. A key text for systems thinking.

## 2. History & Intellectual Evolution

- [ ] **Printing and the Mind of Man** (1963) - *British Museum Catalogue* (Rec: Brian Eno)
    - *Context:* Primary source for understanding the ideas that constructed Western culture over the last 500 years.
- [ ] **Naples '44** - *Norman Lewis* (Rec: Brian Eno)
    - *Context:* A British intelligence officer's diary in post-war Naples. Deep observation of a world that felt like "another planet."

## 3. Periodicals

- [ ] **London Review of Books** (Subscription) (Rec: Brian Eno)
    - *Context:* Cited as the best intellectual reading in the English language.
