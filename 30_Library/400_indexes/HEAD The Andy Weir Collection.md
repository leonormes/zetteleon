## Sci-Fi Thrillers & Space Races

- Saturn Run: Set in the near future, a mysterious object is spotted decelerating toward Saturn, sparking a high-stakes space race between nations. It features fascinating discussions on orbital mechanics and realistic astronaut life. \
- Pushing Ice (Alastair Reynolds): A crew of ice miners is sent to investigate a moon that has suddenly left its orbit. It captures the "mystery-solving" aspect of _Project Hail Mary_ and explores humanity's role in a much larger, high-stakes universe. \

## Light-hearted & Humorous

- We Are Legion (We Are Bob) (Dennis E. Taylor): The first in the _Bobiverse_ series. A man's consciousness is uploaded into a space probe designed to explore the galaxy. Like _Project Hail Mary_, it is incredibly fun, filled with pop culture references, and features a very relatable, witty protagonist. \
- The Fold (Peter Clines): Though part of a series, it can be read as a standalone. It follows a brilliant teacher with a photographic memory who is asked to investigate a "teleportation" gate that isn't quite what it seems. It mirrors the "brilliant person solving an impossible problem" trope. \
- Star Nomad (Lindsay Buroker): A space opera following a ragtag group of survivors in a failed alliance. If you loved the friendships and banter in _Project Hail Mary_, this "found family" adventure might scratch that same itch. \

## Modern Classics & Concept-Driven Sci-Fi

- Spin (Robert Charles Wilson): One night, the stars simply disappear, replaced by a black barrier. The story follows a group of friends as they grow up and try to understand what happened to the universe. It's an accessible modern classic with massive global stakes. \[
