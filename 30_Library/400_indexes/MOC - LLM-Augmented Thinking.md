## MOC - LLM-Augmented Thinking

> [!abstract] Executive Summary
> This MOC aggregates the methods, skills, and philosophies for using Large Language Models not just as "doers" (execution) but as "thinkers" (cognition). It focuses on the **Process of Thinking** in the Inspiration Economy, where the human role shifts to **Context Engineering** and **Problem Definition**.

---

### 1. The Core Shift: From Answer-Seeking to Problem-Defining

The fundamental shift in the "Human 3.0" era is moving from finding answers (which AI can do) to framing the right questions (which requires Intent).

- **[[SoT - AI-Resilient Task Taxonomy (Human 3.0)]]**: The foundational framework. Defines the move from "Problem Solver" to "Problem Definer".
- **[[LLMs as tools for thought]]**: Explores the cognitive affordances of LLMs beyond simple automation.
- **[[Why LLM Responses Feel Validating MOC]]**: Understanding the psychological feedback loops in human-AI interaction.

---

### 2. Core Skill: Context Engineering

If the Model is the Engine, **Context** is the Fuel. The quality of the output is strictly determined by the quality of the Context provided.

- **[[Context Engineering for LLMs]]**: The master skill. Curating the information environment.
- **[[Context Pruning]]**: The art of removing irrelevant noise to prevent distraction.
- **[[Context Quarantine]]**: Isolating specific problem domains to prevent "context bleeding".
- **[[Dynamic Tool Loadout]]**: Equipping the AI with the right tools for the specific thinking phase.
- **[[Prompt - User Context (The Leon Model)]]**: The "Policy Layer" that gives the AI its persona and operating constraints.

How do we actually *do* the thinking?

- **[[MOC - AI as Thought Partner]]**: The broader architectural view of Human-AI collaboration.
- **[[AI Agentic Workflows]]**: Moving from simple chat to multi-step, goal-oriented agent loops.

---

### 4. The "Thinking" Stack

In ProdOS, "Thinking" is an active process that moves through stages:

1. **The Spark**: A raw idea or "vomit" (Ingested into `00_Inbox`).
2. **The Workbench**: A **HEAD Note** is created. The LLM is invoked to:
    - *Lint* the emotional noise.
    - *Structure* the chaos.
    - *Challenge* the assumptions (The "Sparring Partner" mode).
3. **The Synthesis**: The insight is merged into a **SoT Note** (Source of Truth).
4. **The Action**: A verifiable next step is defined.
