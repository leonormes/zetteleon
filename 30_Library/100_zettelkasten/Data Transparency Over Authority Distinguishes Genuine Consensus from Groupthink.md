## Data Transparency Over Authority Distinguishes Genuine Consensus from Groupthink

A valid consensus points to the data; groupthink points to the hierarchy. When challenged, a legitimate consensus relies on open methodology, public datasets, and repeatable steps—"here is the raw data and the method, attempt to break it yourself." Groupthink relies on appeals to authority, credentialism, and the assertion that outsiders simply lack the capacity to understand the truth.

### Scope & Conditions

One of five diagnostics (with Atom 006–009) for telling a legitimate scientific consensus apart from institutional groupthink.
