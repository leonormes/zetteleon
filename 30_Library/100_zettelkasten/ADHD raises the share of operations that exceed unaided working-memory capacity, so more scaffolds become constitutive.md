## ADHD raises the share of operations that exceed unaided working-memory capacity, so more scaffolds become constitutive

ADHD raises the proportion of everyday operations that exceed unaided internal working-memory capacity, so more external scaffolds become constitutive rather than merely assistive. This is a claim of degree: the coupling conditions themselves are generic, but the capacity threshold is crossed earlier and more often in ADHD. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- **Claim of degree, not kind:** Neurotypical cognition also relies on constitutive scaffolds (such as a diary or scratchpad), so parity alone does not differentiate ADHD; the difference lies in how many routine operations cross the unaided capacity ceiling.
