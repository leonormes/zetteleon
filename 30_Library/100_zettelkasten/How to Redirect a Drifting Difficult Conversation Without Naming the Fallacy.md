## How to Redirect a Drifting Difficult Conversation Without Naming the Fallacy

Redirect drift with neutral scripts that park, acknowledge or ask, and never name the fallacy aloud, so the conversation returns to the present topic without anyone being cornered. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to high-stakes conversations with a partner held away from bedtime and away from the heat of a row. It does not apply when the drift is the real topic, which is a decision for both partners.

### Evidence

This is my own protocol, written for one household, and no outside source has been checked. The reasoning is in [[De-escalating Partner Conversations Requires Redirecting Drift Without Naming Fallacies Aloud]].

### Implications

1. Counter-grievance or you do it too: say let's park that, it's written down, back to the topic, and give it a fixed slot straight after.
2. But you said: acknowledge it once, name the present topic, restate the boundary.
3. There is no solution: read it as no solution I am willing to implement, and ask which one she would run.
4. Either of us is heated: stop, and resume the next day.
5. Never name a fallacy aloud, and never ask a question meant to corner her.
