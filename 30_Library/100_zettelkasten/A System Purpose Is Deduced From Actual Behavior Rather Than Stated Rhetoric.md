## A System Purpose Is Deduced From Actual Behavior Rather Than Stated Rhetoric

A system's true purpose is deduced from its actual, observed behavior over time rather than its stated goals, mission statements, or designer rhetoric. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Holds for complex human, organisational, and socio-technical systems where incentives, constraints, and feedback loops can diverge from official intentions. Does not require conscious intent on the part of the system itself.

### Evidence

Extracted from [[SoT - Systems Thinking]] (Meadows and Open University synthesis). No verbatim book quote has been verified for this seed note yet.

### Implications

- Diagnosing a failing policy or organisation requires tracing what outcomes the structure reliably produces, not auditing its stated values.
- Changing rhetoric without altering feedback loops or incentives leaves the real purpose untouched.
