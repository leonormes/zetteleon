## Minimum Viable Understanding
Many experienced software engineers argue that the actual output of a software team isn't the lines of code committed to a repository, but rather the shared mental model of how the system works, what it is supposed to do, and why it is built the way it is. Code is merely the materialized artifact of that understanding.

## Mechanisms and Fragility
- **Minds as primary storage:** The deep meaning of the software is stored as "cache state" in the developers' minds.
- **Collectivist nature:** Because understanding is distributed across team members' brains, software engineering is exquisitely sensitive to relationship dynamics, communication, and emotional valence.
- **Divergence:** This model is fragile because human minds are forgetful and distractible, and the internal mental models perpetually drift from the reality of what users actually experience in production.

## Contrasting View
Site Reliability Engineers (SREs) often push back on this, asserting that "the true product of every good software engineering team is production"—because only production reflects the objective reality of the system ("Test in prod, or live a lie").
