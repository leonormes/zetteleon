## How to Respond to a Distressed Child With Empathy Then Accountability

Use empathy as the entry point and accountability as the push forward. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to children who can calm and talk. If anyone is in Red Brain, regulate first and skip the bridge.

### Evidence

> "Empathy is the Entry Point (The Soft Landing): One parent validates the emotional distress first"
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §3 Protocol C)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

1. Validate the feeling.
2. Wait for calm.
3. Bridge to the plan.
4. Open the checklist together.
5. The other parent repeats the same order.
