## Cognitive Load

The amount of working-memory demand a task imposes at a given moment. Past a ceiling, a task tips from "effortful" into "overwhelming", and avoidance follows.

### Why it Matters here

- Reviewing and categorising captured notes is load-heavy; with reduced working-memory capacity the ceiling is reached sooner.
- Many ADHD-friendly tactics are really load-reduction tactics: flat linking, micro-tasks, context lists.
