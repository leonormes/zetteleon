## Recursive Agent Improvement

AI agents can identify and correct their own procedural errors by reviewing session logs and proposing updates to their own skill definitions or documentation. This self-referential process creates an autonomous maintenance cycle that continuously refines the agent's performance and knowledge base without direct human intervention.

### Scope & Conditions

Part of the autonomous maintenance and refinement cycle in advanced agentic harnesses.

### Evidence

> "Agents are instructed to review their own session logs to identify mistakes or missing context, then propose updates to their own 'skills' or documentation to prevent future errors."

### Implications

- Creates self-healing development workflows that improve in reliability over time.
- Enables the continuous, autonomous refinement of the "harness" and its underlying operating procedures.
