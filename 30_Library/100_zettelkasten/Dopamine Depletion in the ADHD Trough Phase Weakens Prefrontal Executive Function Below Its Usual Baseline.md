## Dopamine Depletion in the ADHD Trough Phase Weakens Prefrontal Executive Function Below Its Usual Baseline

Executive functions rely on dopamine signalling in the prefrontal cortex. The dopamine deficit of the trough phase therefore compromises them even more than the ADHD baseline does. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Assumes the trough is a dopamine-deficit state, as described in the hyperfixation-burnout cycle. It adds to the baseline catecholamine insufficiency in ADHD and does not replace it.

### Implications

- Executive demands made during the trough meet a weaker system than the same demands made on a normal ADHD day.
