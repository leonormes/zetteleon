### Proposition-Centred Notes Create Cognitive Leverage That Topical Notes Lack
Topical wiki notes (answering "What is X?") are epistemically inert — they contain information but make no claim that can be evaluated, contested, or connected to a reasoning chain. A proposition-centred note (an arguable claim) immediately connects to adjacent propositions and creates cognitive leverage for argumentation and decision-making.

#### Scope & Conditions
The distinction is architectural: a topical note optimises for storage and retrieval of established facts, while a proposition-centred note optimises for thinking and argumentation.

#### Evidence
> "A note titled 'Dopamine' that summarises what dopamine is creates no cognitive leverage. By contrast, a note asserting 'Dopamine encodes prediction error, not pleasure, which means outcome-goal motivation is structurally unsustainable' is a proposition."

#### Implications
- When atomising source material, prefer phrasing each note as an arguable claim rather than a bare definition, wherever the source supports a claim.
- A vault built from propositions models how knowledge is actually used — in service of decisions and arguments — rather than as a reference library.
