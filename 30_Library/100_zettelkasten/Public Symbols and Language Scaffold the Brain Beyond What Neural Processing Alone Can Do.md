## Public Symbols and Language Scaffold the Brain Beyond What Neural Processing Alone Can Do

Public symbols and language act as material symbols, active computational tools that scaffold the brain to achieve feats that neural processing alone cannot manage. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Distributed cognition, the third dimension: thinking is spread across people and tools.

### Evidence

This is my own summary of the thesis as set out in the earlier text of [[SoT - The Extended Mind]], which attributes it to Clark and Chalmers. No book quotation has been verified for this card.

### Implications

- Writing and shared symbols are part of the machinery of thought, not only its output.
