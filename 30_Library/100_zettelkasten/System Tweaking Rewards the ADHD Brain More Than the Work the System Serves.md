## System Tweaking Rewards the ADHD Brain More Than the Work the System Serves

System tweaking is a common form of productive procrastination in ADHD: excessive time goes into refining the appearance, function or organisation of tools, such as a PKM or to-do list, instead of using them for the actual work. The brain can lock into this loop of perfecting the system because it is more stimulating than the often mundane work the system was designed to manage. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to people with ADHD refining their own tools. Tweaking is engaging because it activates several motivators at once:

- **Novelty:** creating new layouts, functions or aesthetics.
- **Challenge:** the puzzle of working out how to make something behave.
- **Interest:** it is often a creative, hands-on task.
- **Immediate feedback:** each successful tweak gives a small, instant reward.

### Evidence

No source or quotation is attached; the claim rests on the reasoning given here.

### Implications

- The state can be read as [[Hyperfocus]] misapplied to the system instead of the work.
- A tweak that feels productive is worth checking against the work it was meant to serve.
