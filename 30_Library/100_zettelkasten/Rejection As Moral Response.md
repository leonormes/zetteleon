title: Rejection As Moral Response
type: concept

Rejection can serve as a counterbalance to harmful power dynamics when applied collectively. When multiple parties reject harmful behavior together, they can effectively shift power away from a single dominant actor by removing resources and support that enable the harmful behavior.
