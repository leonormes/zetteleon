## Control Over Your Own Time Is the Highest Form of Wealth

Being able to wake up and decide what to do with your time is the highest form of wealth available. The ultimate goal of managing money is therefore to buy autonomy. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

A value judgement about what wealth is for. It presumes essential needs can be met.

### Evidence

Carried over from the earlier prose version of [[SoT - Financial Philosophy and Spendfulness]]; no outside source is cited.

### Implications

- Spending and saving can be judged by how much control over time they add.
