## Identity Integration Shifts Habit Execution From Doing to Being

Consistency deepens when a practice shifts from temporary doing ("I am going for a run") to identity-level being ("I am a runner"). When behaviour expresses a chosen identity, each repetition reinforces self-concept rather than feeling like an external obligation negotiated from scratch.

### Scope & Conditions

Holds when identity is grounded in observable actions and personal values rather than rigid perfectionism. Binding self-worth to flawless performance can backfire when normal capacity limits interrupt a streak.

### Evidence

This claim captures point 8 of the 9-Point Consistency Framework in [[SoT - Intentional Living]] and connects to [[SoT - Habit Formation Framework]].

### Implications

- Small daily repetitions serve as votes for the identity you want to inhabit, even when volume is minimal.
- Pairing identity framing with compassionate setback recovery prevents missed days from triggering abandonment.
