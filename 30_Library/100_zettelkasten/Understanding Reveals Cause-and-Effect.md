Beyond mere prediction, a deeper understanding allows individuals to identify and comprehend the cause-and-effect relationships between different phenomena. This insight into how elements interact and influence each other is crucial for problem-solving and systemic comprehension.

## Three Ideas Inside It

An earlier pass split the paragraph into three separable ideas, kept here in one place.

1. Nature. There is a qualitative difference between predicting an outcome and understanding it. Prediction can come from correlation or pattern recognition alone; one can predict rain from dark clouds without understanding meteorology.
2. Mechanism. Deep understanding is characterised by seeing the network of cause and effect: not just that B follows A, but how A causes B.
3. Application. Grasping cause and effect is a prerequisite for effective problem-solving (address the root cause, not the symptom) and for understanding complex systems (how the components interact).

The argument map [[Myopic Understanding]] treats these as one step, the "stronger claim" after prediction, so they are not split into separate notes.
