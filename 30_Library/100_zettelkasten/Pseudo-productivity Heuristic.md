## Pseudo-productivity Heuristic

In knowledge work environments where actual value creation is difficult to quantify, managers and institutions often use "visible activity" as a proxy for productivity. This heuristic prioritizes tools and behaviors that generate high levels of digital engagement (e.g., Slack activity, email volume) over the quiet, focused cognition required for deep work.

### Scope & Conditions

Common in corporate and collaborative environments where output metrics are poorly defined or qualitative.

### Evidence

> "In knowledge work, where output is difficult to quantify… 'visible activity' serves as a proxy for value."

### Implications

- Institutions may unintentionally incentivize performative busyness over actual problem-solving or innovation.
- This demand for visible activity sustains counter-productive workflows that fragment attention and prevent deep concentration.
