## Nozick's Tracking Theory Grounds Knowledge in Truth-Tracking Across Counterfactuals

Externalist tracking theory (Nozick) argues that a belief counts as knowledge if it accurately tracks the truth across different hypothetical scenarios, rather than requiring the knower to consciously access their justifying reasons.

### Scope & Conditions

The second externalist account, alongside Goldman's reliabilism; contrasts with internalism.

### Evidence

> "or if your belief accurately _tracks the truth_ across different hypothetical scenarios (Tracking Theory, Nozick)."

### Implications

- A distinct externalist mechanism from reliabilism—counterfactual sensitivity of the belief itself, rather than reliability of the process that produced it.
