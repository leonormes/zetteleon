## Aerobic Function Efficiently Burns Fat for Long-Term Energy

**Summary:** Aerobic function utilizes oxygen to burn body fat for sustainable, long-term energy and endurance.

**Details:** This system is characterized by low-intensity activity that feels easy and repeatable. Building a strong aerobic base improves health, reduces the risk of injury, and enhances the body's ability to recover from stress.
