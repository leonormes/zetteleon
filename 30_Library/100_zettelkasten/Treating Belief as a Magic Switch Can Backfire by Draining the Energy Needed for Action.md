## Treating Belief as a Magic Switch Can Backfire by Draining the Energy Needed for Action

Slogans such as "just believe" or "think positive" treat beliefs as magic switches or passive wishes. The source says this can backfire by relaxing the body as if the goal were already achieved, which drains the energy that action requires.

### Scope & Conditions

Eyal separates this from choosing a useful belief. The mechanism, relaxing the body, is asserted in the LLM summary without a cited study, so treat it as unverified.

### Evidence

> "The 'just believe' or 'think positive' mantras treat beliefs as magic switches or passive wishes, which can backfire by relaxing the body as if the goal were already achieved, thereby draining the energy required for action."
> (Nir Eyal, Beyond Belief, via an LLM summary; chapter and page not captured)

### Implications

- Marks the boundary between a practical belief and manifesting or self-deception.
- Gives the reason a good belief-tool must engage with real feedback.
