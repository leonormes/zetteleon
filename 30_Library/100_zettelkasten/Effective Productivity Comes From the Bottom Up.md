## Effective Productivity Comes From the Bottom Up

Summary: Effective productivity is built from the "bottom up," starting with the most mundane, ground-floor level of current activity and commitments.

Details: This principle emphasizes that while high-level goals and projects are important, true progress is made by consistently executing well-defined [[SoT - Execution Protocol (GTD & PARA)|atomic actions]]. These small, foundational steps are the bedrock upon which larger achievements are built, aligning with the "Ground Floor" of the [[LLM GTD Context|GTD Horizons of Focus]].
