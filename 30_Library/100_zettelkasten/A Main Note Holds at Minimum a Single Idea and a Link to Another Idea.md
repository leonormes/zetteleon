## A Main Note Holds at Minimum a Single Idea and a Link to Another Idea

At the very least, a main note needs a single idea and a link to another idea in the zettelkasten. The source is Bob Doto, A System for Writing.

### Scope & Conditions

Doto's minimum for a main note; other elements may be added but these two are the essential ones. He also says main notes need not be perfect and their contents can be as simple as an idea and a link.

### Evidence

> "At the very least, a main note should have a single idea and a link to another idea stored in your zettelkasten."
> (Doto, A System for Writing, section "Making Main Notes")

> "A main note's contents can be as simple as an idea and a link to other ideas."
> (Doto, A System for Writing, section "Making Main Notes")

### Implications

- A note that has no link, or no single idea, has not yet met the minimum.
- Perfection is not required to start; the link is what places the idea in the network.
