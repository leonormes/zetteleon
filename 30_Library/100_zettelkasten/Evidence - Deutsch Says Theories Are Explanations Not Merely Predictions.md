## Evidence - Deutsch Says Theories Are Explanations Not Merely Predictions

> As I have said, theories are explanations, not merely predictions.

### What it Supports

The first half of [[Understanding Reveals Cause-and-Effect]]: prediction and understanding are different things, and understanding is the explanation of why.

### What it Does not Show

It is a philosopher-physicist's position on the aim of science, argued at length in the book. It supports the prediction-versus-explanation distinction, not the practical claim about problem-solving.
