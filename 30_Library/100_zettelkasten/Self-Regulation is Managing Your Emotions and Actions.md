## Self-Regulation is Managing Your Emotions and Actions

Self-regulation is managing your emotions, thoughts and actions in a controlled and deliberate way. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It means thinking before acting and responding with awareness rather than on impulse.
- It is treated here as part of emotional intelligence and is the child-worded version of the formal strength.

### Evidence

> "You can easily hold your desires, needs, and impulses in check when it is appropriate."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 250) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.
