## The Brain Recruits Whichever Neural Bodily or Digital Resource Is Most Cost-Effective for the Task

The brain acts as a locus of recruitment that uses whatever neural, bodily or digital resource is most cost-effective for the task, and does not privilege biological neurons over reliable silicon. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Called the Hypothesis of Cognitive Impartiality in the earlier SoT text. It assumes the external resource is reliable.

### Evidence

This is my own summary of the thesis as set out in the earlier text of [[SoT - The Extended Mind]], which attributes it to Clark and Chalmers. No book quotation has been verified for this card.

### Implications

- Choose the resource by cost and reliability, not by whether it is inside the head.
- A trusted digital tool is as legitimate a part of the system as a memory.
