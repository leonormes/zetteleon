## Horizontal vs. Vertical Execution in the ADHD Mind

For the ADHD brain, vertical execution (staying within a single project for a session) provides the visible movement and dopamine reward that horizontal execution (interleaving small tasks across disparate projects) fails to trigger.

### Scope & Conditions

Addresses workflow design for ADHD individuals who struggle with the "interleaving penalty" and the cognitive friction of context-switching.

### Evidence

> "By executing vertically… visible movement is generated automatically, eliminating the need to continuously reload context just to feel a sense of completion."

### Implications

- Horizontal systems like GTD may need modification for ADHD brains to reduce context-reloading costs.
- Deep, single-project focus generates the overarching context needed to trigger dopamine rewards.
