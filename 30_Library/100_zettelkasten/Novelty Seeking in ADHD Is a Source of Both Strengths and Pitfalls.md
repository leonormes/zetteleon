## Novelty Seeking in ADHD Is a Source of Both Strengths and Pitfalls

Novelty seeking in ADHD is a double-edged sword. The same pull towards the new that produces significant strengths also produces recurring pitfalls. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the novelty-seeking trait as it shows up in ADHD. Hyperfocus depends on novelty aligning with genuine interest.

### Evidence

No source or quotation is attached; the claim rests on the patterns listed here.

### Implications

**Strengths**

- **Curiosity and exploration:** the search for novelty fuels curiosity and a love of learning.
- **Hyperfocus:** novelty that aligns with interest can trigger intense, productive focus.
- **Creativity and innovation:** exploring new ideas and connecting disparate concepts supports original thinking.

**Pitfalls**

- **Research rabbit hole:** hunting for new information becomes more rewarding than using what is already known.
- **Shiny object syndrome:** working systems or projects are abandoned for newer ones.
- **System tweaking:** tools and workflows are optimised in place of doing the work.
