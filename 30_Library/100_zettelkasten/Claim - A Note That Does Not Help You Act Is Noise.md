## The Position

Extracted verbatim from [[SoT - PRODOS Core Specification]] §1.2, Axiom 1 (authored 2026-01-03):

> "**Utility Over Truth:** If a note doesn't help you act, it is noise."

## Why It Was Held

The axiom was written as the first of five governing ProdOS, against a real and well-evidenced failure mode: [[Tool tinkering is a form of productive procrastination]], [[The Danger of Perpetual System-Building]], and the Collector's Fallacy ([[SoT - Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IoED)]]). As a guardrail against meta-work it was doing genuine work.

## Why It Was Retired — 2026-08-29

Not because it is false, but because it applies the wrong **unit**, **latency** and **direction**:

- **Unit.** It tests a single note. Mental models form across a corpus, not per note.
- **Latency.** It demands the payoff be visible now. Model formation is slow.
- **Direction.** It asks a note to justify itself forward. Models are validated backward, by the quality of the action taken under them.

It is therefore a category error about granularity rather than a false claim. Read against the thinking layer it defines the entire Zettelkasten as noise, because a proposition note exists to be *falsifiable*, not actionable ([[Propositions Are the only Thing that Can Be Wrong]]).

Its procedural twin, retired at the same time, is [[Claim - Thinking That Cannot Name Its Resulting Action Is Procrastination]].

## Scope Note

The axiom remains sound **inside execution**. What was wrong was stating it without scope at the top of a hierarchy that governs both domains — see [[SoT - Processing IS the Work]] §6.
