## Digital Tools Cannot Replace the Cognitive Processes of Understanding and Connecting Ideas

Digital tools cannot replace the cognitive processes involved in understanding and connecting ideas.

### Scope & Conditions

The source grants that such tools can aid the storage and retrieval of information.

### Evidence

> "Digital tools can aid in the storage and retrieval of information, but they cannot replace the cognitive processes involved in understanding and connecting ideas."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 50).

### Implications

- The source says a PKM system should be seen as a tool to augment thinking, not replace it (source line 55).
- It holds that the real work of connecting ideas happens in the mind (source line 55).
