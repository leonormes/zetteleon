## Digital Tools Cannot Replace the Cognitive Processes of Understanding and Connecting Ideas

Digital tools cannot replace the cognitive processes involved in understanding and connecting ideas.

### Scope & Conditions

The source grants that such tools can aid the storage and retrieval of information, as well as mechanical graph bookkeeping (`edge_lint.py` and K-Plex), provided understanding and connection-making remain human.

### Evidence

> "Digital tools can aid in the storage and retrieval of information, but they cannot replace the cognitive processes involved in understanding and connecting ideas."

Source: [[Beyond Words]] (line 50).

### Implications

- A PKM system and its knowledge compiler ([[SoT - Knowledge Compiler (Argument Graph Spec)]]) are tools to augment and verify thinking, not replace it (source line 55).
- The real work of understanding and connecting ideas happens in the mind through productive cognitive friction ([[SoT - Processing IS the Work]], [[Eufriction - Productive Friction Strengthens Thinking]]).
