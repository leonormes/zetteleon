## First-Principles Causal Modelling Can Serve Both Agentic Situational Awareness and Threat-Led Certainty Seeking

Proposed as Candidate Driver 2 (Epistemic Safety Through Situational Awareness, proposed), building causal first-principles models of interacted systems provides genuine agency by clarifying dependencies, boundaries, and available responses. When failure or rejection sensitivity is active, that curiosity-led orientation can fuse with threat-led certainty seeking aimed at preventing shame and perceived exposure without collapsing the two motives into a single defensive label. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Synthesises the candidate-driver formulation from [[tmp_atoms_chaos-and-situational-awareness]] and [[tmp_atoms_psychology-of-over-preparing]] across cloud engineering, programming, PKM, and mathematics. Preserves distinct sub-labels—competence security, agency through understanding, shame-avoidant overlearning, and aesthetic-systemic curiosity—alongside co-existing or rival explanations such as professional conscientiousness and production-risk management.

### Evidence

> "I am drawn to first principles because understanding creates agency, competence, and epistemic safety. When rejection or failure sensitivity is active, that healthy drive can become fused with a need to prevent shame and prove adequacy. My task is not to abandon depth, but to distinguish curiosity-led understanding from threat-led certainty seeking."
> ([[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph — Interest Seed and Candidate Drivers]])

### Implications

- Self-inquiry should separate decision-relevant causal understanding from reassurance-seeking safety behaviours rather than abandoning analytical depth.
- Reducing deep inquiry solely to fear of looking foolish erases structural curiosity, while ignoring evaluative threat misses how exposure raises uncertainty costs.

### Falsifier

Repeatedly choosing difficult private inquiry with zero audience, operational risk, status, or performance payoff proves the drive exceeds threat defence alone; vanishing whenever evaluative exposure disappears shows threat reduction doing primary work.
