## Fly-by-Wire Exploratory Programming

Fly-by-wire programming is a real-time, interactive collaboration between humans and AI in stateful environments (e.g., Jupyter notebooks). This approach leverages AI for execution speed while preserving human agency, building the necessary technical intuition through immediate feedback from the system.

### Scope & Conditions

Uses interactive environments to keep the human engineer "in the loop" during the exploratory phase of development.

### Evidence

> "Howard advocates for a 'fly-by-wire' approach… an interactive, stateful environment… where humans and AI collaborate in real-time."

### Implications

- Provides immediate feedback for human manipulation of system objects and logic.
- Maintains human control over architectural direction while automating repetitive or low-level implementation tasks.
