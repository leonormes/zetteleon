## Storing Items at Their Point of Use Reduces Put-Away Friction More Than Conventional Placement

An item that keeps landing in one spot is showing where its home should be, so anchor a small tray or drop basket there to contain the pile and remove a transit step. Where an item is actually used matters more than where convention says it belongs. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to high-frequency items such as keys, post and chargers. It does not cover hazardous items, which stay where the safety rules say.

### Evidence

> "Store items where they are actually used, rather than where conventional organizing rules dictate."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against a primary source)

It agrees with the vault's own drop-zone design. The chat's sources are unchecked, so confidence is low.

### Implications

- Follow the pile: put the tray where things already land.
- A bounded tray looks intentional, which makes it easier to keep than a bare surface.
