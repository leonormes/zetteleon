## The Body Shapes Thought Because Bodily Boundaries Are Plastic and Tools Are Incorporated and Not Merely Used

Thinking is a physical act, and because bodily boundaries are plastic we incorporate tools such as a cane or a keyboard into the body schema and do not merely use them. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Embodied cognition, the first dimension of extension. Movement, natural environments and interoceptive signals can prepare the mind before capture, which [[Stage 0 Prepare (The Extended Mind Layer)]] applies.

### Evidence

This is my own summary of the thesis as set out in the earlier text of [[SoT - The Extended Mind]], which attributes it to Clark and Chalmers. No book quotation has been verified for this card.

### Implications

- Treat posture, movement and the physical setup as part of the thinking system.
- Reliable user-sensitive tools can become transparent equipment.
