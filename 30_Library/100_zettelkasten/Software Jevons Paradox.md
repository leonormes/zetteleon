## Software Jevons Paradox

Total demand for software increases as the cost of production drops, while the scarcity of manual coding skills decreases. This theoretical extension of the Jevons Paradox suggests that as implementation becomes cheaper through AI, the industry will pivot toward more complex systems and high-level coordination rather than reducing the total number of engineers.

### Scope & Conditions

Theoretical extension of the Jevons Paradox to software production. It warns against over-reliance on manual coding as a career moat while predicting an expansion in software scope.

### Evidence

> "The 'Peak Programmer' concept is an extension of the Jevons Paradox; as the cost of software production drops, the demand for software increases…"

### Implications

- Suggests a shift in labor demand toward high-level coordination.
- Warns against over-reliance on manual coding as a career moat.
