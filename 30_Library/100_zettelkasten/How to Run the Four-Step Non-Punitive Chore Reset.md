## How to Run the Four-Step Non-Punitive Chore Reset

Reset a missed or stalled chore through calm inspection, a two-minute starter action, optional body doubling, and a Sunday system tweak. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies when a daily or weekly chore is incomplete at check-in or stalls mid-task. Never run Steps 1 to 4 during Red Brain meltdown; regulate first.

### Evidence

> "When a task is missed or stalls, parents use this fixed, blame-free loop rather than shouting or lecturing: 1. Calm Check (Green Brain) ... 2. Starter 2-Minute Step ... 3. Tag-Team Support ... 4. Failure Autopsy & Sunday Tweak."
> ([[SoT - Family Household Chores]], text before the 2026-11-24 compilation refactor, §3.2)

This is my own operational procedure combining ADHD starter-step mechanics with the family Failure Autopsy loop.

### Implications

- Replaces shouting and shame spirals with immediate physical momentum.
- Converts recurring friction into a concrete checklist or timing adjustment at the Sunday review.
