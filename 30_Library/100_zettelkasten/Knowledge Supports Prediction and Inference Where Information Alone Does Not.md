## Knowledge Supports Prediction and Inference Where Information Alone Does Not

Because knowledge combines information with experience, it allows predictions, inferences and sound judgements, whereas information alone provides facts without insight into their implications.

### Scope & Conditions

The source frames information as generally insufficient for prediction or generalisation, not as incapable of it in every case.

### Evidence

> "Information alone is generally not sufficient to make predictions or generalizations."

> "Knowledge, due to its combination of information and experience, allows for predictions, inferences, and sound judgments."

### Implications

- Knowledge lets you understand patterns and their driving factors, not just their occurrence.
- Holding more facts does not by itself improve prediction.
