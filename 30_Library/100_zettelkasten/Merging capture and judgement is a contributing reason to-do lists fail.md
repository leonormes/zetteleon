## Merging capture and judgement is a contributing reason to-do lists fail

Merging raw capture with evaluation is one contributing reason traditional to-do lists break down, though it is not established as the primary cause. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- **Demoted from "primary reason":** Competing explanations for to-do list failure include missing physical next actions or contexts, absence of a weekly review habit, chronic overcommitment, and lack of a trusted system.

### Evidence

- **Unsourced hypothesis:** Derived from GTD practitioner lore; the earlier uncited quote asserting it as *"why traditional to-do lists fail"* has not yet been located in Allen (*Getting Things Done*) or any primary source. *(No Calibre book quotation attached yet.)*

### Implications

- Treat unjudged capture as one necessary safeguard among others (next-action wording, context filtering, and regular review), not a silver bullet.
