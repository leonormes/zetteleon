## Minimum Viable Experiment (MVE)

A Minimum Viable Experiment (MVE) is an action defined by its extreme brevity (less than two minutes), designed to bypass the mind's automatic rebellion against effort. By reducing the required action to its irreducible minimum, the MVE lowers activation energy and prioritizes the establishment of a routine over output volume.

### Scope & Conditions

Used to initiate new habits or overcome significant task inertia by making the first step "laughably small."

### Evidence

> "Design your action as an experiment so small it cannot trigger that rebellion… Implement the 'Minimum Viable Habit'. Cut the required action down to the absolute bare minimum."

### Implications

- Bypasses the "Childish Mind's" allergy to effort by presenting a non-threatening requirement.
- Successfully establishing the routine provides the platform for later scaling of effort.
