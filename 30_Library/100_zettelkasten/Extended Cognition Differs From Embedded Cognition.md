## Extended Cognition Differs From Embedded Cognition

Extended cognition (HEC) holds that external objects are literal constituents of the cognitive process, while embedded cognition (HEMC) holds that they are only useful aids or inputs to a mind that stays inside the organism. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

The distinction sits at the heart of the critics debate about the Extended Mind Thesis. This card takes the HEC side, in its organism-centred form.

### Evidence

This is my own summary of the thesis as set out in the earlier text of [[SoT - The Extended Mind]], which attributes it to Clark and Chalmers. No book quotation has been verified for this card.

### Implications

- Which reading you take decides whether losing a tool removes a faculty or only makes tasks harder.
