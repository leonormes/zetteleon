## Curiosity is Taking an Interest in Experience for its Own Sake

Curiosity is taking an interest in ongoing experience for its own sake, through exploring and discovering. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- Passively taking in information does not by itself count.

### Evidence

> "Curiosity is actively engaging novelty, and the passive absorption of information does not display this strength"
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 236) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what here is worth exploring actively.
