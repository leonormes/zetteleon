## A Weekly Family Meeting Separates System Adjustment From Daily Chore Execution

A once or twice weekly family meeting gives the household a dedicated, non-critical time to refine the system, which separates system changes from daily chore execution. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to households running a shared chore system. The proposed slot is Sunday 5 pm, to be confirmed at the Family Kickoff.

### Evidence

> "Set a dedicated, non-critical time for team alignment and process refinement, separating system adjustments from daily chore execution."
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §6)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

- Raise a job that is too hard or unfair here, not mid-week.
- Test each tweak as a small joint experiment.
