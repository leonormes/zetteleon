## Humans Engineer Their Workspaces and Tools to Reshape How the Brain Processes Information

Humans practise niche construction by engineering structural layers such as specialised workspaces and IDEs, which reshape how the biological brain processes information. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Situated cognition, the second dimension. It applies to environments a person can actually change.

### Evidence

This is my own summary of the thesis as set out in the earlier text of [[SoT - The Extended Mind]], which attributes it to Clark and Chalmers. No book quotation has been verified for this card.

### Implications

- Designing the environment is a cognitive act.
- A change of workspace can change what is easy to think.
