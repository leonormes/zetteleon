## Understanding Emotion

Emotions arise as involuntary physiological signals rather than conscious choices. Faced with strong affect, people often assume a false binary between impulsive reaction and effortful suppression. Musashi's strategy introduces a third option: embrace and observe emotion as a transient vector without obeying it.

### Scope & Conditions

- **Triadic Choice**: Relate to primary affect ([[SoT - Mindfulness and Emotional Regulation]]) by accepting rather than suppressing or acting on it ([[Your emotions are real but they are your responsibility]]).
- **Both Extremes Fail**: *Reaction* surrenders agency; *suppression* fuels rebound friction ([[Ownership of Emotions]]).
- **Musashi Alignment**: Acknowledging feeling preserves [[Heijo-shin (Ordinary Mind)]], prevents sticking ([[Fudo-shin Is a Mind That Does Not Stick on Anything, So Immovable Means Fluid]]), and clears perception toward [[The Void (Ku) Is Perceiving the Situation as It Is Without the Distortion of Fear or Desire]].

### Evidence

> "Musashi's Emotional Mastery is not the suppression of feeling, but the cultivation of Heijo-shin (Ordinary Mind)—a state where the mind remains constant during the chaos of battle (or crisis) as it is in everyday life."
> ([[SoT - Miyamoto Musashi's Philosophy on Mastering Emotions]], §1 Definitive Statement)

This converges with Morita *arugamama* ([[Morita Therapy - Arugamama is Accepting Reality As It Is]]) and affective-labelling findings.

### Implications

- **Signal, Not Verdict**: Validate the felt state without treating it as proof of external facts.
- **Tactical Pause**: Use [[How to Use the Pause to Separate the Signal From the Emotional Noise]] to name feelings before acting.
- **ADHD Buffer**: During [[Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD)]], refusing suppression and impulsive discharge prevents cognitive tunnelling.
