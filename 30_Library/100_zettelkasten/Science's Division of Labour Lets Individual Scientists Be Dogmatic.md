## Science's Division of Labour Lets Individual Scientists Be Dogmatic

Whereas Popper thought every individual scientist must be constantly open-minded and self-critical, Godfrey-Smith suggests that individual scientists can actually be quite dogmatic about their own theories. The scientific method works because the community distributes the labour, and rival scientists will eagerly do the critical testing for them.

### Scope & Conditions

Godfrey-Smith's naturalistic, sociological account of science, contra Popper's demand that individuals themselves be constantly self-critical.

### Evidence

> "individual scientists can actually be quite dogmatic about their own theories; the scientific method works because the community distributes the labor, and rival scientists will eagerly do the critical testing for them"

### Implications

- Relocates the open-mindedness requirement from the individual scientist to the community structure.
- Supports treating consensus survival as evidence of adversarial testing having occurred, even when individual researchers are personally biased.
