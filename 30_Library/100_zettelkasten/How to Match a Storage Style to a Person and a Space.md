## How to Match a Storage Style to a Person and a Space

Use the two Clutterbug axes to choose a storage style for one person in one space, then trial it. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to one zone and one main user at a time. For shared spaces, agree the compromise at the weekly family meeting.

### Evidence

This procedure is my own construction from [[Clutterbug Storage Taxonomy]]. The source chat only offered to match profiles to spaces and gave no steps, so confidence is low.

### Implications

1. Name the main user of the space.
2. Ask whether they need to see items or have them hidden.
3. Ask whether they sort broadly or finely when tired.
4. Pick the matching profile and container type.
5. Trial it for two weeks and keep it or go back.
