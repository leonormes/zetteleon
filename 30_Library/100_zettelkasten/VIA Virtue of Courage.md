The virtue of Courage, as defined by the [[MOC - Character and Virtue|VIA Character Strengths]] framework, involves emotional strengths that enable the exercise of will to accomplish goals in the face of opposition, whether external or internal.

## Strengths of Courage

- [[Bravery is Not Shrinking From What is Frightening|Bravery]]: Not shrinking from threat, challenge, difficulty, or pain; speaking up for what is right.
- [[Perseverance is Finishing What One Starts Despite Obstacles|Perseverance (Persistence)]]: Finishing what one starts; persisting in a course of action despite obstacles.
- [[Honesty is Being Truthful and Sincere|Honesty (Integrity)]]: Speaking the truth and presenting oneself in a genuine and sincere way; taking responsibility for one's feelings and actions.
- [[Zest is Approaching Life with Excitement and Energy|Zest (Vitality)]]: Approaching life with excitement and energy; living life as an adventure.
