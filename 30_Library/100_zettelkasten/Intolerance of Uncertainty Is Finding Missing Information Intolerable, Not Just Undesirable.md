## Intolerance of Uncertainty Is Finding Missing Information Intolerable, Not Just Undesirable

Intolerance of uncertainty (IU) is a tendency to find uncertainty itself aversive or dangerous enough that it must be resolved, rather than merely a preference for clarity; a prospective form of IU—anxious anticipation of future uncertainty—is distinguished in the research from other forms involving paralysis or inhibition in the face of uncertainty.

### Scope & Conditions

A named research construct with sub-forms; high IU can lead people to seek information even where that information has little practical value.

### Evidence

> "A useful construct here is intolerance of uncertainty: a tendency to find uncertainty itself aversive, threatening, or difficult to endure. It is not merely preferring clarity… Research distinguishes a prospective form—anxious anticipation of future uncertainty, often associated with worry—from other forms involving paralysis or inhibition."

### Implications

- "I need the full picture before I can act" can be IU manifesting as excess information-seeking rather than sound risk assessment.
