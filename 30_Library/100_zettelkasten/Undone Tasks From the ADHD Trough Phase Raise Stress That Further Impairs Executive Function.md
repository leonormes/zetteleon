## Undone Tasks From the ADHD Trough Phase Raise Stress That Further Impairs Executive Function

Executive dysfunction in the trough leaves tasks undone, the accumulating responsibilities raise stress, and the stress impairs executive function further, producing a vicious cycle of overwhelm. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Describes a feedback loop that runs while the trough lasts. It assumes undone tasks accumulate, which depends on how many demands arrive during the trough.

### Implications

- Breaking the loop at the task end, by lowering the number of live demands, may help more than trying to raise executive function directly.
