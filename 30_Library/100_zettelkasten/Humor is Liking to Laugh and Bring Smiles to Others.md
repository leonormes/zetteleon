## Humor is Liking to Laugh and Bring Smiles to Others

Humor is liking to laugh and tease, bringing smiles to others and seeing the light side of life. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It is also called playfulness.

### Evidence

> "You like to laugh and bring smiles to other people."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 258) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask whether lightness here would bring others in.
