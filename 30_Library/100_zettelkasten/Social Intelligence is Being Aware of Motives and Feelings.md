## Social Intelligence is Being Aware of Motives and Feelings

Social Intelligence is awareness of the motives and feelings of yourself and others, and knowing how to respond across social situations. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It includes emotional intelligence.
- It is not mere introspection or rumination.

### Evidence

> "You are aware of the motives and feelings of others, and you can respond well to them."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 240) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

This vault files it under Humanity, while Seligman lists it under Wisdom and Knowledge.

### Implications

- Ask what the other person is feeling and wanting before you respond.
