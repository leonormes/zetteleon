## Bodily Feelings Act as Pre-Verbal Cognition

Bodily feelings work as a primary form of cognition that guides decisions before conscious verbal reasoning occurs.

### Scope & Conditions

The source attributes this to Antonio Damasio's research on somatic markers; the attribution is not verified here and appears once in the source.

### Evidence

> "Antonio Damasio's research on somatic markers suggests that bodily feelings serve as a primary form of cognition, guiding decisions before conscious verbal reasoning occurs."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 284).

### Implications

- The source observes that we often feel before we can articulate what we feel (source line 284).
- It treats emotion as a pre-linguistic form of understanding (source line 284).
