## Critiquing Ideas with Ego-Fused People Requires Linguistic Decoupling

Summary: When the other person's beliefs are fused to their identity, your critique of the idea registers as an attack on them—manage the interaction at the interface level rather than pushing harder with logic.

Details: Four structural adjustments. (1) Linguistic decoupling: remove "you/your" from the proposition—"this policy misses the economic data", not "your reasoning doesn't make sense"—relentlessly placing the claim back on the table. (2) Shift from error-correction to trace-logging: ask "what led to that conclusion?" so they must pick up the specimen and examine it themselves, initiating their own decoupling. (3) Distress tolerance: their defensiveness is a threat response; remain warm and present without validating the false belief or rescuing them from friction. (4) Abort condition: if a belief is load-bearing for identity and they escalate or shut down, exit—"I look at the data differently, but I understand why you value that approach." The goal is to maintain your model, not to fix theirs.
