## De-escalating Partner Conversations Requires Redirecting Drift Without Naming Fallacies Aloud

During a difficult partner conversation, redirecting topic drift with neutral agenda-parking scripts preserves psychological safety far better than naming logical fallacies or cornering questions aloud.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to high-stakes domestic negotiations held away from bedtime and immediate conflict; pauses and resumes the next day if either partner becomes heated.

### Evidence

> "One topic at a time. If it drifts: 'let's park that, it's written down, back to X'–never name the fallacy aloud. ... questions asked to corner her would break the spirit of this protocol."
> ([[Household Routines Proposal]], §7)

### Implications

- Using neutral scripts handles counter-grievances and deflections without triggering defensiveness.
- Restating the partner's perspective before one's own keeps the conversation collaborative rather than adversarial.
