## Humility is Letting Achievements Speak for Themselves

Humility is the strength of not seeking the spotlight and letting your accomplishments speak for themselves. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- A humble person is modest, does not boast and does not regard themselves as special.
- The vault reads this as thinking about yourself less, not as thinking poorly of yourself.
- The vault has also described humility as supporting wisdom and continued learning. That is not part of the definition.

### Evidence

> "You do not seek the spotlight, preferring to let your accomplishments speak for themselves."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 252) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what the work shows without you drawing attention to yourself.
- Related claim: [[Humility Makes Self-Assessment More Accurate]].
