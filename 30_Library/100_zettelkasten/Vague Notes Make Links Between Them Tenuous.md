## Vague Notes Make Links Between Them Tenuous

When notes are vague or lack depth, the links between them are tenuous, because interpreting the connection depends on the existing knowledge and mental models of the reader.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies where the reader's own model, not the note, supplies the meaning of the connection.

### Evidence

> "If the notes are vague or lack depth, the links become even more tenuous. The interpretation of the connection relies heavily on our existing knowledge and mental models, which may not be fully captured in the notes."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 44).

### Implications

- The source recommends depth over quantity of links (source line 45).
- Notes should carry enough context, examples and reasoning to reduce ambiguity (source line 46).
