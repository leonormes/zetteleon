## A PKM Is Personal Because Notes Only Represent Ideas Held in One Head

Because the ideas are mostly in the head of the owner and the notes only represent them, another person cannot take the notes and understand the same things.

### Scope & Conditions

Follows from the claim that notes are representations rather than the ideas themselves.

### Evidence

> "Because the ideas are mostly in our heads, and the notes are just representations, a PKM is a very personal thing. Another person could not simply take your notes and understand the same things."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 51).

### Implications

- The source calls a PKM "a very personal thing" (source line 51).
- Notes are described as "just representations", so the understanding does not travel with them (source line 51).
