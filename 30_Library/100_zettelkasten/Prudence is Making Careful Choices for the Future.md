## Prudence is Making Careful Choices for the Future

Prudence is the strength of making careful choices with the future in view, reasoning about consequences before acting. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It involves foresight and caution.

### Evidence

> "You are a careful person. You do not say or do things you might regret later."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 251) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

> "Prudent individuals are farsighted and deliberative."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 251) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what you might regret later before you commit.
