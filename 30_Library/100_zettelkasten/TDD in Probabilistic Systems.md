## TDD in Probabilistic Systems

Test-Driven Development (TDD) provides the necessary deterministic "truth" required to verify the output of probabilistic AI systems. By writing tests first, the engineer creates a verifiable anchor of correct behaviour that the AI-generated logic must satisfy.

### Scope & Conditions

Essential practice for maintaining software reliability in AI-assisted development.

### Evidence

> "Kent Beck notes that TDD's value is increasing as a tool for verifying AI-generated output."

### Implications

- Automated tests serve as the ultimate guardrail for non-deterministic AI logic.
- TDD prevents the accumulation of "understanding debt" by enforcing verifiable logic.
