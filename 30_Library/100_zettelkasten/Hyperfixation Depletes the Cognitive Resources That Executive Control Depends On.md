## Hyperfixation Depletes the Cognitive Resources That Executive Control Depends On

Intense hyperfixation spends the mental resources that executive control draws on, so the person enters the post-hyperfixation trough with less of them available. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the phase straight after a hyperfixation episode in ADHD. It does not claim every hyperfixation episode is followed by a trough of the same depth.

### Implications

- The trough is partly a recovery period, so the cost of the next executive demand is higher than usual.
