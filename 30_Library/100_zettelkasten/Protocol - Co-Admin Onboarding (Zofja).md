> Trigger: onboarding Zofja as co-administrator of the household system.

## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Onboarding Zofja as co-administrator works when the system is framed as a stress-reduction sanctuary, not a critique of her standards. The visual checklists and check-in gate play "the bad guy", fixed scripts are rehearsed together, and her invisible mental load is made explicit on the Whole-Family Ownership Map, so both parents enforce the same rules calmly without nagging or conflict.

---

## 1. The Framing

Frame the system as a stress-reduction tool, not a criticism of existing standards:

1. **Sanctuary, not standards.** Use the vault's own language: a calm, restful sanctuary; fewer arguments; more free time. Avoid the words "standards", "mess", and "discipline".
2. **The system is the bad guy.** Evening screens and Wi-Fi pause automatically when checklists are unfinished—it is the shared agreement, not Mum, saying no ([[Tying Discretionary Privileges to Completed Checklists Automates Accountability Without Nagging]]). Neutral `Work = Access` means she never has to argue, negotiate, or be the villain.
3. **Scripts replace improvisation.** Fixed scripts cover the single 30-minute reminder ([[How to Run the Daily Chore Check-In]]), the 4-step non-punitive reset ([[How to Run the Four-Step Non-Punitive Chore Reset]]), and the Study Sprint Failure Autopsy ([[Protocol - Bessie Study Cadence & Neurodiverse Scaffolding]]).
4. **The Tap-Out is her safety valve.** Validated by the Parental Alignment SLA and Tag-Team Handover in [[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]] and [[How to Tap Out and Tap In During Household Overload]].
5. **Her invisible work is on the map.** The Whole-Family Ownership Ledger in [[Protocol - Household Zone Standards & Pet Care SOPs]] §2 and [[Family Chores - Everyone's Guide]] §2 shows the children that Mum and Dad's quieter jobs count as real household work.
6. **Joint agreement & small trials.** Routine rules require joint parental agreement before enforcement ([[Shared Parental Agreement Must Precede Unilateral Routine Enforcement]]) and are tested as small, reversible experiments ([[Household Routine Changes Should Be Small Joint and Reversible Experiments]]).

---

## 2. Onboarding Sequence

1. [ ] Review [[Family Chores - Everyone's Guide]] and [[Protocol - Household Zone Standards & Pet Care SOPs]] §2 (Whole-Family Ownership Ledger) together.
2. [ ] Confirm or adjust the proposed check-in times (**6:00 pm daily** / **Sunday 5:00 pm** weekly review, with **one calm, neutral reminder ~30 minutes beforehand**), the privilege list, and access-control mechanics at Family Kickoff.
3. [ ] Agree the two shared administration boundaries: No Rescue (don't absorb unfinished child jobs) and calm, non-escalating responses (Grey Rock / Green Brain only).
4. [ ] Rehearse the two scripts aloud once each (the 30-minute neutral reminder + calm `Work = Access` boundary line).
5. [ ] Agree the Tap-Out signal (a word or gesture) for Tag-Team Handovers ([[How to Tap Out and Tap In During Household Overload]]).
6. [ ] Run a friendly 1-week trial with one parent leading check-ins and the other supporting, then review together on Sunday.
