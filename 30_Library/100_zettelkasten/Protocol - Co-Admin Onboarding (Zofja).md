## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Onboarding Zofja as co-administrator of the household system succeeds only when the system is framed as a stress-reduction sanctuary rather than a critique of her standards. By letting the Audit Gate play "the bad guy", rehearsing fixed scripts together, and making her invisible mental load explicit on the Ownership Map, both parents enforce the same rules without nagging or conflict.

---

## 1. The Framing

When aligning on system administration, the system must be framed as a stress-reduction tool, not a criticism of existing standards:

1. Sanctuary, not standards. Use the vault's own language: a calm, restful sanctuary; fewer arguments; more free time. Avoid the words "standards", "mess", and "discipline".
2. The system is the bad guy. Wi-Fi and screens are locked by the Audit Gate, not by her. "Work = Access" means she never has to argue, negotiate, or be the villain.
3. Scripts replace improvisation. The Grace Period and Failure Autopsy ([[Protocol - Bessie Study Cadence & Neurodiverse Scaffolding]]) and Grey Rock line ([[SoT - Family Household Governance]]) provide fixed scripts.
4. The Tap-Out is her safety valve. Validated by the Parental Alignment SLA and Tag-Team Handover in [[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]].
5. Her invisible work is on the map. The Whole-Family Ownership Map in [[SoT - Family Household Governance]] shows the children that Mum's quieter jobs count as jobs.

---

## 2. Onboarding Sequence

1. Review [[Family Chores - Everyone's Guide]] together.
2. Co-decide audit times (e.g., 18:00 daily / Sunday 17:00), the exact privilege list, and access-control mechanics.
3. Agree the two non-negotiables for her role: No Rescue and Grey Rock.
4. Rehearse the two scripts aloud once each (Grace Period reminder + Grey Rock line).
5. Agree the Tap-Out signal (a word or gesture).
6. Run one practice week with one parent as lead Inspector and the other observing.
