## Calculus Prerequisite Domains

Proficiency in algebra, functions, and trigonometry is required to analyse piecewise rational functions without calculus-specific tools. Mastery of these prerequisite domains is essential for building the foundations necessary to understand limits and derivatives.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to students and practitioners building the necessary mathematical base for advanced calculus.

### Evidence

> "Students must master three key areas: algebra, functions, and trigonometry… sketch complex piecewise rational functions without needing to rely on limits or derivatives."

### Implications

- Pre-calculus mastery significantly reduces cognitive load when introducing limits.
- Algebraic errors remain the primary bottleneck in overall calculus performance.
