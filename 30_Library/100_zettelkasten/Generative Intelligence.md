## Generative Intelligence

Generative Intelligence is the rare cognitive capacity to synthesise entirely new frameworks of understanding—new paradigms—rather than solving puzzles inside an established rulebook. Analogous to Thomas Kuhn's "Scientific Revolution," it operates by surfacing invisible assumptions, dwelling in unknown unknowns, and formulating new questions when an old paradigm breaks down.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to domains where accumulating anomalies or structural limits expose the inadequacy of current mental models. Once a new framework succeeds, subsequent work shifts back to [[Applicative Intelligence]] to refine and exploit it ([[Impact of Set 2 Breakthroughs]]).

### Evidence

Defined in [[SoT - Generative Intelligence]] by mapping Thomas Kuhn's normal-versus-revolutionary science distinction onto individual cognitive modes.

### Implications

- Confusing mastery of existing facts with generative intelligence leads experts to defend dying frameworks instead of questioning their premises.
- Generative intelligence carries high social and epistemic risk because new frameworks initially lack institutional consensus.
