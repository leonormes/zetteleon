## Archery Shot Process - Setup

Summary:
Setup involves setting the hands onto the bow, including hooking onto the string and establishing the pressure point in the bow grip.

Details:
- **Order of Execution**: Always **hook the string first**, then place the hand into the bow grip. Setting the bow hand first forces you to grip tightly; setting the hook first allows the bow hand to remain relaxed.
- **Shoulder Positioning**: Keep shoulders **open** relative to the target line. Avoid aligning the shoulders prematurely in this phase (premature shoulder alignment forces an excessively high raise and relies on gravity/momentum to drop into full draw, straining the shoulder joints).
- **The Hook (Draw Hand)**:
  - **Hook as One Unit**: The index, middle, and ring fingers must act together as a single, relaxed unit. Avoid bulging knuckles or an angled wrist joint.
  - **Hook Depth (First Joint Sweet Spot)**:
    - *Avoid Too Deep*: Placing the string past the first knuckle (towards the second joint) makes a clean release impossible, forcing you to uncurl two entire finger digits.
    - *Avoid Too Shallow (Fingertips)*: Hooking on the very fingertips introduces massive isometric tension. Fingertip hooks deflect the string twice as much because tense muscles cannot relax quickly.
    - *The Sweet Spot*: Position the string directly in the first groove of your index finger, in or slightly behind the groove on your middle finger, and on the pad (slightly in front of the groove) of your ring finger.
  - Align fingers starting with the first groove of the middle finger. Note that all four fingers (including the pinky) affect finger tension on the string.
  - Place one finger above the arrow and two below (split finger setup).
  - Weight/pressure distribution: 40% on the middle finger, 30% on the index and ring fingers.
  - Keep the pinky relaxed and neutral. The thumb should rest just below the shelf.
  - Maintain a neutral wrist.
- **The Grip (Bow Hand)**:
  - Place the 'Y' of your hand into the grip so the pressure point sits directly on the thumb pad.
  - Push into the riser using the flat bone intersection between the index finger and thumb metacarpals (the knuckle bone joint), rather than the soft webbing of the thumb.
  - Drive tension into the pit/pressure point of the bow hand towards the target.
  - Keep the thumb alongside the bow and slightly retract your knuckles.
  - Keep fingers relaxed, curling them in without squeezing or touching the bow.
  - Relax the thumb downwards, maintaining slight tension into the riser.
