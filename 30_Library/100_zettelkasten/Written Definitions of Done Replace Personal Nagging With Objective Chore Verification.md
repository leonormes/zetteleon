## Written Definitions of Done Replace Personal Nagging With Objective Chore Verification

Written checklists that define completed work from start to finish let parents verify household tasks against an objective standard rather than personal mood or repeated nagging. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to shared household chores and zone resets where unwritten expectations create loopholes, arguments, or resentment between parents and children.

### Evidence

> "Objective Standards (Zero Loopholes): Written checklists define "done" from start to finish (e.g., wiping the dining table includes sweeping up crumbs; emptying a bin includes fitting a fresh liner) so parents inspect against the list rather than personal mood."
> ([[SoT - Family Household Chores]], text before the 2026-11-24 compilation refactor, §1.2)

This is my own reasoning synthesised from household practice, not an externally sourced empirical study.

### Implications

- Inspect chores with the zone checklist in hand and point to the written step rather than lecturing.
- Treat any recurring loophole as a missing checklist line to refine at the Sunday review, not as a character flaw.
