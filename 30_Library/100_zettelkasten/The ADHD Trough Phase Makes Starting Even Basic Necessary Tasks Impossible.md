## The ADHD Trough Phase Makes Starting Even Basic Necessary Tasks Impossible

During the trough a person cannot start even simple and necessary tasks such as showering, eating or answering messages. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Describes the felt experience of task initiation in the trough, as reported in the source note. It is a description of manifestation, and the cause is covered by the dopamine and activation claims.

### Implications

- Basic self-care and communication can lapse in the trough, and this reflects impaired initiation rather than choice.
