## Minimum Viable Understanding
We instinctively categorize people based on their beliefs because doing so is a "factory setting" of the brain, driven by three stacked mechanisms:
1. **Cognitive Economy:** Categorising is cheap; suspended judgement is expensive. Brains under load sort people into bins because bins are fast.
2. **Tribal Badges:** Opinions do not just describe the world; they signal group membership. Judging people by their beliefs is ancient social sorting machinery doing its job.
3. **Ego-Fusion:** When beliefs are fused with the *self*, disagreement registers as a personal attack. The emotional sting of disagreement is a result of that fusion, not a character flaw.

Recognising this machinery as normal evolutionary behavior removes the shame from the habit and makes deliberate cognitive override possible.
