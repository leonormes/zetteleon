## Perspective is Providing Wise Counsel to Others

Perspective is the ability to give wise counsel to others, drawing on ways of looking at the world that make sense to yourself and to them. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- The test is whether the view helps other people with their problems.

### Evidence

> "Others seek you out to draw on your experience to help them solve problems and gain perspective."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 241) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what view would help the other person decide.
