## Wicked Learning Environments Are Dynamic and Unpredictable

Summary: A "wicked" learning environment is dynamic, unpredictable, and provides delayed, unclear, or inaccurate feedback on actions.

Details: In contrast to [[Kind Learning Environments are Predictable and Rule-Based|kind environments]], wicked environments lack stable rules and clear cause-and-effect relationships. Success often requires adaptability, experimentation, and the ability to synthesize information from diverse sources. Most [[Modern Work Environments are Typically Wicked|modern work environments]] are considered wicked, favoring individuals with broad experience and flexible problem-solving skills over narrow specialists.
