The virtue of Wisdom, as defined by the [[MOC - Character and Virtue|VIA Character Strengths]] framework, encompasses character strengths related to the acquisition and use of knowledge.

## Strengths of Wisdom

- [[Creativity is Thinking of Novel and Productive Ways to Do Things|Creativity]]: Thinking of new and productive ways to conceptualize and do things.
- [[Curiosity is Taking an Interest in Experience for its Own Sake|Curiosity]]: Taking an interest in ongoing experience for its own sake; exploring and discovering.
- [[Judgment is Thinking Things Through and Examining All Sides|Judgement (Critical Thinking)]]: Thinking things through and examining them from all sides; not jumping to conclusions.
- [[Love of Learning is Mastering New Skills and Knowledge|Love of Learning]]: Mastering new skills, topics, and bodies of knowledge.
- [[Perspective is Providing Wise Counsel to Others|Perspective]]: Being able to provide wise counsel to others; having ways of looking at the world that make sense to oneself and others.
