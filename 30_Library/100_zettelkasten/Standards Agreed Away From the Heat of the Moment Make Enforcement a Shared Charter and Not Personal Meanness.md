## Standards Agreed Away From the Heat of the Moment Make Enforcement a Shared Charter and Not Personal Meanness

Non-negotiable standards agreed away from the heat of the moment turn the enforcement by a parent into carrying out a shared charter and not into being mean. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to standards both parents have actually agreed to. It fails if one parent never agreed.

### Evidence

> "When a parent enforces a rule, they are not being "mean"; they are executing the Shared Team Charter."
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §3 Protocol B)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

- The efficiency partner feels backed up.
- The immediacy partner does not have to be the sole enforcer.
