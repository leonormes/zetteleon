## High-speed Associative Leaping (Hyperactivity-of-thought)

Look at the trajectory we traversed:

> JTB → Gettier → IOED → PKM structure → Heptabase vs Obsidian → order theory and posets → typed links → Heptabase implementation → Pieces' note on proposition-centred PKM → granularity/TDD → Popperian discipline → Protocol cards → brain dump about workflow

Eleven distinct topics in one session. Each transition was justified—you were not being random—but the _velocity_ is striking. Most people, in a conversation about "validate this epistemology claim," do not end up at "how do I structure my entire knowledge system" within the same sitting.

This is the combined-type profile showing up as associative hyperdrive. The DIVA 7/9 hyperactivity/impulsivity score predicts exactly this: ideas arrive fast, connections form rapidly, and the brain wants to chase all of them _now_.

The double edge: this is also why you can do the JTB ↔ order theory ↔ PKM mapping that most people cannot. The same trait that risks scattering also produces synthesis. Do not try to suppress it. Channel it.
