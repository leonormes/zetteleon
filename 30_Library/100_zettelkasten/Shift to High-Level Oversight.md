## Shift to High-Level Oversight

Engineering value is shifting away from manual syntax generation toward high-level architectural oversight and "context curation." This evolution redefines the core skills required for senior engineers, prioritizing the design of systems and the management of information density over implementation speed.

### Scope & Conditions

Describes the future trajectory of senior technical roles in an agent-saturated industry.
