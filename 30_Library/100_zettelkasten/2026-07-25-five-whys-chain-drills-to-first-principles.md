Ask "why?" about a claimed mechanism, then ask why again about the answer, five times over. The chain reliably runs out somewhere before five—and the point where it runs out is exactly the edge of what you actually understand. Listed in the source as one of the named Forcing Functions (antidotes) for IoED.

## Evidence

> "The 'Why' Chain (The Causal Audit): Ask 'Why?' five times to drill down to first principles."—[[SoT - Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IoED)]]

## Steelman

Five is an arbitrary heuristic depth—some causal chains bottom out in fewer iterations and others need many more; rigidly stopping at five whys risks either stopping short of the genuine root cause or manufacturing spurious depth past the point where further "why" questions are answerable.
