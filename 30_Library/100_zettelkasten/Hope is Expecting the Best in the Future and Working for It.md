## Hope is Expecting the Best in the Future and Working for It

Hope is expecting the best of the future and working to achieve it, believing that a good future can be brought about. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It includes optimism and future-mindedness and is not expectation without effort.

### Evidence

> "You expect the best in the future, and you plan and work in order to achieve it."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 255) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what you can plan and do to bring the expected good about.
