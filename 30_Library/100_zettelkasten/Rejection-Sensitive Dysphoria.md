An intense, often disproportionate emotional response to perceived failure, criticism or rejection, commonly associated with ADHD.

## Why it Matters here

- It raises the stakes of any lapse in a system: a missed review is not neutral, it reads as evidence of inadequacy.
- This is part of the engine behind perfectionism and the hunt for a flawless setup. See [[Claim - ADHD perfectionism is maladaptive compensation]].
