## SoT - Cognitive Engineering Protocols

> [!definition] Definition
> Cognitive Engineering is the deliberate use of external tools (pen, paper, syntax) to regulate the nervous system and optimize Executive Function. It treats "Writing" not as an artistic output, but as a System Maintenance Tool (Defrag/Clear Cache).

### Core Summary (Conserved Elsewhere)

1. **Neuro-Synchronization:** Translating abstract feeling into linear language activates the Prefrontal Cortex (PFC), dampening Amygdala/Mid-Cingulate loops and freeing working memory RAM — conserved in [[SoT - ProdOS Thinking Stream]].
2. **Module A — The Brain Dump (Clear RAM):** Dump everything unfiltered into one capture point (capped at the top 10 loudest items when overloaded), then triage into Todoist or Obsidian via LLM-assisted sorting — conserved in [[Protocol - Action-First GTD (LLM Chief of Staff)]].
