## Perseverance is Finishing What You Start Despite Difficulty

Perseverance, or grit, is finishing what you start even when it becomes difficult. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It combines passion with persistence toward long-term goals, keeping focus despite setbacks.
- This is the child-worded version of the formal strength.

### Evidence

> "You finish what you start."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 243) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.
