## How to Build a Room-Reset Kit for the 5 Things Tidy

Pre-place the bag, hamper, dish spot and basket so a reset can start the moment energy appears. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the main rooms where resets happen. It adds clutter if the kit lives in every room, so start with the busiest one.

### Evidence

This procedure is my own construction from [[5 Things Tidying Method]]. The source chat only offered to design a room-reset kit and gave no steps, so confidence is low.

### Implications

1. Put a rubbish bag within reach of the room.
2. Place a hamper in or near the room.
3. Choose where dirty dishes are stacked on the way to the sink.
4. Set one basket in the room for items without a place.
5. Test the kit on one reset and adjust what slowed you down.
