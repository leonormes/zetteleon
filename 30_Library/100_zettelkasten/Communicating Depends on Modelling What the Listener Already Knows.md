## Communicating Depends on Modelling What the Listener Already Knows

Effective communication depends on the implicit model a speaker holds of what the listener already knows and is likely to misunderstand.

### Scope & Conditions

Described in the source as theory of mind, present "to varying extents" in different people.

### Evidence

> "Theory of Mind: We have, to varying extents, the ability to model the mental states of others. When communicating, we are implicitly making best guesses about what our interlocutor already knows, doesn't know, and is likely to misunderstand. This is essential for effective communication."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 191).

### Implications

- The source names this modelling as essential for effective communication (source line 191).
- Its teacher example refines an explanation by observing confused expressions and fielding questions (source line 322).
