## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Cognition is not confined to the brain. It runs through the body, the environment, other people and digital tools, and a tool that is reliable, always at hand and trusted counts as part of the cognitive system. ProdOS is built on that reading, so availability, reliability and backup are cognitive design concerns and not conveniences.
