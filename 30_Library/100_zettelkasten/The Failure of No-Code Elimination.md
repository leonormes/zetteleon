## The Failure of "No-Code" Elimination

Historical attempts to eliminate programmers through high-level abstractions have consistently failed because they cannot remove the requirement for absolute logical precision. AI-driven development is likely to follow this pattern, as the engineer's core value remains their capacity for rigorous logical thought.

### Scope & Conditions

Reflects a historical pattern (COBOL, 4GL, CASE tools) likely to repeat in the AI era.

### Evidence

> "Historically, attempts to eliminate programmers… failed because they could not eliminate the requirement for logical precision. AI is likely to follow this pattern."

### Implications

- Logical thinking remains the core competency of the software engineer.
- AI replaces the burden of syntax generation but not the requirement for logical rigour.
