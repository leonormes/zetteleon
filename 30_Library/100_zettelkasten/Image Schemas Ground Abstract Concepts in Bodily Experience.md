## Image Schemas Ground Abstract Concepts in Bodily Experience

Recurring patterns of bodily experience, called image schemas, structure abstract concepts, as physical containment underlies expressions such as being in a good mood.

### Scope & Conditions

The source hedges the first passage ("might operate through") and attributes the related conceptual-metaphor work to George Lakoff without citation.

### Evidence

> "Abstract conceptual thinking might operate through what philosophers call image schemas—recurring patterns of bodily experience that structure our understanding."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 286).

### Implications

- The vertical dimension of physical space grounds expressions such as "high status" and "falling into depression" (source line 318).
- Shared bodies give different languages shared conceptual anchors.
