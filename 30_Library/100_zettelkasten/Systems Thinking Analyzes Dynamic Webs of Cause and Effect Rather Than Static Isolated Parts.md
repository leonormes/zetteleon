## Systems Thinking Analyzes Dynamic Webs of Cause and Effect Rather Than Static Isolated Parts

Systems thinking counters reductionism by analysing dynamic webs of circular cause and effect over time rather than isolating static parts and assuming linear one-way causation. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Most valuable in complex or nonlinear domains where interactions, feedback loops, and delays dominate behavior. Reductionism remains effective for decomposable, linear problems where part-level properties sum predictably.

### Evidence

Extracted from [[SoT - Systems Thinking]] (Meadows and Open University synthesis). No verbatim book quote has been verified for this seed note yet.

### Implications

- Fixing a component in isolation can worsen overall performance if circular dependencies shift the bottleneck elsewhere.
- Effective diagnosis maps causal loops and time horizons before choosing where to intervene.
