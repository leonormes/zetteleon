## Evidence - Levitin Says Attention Is a Limited-Capacity Resource With Definite Limits

> A critical point that bears repeating is that attention is a limited-capacity resource—there are definite limits to the number of things we can attend to at once.

Source: Daniel J. Levitin, _The Organized Mind_.

### What it Supports

The capacity mechanism behind [[Human Understanding is Inherently Myopic]]: there are definite limits to how many things can be attended to at once, so only a small part of a situation can be held in view.

### What it Does not Show

It is a general statement about attention, illustrated with everyday examples, not a quantified capacity measure for understanding complex systems.
