## The Four Themes of the Enlightenment

Reason, science, humanism and progress are the four themes that tie together the ideas of the Enlightenment. The source is Steven Pinker, Enlightenment Now.

### Scope & Conditions

Pinker says the Enlightenment was a "cornucopia of ideas, some of them contradictory", and has no official definition or creed.

### Evidence

> "The era was a cornucopia of ideas, some of them contradictory, but four themes tie them together: reason, science, humanism, and progress."
> (Enlightenment Now, Chapter 1: Dare to Understand!)

### Implications

- The four ideals are a unifying summary, not a complete list of Enlightenment ideas.
- They form the frame for the rest of the book's argument.
