## Executive Dysfunction Has Causes Beyond ADHD Including Autism and Degenerative Disease

Summary: Multiple neurodevelopmental, psychiatric, and neurological conditions cause executive dysfunction—ADHD is one of several, not the exclusive cause.

Details: Executive dysfunction is a recognised symptom of ADHD and autism spectrum disorder among neurodevelopmental conditions. It is also caused by brain damage and degenerative brain disorders, including brain tumours, dementia, and Alzheimer's disease. Because the frontal lobe is the brain's primary seat of executive control, any condition that damages frontal-lobe tissue—regardless of diagnostic category—can produce the same downstream impairment in planning, problem-solving, and adaptability.
