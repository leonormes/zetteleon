## Engineering Action and Bypassing Resistance

Action can be engineered to bypass resistance by using implementation intentions (if-then plans), guaranteeing leisure first (the Unschedule), defining the Minimum Viable Action, and making work and limits visible (Kanban and WIP limits).

### Scope & Conditions

Applicable to workflow and system design intended to circumvent perfectionism, threat response, and reliance on spontaneous mental energy.

### Evidence

> "To bypass the brain's threat response and perfectionism, the task must be distilled down to the absolute smallest, least threatening physical movement."

### Implications

- Scheduling fun/leisure before work attacks the underlying causes of procrastination and resentment.
- Spontaneous motivation is unreliable; structural cues and strict WIP limits enforce completion.
