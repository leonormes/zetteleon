## Persons Are Outcomes of Nature and Nurture, not Right or Wrong Axioms

Rightness is a predicate over propositions, not people. Human minds are like unique plants shaped by their specific environment and genetics, rather than rigid logic engines that are either functionally correct or broken.

### Scope & Conditions

This applies to evaluating human beings and their inherent worth, especially when encountering disagreement or conflicting worldviews.

### Evidence

> "Persons aren't right or wrong—they're outcomes of nature and nurture… Rightness is a predicate over propositions, not people"

#### Implications

- It shifts interactions from conflict (disagreement as a failure in logic) to data collection (investigating the inputs that led to a belief).
- It allows for compassion toward the person while maintaining analytical rigor toward their ideas.
