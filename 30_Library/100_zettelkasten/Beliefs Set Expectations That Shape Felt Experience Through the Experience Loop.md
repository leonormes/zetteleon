## Beliefs Set Expectations That Shape Felt Experience Through the Experience Loop

Beliefs set up expectations that produce physiological and emotional responses. The source names the cycle the Experience Loop: believe, anticipate, feel, confirm.

### Scope & Conditions

The second of the source's three powers, Anticipation (the power to feel). The summary says the loop modulates pain perception, placebo and nocebo reactions, taste and endurance, but it cites no studies, so those effects are unverified here.

### Evidence

> "Through the Experience Loop (Believe → Anticipate → Feel → Confirm), expectations modulate everything from pain perception and placebo/nocebo reactions to taste and endurance."
> (Nir Eyal, Beyond Belief, via an LLM summary; chapter and page not captured)

### Implications

- Confirmation closes the loop and can reinforce the original belief.
- Links belief to placebo and nocebo effects, which the vault does not yet cover.
