## Extravert

Summary: Extroverts often engage in socially validated thinking, processing ideas in public where their thoughts are bounded by immediate social feedback, whereas introverts spend more time thinking privately.

Details: Sometimes extroverts come across as less intelligent or less thoughtful. This feeds into the idea of socially validated thinking. If extroverts are thinking in public, then their thinking is bounded by those around them. They don't spend as much time thinking privately, relying instead on the immediate environment to shape their cognition.
