## Centralisation Risk

The primary societal and economic risk of AI development is the centralization of power and the creation of corporate monopolies on the "means of production." This concentration of control over the world's most powerful productivity tools threatens to stagnate innovation and erode civil liberties by prioritizing institutional control over distributed open-source access.

### Scope & Conditions

Contrast to speculative "existential risk" narratives; focuses on immediate economic and political impacts.

### Evidence

> "The primary factual risk is the centralization of power… creating a monopoly on the 'means of production'…"

### Implications

- Encourages the support and distribution of open-source AI models to ensure decentralised access.
- Highlights the need for policy and architectural decisions that prevent the capture of AI capabilities by a small number of actors.
