## Self-Regulation is Disciplining One's Feelings and Appetites

Self-Regulation is the strength of regulating what you feel and do, with discipline over appetites and emotions. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It is also called self-control.
- The child-worded version of this definition is a separate note.

### Evidence

> "You can easily hold your desires, needs, and impulses in check when it is appropriate."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 250) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask which impulse needs holding and what structure could help hold it.
