## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

This protocol holds the **parent-grade technical specifications, storage zoning rules, whole-family labour ledger, and strict pet-medical SOPs** that operationalise [[SoT - Family Household Chores]]. Everyday family-facing rotas and plain-English checklists live in [[Family Chores - Everyone's Guide]] so the household never maintains duplicate rota tables; parents use this protocol for physical zone setup, monthly infrastructure, and safety-critical rules.

---

## 1. Storage Architecture (L1 / L2 / L3 Zoning Law)

Organise physical storage by **Access Frequency**, not volume, so daily tools stay visible and effortless ([[Minimize Access Friction with One-Motion Storage]], [[Default Environmental Design Beats Compliance Policing in Shared Household Routines]]):

| Tier | Category | Frequency | Allowed Assets | Prohibited Assets |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |:--- |:--- |
| **L1** | Hot Storage | Daily | Kettle, keys, bin bags, chargers, entryway drop-zone gear (shoes, coats, daily exit items in dedicated slots). | Anything used less than daily. |
| **L2** | Warm Storage | Weekly | Vacuum, mop, cleaning caddy, tools, laundry cupboards. | Seasonal items, archives. |
| **L3** | Cold Storage | Annual | Seasonal decorations, suitcases, camping kit (loft/garage). | Items needed for daily or weekly chores. |

- [ ] **20% Buffer Rule:** Keep L1 and L2 zones at or below 80% capacity so putting items away takes one motion.
- [ ] **One-In, One-Out:** When new items enter the home (birthdays, Christmas, shopping), donate or recycle an equivalent older item to preserve L1/L2 headroom.
- [ ] **Point-of-Performance Scaffolding:** Print zone checklists on **blue paper** (Meares-Irlen / dyslexia accommodation), laminate them, and mount them in each zone alongside the central **Chores Binders** and **Fridge Command Center** (displaying active Week A / B / C rotation and check-in times).

## 2. Whole-Family Ownership Ledger & Parent Infrastructure

Making Mum (Zofja) and Dad (Leon)'s invisible cognitive and logistical load explicit ([[Emotional Labor is the Invisible Work of Managing a Household and Family]], [[Shared Responsibility in a Partnership Includes Physical Mental and Emotional Labor]]) keeps fairness transparent across the team:

| # | Family Job Category | Primary Ownership |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| 1 | Income generation | **Mum (Zofja) & Dad (Leon)** |
| 2 | Household chores & maintenance | **Whole family:** Rae, Bessie & Pearl rotate weekly shared nodes + daily quarters ([[Family Chores - Everyone's Guide]]); **Mum & Dad** own laundry wash/dry/fold, deeper repairs, and monthly infrastructure |
| 3 | Meal planning & food logistics | **Mum & Dad** (menus, weekly food shop, pantry/batch cooking, school lunches; periodically shop pantry/freezer first and batch-cook extra portions) |
| 4 | Education & school logistics | **Mum & Dad** (homework scaffolding, SEN/EHCP/AP coordination, blue-paper stock, school admin — see [[Protocol - Bessie Study Cadence & Neurodiverse Scaffolding]]) |
| 5 | Transport & scheduling | **Mum & Dad** (Family Taxi, school/AP transitions, family calendar) |
| 6 | Health & medical management | **Mum & Dad** (appointments, prescriptions, sick days, and strictly parent-only Addison's dog care) |
| 7 | Finance & household administration | **Mum & Dad** (bills, insurance, budgeting, tax — see [[SoT - Family Financial Wellness]]) |
| 8 | Social, emotional & relationship labour | **Mum & Dad** (co-regulation, extended family, meta-parenting — see [[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]]) |
| 9 | Special occasions & seasonal planning | **Mum & Dad** (birthdays, holidays, school-year prep) |
| 10 | Pet care | **Two tiers:** **Mum & Dad** (Addison's dog medical care, §4.1) · **Rae, Bessie & Pearl** (rotating Cat + 2 Snakes care, §4.2) |

- [ ] **Explicit Co-Parent Split:** Mum and Dad agree explicitly how parent-owned categories and monthly infrastructure (washing machine & dishwasher filter cleans, vehicle fluids/tyres/safety checks, deep bin/fridge sanitisation) are divided so neither partner silently absorbs an unagreed default load.
- [ ] **Laundry Split:** Parents wash, dry, and sort clean laundry into piles by owner; each family member puts away their own folded pile and places dirty clothes *inside* the downstairs basket.

## 3. Canonical Zone Technical SOPs & Definitions of Done (DoDs)

*(Active Week A / B / C assignment table lives in [[Family Chores - Everyone's Guide]] §4.)*

### 3.1 Kitchen & Fridge
- [ ] **Daily Reset (Kitchen Helper):** Dining table cleared and wiped crumb-free; dishwasher loaded (spray arms spin freely) and clean dry items unloaded; worktops wiped; sink strainer emptied and basin scrubbed/rinsed; floor spot-swept after meals.
- [ ] **Weekly Node:** Steam/wipe microwave interior; scrub hob and splashback/tiles; wipe cupboard doors and handles (fingerprints); vacuum kitchen edges and mop entire floor; run **Quick Fridge Expiry Check** before weekly shop (discard expired items, wipe sticky jars/spills).
- [ ] **Monthly Cold-Storage & Filter SOP (Parent-Led):** Clear one fridge shelf at a time; spray antibacterial, wipe damp, dry with microfibre; **verify back drainage hole is clear of ice/debris**; restock safely (**ready-to-eat top, raw meat sealed bottom, veg in drawers**); disinfect indoor kitchen bin; remove and rinse dishwasher filter.

### 3.2 Bathrooms (×2) & Hall/Stairs
- [ ] **Daily (Everyone After Use):** Hang damp towels (dirty ones to basket); rinse toothpaste/soap marks from basin.
- [ ] **Weekly Node:** Scrub both toilet bowls with cleaner and wipe seats/lids/flush handles; scrub and rinse bath and shower; clean basins and polish chrome taps water-spot free; clean mirrors with glass cleaner; mop both floors with disinfectant; restock loo roll and fresh hand towels; empty small bathroom bins into main kitchen/utility collection; vacuum hall, landing, and stair treads/risers (including corners); dust banisters/spindles; verify L1 entryway drop zone is clear.
- [ ] **Bathrooms DoD:** Mirrors and chrome spot-free; zero hair/dust on basins or toilet lids; fresh towels and loo roll stocked.

### 3.3 Bins, Front Room & Car
- [ ] **Sunday Evening Waste Reset:** Collect indoor bin bags (bathrooms, bedrooms, office, kitchen) into outdoor **Wheelie Bins**; fit **fresh liners** in all indoor bins; wheel outdoor bins kerbside on Sunday evening for Monday collection.
- [ ] **Front Room:** Daily 2-minute evening clear (mugs, shoes, loose items, cushions plumped, throws folded); weekly dust (TV stand, shelves, skirting), thorough vacuum (under coffee table and sofa edges), dry-microfibre TV screen wipe, and interior glass/mirror clean.
- [ ] **Car Interior 5-Step Valet (Weekly):** (1) De-clutter loose items/rubbish; (2) Gravity clean—brush crumbs down and shake mats outside; (3) Vacuum seats then footwells with crevice tool; (4) Clean interior windows (spray glass cleaner onto cloth, never directly onto glass); (5) Wipe steering wheel and gear stick with antibacterial wipe. **Car DoD:** Door pockets rubbish-free, mats grit-free, glass smear-free.

### 3.4 Personal Quarters & Bessie's Night-Before / Study Workspace DoDs
- [ ] **Daily Personal Quarters (Everyone):** Bed made; floor clear of clothes/rubbish; dirty clothes inside downstairs basket; small bin emptied when full; shoes/coats/keys in L1 slots and personal items removed from car on exit; weekly bedding change, dust, and vacuum.
- [ ] **Bessie's Study Workspace DoD (Binary Pre-Sprint Gate):** (1) Desk clear of non-study clutter; (2) Only authorised school apps open on laptop, phone in kitchen drawer proxy; (3) Task on **blue paper** broken into **exactly 3 verb-driven physical actions**; (4) Low-stimulation headphones paired with agreed music/podcast while actively working ([[Protocol - Bessie Study Cadence & Neurodiverse Scaffolding]]).
- [ ] **Bessie's 21:00 Night-Before Sight-Check:** Laptop packed with charger and battery ≥80%; blue-paper notebooks/overlays inside binder; school/AP uniform and sensory-friendly base layers laid out. *(Unfinished items log neutrally to the morning ledger without fault.)*

## 4. Pet Care & Medical Safety SOPs

### 4.1 Dog Medical Care — Addison's Disease (Strictly Parent-Only: Mum & Dad)
- [ ] **Daily Steroid Tablets:** Administer prescribed prednisone/prednisolone every morning with food to replace cortisol (*missing doses risks immediate relapse*).
- [ ] **Monthly Injection:** Schedule and attend vet-administered Zycortal / fludrocortisone injection on time each month.
- [ ] **Predictable Stressors:** Keep routine calm; consult vet beforehand about temporary dose increases ahead of fireworks, house moves, or boarding.
- [ ] **Emergency Watch-List (Same-Day Vet Mandatory):** Lethargy/extreme tiredness, vomiting, diarrhoea, loss of appetite, or collapse. Children report any sign to Mum or Dad immediately.
- [ ] **NSAID Prohibition:** Rimadyl, Metacam, and Meloxicam are **unsafe**; use only vet-approved pain relief (e.g., Tramadol, Gabapentin).
- [ ] **Blood Monitoring:** Run electrolyte checks (sodium/potassium) every 2–3 months per vet guidance.

### 4.2 Shared Pet Care — Cat & Snakes (×2) (Rae, Bessie & Pearl)
*(Rotates Mondays across Week A / B / C per [[Family Chores - Everyone's Guide]] §4.)*
- [ ] **Cat:** Feed AM/PM, verify fresh water, scoop/clean litter tray daily, brush regularly, and tidy pet area.
- [ ] **Snakes (×2):** Verify enclosure temperature gradient and humidity daily; refresh water daily; spot-clean substrate and check for incomplete sheds (eyes and tail tip); feed every 1–2 weeks per family calendar with Mum or Dad oversight.
