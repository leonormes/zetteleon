## Evidence - Kahneman WYSIATI Says We Treat the Limited Information We Have as if It Were All There Is

> You cannot help dealing with the limited information you have as if it were all there is to know.

The same passage adds: "Our comforting conviction that the world makes sense rests on a secure foundation: our almost unlimited ability to ignore our ignorance."

Source: Daniel Kahneman, _Thinking, Fast and Slow_.

### What it Supports

The bias half of [[Human Understanding is Inherently Myopic]]: the view is not only partial but treated as complete, because a coherent story is easier to build from less information.

### What it Does not Show

It is one author's synthesis of heuristics-and-biases research and does not by itself measure how small a fraction of reality we perceive. It supports the incompleteness-and-bias claim, not the specific claim that the limit is capacity.
