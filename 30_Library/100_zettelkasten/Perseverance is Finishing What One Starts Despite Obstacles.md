## Perseverance is Finishing What One Starts Despite Obstacles

Perseverance is the strength of finishing what you start and persisting despite obstacles, with satisfaction in completing tasks. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It does not mean obsessive pursuit of unattainable goals.
- The child-worded version of this definition is a separate note.

### Evidence

> "You finish what you start."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 243) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

> "perseverance does not mean dogged or obsessive persistence of unattainable goals."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 243) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask whether the goal is still attainable, and if so what finishing the next part requires.
