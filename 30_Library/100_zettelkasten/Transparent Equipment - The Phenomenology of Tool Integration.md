Transparent Equipment refers to technologies or tools that are so reliable and user-sensitive that they effectively disappear from the user's conscious focus.

## 🧠 The Phenomenological Shift

When a tool becomes transparent, the user does not focus _on_ the tool, but looks _through_ it to the task.

- Example: A writer does not think about the pen; they think about the sentence. The pen becomes an extension of the hand.
- Example: A blind person does not "feel" the cane; they "feel" the pavement at the end of the cane.

## 🧩 Requirements for Transparency

1. Reliability: The tool must work predictably every time.
2. User-Sensitivity: The interface must respond to the user's intent with minimal lag (Low MTTFA).

## 🛰️ Relation to ProdOS

For an Obsidian vault to act as part of the mind, it must achieve transparency. If the user has to struggle with metadata or organisation (Toil), the tool remains "opaque" and cognitive extension fails.
