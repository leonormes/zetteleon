## The Purpose of a System Dynamics Model Is to Make Assumptions Explicit, Not to Predict Outcomes

Computer models of complex systems are not predictive black boxes meant to output future dates or numbers; their real purpose is to force the modeller to make assumptions about causal relationships and feedback loops explicit, which improves the mental models humans use to understand the system.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to system dynamics models specifically (e.g. World3 / Limits to Growth); doesn't claim all quantitative models are non-predictive, only that treating prediction as the point misreads their main value.

### Evidence

> "Computer models of systems are not predictive 'black boxes' meant merely to output future dates or numbers. Rather, their purpose is to force researchers to make their assumptions about causal relationships and feedback loops explicit, thereby improving the internal 'mental models' humans use to understand complex systems."

### Implications

- Criticising a system dynamics model for a wrong date or number misses the point; the right critique targets whether its causal assumptions were made explicit and are defensible.
