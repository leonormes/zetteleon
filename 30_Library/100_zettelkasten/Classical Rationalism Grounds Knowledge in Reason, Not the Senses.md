## Classical Rationalism Grounds Knowledge in Reason, Not the Senses

Rationalists such as Descartes and Plato hold that the senses are deceptive and that true knowledge comes from pure reasoning, logic, and innate ideas rather than observation.

### Scope & Conditions

Classical rationalist epistemology, pre-dating and standing in contrast to classical empiricism.

### Evidence

> "Rationalists argue that the senses are deceptive and that true knowledge comes from pure reasoning, logic, and innate ideas."

### Implications

- Frames observation as an unreliable source, the inverse starting point from empiricism.
- Sets up the classical rationalism/empiricism split that later positions (logical positivism, constructive empiricism) both react against.
