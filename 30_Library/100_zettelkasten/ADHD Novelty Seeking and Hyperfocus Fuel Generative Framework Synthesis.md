## ADHD Novelty Seeking and Hyperfocus Fuel Generative Framework Synthesis

The ADHD interest-based nervous system is naturally biased toward generative exploration rather than routine maintenance. Dopamine release on novelty and prediction error pulls attention toward unknown territory, while interest-driven hyperfocus sustains the prolonged immersion in ambiguity needed to birth a new conceptual framework.

### Scope & Conditions

Holds when novelty seeking is channelled into deep problem-solving rather than fragmented task switching. Without structural guardrails, the same novelty drive produces self-defeating system-hopping ([[Claim - Novelty-craving drives self-defeating system-hopping]]).

### Evidence

This claim synthesises §4 of [[SoT - Generative Intelligence]], grounded in [[Dopamine Neurons Encode Reward Prediction Error, Not Pleasure]], [[Hyperfocus]], and [[SoT - The Interest-Based Nervous System in ADHD]].

### Implications

- Environments that only reward routine applicative compliance systematically underuse ADHD generative strengths.
- Pairing novelty-driven exploration with external accountability and completion structures prevents insights from being abandoned half-built.
