## How to Run Lifestyle-Centric Planning

Lifestyle-centric planning replaces brittle, distant output goals with declarative properties of your ideal daily life across Constitution, Craft, and Community. By assessing where you stand, identifying skills or assets that buy those properties, and sprinting on one bucket per month, you build a compelling life iteratively without overextension.

### Scope & Conditions

Best used during monthly or quarterly life reviews when existing goals feel hollow or disconnected from daily well-being. Not a substitute for daily task triage.

### Evidence

Derived from the Lifestyle-Centric Planning protocol in [[SoT - Intentional Living]] (adapted from Cal Newport's Deep Life framework).

### Implications

- Designing for properties (such as unhurried evenings) directly changes weekly constraints rather than deferring satisfaction to a distant milestone.
- Restricting active change to one bucket per month prevents the snap-back failure of simultaneous life overhauls.
