## Writing Creates Cognitive Distance for Objective Evaluation

Writing creates psychological distance between you and your thoughts by turning internal monologue into an external artifact. This separation allows you to examine ideas with the same critical scrutiny you would apply to someone else's words, reducing emotional bias.

### Scope & Conditions

Requires personal authorship of the written artifact: delegating drafting to AI creates distance from text you did not produce rather than from your own thinking ([[Outsourcing Writing to AI Bypasses the Cognitive Strain That Builds Professional Competence]]). Works best when thoughts are written in complete propositions rather than shorthand fragments.

### Evidence

This claim is derived from cognitive synthesis across the writing-to-think and metacognitive calibration notes in the vault rather than a single external quotation.

### Implications

- Evaluating ideas as external specimens lowers ego-defensiveness and makes self-correction affordable.
- Treating felt profundity as a prompt to write rather than proof of validity filters out echo-chamber biases.
