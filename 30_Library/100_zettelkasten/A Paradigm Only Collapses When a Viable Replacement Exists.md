### A Paradigm Only Collapses When a Viable Replacement Exists

Crucially, a scientific consensus never collapses simply because it is proven wrong. It only collapses when a viable alternative is ready to take its place — one that must do two things simultaneously: successfully explain almost everything the old model explained, and elegantly resolve the anomalies that caused the crisis.

#### Scope & Conditions

A corollary of [[Kuhn's Four-Stage Model of Paradigm Collapse]] (the Model Revolution stage); constrains what counts as a *sufficient* condition for paradigm change — being wrong is necessary but not sufficient.

#### Evidence

> "a scientific consensus never collapses simply because it is proven wrong. It only collapses when a viable alternative is ready to take its place."

#### Implications

- Pointing out that a paradigm is broken is not, by itself, sufficient to dislodge it — a replacement has to be ready to do the same job.
