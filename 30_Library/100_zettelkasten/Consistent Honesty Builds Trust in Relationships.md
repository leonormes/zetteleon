## Consistent Honesty Builds Trust in Relationships

Being consistently truthful and sincere builds trust in personal and working relationships. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- Trust also depends on competence and goodwill, which honesty alone does not supply.

### Evidence

This comes from the earlier Honesty note. No source has been verified, so it is my own reasoning.

### Falsifier

- If consistently honest partners or colleagues are trusted no more over time, the claim is wrong.
