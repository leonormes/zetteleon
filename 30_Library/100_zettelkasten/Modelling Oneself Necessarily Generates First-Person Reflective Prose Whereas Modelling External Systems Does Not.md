## Modelling Oneself Necessarily Generates First-Person Reflective Prose Whereas Modelling External Systems Does Not

Constructing a mechanism-level model of an external technical or formal system requires no autobiographical narration, whereas modelling personal cognition, habits, or executive function necessarily places the writer inside the model and produces first-person prose. Differences in reflective-writing density across a PKM vault can therefore reflect whether the system under analysis is external or internal rather than measuring comparative interest depth. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies when comparing prose styles across [[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph — Interest Seed and Candidate Drivers]], contrasting high-volume third-person technical clusters (cloud infrastructure, security, agentic AI, physics, epistemology) against compact, voice-rich personal clusters (PKM, ADHD, behavioural design, archery). Remains compatible with off-vault operational reflection in paid technical work.

### Evidence

> "modelling an external system doesn't require narrating why it matters to you; modelling yourself does, by necessity. If this holds, richness of reflective writing is not evidence of a stronger interest—it's an artefact of whether the system being modelled is external or is himself."
> ([[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph — Interest Seed and Candidate Drivers]])

Supported by contrasting technical clusters in [[Reference - Vault Interest Map]] against self-modelling notes like [[Why I Do a PKM and What I Think Knowledge Is For]].

### Implications

- Vault audits must not rank personal domains above technical domains merely because first-person pronouns cluster in self-referential notes.
- Comparing inward and outward domains requires live elicitation rather than treating prose register as a proxy for motivational pull.

### Falsifier

Falsified if live elicitation shows third-person technical clusters carry weaker intrinsic pull than first-person clusters after controlling for off-vault practice and target locus.
