### Maximize Vertical Real Estate Over Decorative Space

When floor space reaches capacity, replace passive decorative items with modular vertical storage systems.

#### Scope & Conditions

High-density workspaces, small studios, or areas with limited footprint.

#### Evidence

> "Decorative or passive items (like corkboards) that invite visual clutter are replaced with modular French cleat systems"

#### Implications

- Frees up critical flat surfaces for active work.
- Allows for high-density, easily accessible tool storage on walls.
