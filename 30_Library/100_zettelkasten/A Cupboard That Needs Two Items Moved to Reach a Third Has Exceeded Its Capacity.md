## A Cupboard That Needs Two Items Moved to Reach a Third Has Exceeded Its Capacity

If retrieving or storing one item means shifting two others, the cupboard holds more than its volume comfortably allows. The remedy is to move the excess to donation or secondary storage, and a cap on high-turnover items, such as two sets of tableware in rotation, stops it building again. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to everyday cupboards, shelves and baskets in shared use. Seasonal or archive storage can be tighter, because it is rarely accessed.

### Evidence

> "If a cupboard requires shifting two items to retrieve a third, it has exceeded capacity."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against a primary source)

The vault's own 20% buffer rule points the same way. The chat's sources are unchecked, so confidence is low.

### Implications

- The two-item test is a quick check anyone can run without judging how tidy a person is.
- Donation, not clever packing, is the fix when the test fails.
