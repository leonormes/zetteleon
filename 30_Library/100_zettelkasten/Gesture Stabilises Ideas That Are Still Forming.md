## Gesture Stabilises Ideas That Are Still Forming

Gesture acts as a virtual diagram that stabilises and advances ideas that are still forming in the mind.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to inchoate ideas not yet consciously formulated. The source attributes a supporting quotation to Sian Beilock; that attribution is not verified here.

### Evidence

> "Gesture plays a crucial role in shaping inchoate notions still forming in our minds, acting as a virtual diagram to stabilise and advance our emerging understanding."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 230).

### Implications

- The source quotes Sian Beilock: moving the body "can alter the mind by unconsciously putting ideas in our head before we are able to consciously contemplate them on our own" (source line 230).
- Gesture therefore bears on how ideas form, not only on how they are shown.
