## A No-Defeaters Condition Handles Many Gettier Cases but Cannot Say Which True Facts Count as Defeaters

A no-defeaters condition says that a person knows only if no true fact they do not know would have undermined their justification, and it handles many Gettier cases. It fails in two ways: some defeaters leave knowledge intact, and the line between a defeater and a harmless fact is vague. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- It adds a fourth condition to justified true belief and does not repair the notion of justification. Every false belief has a defeater, namely the fact that it is false, so the condition cannot be part of justification itself.
- It gets the standard cases right. Had Joan known that Jack did not get the job, her justification would have collapsed, and had Max known that he was looking at decoys, his would have too.
- It fails on cases like Stu and Pru. Stu rightly expects Pru in five minutes, and a coincidental phone call will drown out the doorbell. The call is a defeater, yet Stu does know that Pru is coming.
- Facts that only reduce the justification a little, such as two decoys among hundreds of ducks, raise the vagueness problem. A demand to take in every relevant fact also approaches omniscience.

### Evidence

One book defines the condition:

> "A ‘defeater’ is a true proposition that S does not know, but that would have undermined S’s evidence for p."
> (Martin 2014, "The no-falsehood reply", PDF p. 42) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/298/EPUB?open_at=toc:The%20no-falsehood%20reply)

and draws the lesson from the Stu and Pru case:

> "So the presence of a defeater does not mean there’s no knowledge."
> (Martin 2014, "The no-falsehood reply", PDF p. 44) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/298/EPUB?open_at=toc:The%20no-falsehood%20reply)

### Implications

- The condition is attractive because it does not require a false belief, which is why it avoids the main weakness of the no-falsehood repair.
- It would need a way to tell misleading defeaters from harmless ones, and that way has not been agreed.

### Falsifier

- A non-circular definition of a misleading defeater that classes the Stu and Pru case as knowledge and the Max case as not knowledge without ad hoc exceptions.
