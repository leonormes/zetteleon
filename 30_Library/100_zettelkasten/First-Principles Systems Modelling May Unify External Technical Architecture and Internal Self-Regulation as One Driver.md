## First-Principles Systems Modelling May Unify External Technical Architecture and Internal Self-Regulation as One Driver

Proposed as Candidate Driver 3 (First-Principles Modelling, Turned Inward and Outward, proposed), a single recurring pull toward building legible, mechanism-level models may connect external technical architecture, foundational mathematics, and internal behavioural self-engineering. This candidate hypothesis gains specificity because it cleanly excludes domains such as music appreciation, where evaluation turns on artistic authenticity rather than systems mechanics. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Covers outward technical and formal systems (cloud infrastructure, security, agentic AI internals, physics, and the engineer-creator view of mathematics in [[Public perception of maths is incomplete]]) alongside inward cognitive and behavioural engineering (studying personal cognition as an engineering problem in [[Reference - Vault Interest Map]] and modelling habit execution via environmental cues and choice architecture in [[Discipline Is a Property of External Structure More Than of Will]]). Bounded by specificity in music, where [[Asking Why a Song Exists Helps Determine its Authenticity]] evaluates originating artistic intent rather than constructing a mechanistic system model.

### Evidence

> "A pull towards systems and first principles—whether the system is a Kubernetes cluster, an ADHD brain, an argument graph, a recurve bow shot cycle, or a family household"
> ([[Reference - Vault Interest Map]])

### Implications

- Explaining a defined subset of interests while failing to stretch across unrelated domains such as music satisfies the project's specificity standard better than a catch-all personal narrative.
- Testing this candidate requires distinguishing intrinsic mechanism-building from professional conscientiousness in paid technical domains once paycheck and audience stakes are removed.

### Falsifier

Weakened or falsified if mechanism-level investigation disappears in systems carrying zero audience, professional utility, or performance payoff, or if live comparison reveals that outward technical architecture and inward self-regulation answer wholly separate motivational ladders.
