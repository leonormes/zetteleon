## How to Establish Common Ground Using I-CARE

Active calibration establishes shared experiential anchors before complex communication begins, reducing the risk that diverging filters distort the exchange. Use the I-CARE dimensions—Interests, Convictions, Activities, Roles, and Experiences—to locate the overlap between two experiential worlds before introducing abstract claims.

### Scope & Conditions

Use at the start of cross-disciplinary discussions, mentoring, difficult conversations, or onboarding. Skip formal calibration in routine, low-stakes exchanges where shared vocabulary and context are already verified.

### Evidence

Derived from the active-calibration and I-CARE framework in [[SoT - Communication & Misunderstanding (The Experiential Filter)]] and supported by [[Common experiential anchors ground abstract concepts]].

### Implications

- Spending two minutes calibrating via I-CARE prevents prolonged talking past one another later in the conversation.
- Anchoring in the listener's Activities and Roles turns abstract advice into immediately actionable mental models.
