Humans are **"Natural-Born Cyborgs"** not because of silicon implants, but because our biological brains possess extreme **plasticity** designed to co-opt and annex external props.

## 🧠 The Biological Hook

The brain is specifically evolved to "dovetail" its activities with reliable external resources. It does not distinguish between a neural circuit and a reliable external tool (like a pen or a search engine) if the tool produces a cost-effective solution.

## 🧩 Key Logic

- **Recruitment:** The brain is the "locus of recruitment"—it spins and maintains webs of external scaffolding to offload cognitive load.
- **Dovetailing:** The seamless loop between biological processing and environmental feedback.

## 🛰️ Relation to ProdOS

The ADHD brain, which often struggles with internal regulation and working memory, is the ultimate "Natural-Born Cyborg," requiring robust external systems (ProdOS) to stabilise its cognitive output.
