## Vector Calculus Application

Vector calculus models the flow, spread, and circulation of quantities through multi-dimensional space. It extends the principles of single-variable calculus to multi-variable fields, making it essential for analysing physical systems such as fluid dynamics and electromagnetism.

### Scope & Conditions

Applicable in fields like applied mathematics and physics for modelling spatial movement and flux.

### Evidence

> "…study how quantities move, spread, or circulate throughout space… applicable in fields like applied mathematics and physics for modeling the flow of water, atmospheric air circulation."

### Implications

- Extends foundational calculus to the physical world of three or more dimensions.
- Utilises line and surface integrals to measure flux and circulation within vector fields.
