Transform your perception of criticism by consciously shifting the frame from a personal attack to an objective input. This is a cognitive procedure to reduce defensive hyperarousal.

### Core Strategies

- **View as free coaching:** Assume the intent, regardless of delivery, is to provide you with data to improve.
- **Separate message from delivery:** Strip away the emotional tone or poor communication style of the critic to find the underlying factual claim.
- **Focus on actionable elements:** Discard vague judgements and isolate specific behaviours or outputs that can be changed.

### Practical Techniques (Self-Talk)

- *"This helps me improve X."* (Redirects focus to future growth rather than past failure).
- *"What part can I control?"* (Applies Stoic dichotomy of control to discard unhelpful noise).
- *"Is there a pattern to address?"* (Moves the feedback from a singular emotional event to a systemic data point).
