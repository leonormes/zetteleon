> [!claim] Statement
> Linking-and-tagging on a flat structure fits ADHD cognition better than strict hierarchical filing, because it mirrors non-linear thought and removes the up-front "where does this go?" decision.

## Supporting Evidence

- A flat, cross-referenced structure sidesteps the paralysis of an over-engineered filing schema.
- Associative linking matches the non-linear way ideas actually arrive.

## Mechanism

- Hierarchy demands a correct placement decision at capture time (high [[Cognitive Load]]); linking defers and distributes that decision, lowering the activation cost of writing a note.

## Counter-positions

- Pure flatness can become an un-navigable swamp; some scaffolding (MOCs, a few stable categories) is needed for retrieval.
- The decision cost may shift rather than vanish—choosing _links_ is also a decision.

## Crux

- Whether retrieval at scale survives without hierarchy, or whether emergent structure (hubs, clusters) must eventually be imposed anyway.

## Falsifier

- If flat-linked vaults reliably degraded into unsearchable piles past a certain size while hierarchical ones stayed usable, the claim would need qualifying.
