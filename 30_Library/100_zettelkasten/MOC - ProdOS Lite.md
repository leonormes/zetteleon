## ProdOS Lite: The Thinking Stream

> [!important] The Rule of Zero Toil
> If it feels like "organizing," stop. This view is for **Thinking** and **Doing** only.

---

### 1. THE PROCESS (The "How")

1. **CAPTURE:** Dump the noise into your **Daily Note**. No tags. No links. Just raw text.
2. **REFINE:** If an item feels "heavy" or confusing, create a **HEAD Note** in `20_Thinking`.
3. **PROCESS:** Use the **120s Loop** (below) to find the exit.
4. **EXIT:**
    - **Action?** Send the MVA to **Todoist**.
    - **Fact?** Move to a **SoT** note (only if high value).
    - **Trash?** **DELETE** the HEAD note. (Zero storage debt).

---

### 2. THE 120s LOOP (The Protocol)

Inside a `HEAD` note, only answer these three questions. Spend no more than 2 minutes.

1. **THE GOAL:** What is the single outcome I want right now?
2. **THE BLOCK:** What is the *exact* point of confusion or friction?
3. **THE MVA:** What is the next **physical** step I can take in <120s?

---

### 3. THE GUARDRAILS (Strict)

- **NO FOLDERS:** Dump everything in `20_Thinking`. Don't file it.
- **NO METADATA:** Skip the YAML header if it slows you down.
- **NO POLISHING:** This is a workbench, not a museum. Messy is fine.
- **NO STORAGE:** If a note hasn't turned into an Action or a Fact in 24 hours, **Delete it**.
