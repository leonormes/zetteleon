## Redundancy Across Channels Corrects Communication Errors

Communication carries meaning through several channels at once, so a receiver can reconstruct the intended meaning when part of the signal is missed or misread.

### Scope & Conditions

Channels named in the source are words, tone, facial expressions, gestures and context; languages also repeat an idea in several ways within one message.

### Evidence

> "Redundancy and multimodal communication provide error correction. Human communication rarely relies on a single channel. We simultaneously use words, tone, facial expressions, gestures, and contextual cues to convey meaning."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 330).

### Implications

- Receivers can detect misunderstanding through facial expressions, questions or unexpected responses (source line 127).
- Redundancy within language helps overcome noise in the communication channel (source line 135).
