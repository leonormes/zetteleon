## Goldman Reliabilism Grounds Knowledge in a Reliable Belief-Forming Mechanism

Externalist reliabilism (Goldman) argues that you can know something even if you cannot explain your reasons, provided your belief was generated by a reliable mechanism.

### Scope & Conditions

One of two externalist accounts of justification, contrasting with internalism.

### Evidence

> "Externalism (Alvin Goldman, Robert Nozick): Argues that knowledge is a relationship between a person and a fact. You can know something even if you cannot explain your reasons, provided your belief was generated by a _reliable mechanism_ (Reliabilism, Goldman)."

### Implications

- Lets non-experts count as knowers via a reliable process, without needing conscious access to the underlying justification.
