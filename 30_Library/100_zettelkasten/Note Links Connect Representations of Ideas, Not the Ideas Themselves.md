## Note Links Connect Representations of Ideas, Not the Ideas Themselves

A link between two notes joins representations of ideas, so linking on shared keywords or superficial similarity does not connect the ideas themselves.

### Scope & Conditions

The source frames this as an "illusion of linked ideas" in a personal knowledge management context; it applies most to links based on surface similarity.

### Evidence

> "When we link two notes based on shared keywords or superficial similarities, we're often creating links between representations of ideas, not the ideas themselves."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 42).

### Implications

- The source recommends developing deeper, more meaningful notes over simply creating numerous links (source line 45).
- It says the real connections still reside primarily in the mind (source line 43).
