## Knowledge Linting

Knowledge linting is a periodic maintenance operation where an AI agent performs "health checks" across a knowledge base to identify stale information, orphan pages, or logical contradictions. This quality control mechanism prevents the accumulation of "knowledge debt" and ensures the continued relevance and connectivity of the internal knowledge graph.

### Scope & Conditions

Standard quality assurance procedure for persistent and automated AI memory systems.

### Evidence

> "Lint: A 'health check' where the AI looks for stale information, orphan pages, or contradictions."

### Implications

- Prevents the structural decay of the knowledge base by identifying and repairing broken links or isolated nodes.
- Maintains the overall logical consistency of the system by surfacing and resolving conflicting claims.
