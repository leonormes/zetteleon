## Clearing Rubbish and Dishes First Visibly Changes a Room and Builds Momentum

Removing rubbish and dishes first changes a room visibly within minutes. The early progress is meant to generate the momentum to keep going. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the opening steps of a room reset. The reward-centre explanation in the source is an assertion I could not check, so the claim rests on the visible-progress effect and not on any neuroscience.

### Evidence

> "Removing health hazards (trash and dishes) first visibly changes the room within minutes."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Davis's own book)

### Implications

- Hazards go first so that stopping early still leaves a safer room.
- Visible progress is a cue worth pointing out to children, for example by standing back and looking.
