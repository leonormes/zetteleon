## Running the Three-Domain Rule on Two Live Candidates Cleared One and Correctly Withheld the Other

Two candidates from the 2026-09-15 interest seed were tested against the rule that a term is promoted to a theme only when it links at least three distinct domains. First-Principles Modelling, Turned Inward and Outward cleared the bar easily. Process/Construction Legitimises, Consumption Needs Defending did not, and withholding it was the rule doing its job.

### Scope & Conditions

Two candidates only, both from one project note, so this shows the rule behaving sensibly and does not validate it. The extra domains for First-Principles Modelling come from Leon's direct correction about work practice, not from vault text.

- First-Principles Modelling recurs across ADHD, discipline, PKM, mathematics and archery in the vault's own writing, and plausibly also cloud and DevOps, security and LLM engineering. It is nonetheless treated as a candidate driver, not a theme, because it purports to explain the pull and not just describe a pattern in it.
- Process/Construction Legitimises, Consumption Needs Defending was proposed on maths, PKM and music. The music leg was retracted the same day: the two notes it rested on were a conversation with Leon's daughter and an unrelated note about F1 and film, and the real music history was serious daily practice, not consumption. Two domains is below the threshold.

### Evidence

> "Consequence for Process/Construction Legitimises: downgraded—two domains (maths, PKM), not three."
> (A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph — Interest Seed and Candidate Drivers, 2026-09-15)

The rest is the author's own reasoning from the same note, summarised here, so check the original before relying on the detail.

### Implications

- A withheld promotion is a correct result, not a gap in evidence-gathering.
- The First-Principles case needs the theme or drive distinction before it can be classified at all.
