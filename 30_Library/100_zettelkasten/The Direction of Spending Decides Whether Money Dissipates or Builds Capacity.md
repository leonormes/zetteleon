## The Direction of Spending Decides Whether Money Dissipates or Builds Capacity

Spending has a direction as well as an amount. Directed at consumption such as status signals it is converted into heat and vanishes, whereas directed at capital, infrastructure or skills it builds a machine that lowers the cost of future tasks. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

A two-way analogy for judging allocation. Real purchases may mix both directions.

### Evidence

Carried over from the earlier prose version of [[SoT - Financial Philosophy and Spendfulness]]; no outside source is cited.

### Implications

- Compare purchases by what they leave behind as well as by their price.
