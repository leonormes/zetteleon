## Convergence of Developer and Agent Experience

The architectural and documentation requirements for a high-quality human developer experience (DX) are increasingly identical to those for a high-quality AI agent experience (AX). Codebases optimized for human readability and modularity are inherently better suited for agentic reasoning and context management.

### Scope & Conditions

Observed trend in modern developer tooling and architectural design.

### Evidence

> "Fowler argues that the Venn diagram of developer experience and agent experience is becoming a circle."

### Implications

- Good documentation and clean APIs now serve two audiences: human maintainers and LLM agents.
- Investing in DX is simultaneously an investment in AX (Agentic Experience).
