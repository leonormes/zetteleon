## Human Cognitive Processes Extend Into Bodily Actions and External Artifacts

Human cognitive processes are not confined to the brain or skin, because external features such as bodily actions, notebooks, digital tools and language play a causal role in driving cognition in the here and now. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

This is the Extended Mind Thesis in its active externalism form. It does not say every tool is part of the mind, only those that satisfy the conditions in the related cards.

### Evidence

This is my own summary of the thesis as set out in the earlier text of [[SoT - The Extended Mind]], which attributes it to Clark and Chalmers. No book quotation has been verified for this card.

### Implications

- Cognition can be designed across brain, body and tools, not only inside the head.
- Removing a constitutive tool removes part of the faculty, not just an aid.
