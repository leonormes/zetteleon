## Clusters of Ideas in a Zettelkasten Are Suggestions Not Mandates

Areas of dense connection in a zettelkasten can guide a writer toward ideas ready for expression, but they suggest and do not dictate what to write. The source is Bob Doto, A System for Writing.

### Scope & Conditions

Doto calls the belief that a slip box tells writers what to write a myth that is "more truth than fact"; he cites Ahrens for "clusters" and Luhmann for "lumps".

### Evidence

> "A zettelkasten will not tell you what to write. It will not literally speak to you. But, visible areas of increased connectivity within the slip box, what Sönke Ahrens calls "clusters," can guide you toward ideas ready for expression."
> (Doto, A System for Writing, section 13.8)

> "Clusters of ideas forming inside the zettelkasten are suggestions, not mandates."
> (Doto, A System for Writing, section 13.8)

### Implications

- The writer still chooses which cluster to work with.
- Dense areas are clues to where ideas are developing.
