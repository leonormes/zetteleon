## Humanism Privileges the Individual Over the Group

Humanism privileges the well-being of individual men, women and children over the glory of the tribe, race, nation or religion. The source is Steven Pinker, Enlightenment Now.

### Scope & Conditions

Pinker's definition of humanism as the secular foundation for morality that the Enlightenment thinkers laid. He grounds it in the point that individuals, not groups, are sentient.

### Evidence

> "They laid that foundation in what we now call humanism, which privileges the well-being of individual men, women, and children over the glory of the tribe, race, nation, or religion. It is individuals, not groups, who are sentient—who feel pleasure and pain, fulfillment and anguish."
> (Enlightenment Now, Chapter 1: Dare to Understand!)

### Implications

- Moral concern attaches to the capacity of a person to suffer and flourish.
- Loyalty to a group does not outrank the well-being of the individuals in it.
