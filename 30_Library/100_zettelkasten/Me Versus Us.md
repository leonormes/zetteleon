## Me Versus Us

Me versus Us is the basic moral problem of selfishness against concern for others. It is the problem that human moral brains were designed to solve, and it is the first of two kinds of moral problem Greene distinguishes.

### Scope & Conditions

Contrasted with Us versus Them, the Tragedy of Commonsense Morality.

### Evidence

> "It's the problem of Me versus Us: selfishness versus concern for others. This is the problem that our moral brains were designed to solve."
> (Joshua Greene, Moral Tribes, Introduction: The Tragedy of Commonsense Morality)

### Implications

- Hardin's tragedy of the commons is the standard illustration of this problem.
- Within-group cooperation is where moral instincts work best.
