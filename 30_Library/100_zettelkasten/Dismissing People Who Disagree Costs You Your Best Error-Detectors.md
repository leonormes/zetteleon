## Dismissing People Who Disagree Costs You Your Best Error-Detectors

Summary: Binary person-judgement filters your relationships by conclusions and your information by source — and the people most likely to find your mistakes are precisely the ones who disagree with you.

Details: Judging people as wrong-as-humans is a highly efficient way to protect a worldview, but the cost is compound: connections missed, conversations shut down, people reduced to their most flawed opinion — and, epistemically worst, the loss of your most valuable error-detection capacity. A disagreeing mind is external test coverage for your beliefs. Dismissal deletes the test suite.
