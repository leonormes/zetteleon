## Preserve Flat Surfaces for Active Work Only

Flat surfaces are sacred zones meant exclusively for active work and must never be used for miscellaneous storage.

### Scope & Conditions

Desks, workbenches, and assembly tables. Exceptions are only for tools that actively facilitate work, kept on perimeters.

### Evidence

> "Flat surfaces are sacred zones meant for active work, not clutter or miscellaneous storage"

### Implications

- Prevents the gradual accumulation of visual clutter.
- Significantly reduces Limibic Friction when initiating a new task.
