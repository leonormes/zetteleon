## Unfinished collection prevents decision closure during clarification

Ongoing idea collection during an evaluation or clarification pass prevents decision closure on the items already under review. Splitting out new captures into a separate inbox preserves finality. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- **Applies:** Batch clarification and triage sessions (such as GTD inbox processing) where decisions must reach a stopping point.
- **Alternative mechanism:** Interference may reflect ordinary task-switching cost or queue instability rather than incompatible psychological states.

### Evidence

- **Unsourced hypothesis:** Plausible practitioner observation, but no empirical study or primary source citation is attached yet. *(No Calibre book quotation attached yet.)*

### Implications

- Route any new thought that surfaces during clarification straight into the inbox queue rather than evaluating it inline.
