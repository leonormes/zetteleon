## Broad Open-Top Containers Make Put-Away a Single Motion Where Fine Categories Do Not

Storage fails when putting something away needs more effort than the person has left, as when it means opening a door, shifting boxes and unclipping a lid. Broad open-top baskets or deep drawers, with lids and doors removed from high-use bins, allow a one-motion drop at the cost of coarser sorting. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to high-frequency storage that several people use. Items that need protection or precise grouping, such as medicines, may still need finer containers.

### Evidence

> "Remove cabinet doors or lids from high-frequency storage bins so items can be dropped directly into place without clearing secondary obstacles."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against a primary source)

The chat credits Cassandra Aarssen (Clutterbug) for the macro-storage idea. I have not checked her work, so confidence is low.

### Implications

- Take lids and doors off the busiest bins first.
- Sort within a broad bin only during a deep-maintenance slot.
