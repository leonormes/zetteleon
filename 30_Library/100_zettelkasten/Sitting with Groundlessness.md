## Sitting with Groundlessness

Groundlessness is the state of uncertainty or visceral dissatisfaction experienced when facing a difficult or boring task. Rather than avoiding this discomfort through distraction, one should pause and observe the sensation, welcoming the discomfort as a transient state.

### Scope & Conditions

A mindfulness practice used to interrupt the automatic urge to procrastinate.

### Evidence

> "When the urge to seek a distraction arises, pause. This discomfort is 'groundlessness'. Instead of running to a comfortable distraction, welcome the discomfort…"

### Implications

- Relaxing the physical struggle against mental discomfort reduces its power over behavior.
- Builds the capacity to tolerate the friction inherent in starting complex work.
