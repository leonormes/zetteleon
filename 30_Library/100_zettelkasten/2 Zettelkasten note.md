## Components of a Zettel

The sources identify three key components that make up a Zettel:

- Unique Identifier: Each Zettel must have a unique identifier so it can be referred to directly, similar to an address. This allows for the creation of a network of notes by connecting individual Zettels. This unique identifier enables the hypertextuality of the Zettelkasten system. There are several methods for creating unique identifiers, such as using the Luhmann ID system, timestamps, or arbitrary unique strings.
- The Body: This section contains the actual piece of knowledge you are capturing in the Zettel. The key is to write the body in your own words, even if it is based on a quote or external source. This encourages deeper processing of the information and makes the knowledge your own. The length of the body should be determined by the principle of atomicity, meaning each Zettel should contain only one thought or unit of knowledge.
- References: This section, usually placed at the bottom of the Zettel, lists the source of the information contained in the note. This could be a book, article, other Zettel, or even your own thoughts if there is no external reference. For external sources, it is recommended to use reference management software and citekeys for easy referencing.

The sources emphasize that these three components are essential for creating a functional and interconnected Zettelkasten system.
