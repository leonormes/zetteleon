## Self-Discipline Predicts Academic Success Better Than Measured Intelligence

Self-discipline predicts the school success of adolescents better than measured intelligence does. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- The finding concerns eighth grade pupils and grades.
- Prediction does not show that raising self-discipline would raise grades.

### Evidence

> "Self-discipline outpredicts IQ for academic success by a factor of about 2"
> (Seligman 2011, ch. 6) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB)

Seligman reports a 2005 study by Duckworth and Seligman, which I have not read.

### Falsifier

- If replications with similar pupils find intelligence the stronger predictor of grades, the claim is wrong.
