## Emotional Regulation Worsens During the ADHD Trough Phase

During the trough a person becomes more reactive and irritable, or emotionally flat. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Describes emotional regulation in the trough, as reported in the source note. It names two opposite presentations, reactive and flat, and the note does not say what decides which appears.

### Implications

- Emotional reactions in the trough partly reflect depleted regulation, which is worth weighing before acting on them.
