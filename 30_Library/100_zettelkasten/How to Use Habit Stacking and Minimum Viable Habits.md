## How to Use Habit Stacking and Minimum Viable Habits

Habit stacking and minimum viable habits eliminate decision fatigue and activation energy when building a new routine. By shrinking the target action to a tiny threshold, tying it directly to an already automated cue, and preparing the environment in advance, the behaviour survives low-motivation days.

### Scope & Conditions

Effective for daily practices such as journaling, exercise, reading, or morning/evening routines. Avoid stacking multiple new habits onto the same anchor simultaneously.

### Evidence

Derived from points 3, 4, 6, and 7 of the 9-Point Consistency Framework in [[SoT - Intentional Living]], consistent with [[Making a behavior easy increases the likelihood it will become a habit]] and [[Habits are Automatic Behaviors Triggered by Environmental Cues]].

### Implications

- Preserving continuity on bad days matters more than hitting peak volume on good days.
- Environmental prep the night before removes morning executive-function bottlenecks.
