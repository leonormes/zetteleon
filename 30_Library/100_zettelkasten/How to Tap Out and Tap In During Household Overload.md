## How to Tap Out and Tap In During Household Overload

Use the pre-agreed tap-out to hand over relief and process in one move. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies after both partners agreed the rule in advance. Do not use it to dodge a standing job.

### Evidence

> "The other partner "Taps In" and takes full authority over the _Process_."
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §3 Protocol A)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

1. Say I am tapping out.
2. Name the rest window.
3. Tap in and run the process.
4. Tell the children what is happening.
5. Give a quiet break to the tapped-out partner.
