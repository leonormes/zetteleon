## Aerobic Deficiency Syndrome Results from an Underdeveloped Aerobic System

**Summary:** Aerobic Deficiency Syndrome (ADS) is a state of physiological imbalance characterized by fatigue, increased body fat, and chronic injury due to an underdeveloped aerobic system.

**Details:** ADS often results from excessive anaerobic training or high cumulative stress. Symptoms include carbohydrate intolerance, mental/emotional stress, and a decreased ability to burn fat efficiently for energy.
