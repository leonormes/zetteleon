## Understanding a Belief's Origins Is Not Endorsement

Summary: You can view someone as a natural manifestation of their environment while still aggressively opposing the beliefs and policies they support.

Details: The goal of the ecological model is not to agree with or excuse a harmful belief but to accurately diagnose how it grew. You can recognise that a plant is poisonous without being angry at it for following its biological programming. This separation protects intellectual boundaries (you never pretend the view is acceptable) while protecting emotional energy (you stop exhausting yourself judging people as broken axioms). Diagnosis and opposition are compatible; comprehension and endorsement are different operations.
