## Reason and Sympathy Can Be Applied to Enhance Human Flourishing

The Enlightenment principle is that we can apply reason and sympathy to enhance human flourishing. The source is Steven Pinker, Enlightenment Now.

### Scope & Conditions

Pinker calls this principle one that "may seem obvious, trite, old-fashioned" and says he wrote the book because it is not.

### Evidence

> "The Enlightenment principle that we can apply reason and sympathy to enhance human flourishing may seem obvious, trite, old-fashioned. I wrote this book because I have come to realize that it is not."
> (Enlightenment Now, Introduction)

### Implications

- Reason and sympathy are named together as the two ingredients of progress.
- The principle is presented as needing defence, not as settled.
