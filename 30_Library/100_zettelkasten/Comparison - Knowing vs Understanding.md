## Comparison - Knowing Vs Understanding

| Criteria        | Knowing                                  | Understanding                            |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Definition  | Possessing information                   | Constructing mental models               |
| Demonstration | Repetition of facts                    | Explanation and application              |
| Cognitive Load | High (raw data)                        | Low (compressed patterns)                |

Key Difference:

Understanding integrates knowledge into actionable frameworks while knowing merely accumulates facts.

[extends:: [[Information vs Knowledge]], confidence=medium]

## Tensions

- Qualification from the library: the table treats knowing as mere accumulation, but _Learn Like a Pro_ says the relationship runs both ways: "It's easier to memorize information when you understand it well, but it's also easier to understand information that you have memorized." Memorised facts can therefore feed understanding, so the two are not strictly separate stages. A boundary condition on the "merely accumulates" wording, not a contradiction.
