## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Adult ADHD impairs executive self-regulation at the point of performance—knowing what to do, yet failing to initiate or sustain it in the moment it matters. Effective compensation shifts load from internal willpower to external scaffolding, but an external tool only functions as a true prosthetic floor when it is EF-independent (pushed by the environment, automated with visible failure alarms, or anchored by a human). Any tool that must be remembered, opened, maintained, and obeyed by the user is an EF-dependent pull tool bounded by the very executive function it is meant to replace.

## 2. Argument Thread—Push Floors vs. Pull Tools

Because initiation and prospective memory are impaired, most "productivity prosthetics" collapse under a Circularity Problem: they demand intact executive function just to be opened, updated, and obeyed:

![[External Scaffolds Fail Through Circularity When They Require Executive Function to Operate]]

![[Task Management Systems Have Limited Efficacy for ADHD Productivity]]

To escape circularity, interventions must be separated by who or what supplies the activation energy—distinguishing an EF-independent push floor from an EF-dependent pull aid:

![[EF-Independent Push Floor]]

When pull tools are not recognised as EF-dependent, they trigger the Trap of the Architect: mistaking elaborate system-building for execution while the actual floor remains unbuilt. Survivable scaffolding instead relies on choice architecture and pre-committed triggers decided in advance:

## 4. Tensions, Failure Modes & Assumptions to Challenge

Two boundary tensions keep prosthetic scaffolding honest. First, treating an external tool as _literally_ constituting executive function (rather than an instrumental aid whose coupling can break) fails whenever the user stops noticing or obeying the tool; second, cognitive offloading cannot substitute for actual comprehension:

![[A trusted, always-available external task scaffold performs the working-memory function for an ADHD person for the operations it holds, rather than assisting an internal one]]

![[Digital Tools Cannot Replace the Cognitive Processes of Understanding and Connecting Ideas]]

Assumptions to challenge (if any proves false in practice, open a `HEAD - <question>?` tension against the underlying claim):

- _Push-vs-pull boundary:_ Assumes the primary failure mode of digital ADHD tools is EF-dependent initiation/maintenance rather than poor task-value calibration (`[[External Scaffolds Fail Through Circularity When They Require Executive Function to Operate]]`).
- _Human activation superiority:_ Assumes scheduled human accountability consistently outperforms automated push cues without introducing prohibitive social friction (`[[Human Accountability Outperforms Solo Digital Tools for ADHD Task Activation]]`).
- _Instrumental vs. constitutive coupling:_ Assumes ADHD scaffolding must be evaluated instrumentally at the coupling boundary rather than treated as constitutive extended mind (`[[A trusted, always-available external task scaffold performs the working-memory function for an ADHD person for the operations it holds, rather than assisting an internal one]]`).
