## Evidence - Gleick on Wiener Frames Cybernetics as Negative Feedback Control Where Information Is the Key

> But when feedback is negative---as in the original mechanical governor of steam engines, first analyzed by James Clerk Maxwell---it can guide a system toward equilibrium; it serves as an agent of stability.

The same passage adds that feedback "can be mechanical" or electrical and that "Either way, the key to the process is information" (the key to the process is information).

Source: James Gleick, _The Information_, in the account of Wiener's work on control and self-regulation.

### What it Supports

The core of [[Cybernetics]]: self-regulation through feedback, with information as the thing that steers. The wider passage describes goal-directed behaviour in servomechanisms and names control, or self-regulation, as the key.

### What it Does not Show

It is a popular history, not Wiener's own text, and the quoted sentence is about negative feedback specifically. The biological half of the definition is covered separately by Paul Nurse's account of feedback loops in cells (_What Is Life?_, listed under Further Reading).
