## ArtemisLite Grouping Diagnostics

When tracking arrow groups in ArtemisLite, a horizontally dispersed group indicates inconsistent string clearance or windage tuning issues, whereas a vertically dispersed group indicates inconsistencies in anchor point verticality, string finger pressure, or bow arm elevation.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies when diagnosing systemic form or equipment flaws using statistical hit plotting.

### Evidence

> "A horizontally dispersed group may indicate inconsistent string clearance or windage tuning issues, while a vertically dispersed group strongly suggests inconsistencies in anchor point verticality, string finger pressure, or bow arm elevation."

### Implications

- Analyzing the geometry of arrow groups allows archers to narrow down root causes of inaccuracy.
