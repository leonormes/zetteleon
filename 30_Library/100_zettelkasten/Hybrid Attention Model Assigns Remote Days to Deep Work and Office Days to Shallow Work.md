## Hybrid Attention Model Assigns Remote Days to Deep Work and Office Days to Shallow Work

In hybrid work environments, designate remote working days exclusively for uninterrupted deep work—no meetings, email, or chat—and batch all collaboration, shallow work, and meetings into office days. The structural separation of attention modes by physical location removes the need for willpower to resist interruptions during focus time.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to teams operating in hybrid environments with predictable office/remote rhythms. Requires team-level agreement; unilateral adoption fails because meeting invites will still arrive on designated deep-work days unless the norm is explicit. Does not generalise to fully remote or fully in-office contexts.

### Evidence

> "Newport suggests adopting a 'hybrid attention model' for teams. This means designating remote working days entirely for uninterrupted deep work—no meetings, emails, or chat." [05:45]

### Implications

- Converts physical location into a context trigger that signals attention mode—an application of environmental cue design to professional focus.
- Minimises context-switching costs by batching cognitively different task types into distinct days rather than hours.
