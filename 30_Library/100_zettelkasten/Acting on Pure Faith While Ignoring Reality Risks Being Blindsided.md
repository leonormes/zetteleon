## Acting on Pure Faith While Ignoring Reality Risks Being Blindsided

Acting on pure faith while ignoring reality leaves a person exposed to being blindsided. Conviction that pays no attention to real-world feedback has no way to warn its holder.

### Scope & Conditions

The opposite failure mode to demanding certainty before acting. It concerns conviction held without reference to real-world feedback, not conviction as such.

### Evidence

> "…whereas acting on pure faith while ignoring reality risks getting blindsided."
> (Nir Eyal, Beyond Belief, via an LLM summary; chapter and page not captured)

### Implications

- Motivates the requirement that a useful belief stay grounded in reality-tested experience.
