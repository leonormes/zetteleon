## External Structure Is the Load-Bearing Backbone of Discipline and Not the Source of Drive

External structure supplies the load-bearing backbone of discipline by holding the shape that lets force be applied, but it does not generate the force, so it takes over the job of carrying the load that motivation was expected to do and leaves open where drive comes from. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the claim that structure beats willpower. It narrows that claim so it is not read as systems replace motivation.

### Evidence

This is my own reasoning, carried over from the earlier text of [[Discipline Is a Property of External Structure More Than of Will]]. No book quotation has been verified for this card.

### Implications

- Judge a system by whether it holds the behaviour in place, not by whether it makes you want it.
- Look separately for what supplies direction, such as values and identity.

### Falsifier

- If a system with no source of drive at all reliably sustains a behaviour a person does not value, the backbone and engine split is wrong.
