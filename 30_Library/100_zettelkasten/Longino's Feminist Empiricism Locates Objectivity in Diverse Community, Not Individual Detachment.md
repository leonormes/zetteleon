## Longino's Feminist Empiricism Locates Objectivity in Diverse Community, Not Individual Detachment

Longino's feminist empiricism argues that true scientific objectivity isn't achieved by individual detachment, but by pooling together a diverse community of different viewpoints to check each other's biases.

### Scope & Conditions

A distinct feminist-epistemology position from standpoint epistemology; relocates objectivity to the community level.

### Evidence

> "Feminist empiricism (Longino) argues that true scientific objectivity isn't achieved by individual detachment, but by pooling together a diverse community of different viewpoints to check each other's biases."

### Implications

- Structurally parallel to Godfrey-Smith's community-division-of-labour account and Merton's reward-system account—a third, independently-arrived-at claim that scientific objectivity is a community-level property, not an individual one.
