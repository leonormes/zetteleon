## Matching the Conversation Frequency Must Come Before Offering Practical Fixes

A conversation is practical, emotional or about social identity, and a person must be met on the frequency they are on, with feelings validated before any practical fix is offered. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to partners and children. The three types come from the Supercommunicator idea, which I have not checked against its source.

### Evidence

> "Match the speaker's frequency. Never offer practical system fixes or logical arguments when a partner or child is having an emotional conversation."
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §4.1)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

- Ask which type is active: practical, emotional or social identity.
- Validate feelings first to establish psychological safety, then bridge to the practical.
