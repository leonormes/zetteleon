"The deduction is clear: a two-fletch system lacks the requisite surface area to counteract the destabilizing lift of a fixed-blade broadhead under imperfect launch conditions." This was demonstrated when the two-fletch system "ranked 21st out of 23 builds in restorative lift, allowing a massive 10.2 inches of lateral broadhead drift" compared to the 3-fletch version.
