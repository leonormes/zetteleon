## Practice—Flat Linking and Tagging

What: Store notes flat and connect them with links and tags rather than sorting them into a deep folder hierarchy.

Why it suits ADHD: Removes the "where does this go?" decision at capture time—a common point of paralysis—and matches associative thinking.

Micro-step: On your next new note, add one [[wiki-link]] to an existing note instead of deciding on a folder. That is the whole step.
