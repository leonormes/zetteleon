## Judgment-Based Productivity Gains

Senior developers achieve significant productivity gains (estimated at 5x–10x) by applying their technical judgment to audit, validate, and "merit-check" agent-generated output. Their ability to identify edge cases and ensure architectural integrity allows them to leverage agents as high-output executors.

### Scope & Conditions

Estimated at 5x–10x gains compared to manual implementation. This applies specifically to experienced engineers who can reliably oversee autonomous implementers.

### Evidence

> "Senior Developers: Experience 5x–10x productivity gains because they possess the requisite judgment to validate and 'merit-check' agent output."

### Implications

- Increases the value of broad architectural experience.
- Prioritises "merit-checking" skills over syntax familiarity.
