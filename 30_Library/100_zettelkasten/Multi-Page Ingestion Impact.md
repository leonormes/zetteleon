## Multi-Page Ingestion Impact

A single source ingestion within an LLM Wiki can trigger simultaneous updates across multiple synthesised concept pages. This mechanism ensures system-wide information consistency by automatically propagating new insights and cross-references across all relevant parts of the knowledge graph, rather than leaving them in isolated files.

### Scope & Conditions

The "Ingest" operation within a highly interlinked AI-maintained knowledge base.

### Evidence

> "A single source may update 10–15 different pages simultaneously."

### Implications

- Automates the complex task of maintaining global consistency as new evidence is introduced.
- Ensures that the knowledge base remains a coherent, integrated whole rather than a fragmented collection of independent summaries.
