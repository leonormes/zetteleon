## WTF is Knowledge Anyway

> Source note for an AI chat session (May 2026) in which Leon worked out what knowledge is. The AI answers were largely generic and duplicated existing notes, so only the distinct signal was extracted into atoms. The full transcript is kept in [[21-wtf_is_knowledge_anyway (raw chat)]].

Leon's opening thoughts: knowledge is "bits of information", "sequences of information". The difference from information is covered in [[What is information]] and [[Information vs Knowledge]].

### The Questions Asked

1. What the fuck is knowledge?
2. what is the difference between information and knowledge
3. what does it mean for data to have meaning? for data to become information
4. but the AI doesn't have insight. it is unaware. the insight is in the brain of a human reading the output of the ai
5. if 2 people share the same knowledge but with vastly different ways of gaining and understanding that knowledge, what properties of the shared knowledge are the same
6. how does ai represent knowledge internally that allows it to discern what is important and relevant
7. in the context of data what is meaning? how is meaning formally defined
8. staying with data knowledge and meaning what is the formal definition of relationship? for instance 2 piece of knowledge can have different relationships or a datum can be related to a context
