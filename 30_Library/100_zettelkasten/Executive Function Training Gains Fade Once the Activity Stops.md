## Executive Function Training Gains Fade Once the Activity Stops

Summary: Executive function gains from training are not permanent—they depend on continued practice.

Details: Across the different approaches used to improve executive function—computerised training, neurofeedback, mindfulness, and exercise—research consistently shows that gains do not persist once the activity is discontinued. This mirrors the general "use it or lose it" pattern in skill acquisition: executive function is better treated as a capacity that must be continuously exercised than as a deficit that can be permanently fixed by a course of training.
