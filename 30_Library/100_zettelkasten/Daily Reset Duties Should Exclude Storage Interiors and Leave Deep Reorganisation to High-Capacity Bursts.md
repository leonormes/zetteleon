## Daily Reset Duties Should Exclude Storage Interiors and Leave Deep Reorganisation to High-Capacity Bursts

The daily minimum should cover only what the day needs: a clear sink, a wiped prep surface and the rubbish out. Opening cupboards to sort or reorganise belongs to a separate deep-maintenance tier, used when capacity or hyperfocus allows and tracked on a visible list of deep jobs. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the household closing routine and to anyone whose capacity swings from day to day. The source calls these tiers Zone 1 and Zone 3. This vault already uses zone and L1 to L3 for rooms and storage, so this card says daily minimum and deep maintenance instead.

### Evidence

> "Storage interiors are strictly off-limits during this time."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against a primary source)

The quotation refers to the chat's Zone 1 daily minimum. The chat's sources are unchecked, so confidence is low.

### Implications

- Storage interiors are off limits during the daily check-in.
- A deep-jobs list turns spontaneous motivation into one finished job instead of a derailed evening.
