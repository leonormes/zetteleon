The "Performer Upgrade Framework" provides the necessary environment to ~~[[Train the Performer to Escape the Intelligence Trap]]~~ (unresolved—no note by this title, or by the closely related "The Intelligence Trap - Strategist vs Performer" naming that [[Inaction Erodes Self-Trust]] already uses as this note's display alias, exists anywhere in the vault; left unresolved rather than guessed, worth authoring if the Strategist/Performer distinction itself is wanted as its own note). It consists of four key elements designed to rebuild the atrophied "Performer" persona:

1. Simplicity: The Performer is overwhelmed by complexity. It needs very small, clear, simple steps rather than grand, complex plans. [depends_on:: [[Making minimums truly attainable addresses executive dysfunction]], confidence=medium]
2. Psychological Safety: To overcome the fear of failure, the Performer needs a safe space for "controlled exposure to risk." This allows for practice without devastating consequences (e.g., recording a video privately before posting publicly).
3. External Pressure (Critical): The Performer instinctively avoids discomfort. Self-imposed deadlines often fail because the brain knows there are no real consequences. Genuine external pressure (from clients, accountability groups, social stakes) makes it "more painful to stay there than it is to move." [depends_on:: [[External Accountability Sustains ADHD Project Commitment]], confidence=medium]
4. Feedback: The Performer needs quality feedback to improve its actions over time.

By creating an environment with these elements, the Performer is forced to act. Repeated action rebuilds its strength and confidence, leading to the development of genuine self-discipline.
