## Implementation Intentions Elevate ADHD Response Inhibition Toward Neurotypical Levels

Gawrilow and Gollwitzer's studies (2008) found that forming implementation intentions elevated response inhibition in children with ADHD to the level observed in neurotypical children, with the combination of implementation intentions and stimulant medication producing the highest inhibition performance. A 2011 study further found if-then plans benefit delay-of-gratification performance in ADHD children specifically.

### Scope & Conditions

Effective implementation intentions require a specific, observable environmental cue (not a time or a feeling) paired with a first physical action requiring zero decisions at the moment of execution—the if-then structure delegates the initiation decision to the environment rather than to willed deliberation.

### Evidence

> "Two empirical studies by Gawrilow and Gollwitzer (2008) showed that forming implementation intentions elevated response inhibition in children with ADHD to the level observed in neurotypical children—and combining implementation intentions with stimulant medication produced the highest inhibition performance."

### Implications

- Format: "If I [specific observable cue], then I will [first physical action requiring zero decisions]"—e.g. "If I open my terminal at 09:00, then I will open the project directory and run the status check command."
- Implementation intentions are one of the few interventions with ADHD-specific controlled-trial evidence, not just general behavioural-science plausibility.
