## Household Routine Changes Should Be Small Joint and Reversible Experiments

Introducing one jointly chosen household routine element as a time-boxed, reversible two-week experiment avoids system-building paralysis and respects shared load.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies when introducing or repairing household routines after conflict; pairs with a scheduled review date and mutual reciprocity.

### Evidence

> "One routine element only, for two weeks, chosen _together_ (e.g. a jointly agreed phone cut-off time, enforced at device level so neither of us has to remember or override)."
> ([[Household Routines Proposal]], §6)

### Implications

- Limiting scope to a single element makes adherence testable and low-risk.
- A two-week review horizon turns system design into an iterative partnership experiment.
