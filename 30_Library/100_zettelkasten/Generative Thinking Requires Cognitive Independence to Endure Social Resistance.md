## Generative Thinking Requires Cognitive Independence to Endure Social Resistance

Generative intelligence is not merely a cognitive skill; it requires cognitive independence and character bravery. Because institutions and peers defend established paradigms, challengers cannot rely on social proof—unlike performative conformity to an already-accepted cause—and must endure isolation while the consensus is still against them.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies when a new framework directly threatens entrenched institutional incentives, reputations, or identity-bound beliefs. In low-stakes technical brainstorming where psychological safety is high, social resistance is minimal.

### Evidence

This claim synthesises §3 of [[SoT - Generative Intelligence]], supported historically by [[Galileo's Heliocentrism Is Feyerabend's Counterexample to Strict Empiricist Rules]] and sociologically by [[Paradigm Shifts Complete Through Generational Turnover, Not Persuasion]].

### Implications

- Seeking peer validation prematurely can extinguish novel frameworks before they are articulated enough to defend.
- Distinguishing performative conformity from genuine dissent prevents mistaking fashionable consensus-following for independent thought.
