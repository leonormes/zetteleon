## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Our household operates as one cooperative team—Mum (Zofja), Dad (Leon), Rae, Bessie, and Pearl—where shared home maintenance builds lifelong independence and protects family warmth from nagging and silent resentment. Because our household navigates ADHD, dyslexia, and working-memory load, we replace verbal policing with point-of-performance choice architecture: blue-paper laminated Definitions of Done, bridged physical-and-digital scaffolds, joint parental consent before enforcement, neutral `Work = Access` privilege gates, Green-Brain-only check-ins, and small reversible Sunday experiments.

## 3. Parental Alignment, Co-Regulation & Reversible Iteration

Neutral system boundaries only hold when Mum and Dad agree them privately in the back-end before activating them in front of the children, never withhold shared household upkeep as leverage during disagreements, and adapt the system through small two-week experiments at the Sunday family review ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], [[Protocol - Co-Admin Onboarding (Zofja)]], [[Protocol - Household Routine Reset Conversation]]):

![[Shared Parental Agreement Must Precede Unilateral Routine Enforcement#^claim]]

![[Household Routine Changes Should Be Small Joint and Reversible Experiments#^claim]]
