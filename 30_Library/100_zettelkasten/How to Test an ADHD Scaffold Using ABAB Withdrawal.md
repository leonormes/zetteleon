## How to Test an ADHD Scaffold Using ABAB Withdrawal

An N-of-1 ABAB withdrawal test prevents mistaking novelty-driven enthusiasm for a durable executive-function floor. By alternating one-week baseline, scaffold-on, scaffold-off, and scaffold-restored phases while tracking objective completion and bad-day survival, you verify whether the tool genuinely works after novelty fades.

### Scope & Conditions

Use before adopting any new productivity app, routine, or automation as permanent ProdOS infrastructure. Skip formal withdrawal on safety-critical automations (such as bill direct debits) where withdrawal risks real-world harm.

### Evidence

Derived from §5 of [[SoT - Prosthetic Executive Function]].

### Implications

- Measuring objective starts, latency to action, or missed obligations avoids the illusion of feeling organised without finishing work.
- Requiring survival on the worst 20% of sleep or mood days filters out brittle Tier 2 pull systems early.
