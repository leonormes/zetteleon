Following [[Phase 2 - Aggressive Initiation to Overcome Paralysis]], the final phase of the [[MOC - Three-Phase Protocol for Execution]] is to establish a routine of ruthless, compartmentalized focus. This is designed to beat distraction and overwhelm during the execution of the work.

1. Isolate and Eliminate Distraction: Create a "deep work" environment by turning off all notifications and removing potential interruptions. This aligns with the concept of using specific contexts like `@DeepWork` as defined in [[Protocol - Action-First GTD (LLM Chief of Staff)|09 - Todoist for Task Execution]].
2. Identify and Leverage High-Value Tasks: Focus only on Key Result Areas (KRAs) or Income Generating Tasks (IGTs) during peak energy windows. This is a direct implementation of the [[MOC - ProdOS]] "Compass Over Clock" principle.
3. Follow the Process and Single-Task: Break the task into manageable pieces and focus only on the present action. The Pomodoro Technique is an excellent method for enforcing this, which is a core concept in [[SoT - Temporal Management (Blocking and Boxing)|11 - Time Management (Timeboxing and The Unschedule)]].
4. Use a "To Leverage" List: Aggressively delegate or eliminate low-value tasks ("posteriorities"). This frees up mental and emotional energy to focus on your core strengths and most important work.
