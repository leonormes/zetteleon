## A Shared Charter Is Enforced by Appeal to the Agreement and Not by Feelings

To stop respect becoming a tool of control, a charter binds both partners and is enforced by appeal to what was agreed and not by appeal to feelings. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to any agreed protocol between partners. It assumes the agreement was made freely by both.

### Evidence

> "Wrong: "I feel disrespected so I won't do it.""
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §5)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

- Right: we agreed to the Tag-Team protocol, and if I tap in I manage the process.
- Mutual accountability means the charter applies to both partners.
