title: Layer 5 Session Layer
---

The Session Layer manages dialogues (sessions) between applications. It establishes, maintains, and terminates connections. It also handles authentication and authorization.

- Problem Solved: Controls the conversations between different applications, ensuring that they can communicate effectively and securely.
