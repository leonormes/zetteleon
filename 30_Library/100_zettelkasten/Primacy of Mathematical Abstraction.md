## Primacy of Mathematical Abstraction

The core difficulty of concurrent systems is a lack of adequate mathematical abstraction rather than implementation complexity. To ensure correctness, the focus must shift from "code-first" to "algorithm-first" thinking, grounded in state machines and mathematical invariants.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the design, reasoning, and verification phases of concurrent and distributed system development.

### Evidence

> "The fundamental challenge… is not a matter of coding complexity, but of inadequate mathematical abstraction."

### Implications

- System correctness should be established through formal mathematical models before implementation.
- State machines and invariants are the primary tools for managing concurrency complexity.
