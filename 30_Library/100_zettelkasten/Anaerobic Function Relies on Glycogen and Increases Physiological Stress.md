## Anaerobic Function Relies on Glycogen and Increases Physiological Stress

Summary: Anaerobic function powers short, high-intensity bursts of activity by burning stored sugar (glycogen) without sufficient oxygen.

Details: While necessary for power and speed, excessive reliance on the anaerobic system stimulates cortisol production. This can lead to a shift away from fat-burning, increased inflammation, and a higher risk of overtraining or injury.
