These workflows, GTD and Zettelkasten, have put me in a lazy mode and tricked me into seeing this process as easy. But effort is required.

Despite initial appearance, Zettelkasten is not easy. It is a system that offers tools that facilitate broad thinking. The job of thinking and learning is not easy. The results are of course coveted. Because of our parsimonious brains we take this system as if it were a shortcut to success. But it is a way of neatly and tidly putting that difficult work right in front of us; here you go, no more talk or showboating if you want this, think.

My mind is especially greedy when collecting ideas. I don't want to miss a thing. When a book is interesting and dense with ideas I want to capture it all. But that is just the book repeated. I should aim to capture just 1 idea and process it properly rather than get frozen in indecisive panic.

## One Example of a Bigger Pattern

Added 2026-10-14 at Leon's request. The framing is his: this note is a single instance of something wider in his life. He keeps trying to make things easier, until the point where there is real work to do, and he procrastinates at several levels at once. The vault already names each level, so this section maps them rather than adding a new claim.

[implements:: [[The Trap of the Architect Is Sophisticated System-Building Substituting for Physical Action]]]

### The Levels

- Capture (the greedy-collector paragraph above): [[Claim - Capture is easy but processing is hard]]—_Capture demands almost no executive function and processing demands a lot, which is the gap this note describes._ [[SoT - Processing IS the Work]]—_The canonical statement that processing is the value, and that ADHD brains file it as boring meta-work._
- Tools: [[Tool tinkering is a form of productive procrastination]]—_Adjusting settings and plugins feels like progress while avoiding the harder task._ [[System Tweaking as a Form of Procrastination in ADHD]]—_Why tweaking is attractive: novelty, control and quick reward._
- Systems: [[The Trap of the Architect Is Sophisticated System-Building Substituting for Physical Action]]—_The umbrella pattern: using a real strength in building systems to avoid the action the system was for._ [[The Danger of Perpetual System-Building]]—_Staying in the build-and-refine phase instead of using the system._ [[Claim - Novelty-craving drives self-defeating system-hopping]]—_The same pull that starts a system also abandons it._
- Thinking and planning: [[The Core Problem Confusing Preparation with Action]]—_Deciding and preparing mistaken for doing._ [[The Comfort of Motion Hinders Genuine Action]]—_Motion gives a sense of accomplishment without the discomfort of action._ [[ADHD Overthinking is a Substitute for Action]]—_Thinking about the task stands in for starting it._
- The cost: [[Inaction Erodes Self-Trust]]—_Repeatedly not acting on one's own plans lowers the trust needed to act next time._

### What Pushes back

- [[Eufriction - Productive Friction Strengthens Thinking]]—_Deliberate friction is what turns collecting into understanding, which is the point of this note's first paragraph._
- [[Intellectual Rigour Means Manufacturing the Friction That Abstraction Removes]]—_If a tool removes the resistance, the resistance has to be built back in by hand._
- [[Digital Productivity Paradox]]—_Tools that lower friction can raise low-value throughput without advancing the project._
- [[Procras-working Builds Momentum by Lowering Task Resistance]]—_A way to start the real work without a heroic push._

### Watch for This Section Being the Pattern

[[Claim - Analysing One's Own Avoidance Can Itself Be an Instance of the Avoidance It Describes]]—_Mapping the pattern is cheap and feels like progress; [[Self-Insights That Prescribe More Planning Are the Least Trustworthy Kind]] applies if the conclusion is to plan more. The test is what single action follows from this note._

### Tensions

- This note's last paragraph says to capture one idea and process it properly. [[Claim - Over-capture plus deferred review is sustainable]] says capturing more than you can process, then reviewing later, is more sustainable for ADHD. Both can hold if the background assumption changes (what is sustainable versus what produces understanding), so no `contradicts` edge. The vault does not yet say which rule applies when.
