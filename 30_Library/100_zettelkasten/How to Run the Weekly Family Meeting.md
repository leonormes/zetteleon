## How to Run the Weekly Family Meeting

Run the meeting in a fixed order: win, rotation, tweaks. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to a household running the chore rotation. The slot is to be confirmed at the Family Kickoff in [[Family Chores - Everyone's Guide]].

### Evidence

> "Win of the Week: Celebrate efforts, improvements, and milestones."
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §6)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

1. Win of the Week.
2. Checklist and rotation review.
3. System tweaks.
4. Change one variable.
5. Close.
