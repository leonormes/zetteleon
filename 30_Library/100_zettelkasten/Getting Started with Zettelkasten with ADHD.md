## Getting Started with Zettelkasten with ADHD

To overcome the inertia of starting a Zettelkasten, especially with ADHD, the key is to allow for imperfection. The goal is not to build a perfect system, but to start thinking and connecting ideas, embracing the concept that your Zettelkasten is a mirror not a monument.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to anyone with ADHD who is starting a Zettelkasten and is stalled by perfectionism or the urge to research the right way first. The Zettelkasten method worked for Luhmann because he developed it organically. An ADHD brain is excellent at seeing connections between diverse topics, so the advice is to trust that strength instead of fighting against how the mind works.

### Evidence

This claim is my own reasoning from practice, not a quoted source. The supporting material is linked under Related and Further Reading.

### Implications

1. Start ridiculously simple: write one note. Do not worry about structure, tags, or links. Just capture something interesting.
2. Create a "no research" rule: give yourself permission to do it "wrong". The value is in the thinking, not perfect implementation.
3. Set a timer for 10 minutes and write one note within that time. When the urge to research "the right way" hits, acknowledge it, but stay with your note.
4. Build incrementally: after a week of single notes, start adding one connection between notes per day.
5. Create a "parking lot" note: when you get the urge to optimize or research, jot the idea down in a separate note to explore later.
