## Appreciation of Beauty is Noticing Excellence in the World

Appreciation of Beauty and Excellence is noticing and appreciating beauty, excellence and skilled performance across life. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It covers nature, art, mathematics, science and everyday things, and includes awe and wonder.

### Evidence

> "You appreciate beauty, excellence, and skill in all domains: nature and art, mathematics and science, and in everyday things."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 253) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what here is excellent enough to stop and notice.
