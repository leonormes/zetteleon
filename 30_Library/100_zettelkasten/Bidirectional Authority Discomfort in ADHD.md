title: Bidirectional Authority Discomfort in ADHD
type: "concept"
updated: 
---

People with ADHD often experience bidirectional discomfort with authority - both resisting external authority (being told what to do) and feeling uncomfortable when in positions of authority themselves (directing others). This suggests a general sensitivity to power dynamics rather than simply a desire for personal power.
