## Laudan Distinguishes Rationally Pursuing a Theory From Accepting It as True

Laudan refined Lakatos's research programmes into "research traditions" and made the distinction that a scientist can rationally pursue an exploratory theory to see if it bears fruit without actually accepting it as true.

### Scope & Conditions

A further refinement of Lakatos's research-programme structure.

### Evidence

> "Laudan refined this into 'research traditions' and made a crucial distinction: a scientist can rationally _pursue_ an exploratory theory to see if it bears fruit without actually _accepting_ it as true."

### Implications

- Separates two epistemic stances (pursuit vs acceptance) that are often conflated, giving room for exploratory work that would otherwise look irrational under a strict acceptance-only model.
