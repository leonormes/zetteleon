## Treating Active Construction as the Default Standard of Legitimate Engagement Remains an Incomplete Candidate Driver

Recorded as Candidate Driver 4 (contested and downgraded), privileging active construction and messy generative process over passive consumption recurs across first-person writing on mathematics and PKM. After direct elicitation retracted a false third leg inferred from third-person music notes, the pattern rests on two domains with an incomplete means-end ladder rather than standing as a confirmed independent driver. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Covers Candidate Driver 4 in [[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph — Interest Seed and Candidate Drivers]], grounded in mathematics ([[Public perception of maths is incomplete]]) and PKM ([[Why I Do a PKM and What I Think Knowledge Is For]], [[PKM as Process vs Product]]). Excludes the retracted music leg after Leon's 15 September 2026 correction established that [[Asking Why a Song Exists Helps Determine its Authenticity]] came from a conversation with Bessie and [[The Distinction Between Appreciation and Creation]] was mis-associated with former daily musicianship.

- Preserves Principle 11's open question: corollary of [[First-Principles Systems Modelling May Unify External Technical Architecture and Internal Self-Regulation as One Driver]] versus an independent epistemic or aesthetic value.

### Falsifier
