## A Simple Note-Taking Structure Lets Complexity Build Up at the Content Level

Keeping the structure of a note-taking system simple lets complexity build up in the notes and their links, where it is useful, instead of in the way they are organised. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- Applies to the structure of a whole note-taking system, such as its folders, categories and rules.
- Simple means a few basic principles, not the absence of structure.
- The notes themselves are expected to grow more complex. That is the aim and not a failure of the system.
- Reading this as a reason for single-idea notes is an extension and not a statement by Ahrens.

### Evidence

> "The best way to deal with complexity is to keep things as simple as possible and to follow a few basic principles. The simplicity of the structure allows complexity to build up where we want it: on the content level."
> (Ahrens 2022, ch. 1, PDF p. 23) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/704/EPUB?open_at=toc:1%20Everything%20You%20Need%20to%20Know)

> "They sort their notes by topics and sub-topics, which makes it look less complex, but quickly becomes very complicated."
> (Ahrens 2022, ch. 1) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/704/EPUB?open_at=toc:1%20Everything%20You%20Need%20to%20Know)

> "Plus, it reduces the likelihood of building and finding surprising connections between the notes themselves"
> (Ahrens 2022, ch. 1) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/704/EPUB?open_at=toc:1%20Everything%20You%20Need%20to%20Know)

Ahrens states the claim and refers to Sull and Eisenhardt (2015) for research on it, which I have not checked.

### Implications

- Test a proposed folder, field or rule by asking whether it helps the ideas connect or only makes the system look tidy.
- Prefer links between notes to sorting them into deeper topic hierarchies.

### Falsifier

- Compare note collections of similar size under a few simple rules and under elaborate topic hierarchies. If the simple ones yield no more useful connections and are no easier to work with, the claim is wrong.
