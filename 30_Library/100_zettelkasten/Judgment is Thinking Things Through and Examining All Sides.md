## Judgment is Thinking Things Through and Examining All Sides

Judgment is the strength of thinking things through and examining them from all sides, without jumping to conclusions and with readiness to change your mind in light of evidence. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It is also called critical thinking.

### Evidence

> "You do not jump to conclusions, and you rely only on solid evidence to make your decisions."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 238) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what evidence would change your mind before concluding.
