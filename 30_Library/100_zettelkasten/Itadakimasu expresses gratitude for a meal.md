title: Itadakimasu expresses gratitude for a meal
type: claim

"Itadakimasu" is a traditional Japanese phrase said before meals to express gratitude. It acknowledges everyone involved in preparing the food and the life that the food represents, fostering a deeper sense of appreciation.
