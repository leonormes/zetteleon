Daily cleaning should focus on "clutter bottlenecks"—areas that frequently and quickly accumulate mess, such as kitchen counters, bathroom counters, and floors. To ensure consistency, these small cleaning tasks should be integrated into existing morning or evening routines rather than being scheduled as separate blocks of time.
