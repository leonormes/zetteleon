## Values Serve as Navigational Compass in Absence of Absolute Truth

When facts are provisional and objective certainty is unavailable, consciously chosen values provide directional coherence for decisions over time. Operating like a compass rather than a fixed map, explicit values do not eliminate uncertainty but allow an agent to pick a bearing, evaluate trade-offs, and stay oriented across course corrections. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to personal agency and decision-making under epistemic uncertainty once correspondence-based certainty has been replaced by pragmatic utility.

### Evidence

Conceptual synthesis bridging pragmatic epistemology ([[Pragmatic Truth Focuses on Utility Over Absolute Correctness]]) and value-based agency ([[SoT - Values and Eudaimonia]]); no direct external quotation is attached (`evidence_links: []`).

### Implications

- Articulate a small set of operational values to translate abstract commitments into daily priorities, heuristics, and default choices.
- Evaluate life and project decisions by alignment with chosen bearings rather than waiting for uncontestable certainty.
