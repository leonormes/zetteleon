## Wealth

Wealth is the aggregate of valuable resources, tangible and intangible, that a person has as capability or possession. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

Broader than money, which is an institutional tool for exchange and measurement.

### Evidence

Carried over from the earlier prose version of [[SoT - Financial Philosophy and Spendfulness]]; no outside source is cited.

### Implications

- A bank balance alone does not measure wealth.
