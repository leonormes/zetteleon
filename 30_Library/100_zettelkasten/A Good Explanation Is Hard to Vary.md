## A Good Explanation Is Hard to Vary

For Deutsch, the absolute core of the scientific method is the quest for good explanations. A "good explanation" is an assertion about reality that is hard to vary—meaning its details play a highly specific, functional role in the explanation and cannot be easily swapped out.

### Scope & Conditions

Deutsch's core criterion for scientific quality; intended to replace predictive success or bare falsifiability as the primary target of a good theory.

### Evidence

> "A 'good explanation' is an assertion about reality that is hard to vary—meaning its details play a highly specific, functional role in the explanation and cannot be easily swapped out"

### Implications

- Gives a concrete test for genuinely explanatory (realist) depth, beyond simply wanting to understand mechanics.
- Distinguishes a theory that explains from one that merely fits the data, relevant wherever multiple theories fit the same evidence.
