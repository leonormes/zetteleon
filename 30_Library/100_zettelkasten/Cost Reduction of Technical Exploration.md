## Cost Reduction of Technical Exploration

AI agents significantly lower the opportunity cost of exploring technical hunches and executing marginal performance optimizations. This enables the pursuit of "non-essential" or floor-level improvements that were previously discarded due to high human opportunity costs, allowing for the continuous refinement of codebases.

### Scope & Conditions

Enables the pursuit of "non-essential" or P1-level improvements. This is economically viable when the marginal cost of agent implementation is low.

### Evidence

> "AI agents significantly lower the cost of exploring technical hunches. This allows for the execution of 'non-essential' or marginal improvements."

### Implications

- Permits the refinement of codebases that were previously "good enough."
- Encourages experimental architecture at lower risk.
