## Children Split Parents Unless Both Parents Respond With Empathy First and Accountability Second

Children split their parents, using one for venting and the other for fixing, unless both parents respond in the same order, empathy first and then accountability. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to parents of children old enough to seek the more sympathetic parent. The ordering is my model and has not been tested.

### Evidence

> "To prevent children from "splitting" the parents (relying on one for emotional venting and the other for fixing), we establish a sequential response contract:"
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §3 Protocol C)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

- Both parents practise both steps in order and show a unified front.
- Empathy is the soft landing and accountability is the gentle push.
