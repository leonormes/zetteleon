## How to Run the Daily Chore Check-In

Give one calm 30-minute reminder and verify daily zones against the written checklist in ten minutes. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies at the agreed daily check-in time (proposed 18:00, confirmed at Family Kickoff) when everyone is in Green Brain. If anyone is dysregulated, pause and co-regulate first.

### Evidence

> "Parents give one calm, neutral reminder ~30 minutes before check-in (e.g., 'Just a reminder—we are checking chores in 30 minutes. Let's look at the checklist.'). No nagging or repeated prompts."
> ([[SoT - Family Household Chores]], text before the 2026-11-24 compilation refactor, §3.1)

This is my own operational procedure designed for the household system.

### Implications

- Eliminates repeated evening nagging between school and bedtime.
- Unlocks discretionary evening screen time and Wi-Fi cleanly once items are ticked off.
