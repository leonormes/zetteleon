## A Fair Financial System Uses a Shared Pot and Equal Personal Spending Money

Summary: A fair financial system pools primary income into a shared budget and allocates each partner an identical, no-questions-asked personal spending amount.

Details: The shared pot covers all joint obligations and goals. Equal personal accounts preserve individual autonomy and eliminate the need for partners to police each other's discretionary spending. This model decouples income contribution from spending authority and prevents a parent-child dynamic in money management.
