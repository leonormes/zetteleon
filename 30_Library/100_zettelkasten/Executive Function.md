## Executive Function

The set of control processes that organise behaviour towards goals: planning, prioritisation, organisation, task initiation, task-switching and working memory.

### Why it Matters here

- ADHD is characterised by impairments across these processes.
- Capture barely touches them; processing depends on nearly all of them—which is why the two feel so different in effort. See [[Claim - Capture is easy but processing is hard]].

### Related

- [[Cognitive Load]]—the moment-to-moment load these processes must manage.
