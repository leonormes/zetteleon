The virtue of Temperance, as defined by the [[MOC - Character and Virtue|VIA Character Strengths]] framework, includes strengths that protect against excess and impulsiveness.

## Strengths of Temperance

- [[Forgiveness is Accepting Shortcomings and Giving Second Chances|Forgiveness]]: Forgiving those who have done wrong; accepting others' shortcomings and giving them a second chance.
- [[Humility is Letting Achievements Speak for Themselves|Humility (Modesty)]]: Letting one's accomplishments speak for themselves; not seeking the spotlight.
- [[Prudence is Making Careful Choices for the Future|Prudence]]: Being careful about one's choices; not taking undue risks; not saying or doing things that might later be regretted.
- [[Self-Regulation is Disciplining One's Feelings and Appetites|Self-Regulation (Self-Control)]]: Regulating what one feels and does; being disciplined; controlling one's appetites and emotions.
