"What I cannot create, I do not understand"—Feynman's line names the actual test. You don't understand a code library until you've built something small with it. You don't understand a mental model until you've applied it to a real problem. This is one of the source's named Forcing Functions (antidotes) for IoED: move from passive consumption to active creation.

## Evidence

> "You do not understand a code library until you have built a small app with it. You do not understand a mental model until you have applied it to a real-life problem."—[[SoT - Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IoED)]]

## Steelman

This standard may set the bar for "understanding" too high for many legitimate use cases—a person can correctly apply, critique, or teach a model without ever having built anything with it, so creation may be sufficient but not necessary evidence of comprehension.
