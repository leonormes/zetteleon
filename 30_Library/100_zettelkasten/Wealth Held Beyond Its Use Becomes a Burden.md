## Wealth Held Beyond Its Use Becomes a Burden

Wealth is better treated as fuel than as a scoreboard on which whoever has the most wins. Carrying a million extra gallons of fuel simply weighs the vehicle down. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Concerns accumulation far beyond foreseeable use. It is not an argument against buffers or saving for known needs.

### Evidence

Carried over from the earlier prose version of [[SoT - Financial Philosophy and Spendfulness]]; no outside source is cited.

### Implications

- Judge accumulation by what it will be used for.
