## Spirituality is Having Beliefs About the Higher Purpose of Life

Spirituality is holding coherent beliefs about the higher purpose and meaning of the universe and your place in it. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- Such beliefs shape conduct and give comfort. They may be religious or secular.

### Evidence

> "You have strong and coherent beliefs about the higher purpose and meaning of the universe."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 256) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask which belief about purpose is shaping this choice.
