## Trinity of Systems

Every system is defined by three interdependent components: its physical or conceptual elements, the interconnections (relationships and information flows) between them, and its overall function or purpose. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to any open or closed system analysed through systems dynamics, from biological organisms and ecosystems to organisations and software architectures. It separates structural anatomy from isolated component inventories.

### Evidence

Extracted from [[SoT - Systems Thinking]] (Meadows and Open University synthesis). No verbatim book quote has been verified for this seed note yet.

### Implications

- Replacing individual elements rarely changes system behavior if the interconnections and purpose remain intact.
- Changing the purpose or the interconnections produces far greater behavioral shifts than swapping parts.
