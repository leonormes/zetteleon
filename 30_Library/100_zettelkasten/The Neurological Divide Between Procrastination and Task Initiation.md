### The Neurological Divide Between Procrastination and Task Initiation

Task Initiation Deficit is a mechanical, neurological failure to switch focus from the Default Mode Network (DMN) to the Task-Positive Network (TPN), while procrastination is an emotion-regulation problem to cope with anxiety or fear of failure. 

#### Scope & Conditions

Applies particularly to ADHD neurology where the DMN and TPN are out of sync and lack typical anticorrelation.

#### Evidence

> "Task Initiation Deficit: This is a mechanical, neurological failure to switch focus, not an emotional choice... The DMN attempts to remain active simultaneously, hijacking attention with internal noise and causing a mechanical failure to launch."

#### Implications

- "Mental energy" drain or boredom is a neurological irritant, not a moral failing.
- Solutions for task initiation must address the mechanical switch (e.g., externalizing focus), while solutions for procrastination must address emotional regulation and perfectionism.
