## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Temporal Management is the discipline of treating time as a physical resource. It moves from "Feral" working (reactive, algorithm-led) to Intentional living by merging the To-Do List (What) with the Calendar (When). Success is defined by Intentionality over Productivity: choosing what to do with time to align with values, rather than merely "cramming in" more output.

## Working Knowledge

### 1. Structural Architecture (The Three Pillars)

_Source: Sarah Hart-Unger_

- The Master Calendar (Single SoT): A single, functional calendar for ALL areas of life (Work, Family, Personal). Acts as a hard boundary for capacity.
- Airtight Task Management (Pipelines): A system for 100% capture from all inputs (Email, Text, Verbal). Must be processed daily into a trusted vessel.
- Nested Goals (Horizons): A multi-level planning system spanning Yearly, Seasonal, Monthly, Weekly, and Daily horizons.

### 2. Formal Distinction: Time Blocking vs. Time Boxing

- **Time Blocking ("When" / Macro-Reservation):** Reserving calendar space in advance to protect Quadrant II capacity from shallow encroachment (Cal Newport, *Deep Work* rhythmic and journalistic scheduling). Time Blocking governs *when* a category of work happens so deep capacity is defended before reactive demands arrive.
- **Time Boxing ("How Long + Done Means" / Micro-Constraint):** Allocating a fixed maximum duration (`15m`, `30m`, or `60m`) paired with an explicit, "acceptable rather than perfect" deliverable and an unyielding hard stop (Marc Zao-Sanders; Nir Eyal, *Indistractable* traction model). Time Boxing governs *how long* a single unit of work may run and what "done" looks like inside that box.

### 3. Neuro-Physiological Mechanism (Why Both Are Required for ADHD)

- **Time Blocking** eliminates point-of-performance scheduling decisions, sparing depleted executive function from having to decide *what* to work on in the moment.
- **Time Boxing** caps perfectionism (Parkinson's Law in reverse), visualises hidden work-in-progress (WIP) against finite time, and pairs with the **Somatic Switch** (a brief physical reset at box boundaries) to toggle from the Default Mode Network (DMN) to the Task Positive Network (TPN) and clear attention residue between blocks.

### 4. Two-Window Batched Shallows ("Drain the Shallows")

Shallow communications (email, Microsoft Teams, NHS trust threads) are quarantined into two fixed **20-minute comms triage boxes** per day at **10:30** and **15:30** so they do not fragment Quadrant II blocks:

- **`< 2m`:** Reply immediately and close the loop inside the box.
- **`> 2m` actionable:** Capture onto the undated Todoist runway for scheduled execution; do not execute ad hoc inside the triage box.
- **Blocked on external party:** Tag `#Waiting` and move on.

### 5. Seasonality (Quintile Planning)

- Divide the year into five 10-week "Quintiles" rather than quarters.
- Designate specific "Reflection Seasons" (e.g., Nov/Dec) for system resets.

## Current Understanding

### The Human in the Machine

Current synthesis integrates the Hart-Unger "Pillars" with the Newport "Slow Productivity" philosophy. The system is tool-agnostic:

- Analog Advantage: Tools like the Hobonichi Cousin are valued for tactile memory and forced intentionality.
- AI Stance: AI should handle the _administrative burden_ of scheduling (e.g., Motion), but the human must remain the Problem Definer, deciding priorities based on values and energy.
