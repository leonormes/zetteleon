## Emotional Co-Regulation Must Precede Household Chore Audits and Failure Autopsies

Household chore check-ins, teaching moments, and failure autopsies only work in a calm regulated state, so any sign of fight-flight-freeze overload requires pausing chores immediately to co-regulate first. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to neurodivergent family routines where post-school masking fatigue, sensory overload, or executive dysfunction can trigger amygdala hijack (Red Brain).

### Evidence

> "Green Brain (Rational): Calm, regulated state. Teachable moments, chores audits, and Failure Autopsies occur _only_ here. ... Red Brain (Amygdala/Hijack): Fight/flight/freeze. Pure reaction. Suspension of all choring, teaching, and auditing. Prioritise regulation and safety."
> ([[Protocol - Fostering Growth Mindset (Neurodivergent Family)]], Part 2 §2)

This claim synthesises the vault household regulation protocol with ADHD emotional-flooding mechanics.

### Implications

- Suspend chore inspection immediately when anyone enters Red Brain and prioritise safety and calm connection first.
- Run blame-free Failure Autopsies and checklist adjustments only after everyone has returned to Green Brain.
