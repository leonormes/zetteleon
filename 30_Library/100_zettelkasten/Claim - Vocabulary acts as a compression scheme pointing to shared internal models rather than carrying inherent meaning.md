## Minimum Viable Understanding

A word like "cat" does not inherently carry the meaning of a cat (as noted by Saussure's signifier vs. signified). Instead, vocabulary is a form of data compression. It succeeds only because communicating parties share roughly similar internal mental models that the word activates.

## The Conflation Error

Because the brain does not naturally flag that it is holding a pointer (the word) rather than the underlying data (the model), people frequently mistake their familiarity with a label for true mastery of the concept. This conflation is the root of the Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IoED) and map-territory confusion.
