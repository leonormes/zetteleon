## AI Output Contains No Insight Until a Human Reads and Interprets It

AI systems process data and find patterns but are unaware of what their output means, so any insight arises in the human who reads and interprets it.

### Scope & Conditions

This was Leon's own claim in the chat, and the AI agreed. It concerns current AI systems and insight in the sense of awareness of implications; it does not address whether that could change.

### Evidence

> "but the AI doesn't have insight. it is unaware. the insight is in the brain of a human reading the output of the ai"

> "The real insight occurs in the human brain when interpreting AI outputs."

### Implications

- The value of AI lies in processing data so that human experts can derive insight, which makes it a tool to augment human intelligence, not replace it.
- Humans supply context, experience, critical questioning and actionable interpretation.
