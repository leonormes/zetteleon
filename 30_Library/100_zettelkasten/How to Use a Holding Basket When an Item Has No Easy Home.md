## How to Use a Holding Basket When an Item Has No Easy Home

Use a holding basket to take an item off the surface without opening crowded storage, and settle where it lives later. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies during routine tidying when an item has no easy home. It is not a permanent store: if the basket stays full for weeks, the storage behind it is over capacity.

### Evidence

> "If putting Item A away requires moving Items B and C or reorganising Cupboard D, **do not open the cupboard**."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against a primary source)

The chat credits the 5 Things Tidying Method. The steps below are my own reasoning from it, not the chat's wording, so confidence is low.

### Implications

1. Stop before opening the cupboard or moving anything.
2. Put the item in the holding basket kept in that room.
3. Carry on with the rest of the tidy.
4. Leave the basket until a deep-maintenance slot.
5. Give the contents homes in one sitting, or send them to donation.
