## Multiplicity Is a Stage to Pass Through, Not a Destination

Summary: William Perry's scheme of intellectual development runs dualism ("one Truth; disagreers are wrong") → multiplicity ("no right or wrong at all") → relativism with commitment (holding positions, fallibly, while understanding how others grew theirs).

Details: "There is no right or wrong" is textbook multiplicity—a transitional stage, not a place to pitch your tent. It is self-undermining: a model with no better-or-worse cannot even express your reason for adopting it. The destination is relativism with commitment: keep the insight that _persons_ are not right or wrong (they are outcomes of nature and nurture) and drop the claim that _nothing_ is right or wrong. You keep analytical standards while abandoning person-judgement.
