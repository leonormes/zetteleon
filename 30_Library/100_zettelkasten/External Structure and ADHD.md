## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Individuals with ADHD often struggle with internal time management, task initiation, and organization due to deficits in internal executive function.

To compensate for this biological hardware limitation, the structure must be externalized. Techniques like Pomodoro or timeboxing are not just productivity hacks; they act as an artificial, external structure that provides clear boundaries and expectations. By moving the burden of time-tracking from the internal working memory to an external timer, the brain is freed to focus purely on task execution.
