> [!question] Central question
> When I hit "the block", how much is procrastination, how much is genuine ADHD executive limitation, and how much is an imagined/internalised block I've inherited rather than tested?

This is a decomposition question, not a single claim. The point is to stop treating one undifferentiated "block" as the explanation, because each component has a _different_ fix—and conflating them means I apply the wrong one and conclude "I'm just bad at this".

---

## The Three Candidate Explanations (Steel-manned)

| Label | What it would mean | Discriminating test (the falsifier) |
|---|---|---|
| Procrastination | Avoidance of a task that is genuinely within my capacity, driven by aversion/low reward salience | If the task gets _done_ the moment novelty/interest fires or a deadline lands → it was reachable all along → procrastination, not limitation |
| Genuine ADHD limitation | A real, structural executive-function constraint (task initiation, working-memory load, mode-switching cost) | If reducing activation energy to a single physical micro-step reliably unblocks it → it was an _initiation_ cost, i.e. genuine but _addressable_ |
| Imagined / inherited block | A belief about my limits absorbed from elsewhere, never actually tested against evidence | If I attempt the thing and it goes _fine_ → the block was a story, not a wall. Look for "I can't do X" claims I've never falsified |

## Open Threads (The Session aGenda)

- [ ] Is my PKM a memory, or an archive? Working hypothesis: it fails the extended-mind retrieval condition, so it's an archive. → does the fix live entirely on the _retrieval_ side?
- [ ] Habit or missing links? The 60-second backlinks diagnostic—when I'm in flow, is the note I'd have searched for already one hop away? (Habit → traverse, don't search. Missing → semantic/LLM retrieval.)
- [ ] Does the generative/consolidative split hold? Mobile = capture (low friction); desktop = consolidate (high tooling). Is forcing convergent work in the dressing gown the actual error?
- [ ] Where does each "block" actually sit? For the _next_ three things I avoid, run the table above. Tally which column they fall into. Don't theorise the proportions—measure them.
- [ ] Which of my "I can't" beliefs have I never tested? List candidates. Each untested one is a suspect imagined block.

## Captured This Session (Raw material—to Be mEtabolised)

- Volume _helps_ a content-addressable system (my brain) and _hurts_ a location-addressable one (keyword search). I've been scaling the wrong architecture.
- Time-boxing should be interest-triggered, not clock-triggered—ride the novelty, don't schedule against it.
- The consolidation phase I keep skipping _is_ my dialectical claim-card work. The tooling exists; the session doesn't.
- Self-blame ("I'm not structured enough") may be a misdiagnosis that costs scarce executive function on the wrong fix.

## Position (Dated)

2026-05-31—I currently _suspect_ the proportions are weighted toward genuine-but-addressable ADHD initiation costs, with a non-trivial layer of imagined blocks, and less raw procrastination than my inner critic claims. Confidence: low. This is a prior to be tested by the tally above, not a conclusion.
