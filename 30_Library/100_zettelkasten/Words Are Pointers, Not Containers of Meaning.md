## Words Are Pointers, Not Containers of Meaning

Words point towards meaning held in the experience of each person instead of containing it.

### Scope & Conditions

Framed in the source as the reason a dictionary definition cannot explain why communication succeeds.

### Evidence

> "Words are pointers, not containers of meaning. Their effectiveness relies on a complex interplay of factors that go far beyond the dictionary definition."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 181).

### Implications

- Shared meaning depends on what the speaker and listener bring to the word.
- The effectiveness of words relies on factors that go far beyond the dictionary definition (source line 181).
