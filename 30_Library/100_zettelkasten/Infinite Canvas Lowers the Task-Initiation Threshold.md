## Infinite Canvas Lowers the Task-Initiation Threshold

A blank linear document demands the user first establish a linear order, which presupposes a mental model already exists. A blank infinite canvas demands only that the user place something somewhere, so the spatial act of placement externalises the first unit of thought without requiring a pre-existing structural commitment.

### Scope & Conditions

Most relevant during states of executive-function depletion or cognitive overwhelm, where the up-front demand of linear ordering itself becomes a barrier to starting.

### Evidence

> "A blank document demands that the user first establish a linear order… A blank infinite canvas demands only that the user place something somewhere… This is why visual-first tools lower the activation energy for engagement during states of executive function depletion."

### Implications

- When facing task-initiation difficulty on a synthesis task, switching from a blank document to a blank canvas can lower the activation energy required to begin.
- Canvas-first capture is architecturally compatible with fragmented, non-sequential thinking-in-progress.
