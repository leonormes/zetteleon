## SN - Sequence: Building a Self Without Absolute Certainty

> Sibling sequence. [[SN - Sequence Building Self and Confidence Without Certainty]] traces the same argument at greater length and carries it further, to grounding confidence. Until 2026-08-01 the two ran on _parallel duplicate note-sets_; four of those pairs have now been merged, so both sequences share the same atomic notes. The fifth pair was kept—see Tensions.

### Framing Question

How can one build a confident and functional self after realising that objective certainty is an illusion?

### Train of Thought Sequence

The journey begins with a disorienting intellectual discovery.

1. [[The Realization that Knowledge is Contingent Can Lead to a Crisis of Certainty]]—_establishes the initial problem: the loss of firm epistemic ground._

This realisation can make the world feel arbitrary, but the first step is to reframe that perception.

1. [[Social Constructs Have Real Effects Despite Lacking Objective Truth]]—_provides a more functional understanding: constructs are real systems to be navigated, not illusions to be dismissed._

With this reframe, the goal of thinking must shift from an unattainable ideal to a practical one.

1. [[Shift from Seeking Rightness to Seeking Utility]]—_proposes a new foundation for confidence, grounded in effectiveness rather than truth._ This is the pivot on which the rest of the sequence turns, and it aligns with [[Systems Drive Progress Through the Compounding Effect of Atomic Habits]]—progress comes from what reliably works, not from being right.

That foundation allows the reconstruction of a stable identity—not by finding a "true self", but by building one.

1. [[The Self is Constructed Through a Commitment to Chosen Values]]—_defines the self as an act of will and integrity._ This is the engine behind [[SoT - Habit Formation Framework#3. Driver: Identity-Based Habits|identity-based habits]], where every action is a vote for the person you are becoming.

It also dissolves the problem of originality.

1. [[Originality is Synthesis Not Creation From Nothing]]—_reframes originality as personal recombination, not creation from nothing._

With these foundational shifts in place, practical mental models become available for acting confidently in an uncertain world.

1. [[Strong Opinions, Loosely Held Balances Conviction and Humility]]—_a framework for decisive action coupled with intellectual flexibility._
2. [[Probabilistic Thinking Treats Beliefs as Hypotheses With Confidence Levels]]—_replaces the binary of true/false with a spectrum of confidence; the mechanism that makes "loosely held" operable rather than merely aspirational._
3. [[The Map is Not the Territory]]—_the underpinning principle: our models are useful but never perfect representations of reality._
