## The Prefrontal Cortex Provides Executive Control Over Action

The prefrontal cortex provides top-down executive control over attention, working memory, impulse inhibition, motor responses, and goal-directed action. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

States the baseline functional role of the prefrontal cortex across executive domains—planning, working memory, inhibitory control, cognitive flexibility, and task initiation—as defined in [[Executive Function]]. Within the prefrontal cortex, [[Task Initiation Activates Medial Prefrontal Regions for Energization]] notes that dorsal and lateral regions regulate attention and motor responses while ventral and medial regions regulate emotion, and [[The Brain as a Distributed Prediction Machine]] links the prefrontal cortex to manipulating and simulating distributed mental models. As [[SoT - Cognitive Architectures (Simulation vs Prototyping)]] notes, routine motor and sensory tasks can still run autonomously without prefrontal supervision, and high-creativity flow states can involve transient prefrontal deactivation.

### Evidence

No verbatim source quotation or dedicated evidence note supports this card yet. It synthesises the baseline prefrontal executive-control premise already stated across [[Executive Function]], [[Task Initiation Activates Medial Prefrontal Regions for Energization]], [[Prefrontal Cortex Catecholamine Insufficiency Impairs Executive Control in ADHD]], and [[Executive Dysfunction Has Causes Beyond ADHD Including Autism and Degenerative Disease]]; a primary neuroscience source should be verified to promote it from `seed` to `stable`.

### Implications

- Executive impairment tracks either prefrontal neurochemical insufficiency (as in [[Prefrontal Cortex Catecholamine Insufficiency Impairs Executive Control in ADHD]]) or frontal-lobe tissue damage across distinct diagnoses (as in [[Executive Dysfunction Has Causes Beyond ADHD Including Autism and Degenerative Disease]]).
- Because prefrontal control is capacity-bounded and metabolically expensive, unreliable internal control motivates offloading working memory and initiation triggers into external scaffolds ([[SoT - Prosthetic Executive Function]]).
