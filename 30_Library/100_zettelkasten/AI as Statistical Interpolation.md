## AI as Statistical Interpolation

Large Language Models (LLMs) do not possess true understanding; instead, they "cosplay" intelligence by finding the non-linear average point (interpolation) between concepts within their training data. This mechanism is highly effective for problems within the distribution of known data but inherently fails when a task requires moving outside that distribution.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the fundamental reasoning and generative capabilities of transformer-based architectures.

### Evidence

> "LLMs do not possess understanding; they 'cosplay' intelligence by finding the non-linear average point between concepts in their training data."

### Implications

- Perceived creativity in AI is primarily the compositional interpolation of existing, human-generated patterns.
- AI performance is structurally limited by the boundaries and diversity of its underlying training set.
