## Fairness is Treating All People the Same

Fairness is the strength of giving everyone a fair chance and not letting personal feelings bias decisions about them. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- The vault title says treating all people the same. Fairness read as proportion to what people deserve is a rival reading, noted below.

### Evidence

> "You do not let your personal feelings bias your decisions about other people. You give everyone a chance."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 248) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask whether you would decide the same way about someone you liked less or more.
