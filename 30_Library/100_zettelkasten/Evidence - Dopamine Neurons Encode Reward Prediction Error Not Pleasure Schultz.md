%%[supports:: [[Dopamine Neurons Encode Reward Prediction Error, Not Pleasure]], strength=4, confidence=high]%%

Midbrain dopamine neurons in the VTA and substantia nigra do not encode the pleasure of receiving a reward. They encode reward prediction error (RPE): the difference between the reward actually received and the reward that was predicted. A fully predicted reward produces no dopaminergic response.

Applies to phasic dopamine signalling as characterised in Schultz's foundational RPE work. Does not describe tonic dopamine or the subjective experience of pleasure, which is handled by a separate "liking" system.

**Source:** Neuro-Variable Execution, Spatial Cognition & Knowledge Architecture: An Investigative Report
