## Hedonic Adaptation to Achieved Goals Is a Biological Certainty, Not a Character Failing

Panel-study evidence for the hedonic treadmill shows individuals return toward near-baseline subjective wellbeing after positive life events and achieved goals. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to positive circumstantial or outcome-state changes, such as finishing a project or reaching a goal. Adaptation is partial rather than absolute for major negative events (unemployment, disability) and does not erase gains from ongoing intrinsic activities.

### Evidence

> "The hedonic treadmill effect… demonstrates that individuals return to near-baseline subjective well-being following positive life events…"

### Implications

- Feeling flat after finishing a big project is an expected biological response, not evidence the project was the wrong one.
