## Standpoint Epistemology Claims Marginalised Groups See Facts the Privileged Cannot

Harding's standpoint epistemology, part of feminist epistemology's examination of how a researcher's "situatedness" (gender, class, physical location) affects science, argues that marginalised groups have unique viewpoints that allow them to see facts invisible to the privileged.

### Scope & Conditions

Feminist epistemology's situatedness-of-the-knower cluster.

### Evidence

> "Standpoint epistemology argues that marginalized groups have unique viewpoints that allow them to see facts invisible to the privileged."

### Implications

- Locates epistemic advantage in social position rather than in method or logic, distinct from both rationalist and classical-empiricist accounts.
