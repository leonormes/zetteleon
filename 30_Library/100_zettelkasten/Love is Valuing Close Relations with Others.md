## Love is Valuing Close Relations with Others

Love is the strength of valuing close relations with others, especially those in which sharing and caring are returned. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It concerns closeness, where kindness can be shown to people you hardly know.

### Evidence

> "You value close and intimate relations with others."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 246) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what would keep this close relationship mutual.
