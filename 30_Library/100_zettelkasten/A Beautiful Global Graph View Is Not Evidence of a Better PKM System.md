## A Beautiful Global Graph View Is Not Evidence of a Better PKM System

Optimising the appearance or density of a vault's global graph view is a PKM anti-pattern, because a visually striking global graph is not itself evidence that the system improves retrieval, reasoning, or action.

### Scope & Conditions

Distinct from using a _local_ graph (depth one or two around a specific note) for genuine neighbourhood auditing, which remains useful.

### Evidence

> "Optimising the global graph: A beautiful global image is not evidence that the system improves retrieval, reasoning, or action."

### Implications

- Graph-view aesthetics should never be a design goal in themselves; local-graph neighbourhood checks and explicit queries are the actual tools for auditing whether the graph is working.
- This directly parallels the separately-documented risk of graph views inducing apophenia (perceiving meaningful connections between unrelated notes merely because software drew a line).
