## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Systems thinking analyses dynamic webs of cause and effect rather than static, isolated parts, showing that a system's structure and actual purpose—not the character of its actors—determine its behaviour over time. Because balancing and reinforcing feedback loops, time delays, and local rationality generate counter-intuitive dynamics such as overshoot and compensating feedback, quick fixes aimed at symptoms routinely fail. True leverage comes from shortening feedback delays, redesigning interconnections, and shifting system goals and mental models rather than tweaking surface parameters.
