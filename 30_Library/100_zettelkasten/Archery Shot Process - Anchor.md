## Archery Shot Process - Anchor

Summary:

Anchor is the final reference for the full draw position and the point of maximal consistency for an aiming reference point.

Details:

- Placement: Settle the drawing hand firmly along the jawbone. Move your hand slightly up to press the index finger firmly against your jawbone with no gaps.
- Posture Integrity: Maintain full front-shoulder extension (pushing forward) and solid core posture as you settle into the anchor. Do not let the front shoulder collapse inward.
- Reference Points (Skeletal): Ensure the index finger and bow shelf are aligned on the same level. Feel both contact points simultaneously to ensure vertical consistency.
- Reference Points (Soft Tissue / String): The string should touch the center (or slightly right) of your nose and slightly right on your chin.
