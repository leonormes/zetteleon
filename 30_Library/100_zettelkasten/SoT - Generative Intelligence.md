## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Generative Intelligence is the capacity to synthesise new paradigms of understanding rather than optimising within existing ones—corresponding to Thomas Kuhn's *Revolutionary Science* versus *Normal Science* (Applicative Intelligence). Because established paradigms are defended by institutional consensus, generative thinking requires cognitive independence to endure social resistance; ADHD novelty-seeking and hyperfocus provide natural fuel for dwelling in uncharted territory, provided that novelty drive is channelled rather than lost to system-hopping.

## 2. Argument Thread — Social Cost, Cognitive Independence & Neurodiversity

Because institutions and peer groups reward applicative alignment, challenging an entrenched paradigm carries a steep social cost. Generative thinkers cannot rely on social proof when the prevailing consensus is wrong:

![[Generative Thinking Requires Cognitive Independence to Endure Social Resistance]]

![[Paradigm Shifts Complete Through Generational Turnover, Not Persuasion]]

Neurodivergent cognitive profiles—particularly the ADHD interest-based nervous system—supply a structural advantage for this kind of work. Dopamine release on prediction error and sustained hyperfocus in ambiguous states allow the thinker to dwell in the "Groan Zone" long enough to birth a new framework:

## 4. Conclusions & Assumptions to Challenge

**Conclusion:** Generative breakthroughs depend less on raw optimisation speed than on the willingness to question foundational assumptions, tolerate social isolation until a viable replacement exists, and channel novelty-seeking into deep immersion rather than restless system-hopping.

**Assumptions to challenge** (if any proves false in practice, open a `HEAD - <question>?` tension against the underlying claim):

- *Hard bifurcation of modes:* Assumes framework synthesis and within-paradigm optimisation are distinct enough to require opposing cognitive traits (`[[Framework Synthesis Differs Fundamentally From Within-Paradigm Optimisation]]`).
- *Kuhnian single-paradigm lens:* Assumes Kuhn's normal-vs-revolutionary cycle captures scientific and personal paradigm shifts better than Lakatos's competing research programmes (`[[Lakatos's Research Programmes Replace Kuhn's Single Paradigm With Competing Cores]]`).
- *Net-positive ADHD novelty:* Assumes novelty-seeking and hyperfocus yield net generative output rather than chronic abandonment (`[[ADHD Novelty Seeking and Hyperfocus Fuel Generative Framework Synthesis]]` vs. `[[Claim - Novelty-craving drives self-defeating system-hopping]]`).
