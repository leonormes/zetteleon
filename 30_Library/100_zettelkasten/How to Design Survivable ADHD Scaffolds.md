## How to Design Survivable ADHD Scaffolds

Designing a survivable ADHD scaffold means shifting initiation, maintenance, and failure detection outside the user's daily executive budget. Apply eight design rules—prioritising deletion, push triggers, one-off setup, visible failure alarms, human activation, physical path cues, graceful degradation, and a 10% cap on architecting—so the system survives low-capacity weeks.

### Scope & Conditions

Use whenever introducing a new ProdOS workflow, automation, or habit support. If a proposed tool requires daily curation or streak upkeep, treat it as a Tier 2 pull aid rather than a Tier 1 floor.

### Evidence

Derived from the Eight Principles for Survivable ADHD Scaffolds in [[SoT - Prosthetic Executive Function]] (§4).

### Implications

- Eliminating a recurring obligation beats optimising a checklist for it.
- Explicit bad-day modes prevent a single low-energy day from collapsing the entire routine into a shame spiral.
