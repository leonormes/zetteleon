## The MAF Test Measures Aerobic Conditioning Progress

What: A regular assessment to measure how much work can be done at the maximum aerobic heart rate.

How:

1. Determine your MAF Heart Rate using the [[The MAF 180-Formula Calculates the Maximum Aerobic Heart Rate]].
2. Perform a standardized workout (e.g., running 3 miles on a track).
3. Maintain your heart rate precisely at the MAF limit throughout.
4. Record the time taken for each segment (e.g., each mile).
5. Compare results over time to see if speed is increasing at the same heart rate.

Failure modes:

- Exceeding the MAF heart rate during the test.
- Inconsistent testing conditions (terrain, weather, time of day).

Example: Running 3 miles where Mile 1 is 10:00, Mile 2 is 10:15, and Mile 3 is 10:30.
