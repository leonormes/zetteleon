## Executive Function Challenges are Central to ADHD

ADHD is not a simple deficit of attention capacity or knowledge, but a neurodevelopmental impairment of executive self-regulation across activation, focus regulation, effort, emotion, working memory, and action inhibition (Barkley and Brown models). Because those management circuits misfire under non-novel conditions, ADHD produces a **performance gap**: the individual knows what to do, yet cannot reliably execute it at the point of performance. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- **Applies to execution, not intellect:** The bottleneck sits in self-regulation at the point of performance ([[ADHD Impairs Execution at the Point of Performance Rather Than Knowledge]]), not in comprehension or intent.
- **Six impaired clusters (Brown/Barkley):** Activation (initiation/prioritising), Focus (zooming and shifting, not just distractibility), Effort (alertness and post-hyperfocus recovery in [[Executive Function Impairment During ADHD Trough Phase]]), Emotion (frustration modulation), Working Memory (holding task threads), and Action (inhibitory "brakes").
- **Not exclusive to ADHD:** Executive dysfunction also arises in autism, sleep deprivation, and degenerative disease ([[Executive Dysfunction Has Causes Beyond ADHD Including Autism and Degenerative Disease]]).

### Evidence

- **Catecholamine & PFC control:** Under-activated prefrontal cortex and reduced tonic dopamine availability impair importance signalling for non-novel tasks ([[Prefrontal Cortex Catecholamine Insufficiency Impairs Executive Control in ADHD]], [[Evidence - Volkow 2009 PET Imaging Shows Reduced Dopamine D2-D3 Receptor Availability in ADHD]], [[SoT - The Interest-Based Nervous System in ADHD]]).
- **Network switching:** Failure to suppress the Default Mode Network when engaging the Task-Positive Network disrupts sustained execution ([[The Glitchy Switch - TPN and DMN Dysregulation in ADHD]]).
- **Working-memory overload:** Fragile working memory causes mid-task thread loss and compulsive re-planning ([[ADHD Working Memory Deficits Create a Compulsive Re-Planning Loop]]). *(No direct Calibre book quotation attached yet.)*

### Implications

- **Prosthetics beat willpower:** If the deficit is point-of-performance self-regulation, adding more information fails; external scaffolds must carry initiation, state, and stopping ([[SoT - Prosthetic Executive Function]]).
- **Explains secondary traps:** Activation failure drives [[Executive Dysfunction - The Root of Analysis Paralysis]], while impaired self-monitoring produces [[Metacognitive Deficits in ADHD Create Practical Impairments]] (misreading neurological friction as laziness).
