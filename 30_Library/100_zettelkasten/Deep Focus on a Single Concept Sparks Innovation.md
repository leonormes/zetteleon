Focusing intently on a small, specific part of a larger topic can unlock novel ideas and insights. This process of "going deep" allows the mind to move beyond a surface-level understanding and discover new connections and possibilities that are missed during a broad, superficial examination.

This deep inquiry is a powerful method for building and refining the complex mental frameworks described in. It is a core component of the [[Thinking & Creating Skills (Your Clever Brain)]] and serves as a potent strategy for [[Problem-Solving Strategies and Heuristics of Expert Developers|expert-level problem-solving]].
