## Tidying and Reorganising Are Separate Tasks That Should Not Share a Session

Tidying returns an object to a place that already works, while reorganising redesigns where things live. Mixing them in one session invites a cascade, so keep tidying in the daily routine and give reorganising its own slot. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to routine resets in shared homes. The summary also treats decluttering and cleaning as distinct processes, but this card covers only the tidy versus reorganise split.

### Evidence

> "Tidying, organising, decluttering, and cleaning are distinct cognitive processes that should never be attempted simultaneously."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against a primary source)

The chat credits the 5 Things Tidying Method. I have not checked it, so confidence is low.

### Implications

- A daily check-in should never include "sort out the cupboard".
- Reorganising becomes a deliberate project that can be postponed without stopping the household.
