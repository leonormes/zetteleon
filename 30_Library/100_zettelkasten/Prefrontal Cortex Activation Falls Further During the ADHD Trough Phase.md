## Prefrontal Cortex Activation Falls Further During the ADHD Trough Phase

The prefrontal cortex is already underactivated in ADHD, and during the trough it becomes even less responsive. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the trough phase after hyperfixation. This is a claim about responsiveness of the prefrontal cortex, and the note does not say how long it lasts.

### Implications

- Executive control has less neural support exactly when accumulated tasks need it most.
