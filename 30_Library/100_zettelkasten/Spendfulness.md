## Spendfulness

Spendfulness is a state of psychological agency in which spending decisions are fully aligned with personal values. It treats money as stored labour waiting to be put to work and not as a scoreboard for status. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to decisions about how to spend. It is about alignment and permission, not restriction.

### Evidence

Carried over from the earlier prose version of [[SoT - Financial Philosophy and Spendfulness]]; no outside source is cited.

### Implications

- A budget serves as a tool for permission rather than deprivation.
