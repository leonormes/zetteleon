## Cognitive Firewalls

Cognitive firewalls are established by rigidly separating "deep work" (high-value cognition) from "shallow work" (administrative maintenance). This structural separation prevents digital tool distractions and low-value task arrivals from infecting core high-effort cognition, thereby preserving the mental energy required for significant advancement.

### Scope & Conditions

Personal or organisational workflow structuring aimed at protecting cognitive resources.

### Evidence

> "Rigidly separating 'deep work' (high-value cognition) from 'shallow work' (administrative maintenance) to prevent tool-driven distractions from infecting core tasks."

### Implications

- Preserves dedicated time for tasks requiring high mental effort and uninterrupted concentration.
- Reduces the significant cognitive cost of context switching between disparate task types (e.g., coding vs. emailing).
