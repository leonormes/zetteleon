## Reactive Spending Creates Financial Stress

Reactive spending, the "spend first, categorise later" approach, is a primary source of financial anxiety. Without a proactive plan, every purchase requires complex mental arithmetic against a single current account balance, which creates constant, low-grade stress. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to spending from a single account balance with no plan that assigns money in advance.

### Evidence

No source or quotation is attached; the claim rests on the reasoning given here.

### Implications

- The fundamental scarcity of money is hidden, which prevents clear decisions.
- The method shows only where money went, not where it is going.
- Aligning spending with long-term goals becomes impossible.
