## Reviewing Notes and Their Links Forces Re-engagement With the Ideas

Reviewing notes and the links between them forces the brain to re-engage with the ideas, which is where the real value of a PKM system lies.

### Scope & Conditions

Presented as the main source of value in a knowledge system, rather than capture or storage.

### Evidence

> "The review of notes, and the links between them, is where the real value of a PKM system is found. The review forces the brain to re-engage with the ideas, and to re-evaluate the links between them."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 49).

### Implications

- The source locates a PKM system's real value in review rather than capture or storage (source line 49).
- Review is also the point at which the links between notes are re-evaluated (source line 49).
