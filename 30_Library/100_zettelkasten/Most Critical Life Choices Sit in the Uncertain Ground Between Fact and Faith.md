## Most Critical Life Choices Sit in the Uncertain Ground Between Fact and Faith

Career, relationship, entrepreneurial and health decisions fall in the uncertain middle ground between fact and faith, where neither certainty nor pure conviction is available. In that ground a working belief, not a verified fact, is the basis for action.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to decisions that cannot be settled by verifiable evidence in advance. It does not claim that every decision is uncertain.

### Evidence

> "Most of life's critical choices—career paths, relationship decisions, entrepreneurial ventures, and health strategies—exist in the uncertain middle ground between fact and faith."
> (Nir Eyal, Beyond Belief, via an LLM summary; chapter and page not captured)

### Implications

- Belief, not fact, is the working basis for most consequential decisions.
- Explains why the quality of the beliefs one holds matters for action.
