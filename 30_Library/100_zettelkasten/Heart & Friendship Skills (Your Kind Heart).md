_These strengths are all about how you connect with other people and show you care._

- [[Kindness is Doing Favors and Good Deeds for Others|Kindness]]: Being generous, compassionate, and doing good things for others just to make them happy. (VIA Strength)
- [[Love is Valuing Close Relations with Others|Love]]: Valuing your close relationships with family and friends and showing them you care. (VIA Strength)
- [[Teamwork is Working Well as a Member of a Group|Teamwork]]: Being a good citizen, working well in a group, and doing your part to help the team succeed. (VIA Strength & PSHE)
- [[Fairness is Treating All People the Same|Fairness]]: Treating everyone equally and with justice; wanting everyone to have a fair chance. (VIA Strength)
- [[Leadership is Encouraging a Group to Get Things Done|Leadership]]: Helping to organise a group to get something done and encouraging others along the way. (VIA Strength)
- [[Social Intelligence is Being Aware of Motives and Feelings|Social Intelligence]]: Understanding your own feelings and the feelings of others. Knowing what makes people "tick." (VIA Strength & PSHE)
- [[Forgiveness is Accepting Shortcomings and Giving Second Chances|Forgiveness]]: Giving people a second chance and not holding grudges when they make a mistake. (VIA Strength)
