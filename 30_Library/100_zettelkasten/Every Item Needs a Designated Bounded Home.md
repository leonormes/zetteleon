## Every Item Needs a Designated Bounded Home

Every item requires a designated, permanent, and bounded dwelling tailored to its specific size and granularity.

### Scope & Conditions

Physical tool storage and inventory management.

### Evidence

> "Every item requires a designated, permanent, and bounded dwelling tailored to the granularity of the object"

### Implications

- Prevents loose accumulation of clutter on surfaces.
- Reduces the cognitive load and friction of searching for tools.
