## Resilience Can Be Taught Rather Than Being Only a Fixed Trait

Resilience can be strengthened by teaching and so is not only a fixed trait. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- The quotation is limited to young civilians.

### Evidence

> "Resilience, at least among young civilians, can be taught."
> (Seligman 2011, ch. 7, PDF p. 130) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Chapter%207:%20Army%20Strong:%20Comprehensive%20Soldier%20Fitness)

Seligman states this about positive education programmes. Extending it to adults and to anyone is untested here.

### Falsifier

- If well-controlled resilience training produces no lasting difference in recovery from setbacks, the claim is wrong.
