## Working One Category at a Time Across the Whole Room Reduces Tidying to One Yes or No Question at a Time

When you sweep the whole room for one category at a time, each step asks only whether an item is of that kind. That replaces a scan of every object and a choice about each one. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to routine room resets by people who stall on open-ended tidying. It assumes the person can tell the five categories apart quickly.

### Evidence

> "your brain only has to answer one low-bandwidth question at a time"
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Davis's own book)

### Implications

- Never mix categories in one sweep, because a mixed sweep brings the full scan back.
- The question can be asked aloud by a child or partner, such as whether something is rubbish.
