## Collective Moral Licensing in Partisan Politics

Voters who identify as the moral resistance to a perceived extreme threat acquire a collective sense of moral superiority ("moral credentials") that then licenses them to tolerate lies, manipulation, or corruption from their own representatives that they would otherwise condemn.

### Scope & Conditions

An application of individual-level moral licensing (where a past good deed licenses a later ethical lapse) at the collective, partisan-identity level. Documented as providing cover even for "apex corruption"—malfeasance at the highest levels of government.

### Evidence

> "Because the voters feel their overarching goal—defeating the extreme leader—is indisputably righteous, they extend moral licensing to their own politicians, tolerating lies, manipulation, clientelism, or hypocrisy that they would otherwise fiercely condemn."

### Implications

- Moral self-certainty about the righteousness of a cause can itself become the mechanism that permits corruption within that cause.

### Related

- [[SoT - Bonhoeffer's Theory of Functional Stupidity]]—shared mechanism: both describe a collective surrender of independent moral scrutiny to a group cause—Bonhoeffer's frame is submission to power, this atom's is self-granted licence from perceived righteousness, but both end in the same suppressed accountability.
