The Silent Void is a communication pattern where a partner consistently fails to engage with, remember, or revisit valid concerns raised by the other. This absence of engagement is not a "lack of response" but an active signal regarding the division of emotional labour.

## 🧩 Logic of the Silent Void

When a partner goes silent after a valid point and never returns to the topic, they are communicating: "This issue is your problem, not our problem."

## 🧠 Meaning

- Mental Load Relegation: The partner says (through actions): "I will respond if forced to, but I will not carry any of this mentally between conversations."
- De-prioritisation: Concerns exit the partner's mind the moment the conversation ends, indicating that those concerns do not warrant sustained mental energy.

## 🧪 The Diagnostic (Acid Test)

Does the partner follow up on things that matter to _them_ (work stress, family plans, personal needs)?

- If Yes: They possess the cognitive capacity to remember; they are choosing not to apply it to your concerns.
- If No: There may be a broader cognitive or memory issue unrelated to the relationship.
