## 5 Things Tidying Method

The 5 Things Tidying Method is a room-reset method, attributed to KC Davis, that treats any messy room as five categories, cleared one at a time while you stay in the room. The five are rubbish, dishes, laundry, things with a place and things without a place. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

Used here as a planning aid for routine room resets, especially for people with executive-function difficulties. The attribution and the description come from an AI chat summary, and the source book, *How to Keep House While Drowning*, is not in the Calibre library, so treat the details as unchecked.

### Evidence

> "no matter how chaotic or catastrophic a room looks, there are only five things in it"
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Davis's own book)

### Implications

- The method gives a fixed order, so nobody has to decide where to start.
- It stops at gathering for dishes, laundry and unhomed items, and leaves the processing for later.
