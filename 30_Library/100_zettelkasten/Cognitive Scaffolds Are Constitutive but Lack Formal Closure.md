## Cognitive Scaffolds Are Constitutive but Lack Formal Closure

The claim that a cognitive scaffold is the faculty it supports has a different epistemic status from the claim that a type is a proof. Types have proof machinery, data models have relational algebra and infrastructure has state reconciliation engines, whereas a cognitive scaffold has no equivalent formal closure. The constitutive reading is defensible through extended mind theory but is not formally checked.

### Scope & Conditions

- This is a qualification of [[An External Scaffold Is the Executive Function for an ADHD System and Not an Aid to It]]. It does not reject it.
- "Formal closure" here means a mechanism that can decide whether the structure and the state agree. The other three domains have one and the scaffold does not.
- The note title was proposed by the SoT itself, in its own Tensions section.

### Evidence

> "Types have formal proof machinery. CDMs have relational algebra. IaC has state reconciliation engines. Cognitive scaffolds have none of these. The claim that "the scaffold IS the executive function" is philosophically defensible (via extended mind theory) but has a different epistemic status than "the type IS the proof.""
> ([[SoT - Structure is Truth is a Unifying Axiom Across Formal Systems]], §7 Tensions & Gaps, T2, emphasis markers omitted)

### Implications

- A scaffold can drift from the person's real needs without anything flagging it, which the infrastructure case avoids with a reconciliation engine. Regular review is the manual substitute (inferred).
- It limits how far lessons transfer from software to the cognitive row of the cross-domain claim.
