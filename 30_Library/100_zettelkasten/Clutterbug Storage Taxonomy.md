## Clutterbug Storage Taxonomy

The Clutterbug storage taxonomy is a model, attributed to Cassandra Aarssen, that places a person on two axes, visual versus hidden storage and macro versus micro sorting, which gives four profiles called Butterfly, Bee, Ladybug and Cricket. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

Used here as a planning aid for choosing storage styles, not as a validated psychological typology. The profile names and their mapping come from an AI chat summary, so treat them as unchecked.

### Evidence

> "two axes: **Visual Preference** (Visual vs Hidden) and **Organizational Granularity** (Macro vs Micro)"
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Aarssen's own work)

### Implications

- Butterfly is visual and macro, so open bins and drop hooks suit it.
- Bee is visual and micro, so clear stacking units and pegboards suit it.
- Ladybug is hidden and macro, so deep opaque baskets and pull-out drawers suit it.
- Cricket is hidden and micro, so sub-divided drawers and labelled boxes suit it.
