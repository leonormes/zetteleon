## Human Accountability Outperforms Solo Digital Tools for ADHD Task Activation

When task initiation and emotional regulation are the bottlenecks, scheduled human scaffolding outperforms solo digital notifications or auto-schedulers. Randomised trials of adult ADHD smartphone organisation tools succeeded specifically when paired with human coaching, while CBT/MCT coaching trials show strong adult efficacy and unguided digital backlogs often worsen shame.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to high-friction initiation, prolonged ambiguous work, and shame-prone tasks where solo app nudges are easily dismissed. Routine, low-friction automated obligations (such as direct debits) can run via software without human involvement.

### Evidence

This claim synthesises §1.3 and §3 of [[SoT - Prosthetic Executive Function]], citing Moëll et al. (2015) on the coaching confound in *Living SMART*, Safren et al. (2010) and Solanto et al. (2010) CBT/MCT trials, and [[Body Doubling for ADHD Focus]].

### Implications

- When solo productivity apps repeatedly fail to trigger work, replace self-nudges with scheduled co-working, pair programming, or coaching check-ins.
- Evaluate digital ADHD tool trials carefully to separate software effects from the human accountability built into the study protocol.
