## Intentionality

Intentionality is the shift from living on reactive autopilot (model-free habit execution) to acting with deliberate, value-aligned purpose (model-based choice). Unlike future-focused goals that name a destination, intentions govern how you show up in the present moment—combining "heart" and "direction."

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to conscious self-direction across daily work, attention management, and long-term life design. Automated habits remain essential for efficiency; intentionality supplies the steering layer above them.

### Evidence

This concept is defined in [[SoT - Intentional Living]], drawing on the Japanese kanji for intention ("Heart" and "Direction") and the ship-at-sea hierarchy in [[The Captain on the Bridge - The Intentionality Analogy]].

### Implications

- Goals give direction, whereas intentions determine the felt quality of life while pursuing them.
- Without active intentionality, strong habits default to unexamined past patterns regardless of stated ambitions.
