### Minimize Access Friction with One-Motion Storage

Frequently used supplies should be accessible in a single physical motion without digging, unstacking, or moving other objects.

#### Scope & Conditions

High-priority, frequently accessed tools and consumables in any workspace.

#### Evidence

> "Minimise friction and barriers to accessing frequently used supplies"

#### Implications

- Lowers the activation cost of a task.
- Prevents flow interruption during deep work by keeping tools instantly ready.
