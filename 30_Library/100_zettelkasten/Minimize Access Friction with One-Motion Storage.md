## Minimize Access Friction with One-Motion Storage

Frequently used supplies should be accessible in a single physical motion without digging, unstacking, or moving other objects. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

High-priority, frequently accessed tools and consumables in any workspace or shared household zone (L1/L2 storage).

### Evidence

> "Minimise friction and barriers to accessing frequently used supplies"

### Implications

- Lowers the activation cost of a task and putting items away.
- Prevents flow interruption during deep work or household resets by keeping daily tools instantly ready.
