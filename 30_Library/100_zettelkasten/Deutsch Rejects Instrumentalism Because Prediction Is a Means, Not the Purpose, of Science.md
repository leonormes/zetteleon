## Deutsch Rejects Instrumentalism Because Prediction Is a Means, Not the Purpose, of Science

Deutsch explicitly warns against "instrumentalism" (the view that science only aims to predict outcomes). He argues that prediction is not the purpose of science; prediction is merely a means to achieve the real purpose, which is to explain the fabric of reality.

### Scope & Conditions

A normative claim about the goal of the discipline of science, not a claim about how any single theory should be tested.

### Evidence

> "He argues that prediction is not the purpose of science; prediction is merely a means to achieve the real purpose, which is to explain the fabric of reality."

### Implications

- Supplies an explicit argument for the realist side of the instrumentalism/realism distinction.
- Sets up a scope-limited tension with accounts that treat output reliability alone as sufficient verification of a theory.
