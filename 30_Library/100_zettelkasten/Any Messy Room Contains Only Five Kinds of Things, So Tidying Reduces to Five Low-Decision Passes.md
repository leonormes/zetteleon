## Any Messy Room Contains Only Five Kinds of Things, So Tidying Reduces to Five Low-Decision Passes

However chaotic a room looks, its contents fall into five kinds: rubbish, dishes, laundry, things with a place and things without one. Running tidying as five category passes replaces hundreds of item-level decisions with five category-level ones. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to ordinary living spaces such as lounges and bedrooms. Specialist items such as medicines or paperwork may need handling outside the five.

### Evidence

> "no matter how chaotic or catastrophic a room looks, there are only five things in it"
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Davis's own book)

### Implications

- Start a reset by naming the five passes, not by scanning the whole room.
- A person can stop after any pass and still have made progress.

### Falsifier

- If many ordinary room contents fitted none of the five kinds, the five-pass structure would not cover a messy room.
