## Evidence - Kahneman Says Confidence Tracks the Quality of the Story Not the Amount of Evidence

> The confidence that individuals have in their beliefs depends mostly on the quality of the story they can tell about what they see, even if they see little.

### What it Supports

The principle behind [[Felt significance of thoughts can mislead about their substance]]: an inner feeling about a thought (here, confidence) follows how coherent the story feels, not how much or how good the evidence is.

### What it Does not Show

It is about confidence in beliefs, not about felt importance or profundity, so it supports the general point that metacognitive feelings do not track validity, not the specific significance-substance gap.
