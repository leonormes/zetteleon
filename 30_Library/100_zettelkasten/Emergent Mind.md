The worry that prompted this, that a wrong instinct proves instincts cannot be trusted, now has its own note: [[One Wrong Instinct Read as Proof That Instincts Cannot Be Trusted Feeds a Worry Loop]].

These ideas and thoughts are emergent properties of my subconscious. I can't control them as that would not be emergent. What I can try to control is the input that creates the subconscious. This generator of ideas.

## Tensions

- [[Decoupling Ego from Outcomes to Improve Decisions]]—_The split-out worry note treats each wrong instinct as proof that instincts cannot be trusted. That note separates self-worth from a single outcome and treats decisions as bets. Both can hold: instincts do not have to be trusted blindly, and one miss is not a verdict._
