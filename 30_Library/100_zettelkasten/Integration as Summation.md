## Integration as Summation

Integration is the process of summing infinitely many infinitesimal components to calculate a total value. This process of accumulation allows for the reconstruction of total quantities from rate-of-change data.

### Scope & Conditions

Core mechanism of integral calculus.

### Evidence

> "Accumulation is the mathematical process of adding up infinitely many tiny pieces to calculate a complete total."

### Implications

- Calculates the exact area between a curve and the x-axis.
- Facilitates the determination of displacement, volume, and other accumulated values in scientific models.
