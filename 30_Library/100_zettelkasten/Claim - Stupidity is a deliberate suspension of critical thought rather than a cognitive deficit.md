## Minimum Viable Understanding
Stupidity, as defined by Dietrich Bonhoeffer, is a more insidious threat than overt malice because it represents an active surrender of cognitive and moral reasoning to external systems or group identities. It relies on emotion and status rather than logic, rendering rational debate ineffective.

## Core Mechanisms
1. **Outsourcing Critical Thinking:** Surrendering cognitive and moral reasoning to external systems, such as algorithms, artificial intelligence, or influencers.
2. **Willful Ignorance (Amathia):** An active resistance to learning, characterised by an anti-intellectual celebration of not knowing and a hostility towards expertise.
3. **Conformity and Indoctrination:** Tethering one's beliefs entirely to group identity. When belief becomes identity, individuals close themselves off to alternative perspectives and view logical counter-arguments as personal attacks or tests of loyalty.

## Epistemic Trespassing
A related cognitive bias is "epistemic trespassing," where highly intelligent subject-matter experts overreach into domains they do not understand due to intellectual pride and an overestimation of transferable skills (closely related to the halo effect and the Dunning-Kruger effect).

## Practical Countermeasures
- Seeking falsifying evidence for one's own beliefs (referencing Karl Popper's principle of falsification).
- Employing Socratic questioning in interpersonal debates.
- Institutionalising dissent to prevent the delegitimisation of political opposition (as noted in Levitsky and Ziblatt’s *How Democracies Die*).
