## The ADHD Trough Phase Breaks Down Planning and Makes Minor Decisions Overwhelming

During the trough a person cannot organise their thoughts or plan next steps, and even minor decisions feel overwhelming. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Describes planning and decision-making in the trough, as reported in the source note. Where decisions are routine or already made, the effect may be smaller.

### Implications

- Reducing the number of decisions in the trough, for example by preparing choices in advance, targets a real bottleneck.
