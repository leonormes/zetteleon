## Merton's Reward System Drives Scientific Norms

Merton's sociology of science highlights the reward system—the race for credit and publication—as the motor that drives scientific norms.

### Scope & Conditions

A sociological (rather than logical or historical) account of why science operates the way it does.

### Evidence
