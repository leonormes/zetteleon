## Time Awareness Is Poor During the ADHD Trough Phase

During the trough a person loses reliable awareness of time, so tasks seem impossible to schedule. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Describes time management in the trough, as reported in the source note. It is distinct from the time blindness that hyperfixation itself creates.

### Implications

- Scheduling is unreliable in the trough, so external reminders matter more than internal estimates.

- [[Hyperfixation Creates Time Blindness in ADHD]]—tension, not contradiction: that note covers time blindness during hyperfixation, this one covers the trough after it.
