## Self-Change Refutes the Determinism of the Plant Metaphor

Summary: Taken literally, plants cannot replant themselves — yet a person deliberately rebuilding their worldview at 52 is doing exactly that, so the metaphor's compassion is right and its determinism is wrong.

Details: The plant model explains how beliefs grow from genes and environment, which grounds compassion. But if it were fully deterministic, no one could step outside their own conditioning to revise it — and the act of adopting the model is itself such a revision. The fact that you are changing is simultaneously the refutation of the metaphor's hard determinism and the encouragement that change is possible. Use the metaphor for diagnosis of others and self, never as an excuse that growth is fixed.
