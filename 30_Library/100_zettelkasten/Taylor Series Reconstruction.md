## Taylor Series Reconstruction

Taylor series reconstruct smooth functions using only derivatives calculated at a single point. This high-level application of differential calculus allows for the representation of complex functions as infinite sums of terms.

### Scope & Conditions

Used for the local analysis and approximation of non-linear functions.

### Evidence

> "…Taylor series, which can completely reconstruct a smooth function using only its derivatives at a single point."

### Implications

- Enables the accurate approximation of complex functions in physics and engineering.
- Simplifies the analysis of non-linear systems by treating them as polynomial series.
