## Types of Non-Linguistic Knowledge

Knowledge that is gained or held without being reducible to language falls into several distinct categories:

- **Experiential knowledge**: Knowing "what it is like" to have certain experiences.
- **Embodied knowledge**: Knowledge stored in bodily habits and skills.
- **Tacit knowledge**: Knowledge that we use but cannot easily articulate.
- **Aesthetic knowledge**: Understanding gained through artistic appreciation.
- **Spiritual or mystical knowledge**: Insights claimed to transcend conceptual understanding.

If language shapes our thinking, it may also limit our access to these forms of knowledge — experiences like love, awe, or aesthetic appreciation may not be fully capturable through language at all, which challenges traditional epistemological frameworks that privilege propositional knowledge.
