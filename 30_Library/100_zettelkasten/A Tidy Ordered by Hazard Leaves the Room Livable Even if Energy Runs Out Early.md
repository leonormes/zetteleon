## A Tidy Ordered by Hazard Leaves the Room Livable Even if Energy Runs Out Early

Ordering a tidy so the sanitary hazards go first means each stopping point is acceptable. Stopping after rubbish or dishes still leaves a livable room, without shame. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the five-pass order of rubbish, dishes, laundry, homed items and unhomed items. A partial finish only counts as acceptable if the hazards were removed first.

### Evidence

> "Even if your energy runs out after Step 1 or Step 2, you have removed sanitary hazards, cleared walkways, and made the room livable without shame."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Davis's own book)

### Implications

- A partial reset is a success, so the family should say so aloud.
- The order of the passes is part of the design, not a preference.
