> [!NOTE] Synthesis
> This strategy is a core component of the 4th Law (Make It Satisfying) and Identity Evidence.
>
> - [[SoT - The Four Laws of Behavior Change#6. Positive Reinforcement Strategy: Small Wins]]
> - [[SoT - Identity-Based Habit Formation#G. Momentum Building (The Small Win)]]

Building and maintaining a routine requires motivation, which can be cultivated through positive reinforcement. Celebrating small successes helps to create a positive feedback loop that strengthens the desired behavior.

When you successfully follow a part of your routine, especially a new or difficult part, acknowledge the achievement. This can be as simple as a mental "good job" or a small, tangible reward (see [[Dopamine Management in ADHD Productivity]]). This practice helps to associate the routine with positive feelings, which in turn builds the intrinsic motivation needed to stick with it over the long term. It shifts the focus from perfection to progress (see [[Kaizen Method Uses Micro-Victories to Build Momentum]]).
