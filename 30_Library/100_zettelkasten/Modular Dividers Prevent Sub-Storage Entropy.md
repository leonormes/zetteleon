## Modular Dividers Prevent Sub-Storage Entropy

Deep or wide storage spaces require modular interlocking dividers to prevent items from sliding into disorganization.

### Scope & Conditions

Flat files, deep drawers, large bins, or any unconstrained storage volume.

### Evidence

> "modular CAD-designed interlocking dividers subdivide drawers into manageable quadrants"

### Implications

- Maintains the strict granularity of an object's bounded home even within larger storage units.
- Prevents the natural entropy of items shifting during movement.
