## Clicker Reaction Time

Elite archers demonstrate a clicker reaction time of approximately 100 milliseconds indicating a subconscious reflex, whereas novices exhibit a 200 to 300 millisecond delay resulting from conscious processing and anticipation.

### Scope & Conditions

Pertains to the neurological processing of the clicker's auditory cue in recurve archery.

### Evidence

> "elite archers demonstrate a clicker reaction time of approximately 100 milliseconds… Novice archers demonstrate a delay of 200 to 300 milliseconds"

### Implications

- The release trigger must be moved from conscious thought to a subconscious reflex to avoid anticipation and muscular collapse.
