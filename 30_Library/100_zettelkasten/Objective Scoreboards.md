## Objective Scoreboards

Professional efficacy should be measured by tracking primary, tangible outcomes—such as features shipped, papers published, or bugs resolved—rather than activity levels or digital engagement. Establishing an "objective scoreboard" decouples performance evaluation from performative busyness and aligns organizational incentives with actual value creation.

### Scope & Conditions

Remediation strategy for administrative bloat and pseudo-productivity in knowledge work.

### Evidence

> "Measuring primary outcomes (e.g., papers published, features shipped) rather than activity levels."

### Implications

- Decouples performance reviews from Slack or Email responsiveness, allowing for longer periods of focused work.
- Provides clear, unambiguous criteria for success that are independent of digital "noise."
