## How to Run the 5 Things Tidy in One Room

Run five category passes in one room, collecting only and never processing, and stop at whichever pass your energy allows. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to one room at a time. Stopping early is acceptable if the rubbish and dishes passes are done first.

### Evidence

> "tackle only one category at a time across the entire space"
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Davis's own book)

### Implications

1. Pick one room and stay in it until the last step is done.
2. Collect rubbish, packaging and broken items into a bag and leave the bag in the room.
3. Gather dirty dishes and move them to the sink or stack them at the side without washing.
4. Gather clothes, towels and textiles into a hamper without sorting or washing.
5. Return the items that already have a place to their places.
6. Put everything left without a place into one basket.
