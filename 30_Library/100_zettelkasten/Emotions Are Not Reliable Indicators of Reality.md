It is essential to recognize that emotions are not always reliable indicators of reality and to evaluate situations objectively, avoiding the tendency to resort to negative thinking. The same stance appears in Morita Therapy's idea of arugamama, accepting a feeling as it is while directing behaviour by the facts: [[Morita Therapy - Arugamama is Accepting Reality As It Is]].

## Scope and Limits

- The claim is about emotions as evidence of external facts, not about emotions as experiences. A feeling is real as a feeling; it is weak evidence that a situation is as bad, or as good, as it feels. See [[Your emotions are real but they are your responsibility]].
- Not trusting a feeling as evidence does not mean suppressing it. Acceptance of the feeling and checking of the facts go together.
