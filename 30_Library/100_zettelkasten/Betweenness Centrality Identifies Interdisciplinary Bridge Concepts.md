## Betweenness Centrality Identifies Interdisciplinary Bridge Concepts

Betweenness centrality is a graph metric, introduced by Linton Freeman, that measures the extent to which a node lies on the shortest paths connecting other nodes in a network; a node with high betweenness centrality need not belong to any single dense cluster but instead acts as a critical bridge connecting otherwise separate communities.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to an already-constructed network graph of notes/concepts where edges represent explicit or co-occurrence relationships.

### Evidence

> "Nodes with high betweenness centrality do not necessarily belong to a single dense cluster. Instead, they act as critical bridges or junctions connecting entirely different communities."

### Implications

- In a personal interest graph, the concept with highest betweenness centrality is a strong empirical candidate for "the foundational interdisciplinary axiom" the user is looking for—it can be computed rather than guessed.
