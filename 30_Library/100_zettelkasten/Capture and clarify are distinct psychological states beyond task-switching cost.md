## Capture and clarify are distinct psychological states beyond task-switching cost

Gathering ideas and evaluating them engage distinct psychological stances whose mutual interference exceeds ordinary task-switching and working-memory overhead. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- **Falsification:** If observed interference between capture and clarification disappears once ordinary task-switching cost and working-memory load are controlled, this stronger claim should be dropped in favour of a simple queue-boundary explanation.

### Evidence

- **Unsourced hypothesis:** No experimental evidence distinguishing "incompatible psychological states" from generic switching cost is attached yet. *(No Calibre book quotation attached yet.)*

### Implications

- Unless corroborated, practical workflow design only needs the weaker behavioural rule in [[Evaluating While Collecting Ideas Reduces What Gets Collected]].
