## Narrative Identity is the Story We Construct About Our Experiences

Personal identity functions as an editable narrative that organises past events, present choices, and future intentions into a coherent trajectory without presupposing a fixed essence. Because narratives are revisable, reframing past setbacks or foregrounding new themes preserves factual honesty while changing the meaning and direction of experience. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to psychological and philosophical models of personal continuity under uncertainty, where identity is maintained through interpretive coherence rather than metaphysical substance.

### Evidence

Conceptual synthesis in narrative psychology and constructed-self epistemology grounded in [[The Self is Constructed Through Curation of Influences]] and [[Ship of Theseus as Model for Constructed Self]]; no direct external quotation is attached (`evidence_links: []`).

### Implications

- Treat personal history as an editable working interpretation rather than a fixed verdict on character.
- Re-anchor self-narrative around chosen values ([[Values Serve as Navigational Compass in Absence of Absolute Truth]]) when old identity labels fail.
