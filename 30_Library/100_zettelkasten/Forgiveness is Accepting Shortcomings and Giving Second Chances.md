## Forgiveness is Accepting Shortcomings and Giving Second Chances

Forgiveness is the strength of forgiving those who have done wrong, accepting their shortcomings and giving a second chance without vengeance. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It is also called mercy.

### Evidence

> "You forgive those who have done you wrong. You always give people a second chance."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 257) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

This vault files it under Temperance, while Seligman lists it under Transcendence.

### Implications

- Ask what giving a second chance would require without seeking to get even.
