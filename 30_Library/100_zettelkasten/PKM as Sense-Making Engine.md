## PKM as Sense-Making Engine

Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) should function as an engine for sense-making and tracking the evolution of opinions rather than acting as a static encyclopaedia. In an age of instant AI retrieval, the value of a PKM lies in its ability to navigate grey areas and manage "Personal Certainty" by prioritizing the "why" behind beliefs.

### Scope & Conditions

Redefines the role of personal notes in a world where LLMs can provide factual summaries instantly.

### Evidence

> "A modern PKM shouldn't be an encyclopaedia; it should be an engine for sense-making. It is the place where you track how your opinions evolve…"

### Implications

- The focus of note-taking shifts from storage to the active mapping of one's own understanding and doubt.
- A successful PKM record documents the trail of thought and the evidence that led to a specific conclusion.
