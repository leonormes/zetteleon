## Outcome-Goal Systems Cause Motivational Decay - Process Milestones Sustain Engagement

A system that places all psychological reward at the terminus of a project reliably depletes engagement across the middle passage, because it generates no reward-prediction-error signal until the very end. Redirecting reward toward process milestones, visible next actions, and implementation-intention triggers generates prediction errors at higher frequency and sustains motivation.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to project and system design for long-horizon work, especially where the working population has reduced tolerance for delayed reward.

### Evidence

> "A system that places all psychological reward at the terminus of a project reliably depletes engagement across the middle passage. Empirically supported interventions redirect the reward signal toward process milestones, visible next actions, and implementation intention triggers."

### Implications

- Decompose long projects into milestones frequent enough to keep generating dopaminergic prediction-error events.
- Treat "reward at the end only" as a design smell in any personal productivity system.
