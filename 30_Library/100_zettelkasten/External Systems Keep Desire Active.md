## External Systems Keep Desire Active

External algorithmic, commercial, and organisational systems continuously re-trigger desire so that wanting does not naturally subside after a goal is met. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to environments engineered for engagement, consumption, or expanding output—specifically algorithmic social media feeds, variable-ratio notification loops, and low-friction obligation networks. Less applicable where hard environmental boundaries or sobriety-style constraints block external triggers.

### Evidence

Synthesised from vault claims showing that algorithmic feeds operate as engineered variable-ratio dopamine delivery mechanisms and that efficiency gains expand perceived obligation surface area rather than creating rest.

### Implications

- Desire cannot be reliably quieted by willpower alone while the surrounding environment actively injects variable-ratio cues.
- Reusable as a demand-side premise in arguments about attention, digital minimalism, consumerism, and hedonic treadmills.

### Falsifier

- Evidence that algorithmic feeds and frictionless digital workflows reduce or leave unchanged baseline craving frequency compared to unengineered environments.
