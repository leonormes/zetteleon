## How to Test a Self-Insight That Prescribes Planning

When a self-insight resolves into a recommendation to plan more carefully, run a discriminating test and one bounded physical execution before acting on the prescription. Behaviour, not subjective conviction, decides whether the insight was tracking a real structural bottleneck or functioning as avoidance.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to GTD and task-execution contexts where a physical action with an external deadline is being deferred, and none of the four planning conditions hold (irreversible first move, undefined outcome, dependency on others, or material branching). Does not apply to PKM or learning work governed by [[SoT - Processing IS the Work]], where cognitive engagement is the deliverable.

### Evidence

Derived from the worked episode in [[Evidence - 29 August GTD Context-Reload Insight Deferred Urgent Azure Migration]] and the rationale in [[Self-insights that prescribe more planning are the least trustworthy kind]].

### Implications

- **Discriminating test**: Ask from memory whether the explanation varies with what is being avoided. A different sophisticated reason each time indicates post-hoc decoration; an explanation that attaches to one class of task and conspicuously not others is probably tracking something real.
- **Separation of artefacts**: Keep the insight in the vault and put the next physical action in Todoist—they do not get to be the same object.
- **Execution gate**: Do not act on a planning prescription until one bounded execution has been run.
