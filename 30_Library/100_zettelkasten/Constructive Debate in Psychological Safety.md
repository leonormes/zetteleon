Constructive debate requires true psychological safety, which enables:

- **Challenging ideas (not people)**: Rigorous evaluation of propositions without attacking human worth.
- **Disagreeing while respecting**: Using conflict as an error-detection mechanism rather than a threat.
- **Resolving over suppressing conflict**: Ensuring unspoken tensions don't breed resentment.

## Key Practices

- **Separating ego from ideas**: See [[Decoupling the person from the proposition allows for rigorous idea evaluation without attacking human worth]].
%%[extends:: [[Decoupling the person from the proposition allows for rigorous idea evaluation without attacking human worth]]]%%
- **Explicit conflict resolution norms**: Adhering to the [[SoT - Framework for Healthy Communication]].
%%[implements:: [[SoT - Framework for Healthy Communication]]]%%
- **Right to dissent**: Ensuring all participants can say no without fear of retaliation, as detailed in [[A True Discussion Requires That Both Partners Can Say No Without Fear]].
