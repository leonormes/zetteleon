## Shared Biological Architecture Enables Communication

Because humans share the same basic neural architecture, they process perceptual, emotional and spatial patterns in broadly similar ways, giving communication a common foundation.

### Scope & Conditions

Concerns basic perception, emotion and spatial processing; it does not claim that cultural expression is shared. Holds "despite individual differences".

### Evidence

> "Humans share a common neurological structure. Our brains process certain patterns similarly because we have evolved the same basic neural architecture."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 119).

### Implications

- Shared biology supplies a floor of mutual understanding before any language is involved.
- The source notes that misunderstandings often arise from differences in the underlying frameworks, and that communication is easier the greater the overlap (source line 199).
