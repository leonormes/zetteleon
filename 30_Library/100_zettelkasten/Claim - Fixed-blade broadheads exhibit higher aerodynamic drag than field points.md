"Every single broadhead evaluated in the 2026 study exhibited a higher aerodynamic drag constant than the baseline Gold Tip field point... As the range extends to 50, 60, and 70 yards, the elevated drag causes the broadhead to drop at a steeper parabolic angle and suffer significantly greater horizontal wind deflection than the field point."
