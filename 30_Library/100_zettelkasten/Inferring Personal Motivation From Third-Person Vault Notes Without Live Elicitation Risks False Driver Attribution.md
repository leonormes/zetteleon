## Inferring Personal Motivation From Third-Person Vault Notes Without Live Elicitation Risks False Driver Attribution

Inferring a person's motivation or mode of engagement from third-person PKM notes without live elicitation conflates captured ideas with personal autobiography. Because a zettelkasten records conversations, conceptual distinctions, and external arguments alongside self-reflection, unverified vault-mining produces false candidate driver attributions. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Operationalises Principle 2 ("elicit before classifying") of [[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph]] whenever human or LLM synthesis attempts to infer personal drives, defences, or engagement modes from existing vault notes.

### Evidence

> "I used to be a musician and spent all day everyday practicing and playing and listening."
> — Leon, 15 September 2026 elicitation correction in [[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph — Interest Seed and Candidate Drivers]]

> "The mentions of music in this vault are just from a conversation I was having with Bessie about the difference between real human music and plastic manufactured music."
> — Leon, 15 September 2026 elicitation correction in [[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph — Interest Seed and Candidate Drivers]]

Vault-mining misread [[Asking Why a Song Exists Helps Determine its Authenticity]] and [[The Distinction Between Appreciation and Creation]] as autobiographical self-defence of passive music listening, fabricating the third leg of a proposed construction-over-consumption driver for a former full-time practising musician.

### Implications

- Never promote a candidate driver leg from third-person conceptual notes until live elicitation confirms the person's actual lived history and mode of engagement.
- Record retracted vault-mining inferences explicitly as negative methodological evidence rather than silently deleting them.

### Falsifier

- Weakened if blind driver attributions inferred solely from third-person zettelkasten notes consistently match independent live biographical elicitation across diverse domains without systematic biographical misattribution.
