## How to Use the Intentional Pause to Break Autopilot

The Intentional Pause inserts a three-second conscious gap before reflexive digital or attention switches. Asking "What am I hiring this tool to do right now?" interrupts unconscious autopilot and restores deliberate choice at the moment of impulse.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to micro-transitions throughout the workday—tab switching, phone checks, and feed opening. Unlike emotional-regulation pauses, this protocol targets reflexive attention habits rather than acute interpersonal emotion.

### Evidence

Derived from the Intentional Pause protocol in [[SoT - Intentional Living]] (§5B).

### Implications

- Most compulsive checking collapses as soon as it must justify a concrete purpose out loud or in thought.
- Even a three-second delay creates enough cognitive space to exercise a [[The Sacred No - Intentionality as Habit Intervention|Sacred No]].
