## Temporal Boundary Acceptance Beats Efficiency Maximisation for Neuro-Variable Profiles

The empirically grounded alternative to efficiency maximisation is decoupling intentional action from outcome-driven anxiety by orienting toward the quality of engagement within a bounded timeframe, accepting a session's predefined end rather than treating the to-do list as something that must reach zero.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies at the level of individual work sessions and daily planning. Maps onto Kahneman's distinction between the experiencing self (lived time) and the remembering self (retrospective achievement narrative).

### Evidence

> "The empirically supported alternative is not the abandonment of intentional action but its decoupling from outcome-driven anxiety: orienting toward the quality of engagement within a bounded timeframe rather than the illusory completion of an infinite list."

### Implications

- Sessions should have a predefined, accepted end-point rather than an implicit goal of "finishing" an unbounded backlog.
- Judge a working session by engagement quality within its time-box, not by whether the list reached zero.
