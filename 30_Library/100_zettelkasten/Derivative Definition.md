## Derivative Definition

A derivative represents the infinitesimal rate of change between variables. It tracks how a function behaves under infinitely small changes, allowing for the precise measurement of instantaneous rates.

### Scope & Conditions

Fundamental concept within differential calculus.

### Evidence

> "Derivatives track how a function behaves under infinitely small changes. They represent the rate of change between variables."

### Implications

- Defines the slope of a tangent line at any given point on a curve.
- Enables the calculation of instantaneous velocity and other rates of change in physical systems.
