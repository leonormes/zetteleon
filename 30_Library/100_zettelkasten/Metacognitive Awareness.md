Metacognitive awareness is the capacity to observe, monitor, and evaluate one's own thought processes — to notice not just what one is thinking, but how, and whether that thinking holds up.

Writing is one of the clearest mechanisms for developing it: it allows us to observe our own thinking process, converting felt understanding into explicit propositions we can actually inspect. [[Writing puts us in the powerful position of being able to observe our thinking]].

[[Metacognitive Awareness in Paraphrasing]] is one specific, narrower application — the conscious monitoring of thought processes during the act of translating ideas into different words.
