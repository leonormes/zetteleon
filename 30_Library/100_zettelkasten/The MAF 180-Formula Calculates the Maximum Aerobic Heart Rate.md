## The MAF 180-Formula Calculates the Maximum Aerobic Heart Rate

Summary: The 180-Formula is a tool used to determine an individual's maximum aerobic heart rate for exercise to ensure they remain in the fat-burning zone.

Details: By subtracting one's age from 180 and adjusting for health and fitness factors, a specific heart rate limit is established. Staying at or below this rate prevents the body from shifting into anaerobic sugar-burning and excessive cortisol production, thereby building the aerobic base more effectively.
