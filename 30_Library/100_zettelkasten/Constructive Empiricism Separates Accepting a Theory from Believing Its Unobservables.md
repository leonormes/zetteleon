## Constructive Empiricism Separates Accepting a Theory from Believing Its Unobservables

A scientist can "accept" and use a theory while remaining completely agnostic about whether the unobservable entities it describes actually exist.

### Scope & Conditions

A corollary of constructive empiricism's empirical-adequacy target.

### Evidence

> "A scientist can 'accept' and use a theory while remaining completely agnostic about whether the unobservable entities it describes actually exist."

### Implications

- Lets a working scientist use realist-sounding theories without committing to realism about them.
