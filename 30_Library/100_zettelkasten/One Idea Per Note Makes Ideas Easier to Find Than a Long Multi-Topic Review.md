## One Idea Per Note Makes Ideas Easier to Find Than a Long Multi-Topic Review

Recording one idea per note makes the ideas easier to find than keeping a long review of a whole book. The source is Dan Allosso, How to Make Notes and Write.

### Scope & Conditions

Allosso speaks from experience of looking for a particular point in a long review. His reason is retrieval, and the connecting benefit is separate (Atom 001).

### Evidence

> "Even so, we should try to write a separate note for each discrete idea. It's not that helpful to have a three-thousand-word review of a book you've just read, when you are looking for a particular point. I say this from experience! Getting in the habit of recording one idea per note makes the ideas easier to find"
> (Allosso, How to Make Notes and Write, chapter on writing as thinking)

### Implications

- A note's title and opening statement can state the point, so it can be found by scanning.
- Long summaries bury the very point the reader will later need.
