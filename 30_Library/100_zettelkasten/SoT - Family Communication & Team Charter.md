## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Household conflict rarely comes from opposing goals. It comes from mismatched process values, long-term efficiency against immediate relief, and from the illusion that explaining harder in the moment will change behaviour ([[The Information Deficit Model is a Fallacy]]). The charter replaces in-the-moment persuasion with protocols agreed when calm: tag-team handovers, intent and impact repair, frequency matching and a weekly family meeting.
