## LLM Wiki Concept

An LLM Wiki is a persistent, structured knowledge base of markdown files maintained by an AI agent to preserve research insights across independent chat sessions. This model moves away from the stateless nature of traditional Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) by ensuring that new information is synthesized and recorded into a compounding codebase of knowledge rather than being rediscovered from scratch in every session.

### Scope & Conditions

Replaces traditional "from scratch" RAG retrieval for long-term projects and continuous research.

### Evidence

> "Andrej Karpathy's 'LLM Wiki' moves away from traditional Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)… it builds and maintains a persistent, structured knowledge base of markdown files."

### Implications

- Prevents the "memory loss" typically associated with terminating individual AI chat sessions.
- Transforms ephemeral AI output into a durable, interlinked asset that grows in value over time.
