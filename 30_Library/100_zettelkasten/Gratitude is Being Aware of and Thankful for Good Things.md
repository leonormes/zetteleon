## Gratitude is Being Aware of and Thankful for Good Things

Gratitude is being aware of and thankful for the good things that happen, and taking time to express thanks. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It includes both noticing and expressing.

### Evidence

> "You are aware of the good things that happen to you, and you never take them for granted."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 254) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask whom you could thank and for what.
