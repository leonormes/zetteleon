## Evidence - Tracy Momentum Principle Says Starting Costs More Energy Than Continuing

> although it may take tremendous amounts of energy to overcome inertia and get started initially, it then takes far less energy to keep going

Source: Brian Tracy, _Eat That Frog!_, the Momentum Principle of Success.

### What it Supports

Rule 1 of [[The Three Rules of Starter Tasks]]: a starter task exists to build momentum, because the cost is in getting started and continuing is cheap.

### What it Does not Show

It is a popular self-help assertion, not a measured study, and it gives no duration. It does not support the five to fifteen minute limit or the rule that the task be simple and physical; those rules remain unevidenced.
