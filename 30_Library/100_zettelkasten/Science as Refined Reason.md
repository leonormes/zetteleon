## Science as Refined Reason

Science is the refining of reason to understand the world. The source is Steven Pinker, Enlightenment Now.

### Scope & Conditions

Pinker names science as the second Enlightenment ideal, following directly from reason.

### Evidence

> "That leads to the second ideal, science, the refining of reason to understand the world."
> (Enlightenment Now, Chapter 1: Dare to Understand!)

### Implications

- Science is presented as a continuation of reason, not a separate authority.
- Understanding the world is the purpose given to it.
