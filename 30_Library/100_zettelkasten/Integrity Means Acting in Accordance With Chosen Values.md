## Integrity Means Acting in Accordance With Chosen Values

Integrity is the alignment of action with consciously chosen values rather than with claims of objective certainty. Living in accordance with endorsed commitments generates stable self-trust even while descriptive beliefs remain provisional and open to revision. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to normative self-regulation and identity stability when operating without absolute metaphysical or epistemic certainty.

### Evidence

Foundational normative premise (`axiom: true`) in the vault's character and eudaimonia architecture ([[SoT - Values and Eudaimonia]]); no external quotation note is attached (`evidence_links: []`).

### Implications

- Relocate personal confidence from being factually right to acting consistently with chosen commitments ([[Strong Opinions, Loosely Held Balances Conviction and Humility]]).
- Pair the freedom to author personal values with direct accountability for the consequences of acting on them.
