## Research-Plan-Implement Workflow

The Research-Plan-Implement (RPI) workflow is a structured three-tier process designed to prevent the generation of low-quality automated code. It separates the discovery of architecture (Research) from the definition of the strategy (Plan) and the actual execution (Implement), ensuring that each step is validated before proceeding.

### Scope & Conditions

Standard protocol for complex agentic engineering tasks. It requires disciplining the human-AI interaction to avoid "premature implementation."

### Evidence

> "To prevent the generation of 'hundreds of lines of bad code,' a structured three-tier workflow is proposed: Research… Plan… Implement."

### Implications

- Forces validation of assumptions before code execution.
- Creates a clear audit trail for technical decisions.
