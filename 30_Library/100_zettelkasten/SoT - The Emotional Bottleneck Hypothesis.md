## The Claim

The primary operational bottleneck in ADHD is not cognitive—it is emotional. Memory deficits and motivation gaps are manageable with external systems (Extended Mind, PINCH, checklists). What actually stops movement is emotional dysregulation: shame, self-doubt, the paralysis that comes from feeling "especially weak," and the Rejection Sensitivity that makes starting feel dangerous.

## Why This Matters for ProdOS

If the bottleneck is cognitive, the fix is better tools (timers, checklists, scope-locks). If the bottleneck is emotional, the fix is pre-clearance—resolving the emotional state _before_ attempting execution. This means:

- The Launch Sequence should include an emotional triage step, not just a task decomposition step.
- "Why can't I start?" is more often answered by "What am I afraid of?" than by "What's the next MVA?"
- Journaling and Brain Dumps are not optional wellness extras—they are load-bearing infrastructure for clearing the emotional blockage that sits upstream of execution.

## The Corollary: Wonder Is Not Waste

Revelling in curiosity, finding joy in the mystery of patterns, enjoying the absurdity of existence—these are not productivity failures. They are the emotional fuel the system runs on. Dismissing them as "not practical" starves the PINCH model of its two strongest drivers (Interest and Play). Protect them.
