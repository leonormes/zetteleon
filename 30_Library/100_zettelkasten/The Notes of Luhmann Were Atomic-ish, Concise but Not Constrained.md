## The Notes of Luhmann Were Atomic-ish, Concise but Not Constrained

Niklas Luhmann's own notes were not strictly atomic; they were concise without being constrained. The source is Bob Doto, A System for Writing.

### Scope & Conditions

Doto's answer to whether Luhmann made "atomic notes"; it qualifies the use of Luhmann as an authority for strict atomicity.

### Evidence

> "Not really. Luhmann's notes were, at best, atomic-ish. Concise, but not constrained."
> (Doto, A System for Writing, Appendix "Brief Answers to Lingering Questions")

### Implications

- Strict single-idea notes are a later practice in the zettelkasten community, not a fixed part of Luhmann's method.
- Conciseness is the constant, and atomicity is a matter of degree.
