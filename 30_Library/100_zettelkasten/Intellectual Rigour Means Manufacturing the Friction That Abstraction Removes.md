If the absence of material resistance is the disease, the treatment is to build resistance in by hand. Concretely: actively hunting the disconfirming case rather than waiting to encounter it, seeking out the strongest opponent rather than the most convenient one, and committing to a prediction specific enough to be wrong.

The framing is more useful than the generic instruction to "avoid confirmation bias", because it names _why_ the bias is structurally worse in abstract work than in craft: not because thinkers are less honest than joiners, but because their medium never contradicts them.

Note what this implies about method. Rigour here is not a character trait; it is an engineered feedback loop standing in for one the domain does not supply—which is the same architectural move this vault makes everywhere else, preferring structure over discipline.

## Steelman

Self-administered friction has a known failure mode: the practitioner chooses which objections to take seriously, and so the resistance is only ever as strong as their willingness to be defeated. A genuinely adversarial reader, a hostile reviewer or a market is more reliable than any amount of intended rigour, which suggests the real prescription is social rather than personal.
