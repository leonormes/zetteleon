## Creativity Combines Memories and Skills in Novel Ways

Creativity works on material that already exists, recombining stored memories and learned skills into arrangements the organism has not used before. This lets it handle problems that past experience did not cover exactly, which is why creativity counts as one of the tools in the problem-solving toolbox.

### Scope & Conditions

This is a claim about the mechanism of creativity, recombination, not about whether any outcome is valuable. It assumes memory and skills exist to be combined.

### Evidence

### Implications

- Creativity is limited by what has been stored and learned, so narrow experience narrows what can be combined.
- It sits downstream of memory and learning in the toolbox.
