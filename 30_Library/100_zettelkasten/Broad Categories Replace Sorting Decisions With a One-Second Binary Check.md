## Broad Categories Replace Sorting Decisions With a One-Second Binary Check

Micro-categories add a decision point to every item, such as whether a pen belongs in the fine-tip drawer or the marker bin. A broad category reduces the choice to a one-second check, such as whether it is bathroom stuff or not. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to high-frequency household storage used by tired or executive-fatigued people. Items that need precise grouping, such as medicines, may still need finer categories.

### Evidence

> "By reducing sorting to a 1-second binary check (*“Is it bathroom stuff or not?”*), macro-storage prevents the cognitive stall that causes items to be abandoned on flat surfaces."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Aarssen's own work)

### Implications

- Replace thirty labelled spice jars with one Cooking bin and one Baking bin where sorting is the obstacle.
- Reserve fine sorting for a deep-maintenance slot.
