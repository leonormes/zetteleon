## Empathy Is Cognitive Work

Understanding another person requires active effort to bridge conceptual gaps, beyond hearing their words.

### Scope & Conditions

Stated as an implication of the gap between thought and language; the source later links it to deep listening and perspective-taking (source line 376).

### Evidence

> "Empathy is cognitive work: Truly understanding others requires active effort to bridge conceptual gaps, not just hearing their words."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 151).

### Implications

- Listening attentively is necessary but not sufficient for empathy.
- Effort is needed because others' experiences exceed what their words convey.
