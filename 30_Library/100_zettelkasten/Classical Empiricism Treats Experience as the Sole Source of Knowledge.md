## Classical Empiricism Treats Experience as the Sole Source of Knowledge

Empiricists such as Locke and Hume argue that the human mind is a "blank slate" and that experience and observation are the only genuine sources of knowledge. Locke specifically emphasised a "historical, plain method" of observing human minds and nature.

### Scope & Conditions

Classical empiricist epistemology, standing in direct contrast to classical rationalism.

### Evidence

> "Empiricists argue that the human mind is a 'blank slate' and that experience and observation are the only genuine sources of knowledge… Locke emphasized the 'historical, plain method' of observing human minds and nature."

### Implications

- Establishes the empiricist starting point that Hume's inductive scepticism then destabilises from within.
