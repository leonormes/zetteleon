## Leadership is Encouraging a Group to Get Things Done

Leadership is the strength of encouraging a group you belong to so that things get done, while keeping good relations within it. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It includes organising group activities and seeing that they happen.

### Evidence

> "You do a good job organizing activities and seeing to it that they happen."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 249) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what the group needs organised and what keeps its relations sound.
