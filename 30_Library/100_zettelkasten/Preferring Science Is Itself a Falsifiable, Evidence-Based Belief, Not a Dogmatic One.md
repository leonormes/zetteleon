## Preferring Science Is Itself a Falsifiable, Evidence-Based Belief, Not a Dogmatic One

Accepting [[Science Is Trustworthy Because Its Structure Corrects for Bias, Not Because Scientists Are Less Biased|why science is preferable]] [depends_on:: [[Science Is Trustworthy Because Its Structure Corrects for Bias, Not Because Scientists Are Less Biased]]] is not the same as treating science as beyond question. The preference itself is held the way any scientific belief is held: provisionally, in proportion to the evidence, and open to revision if the evidence changed. This is what separates the preference from dogma—the dogmatic version would be "science is right and that is settled"; the position actually held is "science is the best available method for the reasons given, and if that stopped being true I would need to change my mind."

### Scope & Conditions

A statement about how the preference for science is held, not a claim that any specific piece of counter-evidence currently exists. It is a stance, not a hedge: it does not weaken the preference, it explains why the preference is not blind.

### Implications

- The test is the same one science applies to any other theory: what observation would count against it? Here, that would be evidence that science's falsifiable, evidence-based, self-correcting structure no longer reliably outperforms rival methods at producing accurate, useful models of reality.
- Holding the preference this way means no specific finding, institution, or scientist has to be trusted uncritically for the preference to survive—only the structural properties named in the parent claim have to keep holding.
- This is the same move [[Falsifiability Distinguishes Science from Dogma]] makes, applied one level up: to the belief that science itself is trustworthy, not just to a first-order scientific claim.

## What Would Change My Mind

Evidence that science's falsifiable, evidence-based, self-correcting structure has stopped reliably outperforming rival methods at producing accurate, useful models of reality—not an isolated failure of one scientist, one study, or one institution, since [[Science's Division of Labour Lets Individual Scientists Be Dogmatic|the method is designed to survive those]].
