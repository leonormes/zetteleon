## Staying in the Room Until the Sweep Is Complete Prevents Derailment by Secondary Chores

Staying inside the room until the sweep is finished stops off-task wandering. Leaving to take out rubbish or return an item elsewhere opens new tasks that derail the reset. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to a single-room reset. The source says to leave the rubbish bag in the room rather than carry it to the bin mid-sweep.

### Evidence

> "remain inside the room until the sweep is complete"
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Davis's own book)

### Implications

- Rubbish, dishes and laundry are gathered in the room and moved out only when the pass ends.
- Anything that needs a trip elsewhere waits for after the sweep.
