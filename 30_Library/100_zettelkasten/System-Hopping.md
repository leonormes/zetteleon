## System-Hopping

The cyclic pattern of adopting a new productivity/PKM tool or method, riding the initial enthusiasm, then abandoning it once novelty fades—and starting again with the next one.

### Why it Matters here

- It is the visible symptom of front-loaded reward: a system is most exciting _before_ it has proven itself.
- Named as a driver in [[Claim - Novelty-craving drives self-defeating system-hopping]].

### Related

- Contrasts with [[Claim - Treat the system as iterative not perfectable]]: same energy, aimed at _this_ system instead of the _next_ one.
