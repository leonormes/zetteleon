title: Focus on the Process Not the Product in Daily Writing
type: "concept"
updated: 
---

For a daily writing habit to be effective as a thinking tool, the focus must be on the process itself, not the product. The goal is not to publish or impress, but to engage in the act of translating vague ideas into concrete words, which is where the cognitive benefits lie.
