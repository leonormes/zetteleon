## Single Sign-On (SSO)

Single Sign-On (SSO) is a user experience pattern that allows individuals to access multiple independent applications through a single authentication event. It simplifies identity management for organizations and reduces password fatigue for users, often implemented using protocols like OIDC or SAML.

### Scope & Conditions

Commonly used in enterprise environments to unify access to emails, dashboards, and internal tools.

### Evidence

> "A user experience rather than a specific protocol. It allows a user to sign in just once to gain access to multiple applications…"

### Implications

- Reduces password fatigue for users.
- Simplifies identity management for organisations.
