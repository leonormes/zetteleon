## Descartes Requires Infallible "Clear and Distinct" Ideas for Rigorous Knowledge

Descartes held that rigorous scientific knowledge requires infallible, "clear and distinct" ideas generated by the mind, independent of the external environment.

### Scope & Conditions

Descartes' specific criterion within classical rationalism; a standard for what counts as certain knowledge.

### Evidence

> "Descartes believed that rigorous scientific knowledge requires infallible, 'clear and distinct' ideas generated by the mind, independent of the external environment."

### Implications

- Makes the mind, not the world, the source of certainty—the sharpest possible contrast to empiricist method.
