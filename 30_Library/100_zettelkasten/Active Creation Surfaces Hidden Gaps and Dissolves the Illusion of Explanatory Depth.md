## Active Creation Surfaces Hidden Gaps and Dissolves the Illusion of Explanatory Depth

Building or applying a model forces hidden gaps and unexamined assumptions to surface, because an artefact or a real problem will not run on a vague grasp. Passive reading leaves the illusion of explanatory depth intact, since nothing in it demands the missing step. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies where the model has a mechanism that can fail when applied wrongly, such as code libraries, technical systems and causal mental models. Falsified if people who build with a model are no better at noticing the limits of their own understanding afterwards than people who only read about it.

### Evidence

This is the mechanism proposed when splitting [[Understanding Is Demonstrated by Creation Rather Than Passive Familiarity]]; no verbatim source quote supports it yet. The [[SoT - Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IoED)]] presents active creation as one of its forcing functions without stating the mechanism in these words.

### Implications

- A build attempt works as a diagnostic: where it stalls marks the exact gap the illusion was hiding.
- Self-rated understanding should be re-taken after a build attempt, since it is the point at which the rating becomes informative.
