## Reference - Vault Interest Map

Updated on 2026-11-13 from an analysis of 2,965 markdown files across the vault, including 1,825 notes in `30_Library/100_zettelkasten` (792 `claim`, 308 `sot`, 190 `concept`, 120 `map`, 95 `atom`, 22 `protocol`). Ranked by theme density and active development across the 12 core interest clusters mapped in [[SoT - Canonical Interest Tags]].

### Core Interests

1. **ADHD & executive function**—the dominant theme (227 notes tagged `TheHuman/Health/ADHD`, anchored by [[MOC - ADHD (The Master Map)]]). Task initiation, hyperfixation-burnout cycles, dopamine regulation, body doubling, routines, and prosthetic executive function. Leon studies his own cognition as an engineering problem.
2. **Knowledge management (PKM) & ProdOS**—Zettelkasten theory, ProdOS, proposition-centred atomic notes ([[SoT - Atomic Note Standard (The Proposition Card)]]), the typed-edge justification graph ([[SoT - Knowledge Compiler (Argument Graph Spec)]]), and process-over-product writing ([[MOC - PKM as Process vs Product]]).
3. **Productivity systems & behavioural design**—GTD/PARA, choice architecture ([[Choice Architecture Designs the Environment to Make Desired Behaviors Easier]]), habit mechanics, timeboxing, momentum ramps, and operational protocols. Closely fused with the ADHD cluster.
4. **Cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes & clinical data**—networking, AKS/EKS, container primitives from raw Linux namespaces/cgroups, Terraform/GitOps, CUE configuration ([[MOC - CUE Configuration]]), FitFile deployment, and OHDSI/OMOP clinical data architecture ([[MOC - OHDSI & OMOP Architecture]]).
5. **AI & agentic engineering**—agentic design patterns ([[SoT - Agentic AI Design Patterns]]), agent skill architecture, context engineering, stateful LLM wikis ([[SoT - LLM Wiki Pattern]]), Typed Answer Contracts ([[SoT - Typed Answer Contract (TAC) for LLM Output]]), and agent failure diagnostics.
6. **Philosophy, epistemology & thought/language**—fallibilism, Popperian criticism, epistemic limits ([[MOC - How Can I Know Something]], [[MOC - Epistemic Limits, Volatility and the Absurd]]), the Illusion of Explanatory Depth ([[SoT - Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IoED)]]), thought-to-language compression ([[MOC - The Gap Between Thought and Language]]), and atelic/virtue philosophy ([[MOC - The Atelic Intellectual Life]], [[MOC - Character and Virtue]]).
7. **Mathematics foundations & physics**—what maths _is_ ([[MOC - What is Maths]]), mathematical logic, order theory, type theory/Curry-Howard, prime numbers, and relativity ([[MOC - Einstein, Relativity & Light Speed]]). First-principles curiosity rather than computation.
8. **Software design philosophy**—data-centric design ([[MOC - Data-Centric Software Engineering]]), type-driven development, Rust (ownership, type mechanics), applied formal methods, and pragmatism vs. rigour.
9. **Family, relationships & parenting**—household governance ([[SoT - Family Household Governance]]), communication & Team Charter ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]]), family finance ([[MOC - Family & Finance]]), and Bessie's GCSE/SEN scaffolding ([[MOC - Bessie]], [[Protocol - Bessie Study Cadence & Neurodiverse Scaffolding]]).

12. **Culture, aesthetics & storytelling**—cyberpunk, hip hop origins and authenticity ([[SoT - The Origins and Evolution of Hip Hop Culture]]), storytelling techniques, Denis Villeneuve, and wabi-sabi.

### The Unifying Pattern

A pull towards systems and first principles—whether the system is a Kubernetes cluster, an ADHD brain, an argument graph, a recurve bow shot cycle, or a family household, nearly every note asks *"what is the underlying mechanism, and how does structure make the right outcome easier?"*
