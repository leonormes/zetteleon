## AI-Led Wiki Maintenance

AI agents automate the "tedious bookkeeping" tasks required to maintain a complex knowledge base, such as cross-referencing, updating indices, and flagging logical contradictions. By offloading these architectural maintenance tasks to an agent, the system prevents the structural decay and information entropy that typically cause human-led wikis to fail over time.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the maintenance and evolution phase of structured knowledge management systems.

### Evidence

> "The AI handles the 'tedious bookkeeping' like cross-referencing and updating indices, which is usually why human-led wikis fail over time."

### Implications

- Significantly reduces the human cognitive load associated with information architecture and manual linking.
- Increases the long-term reliability, consistency, and discoverability of stored information within the vault.
