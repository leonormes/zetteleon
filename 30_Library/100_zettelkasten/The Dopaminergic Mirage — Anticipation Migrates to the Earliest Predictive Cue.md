## The Dopaminergic Mirage—Anticipation Migrates to the Earliest Predictive Cue

As an outcome becomes reliably anticipated, dopaminergic activation progressively shifts from the moment of reward receipt to the earliest predictive cue. The chase becomes neurologically rewarding while the achieved state becomes inert.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies once a goal's reward becomes predictable. Compounded by temporal discounting, which weakens the dopamine response to distant goals relative to immediately available alternatives.

### Evidence

> "As an outcome goal becomes reliably anticipated, dopaminergic activation progressively migrates from the moment of reward receipt to the earliest predictive cue… The chase becomes the high; the achievement becomes neurologically inert."

### Implications

- The vivid motivating pull of an imagined finished project is not sustainable once the project is actually finished—this is a predictable neurological pattern, not a personal flaw.
- Systems should generate fresh, unpredictable "predictive cues" throughout a project rather than relying on the terminal goal alone.
