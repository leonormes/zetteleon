## Out-of-Sight Storage Forces Working Memory to Hold a Fragile Inventory of Where Things Are

For people who rely on seeing things, closed storage means the only record of where items live is internal memory. When that record fails, finding a basic object takes a search through several drawers, and each step drains decision energy. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to people with a visual preference, called Butterflies and Bees in the source. It does not hold for people who need visual calm, who are covered in [[Visible Clutter Costs People Who Need Visual Calm a Continuous Filtering Effort]].

### Evidence

> "Storing everyday items behind solid cupboard doors forces working memory to maintain a fragile internal inventory."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Aarssen's own work)

### Implications

- Open shelving, clear bins, pegboards and surface trays let the eye answer where things are.
- Visibility is a memory aid, so it suits [[Externalize Memory Aggressively (cognitive offloading)]].
