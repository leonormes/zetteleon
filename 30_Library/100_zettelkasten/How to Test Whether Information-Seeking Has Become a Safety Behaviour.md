## How to Test Whether Information-Seeking Has Become a Safety Behaviour

Before continuing to seek information on a topic, ask in sequence: (1) what concrete decision could this specific information change; (2) what new action, rollback, monitoring signal, or escalation path would it enable; (3) if it changes nothing, am I seeking information or seeking relief; (4) can I tolerate the uncertainty for a bounded period (e.g. 20 minutes) while taking a safe, reversible next step instead; (5) is the remaining uncertainty an actual external risk, an identity threat, or both.

### Scope & Conditions

Trigger—about to open another source, re-read documentation, or extend research beyond what a decision plausibly requires. Verification—the test succeeds if it produces either a concrete decision-relevant reason to continue, or a decision to stop and act within a defined boundary instead.

### Implications

- Pairs naturally with pre-committing a stopping rule before research begins, so the boundary isn't being negotiated in the moment by whichever mode (curiosity or anxiety) is currently active.
