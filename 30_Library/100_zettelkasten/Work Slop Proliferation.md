## Work Slop Proliferation

Low-effort automated outputs, or "work slop," often consume more total time through the necessary clarification and editing cycles than a high-effort manual draft would have required. While AI allows for the near-instant generation of text, the lack of precision in the initial output increases the administrative burden of review and correction, negating any initial speed gains.

### Scope & Conditions

Common when using LLMs or chatbots for professional drafting, reporting, or communication.

### Evidence

> "Low-effort outputs masquerade as completed work but lack the precision required… ultimately consuming more total man-hours than a high-effort manual draft would have required."

### Implications

- AI-generated content can introduce significant "understanding debt" if not rigorously audited.
- The perceived speed of AI can be a false metric if it results in a higher total volume of work for the reviewer.
