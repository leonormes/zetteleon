_These skills are about how you find joy, meaning, and your place in the wider world._

- [[Appreciation of Beauty is Noticing Excellence in the World|Appreciation of Beauty & Excellence]]: Noticing and feeling "wow!" about the beauty in nature, art, music, or seeing someone do something really well. (VIA Strength & National Curriculum: Arts)
- [[Gratitude is Being Aware of and Thankful for Good Things|Gratitude]]: Being thankful for the good things in your life and taking the time to show it. (VIA Strength)
- [[Hope is Expecting the Best in the Future and Working for It|Hope / Optimism]]: Expecting the best from the future and working to make it happen. (VIA Strength)
- [[Humor is Liking to Laugh and Bring Smiles to Others|Humour & Playfulness]]: Loving to laugh and bring smiles to other people's faces. Being lighthearted. (VIA Strength)
- [[Zest is Approaching Life with Excitement and Energy|Zest for Life]]: Approaching things with energy and excitement. Doing things wholeheartedly! (VIA Strength)
- Understanding the World: Learning about different places, cultures, beliefs, and history to understand how our world works. (National Curriculum: Geography, History, RE)
- Responsibility & Care: Understanding our duty to care for each other, for our community, and for the environment. (National Curriculum Values)

_PSHE: Personal, Social, Health and Economic education is a key part of the curriculum that teaches many of these life skills._
