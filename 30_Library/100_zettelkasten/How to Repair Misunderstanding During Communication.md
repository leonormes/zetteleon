## How to Repair Misunderstanding During Communication

Active repair treats misunderstanding as the expected baseline and uses explicit feedback loops to converge on shared meaning. Rather than hoping clarity survived transmission, assume divergence, ask explicit clarification questions, paraphrase what you heard, and iterate with concrete examples until functional overlap is reached.

### Scope & Conditions

Essential in high-stakes decisions, conflict resolution, technical alignment, and cross-cultural conversations. Over-applying explicit paraphrasing to trivial routine confirmations can feel pedantic, so reserve full loops for consequential or ambiguous points.

### Implications

- Paraphrasing exposes [[The Illusion of Shared Understanding in Teams]] early, while misalignment is still cheap to fix.
- Normalising clarification questions removes social friction from admitting confusion.
