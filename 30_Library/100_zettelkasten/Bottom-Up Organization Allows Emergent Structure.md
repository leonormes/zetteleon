## Minimum Viable Understanding (MVU)

Bottom-up organization dictates that structure arises from the ground level of individual notes and their direct connections, rather than being imposed from above through predetermined categories. 

In practice, you do not declare upfront schemas ("I need folders for Philosophy, Science, and Art"). Instead, you capture specific, atomic ideas and link them based on genuine conceptual relationships. Over time, clusters naturally form around heavily-connected concepts, creating organic "topics" that map to your actual thought patterns rather than an abstract classification taxonomy.

## The Advantage of Emergence

This approach has profound cognitive advantages: 
- It prevents premature categorization before you understand how ideas truly relate.
- It allows topics to shift and recombine as your understanding deepens.
- It enables genuine interdisciplinary connections (a [[Rhizome Structure - Non-Hierarchical Network]]) that rigid categories would prevent. 

The contrast with top-down is stark: top-down says "decide the structure first, then force ideas to fit"; bottom-up says "capture the ideas truthfully and let the structure reveal itself."
