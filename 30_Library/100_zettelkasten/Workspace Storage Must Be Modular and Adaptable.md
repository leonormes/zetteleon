### Workspace Storage Must Be Modular and Adaptable

Storage setups must be fundamentally adaptable and modular to handle workflow churn without devolving back into disorder.

#### Scope & Conditions

Dynamic workspaces subject to shifting tools, consumables, and project demands (including home maintenance).

#### Evidence

> "Storage setups must be adaptable and modular to handle workflow churn without devolving back into disorder"

#### Implications

- Prevents rigid systems from breaking when the environment or tasks inevitably change.
- Ensures the organization system evolves alongside the user's needs.
