## Intent as High-Level Source Code

Software engineering is transitioning from the manual construction of logic to the precise articulation of domain intent. This shift effectively treats "intent" as a new high-level source code, where the primary requirement for the engineer is the logical precision of their instructions rather than the mastery of syntax.

### Scope & Conditions

A fundamental shift in the level of software abstraction enabled by generative AI.

### Evidence

> "Generative AI shifts focus from syntax constraints to logic and intent… effectively treating 'intent' as the new high-level source code."

### Implications

- Domain-driven design and precise linguistic articulation become critical engineering skills.
- Logical precision remains a non-negotiable requirement for functional software.
