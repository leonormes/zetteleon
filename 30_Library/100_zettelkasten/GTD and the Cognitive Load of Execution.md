## GTD and the Cognitive Load of Execution

The Getting Things Done (GTD) methodology relies on separating the decision-making of planning from the physical execution of a task, avoiding the neurological bottleneck of trying to do both simultaneously.

### Scope & Conditions

Relevant to task management and execution, specifically addressing why undefined or broad tasks ("blobs of undoability") cause resistance.

### Evidence

> "Demanding that the brain hold an expansive, meaning-making vision (DMN) while simultaneously trying to execute a specific, isolated task (TPN) triggers a mechanical failure to launch."

### Implications

- Execution requires acting as a worker who trusts the "planner self."
- A task must be defined down to the absolute next physical, visible activity to avoid subconscious resistance.
