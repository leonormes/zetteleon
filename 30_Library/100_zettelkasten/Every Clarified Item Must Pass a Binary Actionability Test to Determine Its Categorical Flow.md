## Every Clarified Item Must Pass a Binary Actionability Test to Determine Its Categorical Flow

The first and mandatory question applied to every item during the Clarify stage is: "Is it actionable?" This binary test determines the item's categorical fate. A "No" answer routes the item to one of three non-action outcomes: Trash, Incubate (Someday/Maybe list), or Reference. A "Yes" answer opens a second decision branch—defining the desired outcome and next action. No item may proceed to the Organise stage without having first passed through this gate.

### Scope & Conditions

The first question asked for every single item during the Clarify stage—no exceptions. The test's power lies in its binary nature: the brain is forced to commit to one of two branches, preventing the ambiguous "parking" of items as neither-action-nor-reference.

### Evidence

> "For every single item you clarify, you must apply a rigorous, binary thought process by asking: Is it actionable?"

### Implications

- Non-actionable items are definitively diverted to Trash, Incubate, or Reference—each a legitimate and complete disposition.
- Actionable items require further definition: desired outcome and next physical action. The binary gate prevents premature routing.
