## Evidence - Sloman and Fernbach Say We Mistake Knowledge in the Community for Knowledge in Our Heads

> We think the knowledge we have about how things work sits inside our skulls when in fact we're drawing a lot of it from the environment and from other people.

Source: Steven Sloman and Philip Fernbach, _The Knowledge Illusion_.

### What it Supports

Why the individual view is narrower than it feels in [[Human Understanding is Inherently Myopic]]: much of what we believe we understand lives outside our own heads, in the environment and other people.

### What it Does not Show

It shows the illusion of individual understanding, not the capacity limit, and the authors present the reliance on a community of knowledge as partly a strength.
