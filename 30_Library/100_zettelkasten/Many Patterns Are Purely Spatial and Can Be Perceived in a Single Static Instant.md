## Many Patterns Are Purely Spatial and Can Be Perceived in a Single Static Instant

Patterns such as honeycomb cells, fractal branching and spiral galaxy arms are structures of geometry rather than sequence, so they can be perceived in one static instant without time moving in any direction.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to spatial patterns. Temporal patterns, such as day following night, are a separate class that does depend on sequence.

### Evidence

> "You can perceive these patterns in a single, static instant. Their existence doesn't depend on time 'moving' in a particular direction."
> (Gemini session, section Patterns Beyond Linear Time)

### Implications

- Even if the flow of time is an illusion, not every pattern is negated, which weakens the claim that patterns need linear time.
