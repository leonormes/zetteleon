## Visible Clutter Costs People Who Need Visual Calm a Continuous Filtering Effort

For people who need visual calm, exposed items create a constant background stream that the brain must filter and rank. Closed or opaque storage removes that cost, and pairing it with broad categories keeps the upkeep light. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to people whose nervous systems need visual calm, called Ladybugs and Crickets in the source. The source gives no measurement of the filtering cost, so it remains an assumption.

### Evidence

> "Visual clutter overwhelms the visual cortex, forcing the brain to expend energy continuously filtering out irrelevant items and deciding what requires attention."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against a primary source)

### Implications

- Deep opaque baskets, closed cupboards and lidded boxes suit these users.
- A hidden Ladybug system with macro sorting gives calm without complex filing.
