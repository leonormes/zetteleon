## Kindness is Doing Favors and Good Deeds for Others

Kindness is the strength of doing favours and good deeds for others, helping them and taking care of them. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It is also called generosity and can be shown to people you do not know well.

### Evidence

> "You are kind and generous to others, and you are never too busy to do a favor."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 245) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what concrete deed would help this person.
