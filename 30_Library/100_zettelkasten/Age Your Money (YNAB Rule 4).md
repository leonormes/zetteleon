## Age Your Money (YNAB Rule 4)

Rule 4 aims to spend money that was earned at least 30 days ago. The gap between earning and spending is the age of money, and a rising number means a growing buffer. It is the outcome of the first three rules working over time, not a separate technique.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies after the budget is stable enough that next month's essentials can be funded from this month's income, which is the Resilience question in [[Give Every Dollar a Job (YNAB Rule 1)]]. It takes months to build and falls when the buffer is spent down. A low age is a diagnostic reading, not a failing.

### Evidence

This is the household's working method, set out as the fourth of four rules in [[SoT - Family Financial Wellness]]. The 2026-09-13 reconciliation project recorded an age of money of 0 during a period of drift, which shows the measure responds to budget health. The vault holds no outside source for the rule yet.

### Implications

- Bills no longer have to be timed against the next payday, which frees attention for longer-term planning.
- Income forecasting becomes unnecessary because the money is already held.
- Progress has a single number to watch, which suits a visible-awareness approach.
