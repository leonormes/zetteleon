## Archery Shot Process - Release

Summary:
Release is the act of letting go of the string, which should be an involuntary, natural reaction to the clicker when the preceding steps are executed correctly.

Details:
- **Involuntary Action**: Treat the release as a reaction to the clicker rather than a conscious opening of the fingers.
- **Finger Relaxation**: Simply relax the final digits of your drawing fingers when the clicker drops. Do not pluck the string.
- **Cut Rope Visualization**: Imagine the taut rope holding the string was suddenly cut.
- **Posture Retention**: Maintain your exact body posture during the release; do not drop your chin or chest.
- **Poundage & Tension**:
  - Ensure bow poundage is manageable. Over-bowing forces a clenched hook ("string grabbing") to prevent the string from slipping, making a clean, relaxed release mechanically impossible.
  - Relaxation is relative to grip strength; drop bow weight if hand tension remains high.

### Training Drills

#### **Two-Handed Finger Slip-Off Drill**
* **Purpose:** Isolates clean finger relaxation without needing to manage bow weight.
* **How to do it**:
  1. Hook the three fingers of your draw hand directly into the fingers of your opposite hand (or a training partner's hand) in front of your chest.
  2. Create tension by pulling both elbows apart using your back muscles.
  3. Maintain draw-elbow structure and relax only the fingertips of your draw hand, allowing them to slip cleanly away.
