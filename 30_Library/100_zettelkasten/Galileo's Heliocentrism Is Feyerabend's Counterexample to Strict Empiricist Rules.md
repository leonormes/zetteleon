## Galileo's Heliocentrism Is Feyerabend's Counterexample to Strict Empiricist Rules

Feyerabend pointed out that if historical figures like Galileo had followed strict empiricist rules, they would have been forced to abandon their theories, because ordinary observations at the time heavily favoured an Earth-centred universe.
