## Humility Makes Self-Assessment More Accurate

Humility makes a person assess their own abilities and work more accurately. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- It concerns modesty without self-contempt, since harsh self-doubt can distort assessment too.

### Evidence

This comes from the earlier Humility note, which also said humility is essential for learning. No source has been verified, so it is my own reasoning.

### Falsifier

- If more humble people predict their own performance no more accurately than others, the claim is wrong.
