## Judge a Worldview by Its Utility in Its Environment, Not Its Correctness

Summary: Instead of asking "Is this person's worldview right or wrong?", ask "How is this worldview serving them in their specific environment?"

Details: A plant grows a certain way to survive its unique conditions; a worldview is likewise an adaptation to its holder's environment. This diagnostic question replaces the arbiter role (declaring right/wrong) with a functional analysis (what work is the belief doing—providing explanation, belonging, dignity, a target for anger?). It does not concede that the worldview is _true_; it explains why it persists despite being false, which is precisely what pure error-correction fails to do.
