## Minimum Viable Understanding
Just as a plant grows in a certain way to survive its unique conditions, a worldview is an adaptation to its holder's environment. This diagnostic approach replaces the arbiter role (declaring right/wrong) with a functional analysis (e.g., asking what work the belief is doing—providing explanation, belonging, dignity, or a target for anger). This does not concede that the worldview is factually true; instead, it explains why the belief persists despite being false, a nuance that pure error-correction fails to capture.
