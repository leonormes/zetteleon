## Without an Extended Mind the ADHD Brain Spins in Cyclical Thinking and With One Thinking Becomes Cumulative

Without an extended mind the ADHD brain tends to fall into cyclical thinking, spinning in place, while an external system makes thinking cumulative so that insights compound over time. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

This is a claim about my own experience of the ADHD brain, not a tested finding. The effect depends on the system actually being used and reviewed.

### Evidence

This is my own experience, and no external source has been checked.

### Implications

- Hold conversations with past notes so work builds on itself.
- Insights that are not captured evaporate and are re-derived.
