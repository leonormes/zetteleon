## How to Open a Conversation About Chores Without Triggering Identity Defences

Open with identity validation and a shared definition of respect before the chore itself. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to chore and system conversations between partners. For a stalemate use [[Protocol - Household Routine Reset Conversation]].

### Evidence

> "Validate Identity: "I know we are both respectful, kind people.""
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §4)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

1. Validate identity.
2. State the meta-problem.
3. Say what respect feels like for me.
4. Invite her definition.
5. Build the definition together.
