## Language Discretises Continuous Experience

Language maps a continuous range of experience onto a finite set of words, so nuance is lost in the mapping.

### Scope & Conditions

The source's example is the spectrum of emotion reduced to words such as "happy", "ecstatic" or "content".

### Evidence

> "language inherently discretizes continuous experience. The spectrum of emotions we feel must be mapped onto a finite set of words like "happy," "ecstatic," or "content," inevitably losing nuance in the process."

Source: [[beyond words]] (line 296).

### Implications

- Nuance is lost in the mapping itself, not only in transmission.
- The source points to art and music as media that convey experiences difficult to put into words (source line 209).
