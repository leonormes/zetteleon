## Taking On All Household Administration to Prove Worth Creates a Resentment Loop

Taking on all household administration and childcare coordination to prove worth or show love leads to mental overload and resentment, and stepping back is the antidote. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to a partner who absorbs the whole household load, especially with ADHD or postnatal anxiety. Stepping back works only if the other partner is able and willing to step up.

### Evidence

> "A common pitfall is taking on 100% of household administration and childcare coordination to "prove worth" or show love."
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §1.1)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

- Step back from constant rescuing and prioritise self-worth and self-care.
- Leave the partner space to step up and meet in the middle.
- Agree who owns each parent-owned category so neither partner silently absorbs a default load.
