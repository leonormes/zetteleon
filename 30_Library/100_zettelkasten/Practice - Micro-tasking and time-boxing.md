## Practice—Micro-tasking And Time-boxing

**What:** Break tasks into the smallest workable units and run them inside a fixed time box (e.g., a Pomodoro), rather than facing the whole task as one block.

**Why it suits ADHD:** 
- A tiny first unit lowers the activation energy required to cross the initial threshold.
- The time box harnesses hyperfocus while capping it before burnout occurs. 
- Each completed box provides the immediate, concrete feedback that the dopamine-deficient brain craves.

**Micro-step Formulation:** Take the task you are avoiding and write down only its first physical action (verb-plus-object, e.g., "open the IDE"). Do not do it yet—just name it.
