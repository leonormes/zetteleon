## The Mind Movie Fallacy

A "Mind Movie" is a psychological fantasy depicting how perfectly disciplined or productive one should be. This idealised self-image creates internal resistance and frustration when reality inevitably differs from the fantasy, often leading to abandonment of tasks or habits.

### Scope & Conditions

A common source of friction in habit formation and task initiation, particularly for those prone to perfectionism.

### Evidence

> "You likely possess a fantasy—a 'Mind Movie'—of how perfectly disciplined and productive you should be. When your reality… does not match this fantasy, you experience frustration and give up."

### Implications

- Engaging with reality requires letting go of perfectionist self-images.
- Reducing the gap between the "Mind Movie" and actual capacity lowers mental friction.
