## Feedback-Rich Deliberate Practice Holds Durable Interest Only When Paired With Progressive Mastery

As a testable candidate driver hypothesis, durable personal engagement across observability engineering, archery technique, automation, and PKM may reflect a shared pull toward clear challenge, interpretable feedback, and iterative adjustment. However, rapid feedback loops appear likely to sustain attention only when paired with progressive mastery and meaningful stakes rather than bare mechanical repetition. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Recorded as an untested `proposed` candidate driver in [[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph]] (originating in [[tmp_atoms_what-drives-a-persons-interests]]), not an established psychological root. Bounded by the complication noted in [[Near-Zero Personal Reflection in Vault Notes Can Reflect Off-Vault Practice Rather Than Weak Interest]], since feedback-rich external domains (logs, alerts, test suites) do not automatically generate in-vault reflective pull.

### Evidence

> "a hypothesis to examine through evidence in your graph, not a settled explanation of you."
> — [[tmp_atoms_what-drives-a-persons-interests]], §Not Atomised — Routed to the Interest-Driver Project Instead

Across recurve shot-cycle tuning, CI/observability engineering, and Zettelkasten refinement, rapid diagnostic signals recur alongside skill refinement, but live comparison and laddering have not yet separated feedback speed from mastery progression.

### Implications

- Test candidate domains via Method §4 and §5 in [[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph]] rather than assuming rapid feedback alone explains why an interest holds.
- Distinguish embodied or self-referential feedback loops (archery, PKM) from external operational feedback loops (cloud infrastructure, security) during live comparison.

### Falsifier

- Disconfirmed as a compound bundle if feedback-rich activities lacking mastery progression or aesthetic/embodied payoff hold equal durable attention, or conversely if slow-feedback domains with progressive mastery hold equal pull without rapid feedback loops.
