## Relationships Between Pieces of Knowledge Can Be Logical, Hierarchical, Associative, Contextual or Temporal

A relationship is a connection or association between two or more pieces of information, and it can be logical, hierarchical, associative, contextual or temporal.

### Scope & Conditions

A general classification of relationship kinds from an AI answer. It is not the vault's own edge vocabulary, which is a closed set of six typed relationships.

### Evidence

> "In the context of data and knowledge, a "relationship" refers to the connection or association between two or more pieces of information."

### Implications

- Two pieces of knowledge can be related in more than one of these ways at once.
- A datum can be related to a context as well as to other data.
