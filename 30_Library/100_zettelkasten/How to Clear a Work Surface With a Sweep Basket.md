## How to Clear a Work Surface With a Sweep Basket

Sweep displaced items into a basket so a surface is usable now, and defer any sorting. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to high-friction surfaces such as kitchen worktops, desks and dining tables. Sort food and hazardous items properly rather than sweeping them.

### Evidence

> "When a worktop is needed for meal preparation or daily use, sweep the displaced items directly into the basket to restore the surface immediately."
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against a primary source)

The steps below are my own reasoning from the chat, not its wording, so confidence is low.

### Implications

1. Fetch the sweep basket kept nearby.
2. Sweep everything on the surface into it without sorting.
3. Use the cleared surface for the task.
4. Put the basket out of the way, not in a cupboard.
5. Sort it in a high-energy period, or leave it if nothing is blocked.
