## The Quit on the Third Urge Rule

One should pause and allow the urge to stop a task or procrastinate to pass twice, only permitting oneself to quit when the same urge arises a third time. This protocol creates a cognitive buffer between impulse and action, allowing for the distinction between temporary friction and genuine exhaustion.

### Scope & Conditions

A muscle-building technique for increasing task persistence despite internal resistance.

### Evidence

> "When you feel the resistance telling you to stop working, do not act on it immediately… Only on the third distinct urge should you give yourself permission to quit."

### Implications

- Develops the ability to act independently of transient negative emotions or boredom.
- Strengthens the "tenacity muscle" by forcing a deliberate delay of impulse.
