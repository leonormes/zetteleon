## Conflict Lives in the Gap Between Intent and Impact

Between two people with good intentions, conflict happens in the gap between what one meant and how it landed on the other. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies when both partners accept that the other has good intentions. It does not excuse a repeated harmful impact.

### Evidence

> "We agree that we are both good people with good intentions. Conflict happens in the gap between _Intent_ and _Impact_."
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §2.1)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

- Use the script: I know your intention was a good one, but the impact on me was this. Can we close that gap?
- Intent does not delete impact, and impact does not delete intent.
