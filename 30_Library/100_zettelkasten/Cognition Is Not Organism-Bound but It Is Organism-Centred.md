## Cognition Is Not Organism-Bound but It Is Organism-Centred

While cognition is not bound to the organism, it remains organism-centred, because the biological brain is the locus of recruitment that builds and maintains the web of external scaffolding. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

This is the reply to critics who hold that tools are only aids to a mind locked in the head.

### Evidence

This is my own summary of the thesis as set out in the earlier text of [[SoT - The Extended Mind]], which attributes it to Clark and Chalmers. No book quotation has been verified for this card.

### Implications

- The person stays the designer of their system, so review and repair are the person's job.
