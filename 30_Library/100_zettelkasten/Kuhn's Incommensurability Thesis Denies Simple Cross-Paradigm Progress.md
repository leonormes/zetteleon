## Kuhn's Incommensurability Thesis Denies Simple Cross-Paradigm Progress

Kuhn argued that preand post-revolution paradigms are often incommensurable—they use different language and standards of evidence—meaning scientific progress is not a simple march toward objective truth.

### Scope & Conditions

Kuhn's paradigm theory; a distinct thesis from, but a direct corollary of, the four-stage collapse mechanism—during Normal Science researchers share a paradigm and do not question fundamental ideas, anomalies build up and trigger a crisis and a revolution, and incommensurability describes what happens across that revolution boundary.

### Evidence

> "Kuhn argued that preand post-revolution paradigms are often _incommensurable_… meaning progress is not a simple march toward objective truth."

### Implications

- A previously-unrecorded consequence of the same paradigm-collapse mechanism: no simple truth-accumulation across a revolution.
