## Consilience Signals Genuine Scientific Consensus

A genuine consensus rarely relies on a single method or dataset—it emerges through consilience, when completely independent fields of study converge on the same answer. The consensus on plate tectonics wasn't built by geologists looking at rock formations alone; it was corroborated by fossil distribution, magnetic striping on the ocean floor, and later, GPS telemetry. Groupthink, by contrast, relies on a single, circular methodology or a narrow group of self-referencing experts.
