## The Motivation Triangle

Motivation is a triangle of three interconnected sides. Behaviour is what you do, benefit is the outcome you want, and belief is the conviction that your specific actions will lead to that outcome. The source calls belief the foundation of the triangle.

### Scope & Conditions

The source contrasts the triangle with a simple straight line from behaviour to reward. It is one author's model as relayed by an LLM summary, not an established taxonomy.

### Evidence

> "Motivation is not a simple straight line between behavior and reward, but a triangle with three interconnected sides: Behavior… Benefit… Belief: The conviction that your specific actions will actually lead to the desired results."
> (Nir Eyal, Beyond Belief, via an LLM summary; chapter and page not captured)

### Implications

- Incentives alone leave one side of the triangle unaddressed.
- Diagnosing stalled motivation means checking all three sides, including belief.
