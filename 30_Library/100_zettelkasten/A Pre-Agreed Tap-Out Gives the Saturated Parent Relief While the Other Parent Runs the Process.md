## A Pre-Agreed Tap-Out Gives the Saturated Parent Relief While the Other Parent Runs the Process

When one parent reaches emotional saturation, a pre-agreed tap-out hands full authority over the process to the other parent, who validates the need for relief and runs the optimal process. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to partners who have agreed the rule in advance, away from the moment. The parent tapping in takes authority over the process and not only the task.

### Evidence

> "The other partner "Taps In" and takes full authority over the _Process_."
> ([[SoT - Family Communication & Team Charter]], text before the 2026-11-24 refactor, §3 Protocol A)

This is my own reasoning, not an externally sourced finding. No outside source has been checked.

### Implications

- Relief and the optimal process happen together, so neither partner has to lose.
- Schedule a quiet tea or coffee break for the tapped-out parent to offset noise and mental fatigue.
