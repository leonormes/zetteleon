## Forearm Release Strategy

Elite recurve archers actively contract their extensor digitorum (opening the fingers) during release while maintaining deep back tension, rather than passively relaxing their flexor digitorum.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the release phase of a recurve archery shot.

### Evidence

> "elite archers do not simply relax their flexors; they actively contract the extensor digitorum… synchronized perfectly with the audible snap of the clicker, allows the string to slip from the fingers with minimal interference"

### Implications

- Training must focus on actively "opening" the hand upon the clicker drop.
- Passive relaxation leads to jagged releases and lateral string deflection (plucking).
