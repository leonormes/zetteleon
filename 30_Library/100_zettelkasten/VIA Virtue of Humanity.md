The virtue of Humanity, as defined by the [[MOC - Character and Virtue|VIA Character Strengths]] framework, encompasses interpersonal strengths that involve tending and befriending others.

## Strengths of Humanity

- [[Love is Valuing Close Relations with Others|Love]]: Valuing close relations with others, particularly those in which sharing and caring are reciprocated.
- [[Kindness is Doing Favors and Good Deeds for Others|Kindness (Generosity)]]: Doing favors and good deeds for others; helping and taking care of them.
- [[Social Intelligence is Being Aware of Motives and Feelings|Social Intelligence]]: Being aware of the motives and feelings of other people and oneself.
