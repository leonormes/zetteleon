## Durable Wellbeing Comes From Ongoing Activities Rather Than Outcome States

Durable gains in subjective wellbeing come from ongoing activities and practices rather than reaching terminal outcome states. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to sustained engagement in varied, intrinsically meaningful practices (skill mastery, learning, gratitude, relationships) as contrasted with static circumstantial or milestone goals.

### Evidence

> "lasting changes in SWB are possible, but specifically through activities and practices rather than outcome-state changes."

Split from [[Hedonic Adaptation to Achieved Goals Is a Biological Certainty, Not a Character Failing]] so the positive practice claim can be referenced independently of the empirical adaptation claim.

### Implications

- Wellbeing and motivation should be engineered around recurring activities and practices rather than a single terminal achievement.
- Reusable as a constructive premise for process-oriented learning, contentment practices, and intrinsic goal design.
