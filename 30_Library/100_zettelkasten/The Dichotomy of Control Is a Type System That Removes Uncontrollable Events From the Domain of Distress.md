## The Dichotomy of Control Is a Type System That Removes Uncontrollable Events From the Domain of Distress

The Stoic Dichotomy of Control partitions events into those within our power and those not. The SoT reads that partition as a structural pre-classification that decides which events the mind admits as grounds for distress, so uncontrollable events are removed from the domain of valid distress triggers. It calls this "make illegal states unrepresentable" applied to adversity.

### Scope & Conditions

- This is the SoT's reading and an analogy to type systems, so the analogy risk in [[Shared Formal Attributes Across Domains May Be a Family Resemblance and Not an Ontological Equivalence]] applies in full.
- Stoic sources distinguish an involuntary first impression from the assent given to it. On that distinction the schema acts on assent and does not stop the first flash of feeling. This is general knowledge of the tradition and has not been checked against a source in the vault.
- The library holds no Epictetus text, so the claim rests on the SoT's summary.
- The SoT goes on to say that virtue is a structural invariant and not a disposition. That stronger claim is not adopted here and the SoT itself says it needs its own note.

### Evidence

> "The Dichotomy of Control (Epictetus) is a type system for experience: it partitions events into `within_our_power` and `not_within_our_power`. This partition is not a coping strategy applied post-hoc—it is a structural pre-classification that determines which class of events the system admits as suffering."
> ([[SoT - Structure is Truth is a Unifying Axiom Across Formal Systems]], §5 The C15 Bridge)

> "Events classified as `not_within_our_power` are, in the Stoic schema, structurally removed from the domain of valid distress triggers."
> (same section)

### Implications

- It connects a formal-methods idea to emotional regulation. The same move appears in Musashi's account of the Void, where distress comes from the fear added to a situation and not from the situation ([[The Void (Ku) Is Perceiving the Situation as It Is Without the Distortion of Fear or Desire]]).
- For ADHD it gives a concrete design: classify an event before reacting, and spend effort only where the class admits action, which is the circle-of-influence move in [[Habit 1 - Be Proactive]].
