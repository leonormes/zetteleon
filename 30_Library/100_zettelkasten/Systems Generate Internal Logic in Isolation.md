## Systems Generate Internal Logic in Isolation

When a system is [[Systems are Blind to Their Broader Context|blind to external context]], it cannot import rules or values from the outside and must generate a self-referential framework of logic and meaning from its own components and history. This internal operating system enables self-regulation in isolation and gives rise to [[SoT - Emergence|emergent]] behaviours, cultures, and priorities specific to the system itself. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to bounded, self-regulating systems (biological, cognitive, or social) operating without external corrective feedback. It presupposes [[Systems are Blind to Their Broader Context]] as the enabling condition and describes how internal coherence sustains operation in isolation, not whether that coherence remains durable or accurate over time.

No verbatim book quotation is used; this concept is a structural synthesis supported by two cross-domain cases in the vault:

- [[Ptolemaic System as a Patched Flawed Model]]—1,500 years of epicycles preserved local predictive coherence through internal patches rather than external correction, becoming unfalsifiable from within.

[supports:: [[SoT - Bonhoeffer's Theory of Functional Stupidity]], confidence=high]
