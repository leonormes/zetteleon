## Lakatos's Research Programmes Replace Kuhn's Single Paradigm With Competing Cores

Lakatos, attempting to fix the rigidness of Kuhn's single-paradigm view, argued that fields contain multiple competing "research programmes," each with a hard core of ideas and a protective belt of auxiliary hypotheses.

### Scope & Conditions

A structural refinement of Kuhn's paradigm theory; allows plurality within a field at one time, unlike Kuhn's single dominant paradigm.

### Evidence

> "Lakatos suggested that fields contain multiple competing 'research programs,' each with a hard core of ideas and a protective belt of auxiliary hypotheses."

### Implications

- The "protective belt" gives a specific mechanism for how a programme absorbs anomalies without abandoning its hard core—a more granular account than Kuhn's "anomalies accumulate" step.
