## The Collector's Fallacy Conflates Gathering with Assimilation

The Collector's Fallacy is the cognitive bias in which the brain confuses the mechanical act of gathering information into a centralised location with the actual cognitive assimilation of that knowledge.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies specifically to modern digital PKM environments where capture friction has been removed; the saved artefact is stripped of the relational context in which it was discovered and sits dormant.

### Evidence

> "This bias occurs when the brain confuses the mechanical act of gathering information into a centralized location with the actual cognitive assimilation of that knowledge."

### Implications

- A growing note count is not evidence of learning.
- The remedy is forcing active processing (e.g. testing new capture against existing questions) rather than measuring capture volume.
