Epistemic Actions are physical actions performed not to change the world (Pragmatic Actions), but to simplify a cognitive task.

## 📐 The Distinction

- Pragmatic Action: Moving a glass to drink from it (achieving a world-state).
- Epistemic Action: Rotating a Tetris piece physically to see if it fits, rather than rotating it mentally (aiding computation).

## 🧠 Mechanism

By physically manipulating the environment, the brain offloads complex internal simulations to the fast, reliable feedback loop of the physical world. This is "thinking by doing."

## 🚀 Implementation

- Whiteboarding: Sketching diagrams to understand a system.
- Scrabble: Physically moving tiles around to "see" new words.
- Obsidian Graph: Navigating the visual graph to identify patterns that are too complex for mental visualisation.
