## Teamwork is Working Well as a Member of a Group

Teamwork is the strength of working well as a member of a group, being loyal to it and doing your share. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It is also called citizenship or social responsibility.

### Evidence

> "You are a loyal and dedicated teammate, you always do your share, and you work hard for the success of the group."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 247) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what your share is and whether you are doing it.
