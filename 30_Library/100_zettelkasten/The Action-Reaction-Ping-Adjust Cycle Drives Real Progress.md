This is the systematic process for breaking the [[The Overthinking Loop Creates Mental Noise Not Clarity]]:

1. Action: Take a small, manageable action ([[Shrinking the Loop Gathers Experiential Feedback Safely]]).
2. Reaction: Experience the resulting sensation or feedback.
3. Ping: Receive this feedback as a The Ping is a Nervous System Signal from Real-World Action—real-world data.
4. Adjust: Modify your next action based on this new information.

This cycle prioritizes doing over thinking and recognizes that [[Emotional Feedback is a Sign of Genuine Learning]].
