## Writing as Cognitive Rigour

Writing is the primary tool for achieving technical and mathematical rigour; an idea that remains unwritten is merely the illusion of a thought. The act of writing serves as a mechanism for debugging thought and uncovering logical inconsistencies that are hidden in internal monologue.

### Scope & Conditions

A general principle for architecture, engineering, and formal reasoning.

### Evidence

> "Writing is the primary tool for this rigour; an idea that remains unwritten is merely the illusion of a thought."

### Implications

- Formal documentation and specifications are prerequisites for genuine understanding.
- The inability to express a system in writing is diagnostic of a model failure.
