## Information Vs Knowledge

Information is commoditised and infinite, representing the raw data and linguistic patterns accessible through systems like LLMs. In contrast, knowledge is information that has been tested against reality, filtered through personal experience, and integrated into a coherent worldview. Retrieval of an answer does not equate to the acquisition of knowledge.

### Scope & Conditions

Differentiates between the vast availability of data in the digital age and the individual process of understanding.

### Evidence

> "Information is commoditised and infinite. Knowledge is information that has been tested against your reality, filtered through your experiences, and integrated into your worldview."

### Implications

- PKM systems should prioritize the synthesis and sense-making of information over the mere hoarding of facts.
- Effective learning requires active engagement and reality-testing rather than passive consumption of AI-generated content.
