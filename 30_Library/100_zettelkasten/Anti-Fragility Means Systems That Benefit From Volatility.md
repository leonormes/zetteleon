## Anti-Fragility Means Systems That Benefit From Volatility

Antifragility is the property of a system that gains from disorder, stressors, and volatility rather than merely resisting them. By embedding optionality, redundancy, and small reversible bets, an antifragile system converts environmental shocks into diagnostic information and asymmetric upside while bounding downside risk. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to system design, decision posture, and personal skill/confidence building in complex environments with irreducible uncertainty.

### Evidence

Conceptual synthesis of Nassim Nicholas Taleb's antifragility framework, grounded in the vault by [[Local empirical micro-testing beats centralised modelling under shifting rules]]; no verbatim external quotation note is attached (`evidence_links: []`).

### Implications

- Design personal and technical workflows around small, low-cost experiments whose failures yield high-value information.
- Treat uncertainty and volatility as raw material for learning and capability growth rather than threats to eliminate before acting.
