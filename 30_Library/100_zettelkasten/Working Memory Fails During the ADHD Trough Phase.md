## Working Memory Fails During the ADHD Trough Phase

During the trough a person has difficulty holding information in mind or following multi-step instructions. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Describes working memory in the trough, as reported in the source note. Multi-step instructions are the clearest case, and single-step cues may still work.

### Implications

- Writing steps down or using other external aids offloads the working memory that fails in the trough.
