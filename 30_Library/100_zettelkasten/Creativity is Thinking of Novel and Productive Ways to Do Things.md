## Creativity is Thinking of Novel and Productive Ways to Do Things

Creativity is the character strength of thinking of and doing things in ways that are both novel and productive. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- Both halves matter. Novelty without productivity is eccentricity, and productivity without novelty is competent repetition.
- It ranges from art, invention and scientific insight to finding a better route through an everyday problem.
- The vault files it under Wisdom because it is a way of acquiring and using knowledge.

### Evidence

> "When you are faced with something you want, you are outstanding at finding novel yet appropriate behavior to reach that goal."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 239) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman files this under ingenuity and says the category includes what people mean by creativity.

### Implications

- Ask what a new and workable way through the present problem would be.
