title: Success Accumulates Through Thousands of Tiny Daily Choices
type: claim

Success or failure is not the result of a single choice, but the accumulation of thousands of tiny daily decisions. These micro-habits, when compounded over time, lead to significant transformation. The focus should be on consistent, small actions rather than extreme, overnight changes.
