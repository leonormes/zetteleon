## Enduring Life Commitments Operate as Context Modifiers Rather Than Competing Interest Nodes

Enduring life commitments and structural conditions—such as parenting responsibilities or neurodivergent cognition—do not compete for discretionary attention in the way optional interests do. Within a personal interest graph, they operate as context modifiers that enable, amplify, or constrain interests rather than as interest nodes to be explained by utility-removal laddering. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to node-kind classification in [[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph]] (`context` vs `interest`). Accommodates hybrid cases such as ADHD, which can be studied intellectually as a subject of interest while simultaneously acting as a structural working-memory condition shaping how other interests are pursued (as framed in [[Why I Do a PKM and What I Think Knowledge Is For]]).

### Evidence

> "Per Leon, 2026-09-15: these are a focus for life, not an interest in the sense this project studies—they don't compete for attention the way an interest does, and running them through the same driver-elicitation machinery (why does this matter to me? what would I keep if it had no utility?) would be a category error."
> — [[A Portable Interest and PKM Knowledge Graph — Interest Seed and Candidate Drivers]], §Excluded: Life Foci

In the vault's tag-frequency audit, 42 notes tagged `#bessie` (supporting [[Bessie - Education & Support]]) reflected an enduring family commitment rather than a discretionary topic competing with software engineering, archery, or mathematics.

### Implications

- Exclude core family responsibilities from utility-removal and prestige-removal counterfactual tests designed for discretionary interests.
- Model structural conditions and life responsibilities as `context` nodes (`enabled-by`, `amplifies`, `inhibited-by`) so their real vault presence and causal influence remain visible without category error.
