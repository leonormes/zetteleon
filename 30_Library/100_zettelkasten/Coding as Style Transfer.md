## Coding as Style Transfer

Coding—the mechanical act of translating a specific requirement into syntax—is a "style transfer" problem that can be effectively handled by AI. In contrast, software engineering remains a human-centric discipline focused on the high-level design, management of system complexity, and the construction of robust mental models.

### Scope & Conditions

Differentiates between manual syntax execution and high-level architectural oversight in the age of AI.

### Evidence

> "Coding—the act of translating a specification into syntax—is a 'style transfer' problem that AI can handle. Software engineering… remains a human-centric discipline."

### Implications

- AI agents can automate the generation of code syntax but lack the capacity for original architectural design.
- The primary value of a human engineer shifts from implementation speed to the building and maintenance of complex system models.
