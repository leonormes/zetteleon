## Progress Is Multidimensional and Must Specify Its Domain Before Being Judged Real or Illusory

"Progress" is not one variable—life expectancy, scientific knowledge, personal liberty, equality, political stability, material prosperity, ecological sustainability, and subjective wellbeing can all move in different directions at once—so a claim that humanity is or isn't progressing is incomplete until it specifies the dimension, population, time period, and baseline.

### Scope & Conditions

A corrective to both blanket-optimist and blanket-pessimist claims about "progress"; doesn't itself settle whether any given dimension is improving.

### Evidence

> "'Progress' is not one variable… A claim that humanity is or is not progressing is therefore incomplete until it specifies the dimension, population, time period, and baseline."

### Implications

- Any argument that opens with "progress is real" or "progress is a myth" without naming the dimension should be treated as under-specified until it does.
