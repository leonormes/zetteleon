## Gaps in an Own-Words Explanation Show Where Understanding Is Missing

The point where an explanation in your own words stalls or turns vague marks a specific part of the idea that you do not yet understand. The failure therefore tells you what to go back and study. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- Applies to an explanation attempted without leaning on the wording of the source.
- A stall can also come from weak writing skill or a missing word, so check the idea before concluding it is a gap.

### Evidence

> "The attempt to rephrase an argument in our own words confronts us without mercy with all the gaps in our understanding."
> (Ahrens 2022, ch. 10, PDF p. 123) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/704/EPUB?open_at=toc:10%20Read%20for%20Understanding)

Ahrens states that rephrasing exposes gaps. Using the point of failure to direct further study is my own reasoning.

### Implications

- Return to the source for the part that stalled, then explain it again.

### Falsifier

- Have people explain an idea, then test them on the parts where they stalled and the parts they explained fluently. If they do no worse on the stalled parts, the claim is wrong.
