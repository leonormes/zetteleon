## Perseverance Toward Long-Term Goals Predicts Who Completes Demanding Programmes

Perseverance toward long-term goals predicts who completes a demanding programme. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

- The evidence concerns grit in selective and demanding settings.
- The same passage reports that grit did not outpredict traditional tests for grades and performance scores.

### Evidence

> "GRIT predicted which new arrivals completed the grueling summer training"
> (Seligman 2011, ch. 6, PDF p. 124) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Chapter%206:%20GRIT%2C%20Character%2C%20and%20Achievement:%20A%20New%20Theory%20of%20Intelligence)

Seligman reports research by Angela Duckworth. The earlier note claimed more, that perseverance is fundamental to mastery in any field, which this does not show.

### Falsifier

- If grit scores add nothing to prediction of completion beyond other measures in new samples, the claim is wrong.
