Imagine your life is a ship at sea. This model deconstructs the hierarchy of the self:

## 🚢 The Three Components

1. Habits are the Autopilot: Essential for efficiency. They keep the ship moving without needing to man the wheel for every minor wave.
2. Goals are the Ports: The destinations on the map. They provide the direction.
3. Intentionality is the Captain: The agent standing on the bridge. The Captain sets the course (Heart + Direction) and decides _how_ the crew conducts themselves.

## 🧠 The Logic

Without the Captain (Intentionality), the Autopilot (Habit) will keep the ship moving, but it will default to old patterns—potentially sailing in circles or crashing into the same rocks it hit yesterday.

Intentionality ensures that even when the Autopilot is engaged, the ship is heading where the Captain truly desires to go.
