## Consistency Depends on Structural Design Rather Than Willpower

Intentionality in daily execution is not sustained by willpower; it is a product of structural design. Combining explicit intent and timeframe parameters with low activation energy, environmental triggers, visual tracking, friction reduction, identity framing, and external accountability makes desired behaviour the path of least resistance.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to any recurring personal or professional practice where motivation fluctuates across days or weeks. Structural design cannot compensate for impossible physical capacity limits, where scope itself must shrink.

### Evidence

This claim synthesises the 9-Point Consistency Framework in [[SoT - Intentional Living]] and aligns with [[Strong Habits Override Weak Intentions]] and [[Reducing friction is a key strategy for building ADHD-friendly habits]].

### Implications

- When a habit stalls, diagnose missing structural supports (trigger, friction, or minimum viable size) before blaming personal discipline.
- Pairing internal intent ("the why") with external commitment (scheduling and accountability) bridges low-motivation days.
