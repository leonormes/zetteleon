## Claim-Titled Notes Invite Inferential Links That Topical Notes Lack

A claim-titled note commits to one contestable proposition, which raises the question of what supports, contradicts or presupposes it. That lowers the cost of typed inferential links, which a topical note ("What is X?") leaves implicit. ^claim
