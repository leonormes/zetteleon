## Collecting Dishes and Laundry Without Washing or Sorting Keeps a Tidy From Turning Into a Deep Task

During a tidy, dishes and laundry should only be gathered and moved to the sink or a hamper. Washing, sorting or folding them breaks momentum and turns a surface reset into a multi-step chore. ^claim

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to the dishes and laundry passes of a room reset. The washing and the laundry cycle still need doing, but in their own slots.

### Evidence

> "do not stop to wash them"
> "Do not sort, fold, or start a wash cycle yet"
> (AI chat summary pasted 2026-11-24, not checked against Davis's own book)

### Implications

- Dishes stay at the sink and laundry stays in the hamper until the pass ends.
- The processing goes on the weekly chore plan, not in the tidy.
