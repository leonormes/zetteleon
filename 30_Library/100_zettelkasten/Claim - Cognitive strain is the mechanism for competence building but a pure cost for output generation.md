## Minimum Viable Understanding
Whether cognitive friction is "waste" or "the point" depends entirely on the intent of the task. If a person delegates the heavy lifting of thinking (e.g., writing, designing, architecting) to an AI agent, they bypass the very strain that builds professional competence in that specific domain. However, if they are already highly competent in that domain and merely need execution, or if the domain is tangential to their core skills, the strain is a cost that should be aggressively eliminated through agent-first workflows.

## The Operational Implication
Treating all cognitive strain uniformly leads to either deskilling (by over-automating areas where mastery is required) or inefficiency (by hand-cranking tasks where only output matters). The critical skill is no longer just doing the work, but deliberately classifying the work as "skill-building" or "output-only" before applying automation.
