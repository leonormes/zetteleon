An idea born inside a hyperfocus session, or a high-intensity dopamine state, arrives with a strong feeling of breakthrough. That feeling is easy to mistake for evidence that the idea itself is logically sound—but intensity of feeling and soundness of logic are two different things. This is the ADHD-specific intensifier sitting directly on top of [[Externalising Tacit Knowledge Strips the Scaffolding That Made an Idea Feel Deep]].

## Evidence

> "The ADHD Intensifier: If an idea is tied to a hyperfocus session or high-intensity dopamine flood, the feeling of breakthrough can be mistaken for the integrity of the logic."—[[SoT - Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IoED)]]

## Steelman

Hyperfocus sessions may also produce genuinely higher-quality ideas via sustained, undistracted attention, so the correlation between intensity and idea quality could be partly real rather than purely illusory—treating all hyperfocus conviction as suspect risks discounting the sessions where the depth is genuine.
