### Kinovea Live Delay Feedback

Using the "Live Delay" function in Kinovea buffers a live video feed and delays its display on a monitor by a set duration (e.g., 5 to 10 seconds), allowing the archer to view a delayed playback of their shot without leaving the shooting line.

#### Scope & Conditions

Requires a camera, laptop with Kinovea, and a monitor set up at the archery range.

#### Evidence

> "the software can buffer the live video feed in the computer's RAM and delay its display on a monitor by a predetermined amount of time... This allows the archer to stay on the shooting line and maintain their physical rhythm"

#### Implications

- Tightens the temporal gap between the physical sensation of the shot and the visual feedback.
- Accelerates the correction of motor patterns by removing the interruption of manual recording.
