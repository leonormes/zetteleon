## Beliefs Foster an Internal Locus of Control That Sustains Perseverance in Uncertain Environments

Beliefs foster an internal locus of control, the conviction that personal action matters even in chaotic or uncertain environments. The source says this turns internal intention into sustained, strategic perseverance.

### Scope & Conditions

The third of the source's three powers, Agency (the power to do). It is asserted in an LLM summary without cited evidence.

### Evidence

> "Beliefs foster an internal locus of control, creating the conviction that personal action matters even in chaotic or uncertain environments. This transforms internal intention into sustained, strategic perseverance."
> (Nir Eyal, Beyond Belief, via an LLM summary; chapter and page not captured)
