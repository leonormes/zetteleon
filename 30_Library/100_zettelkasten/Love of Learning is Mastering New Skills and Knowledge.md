## Love of Learning is Mastering New Skills and Knowledge

Love of Learning is the strength of mastering new skills, topics and bodies of knowledge, alone or formally. ^def

### Scope & Conditions

- It concerns building skill and knowledge, where curiosity concerns interest in experience itself.

### Evidence

> "You love learning new things, whether you are in a class or on your own."
> (Seligman 2011, "Appendix: Signature Strengths Test", PDF p. 237) · [open in Calibre](calibre://view-book/GCcalibreBooks/294/EPUB?open_at=toc:Appendix:%20Signature%20Strengths%20Test)

Seligman describes this strength in a self-report test. The wording of the definition is this vault’s.

### Implications

- Ask what you would study here without any outside reward.
