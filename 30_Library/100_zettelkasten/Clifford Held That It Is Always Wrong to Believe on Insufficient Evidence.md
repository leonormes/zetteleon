## Clifford Held That It Is Always Wrong to Believe on Insufficient Evidence

Clifford held that it is always wrong to believe on insufficient evidence. The word "always" leaves no exceptions. It is the strict end of the argument about how much evidence belief needs.

### Scope & Conditions

This is a secondhand report from an AI chat that gives only the year, 1877. The chat does not name the work or quote Clifford, so the wording is the chat's summary and not his. It says neither Clifford nor James decisively won.

### Evidence

> "Clifford vs James. Clifford (1877) held it is always wrong to believe on insufficient evidence."
> (AI chat transcript pasted by Leon, 2026-10-23, section "Is there knowledge about God, or only belief?")

### Implications

- If it holds, belief in God on faith alone is wrong whatever its comfort or its consequences.
- It has to say what counts as sufficient, which the chat does not address.
