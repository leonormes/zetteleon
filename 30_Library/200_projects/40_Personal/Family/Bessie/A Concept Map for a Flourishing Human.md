## A Concept Map for a Flourishing Human: Bessie's Skills

Creating a concept map together is a brilliant, hands-on way to help Bessie visualise her growth in a positive and holistic way. It shifts the focus from "things you must do for school" to "all the wonderful parts that make up YOU".

Here is a comprehensive list that combines the [[Character and Virtue MOC|VIA Character Strengths]] with the core skills and values from the English National Curriculum, and broader abilities needed for a happy, healthy life in modern society. You and Bessie can use this as a "pick list" to build your map. You can write "Bessie" in the centre and then draw branches out to these different areas.

This approach aligns with the [[MOC - Character and Virtue|VIA Character Strengths]] framework, which identifies six core virtues that contribute to human flourishing:

- [[VIA Virtue of Wisdom]] - Cognitive strengths for acquiring and using knowledge
- [[VIA Virtue of Courage]] - Emotional strengths for accomplishing goals despite opposition
- [[VIA Virtue of Humanity]] - Interpersonal strengths for tending and befriending others
- [[VIA Virtue of Justice]] - Civic strengths that underlie healthy community life
- [[VIA Virtue of Temperance]] - Strengths that protect against excess and impulsiveness
- [[VIA Virtue of Transcendence]] - Strengths that forge connections to the larger universe

Here are all the skills, strengths and qualities you are building as you grow. Let's map them out to see all the incredible parts of you!

### 🧠 Thinking & Creating Skills (Your Clever Brain)

_These are the tools your brain uses to learn, solve problems, and imagine new things._

- **Curiosity**: Being interested in the world, asking many questions, and loving to explore new topics. (VIA Strength & National Curriculum aim)
- **Love of Learning**: Enjoying mastering new skills and adding to your knowledge, not just because you have to, but because you want to. (VIA Strength & National Curriculum aim)
- **Creativity**: Thinking of new and original ways to do things, whether in art, in your writing, or when solving a problem. (VIA Strength & National Curriculum: Art, Design & Technology)
- **Judgement / Critical Thinking**: Thinking things through properly, looking at all the sides of an argument, and not jumping to conclusions. (VIA Strength)
- **Perspective / Wisdom**: Being able to see the "big picture" and give good advice to friends. Understanding what is truly important. (VIA Strength)
- **Literacy**: The power of reading, writing, and expressing your ideas clearly so others can understand you. (National Curriculum Core)
- **Numeracy**: Understanding numbers and how they work in the world, from telling the time to saving your pocket money. (National Curriculum Core)
- **Digital Literacy / Computing**: Knowing how to use technology safely and effectively to learn, create, and connect with others. (National Curriculum Core)

### ❤️ Heart & Friendship Skills (Your Kind Heart)

_These strengths are all about how you connect with other people and show you care._

- **Kindness**: Being generous, compassionate, and doing good things for others just to make them happy. (VIA Strength)
- **Love**: Valuing your close relationships with family and friends and showing them you care. (VIA Strength)
- **Teamwork**: Being a good citizen, working well in a group, and doing your part to help the team succeed. (VIA Strength & PSHE*)
- **Fairness**: Treating everyone equally and with justice; wanting everyone to have a fair chance. (VIA Strength)
- **Leadership**: Helping to organise a group to get something done and encouraging others along the way. (VIA Strength)
- **Social Intelligence**: Understanding your own feelings and the feelings of others. Knowing what makes people "tick." (VIA Strength & PSHE*)
- **Forgiveness**: Giving people a second chance and not holding grudges when they make a mistake. (VIA Strength)

### 💪 Inner Strength & Resilience Skills (Your Strong Spirit)

_These are the quiet strengths inside you that help you face challenges and stay true to yourself._

- **Bravery**: Not shrinking from things that are scary, whether it's trying something new or speaking up for what is right. (VIA Strength)
- **Perseverance / Grit**: Finishing what you start, even when it gets difficult. Working hard and not giving up. (VIA Strength)
- **Honesty**: Telling the truth, being sincere, and being a person others can trust. (VIA Strength & National Curriculum Values)
- **Self-Regulation**: Being able to manage your emotions and actions. Thinking before you act. (VIA Strength & PSHE*)
- **Prudence**: Making careful choices and thinking about what might happen in the future before you do something. (VIA Strength)
- **Humility**: Being modest and letting your achievements speak for themselves, without showing off. (VIA Strength)
- **Resilience**: The ability to bounce back after a setback or disappointment. (National Curriculum aim & PSHE*)

### ✨ World & Wonder Skills (How You Connect to the World)

_These skills are about how you find joy, meaning, and your place in the wider world._

- **Appreciation of Beauty & Excellence**: Noticing and feeling "wow!" about the beauty in nature, art, music, or seeing someone do something really well. (VIA Strength & National Curriculum: Arts)
- **Gratitude**: Being thankful for the good things in your life and taking the time to show it. (VIA Strength)
- **Hope / Optimism**: Expecting the best from the future and working to make it happen. (VIA Strength)
- **Humour & Playfulness**: Loving to laugh and bring smiles to other people's faces. Being lighthearted. (VIA Strength)
- **Zest for Life**: Approaching things with energy and excitement. Doing things wholeheartedly! (VIA Strength)
- **Understanding the World**: Learning about different places, cultures, beliefs, and history to understand how our world works. (National Curriculum: Geography, History, RE)
- **Responsibility & Care**: Understanding our duty to care for each other, for our community, and for the environment. (National Curriculum Values)

---
_*PSHE: Personal, Social, Health and Economic education is a key part of the curriculum that teaches many of these life skills._
