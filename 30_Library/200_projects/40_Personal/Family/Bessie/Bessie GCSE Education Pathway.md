## [[Year 10]] (Target Exam: Summer 2026)

### 1. Biology (IGCSE)

- Exam Board: Cambridge International (CIE)
- Code: `0610` (Graded A-G) or `0970` (Graded 9-1) - Check which grading system your centre uses.
- Why this board: Best for Home Ed. Includes "Alternative to Practical" paper (no lab required).
- Assessments:
    - Paper 2: Multiple Choice (30%)
    - Paper 4: Theory (50%)
    - Paper 6: Alternative to Practical (20%)

#### Official Resources

- Official Website: [Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-biology-0610/)
- Syllabus (2026): [Download 2026 Syllabus PDF](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662469-2023-2025-syllabus.pdf)
- Learner Guide: [CIE Learner Guide PDF](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/167585-learner-guide-for-examination-from-2016.pdf)
- Past Papers: [Official Past Papers & Mark Schemes](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-biology-0610/past-papers/)
- Textbook: Cambridge IGCSE Biology Coursebook with Digital Access (Mary Jones & Geoff Jones) - Endorsed by Cambridge.

### 2. English Language (GCSE)

- Exam Board: AQA
- Code: `8700`
- Assessments:
    - Paper 1: Explorations in Creative Reading and Writing (50%)
    - Paper 2: Writers' Viewpoints and Perspectives (50%)
    - Note: "Spoken Language Endorsement" is usually required. Check if your exam centre can facilitate this for a private candidate. If not, switch to IGCSE English Language (CIE 0500).

### 3. Photography (GCSE)

- Exam Board: AQA (Art & Design Suite)
- Code: `8206`
- Warning: 100% Non-Exam Assessment (Coursework) + Externally Set Assignment. Requires an authenticated portfolio.
- Assessments:
    - Component 1: Portfolio (60%)
    - Component 2: Externally Set Assignment (10 hours supervised time) (40%)

#### Official Resources

- Official Website: [AQA GCSE Photography](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/art-and-design/gcse/art-and-design-8201)
- Specification: [AQA 8206 Specification](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/art-and-design/gcse/art-and-design-8201/specification-at-a-glance)
- Student Examples: [AQA Exemplification Material](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/art-and-design/gcse/art-and-design-8201/assessment-resources) (Look for "Photography" specific examples to see grade standards).
