# The Anatomy of a Zettel

What does an individual note, a Zettel, look like? There are three components that each Zettel has:

- [A unique identifier. This gives your Zettel an unambiguous address..md](A%20unique%20identifier.%20This%20gives%20your%20Zettel%20an%20unambiguous%20address..md)

- [The body of the Zettel. This is where you write down what you want to capture! The piece of knowl.md](The%20body%20of%20the%20Zettel.%20This%20is%20where%20you%20write%20down%20what%20you%20want%20to%20capture!%20The%20piece%20of%20knowl.md)

- [References. At the bottom of each Zettel, you either reference the source of the knowledge you ca.md](References.%20At%20the%20bottom%20of%20each%20Zettel,%20you%20either%20reference%20the%20source%20of%20the%20knowledge%20you%20ca.md)

It is really that simple. If you are doubting if you are doing it right always come back to the simplicity of these basics. All you need to enable the hypertext, the bare minimum, is an address, that is the unique identifier, and of course some content in the note.
