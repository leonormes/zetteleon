### Card integrity—RESOLVED

Two subtotal boxes don't match the arrows written next to them. Both are now confirmed as addition errors on the card, not transcription errors:

| End | Half | Arrows | Sum | Written | Delta | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | 9,7,7,5,5,3 | 36 | 37 | +1 | Written value is _impossible_—see below |
| 3 | B | 9,7,5,5,5,1 | 32 | 42 | +10 | Arrows verified against the card |

End 1A can be dismissed without re-reading anything. In 5-zone imperial every arrow value is odd (9, 7, 5, 3, 1). Six odd numbers always sum to an even number. 37 is odd, so no six-arrow half-dozen can ever total 37. It is arithmetically impossible regardless of what the arrows were. The correct value is 36.

Parity check across all fourteen half-dozen subtotals on the card: End 1A is the only odd one. Every other box is at least parity-valid.

End 3B is confirmed at 32. You've re-read arrows 7–12 as 9, 7, 5, 5, 5, 1. The written 42 is a ten-point addition slip.

Consequences:

- Grand total: 587 → 576 (−11)
- End 1 dozen: 77 → 76
- End 3 dozen: 78 → 68
- All running totals from End 1 onward shift down; final running total becomes 576
- Handicap: 64.7 → 66.0
- A2 (521): still cleared by +55
- A1 (590): gap widens from 3 to 14

Note also: End 7's Golds box is blank. It should read 6.

Why this matters procedurally: the card was completed with a wrong total. Under AGB Rules of Shooting the archer and scorer are jointly responsible for the card's accuracy, and arithmetic errors are corrected against the recorded arrow values—the arrows govern, not the totals. So the corrected 576 is the legitimate score and there's no integrity issue with the shooting. But hand Jane a card reading 587 that recalculates to 576 and you invite a question you don't need. Correct it on the card, initial the corrections, and get the scorer to countersign if they're available.

### Process Note

The two errors on this card were both _additions_, not arrows. Neither was caught on the day. A parity check catches half of them for free: in 5-zone imperial, every half-dozen subtotal must be even, and every dozen must be even. An odd number in any of those boxes is wrong before you check anything else. Worth a glance down the column before you sign.
