## Photography

GCSE Photography enables students to develop their technical skills with a camera and their understanding of photography as an art form.

- Subtopics:
   - Component 1 (Portfolio): Similar to Art, this coursework component involves developing projects based on themes (e.g., 'Portraiture', 'Landscape', 'Still Life'). Students will experiment with camera settings (aperture, shutter speed, ISO), composition, lighting, and digital editing software (like Adobe Photoshop). They will research photographers to inform their own work.
   - Component 2 (Externally Set Assignment): Students choose a starting point from a paper set by the exam board and produce a portfolio of developmental work, culminating in a final piece created during a 10-hour supervised period.
- Outcomes: Bessie will become technically proficient in using a DSLR camera and editing software. She will learn to communicate ideas and emotions through images and develop a critical understanding of the photographic medium.
