## Combined Science

This course covers content from all three sciences and results in two GCSE grades.

- Subtopics:
   - Biology: Key topics include cell biology, organisation (tissues, organs), infection and response, bioenergetics (photosynthesis and respiration), homeostasis, inheritance, variation, evolution, and ecology.
   - Chemistry: Core concepts include atomic structure, the periodic table, bonding, quantitative chemistry, chemical and energy changes, rates of reaction, organic chemistry, chemical analysis, and the chemistry of the atmosphere.
   - Physics: Pupils will study concepts of energy, electricity, the particle model of matter, atomic structure, forces, waves, and magnetism and electromagnetism.
- Outcomes: Students will gain a solid foundation in the core principles of each science. They will develop their ability to think scientifically, analyse evidence, and understand the implications of science for society.
