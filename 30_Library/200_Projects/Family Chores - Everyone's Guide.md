## Family Chores — Everyone's Guide

**A 10-minute family agreement and everyday guide for Mum (Zofja), Dad (Leon), Rae, Bessie, and Pearl.**

We can read this together, adjust anything that feels off, and start using it straight away.

---

### 1. Our Family Agreement (What We're Signing Up To)

We run our home as **one team** so it stays calm, organised, and fair for everyone.

- **Life skills together:** Learning how to look after a home gives Rae, Bessie, and Pearl real independence for the future.
- **Process over perfection:** Effort and steady habits matter far more than getting everything spotless on day one.
- **Clear checklists, not nagging:** Written checklists show what "done" looks like so nobody has to guess or argue.
- **Tag-team handovers:** If anyone (parent or child) feels overwhelmed, speak up—we step in to support each other without judgement.
- **Red Brain pause:** If someone is in "Red Brain" (overwhelmed, angry, or melting down), all chores and check-ins pause straight away. We calm down and feel safe first, then come back to the job.
- **Music & podcasts welcome:** You are welcome to listen to music, a playlist, an audiobook, or a podcast while you do your jobs.
- **Clear evening screens:** When our shared jobs are done, evening downtime and screens are ready—no nagging needed. (If a job isn't finished at check-in, screens simply wait until it's wrapped up—no drama.)

> **Adjust Together:** Does this agreement feel fair to everyone? Is there anything missing that would make daily jobs calmer or easier?

### 3. Your Daily Rhythm (Morning & After School / Evening)

#### Personal Quarters (Everyone, Every Day)

- **Bed made:** Pull up your duvet and straighten your pillows.
- **Room reset:** Clear clothes and rubbish off the floor.
- **Laundry in the basket:** Put dirty clothes *inside* the downstairs laundry basket (not next to it), and put your clean folded clothes away.
- **Small bin check:** Empty your bedroom bin into the main kitchen/utility bin when it gets full.
- **Entryway & car habit:** Hang up your coat, put shoes in their slot, and take your bags, shoes, and rubbish out of the car when you get out.

#### Daily Communal Reset

- **Kitchen Reset (this week's Kitchen & Fridge helper):**
  - Clear and wipe the dining table (crumb-free).
  - Load the dishwasher (check the spray arms can spin) and unload clean, dry dishes into cupboards.
  - Wipe kitchen worktops, empty the sink strainer, and rinse the sink.
  - Spot-sweep crumbs off the kitchen floor after meals.
- **Front Room 2-minute reset (quick family pick-up):** Clear shoes, mugs, and loose items; straighten cushions and fold throws.
- **Bathroom quick tidy (everyone after use):** Hang up damp towels (put dirty ones in the basket) and rinse toothpaste or soap from the sink.

*Note for Bessie:* On school days, your agreed **Study Sprint** sits alongside your daily jobs before evening screens—worked through calmly on blue paper using your 3-step checklist so there is zero guesswork.

#### Shared Pet Care — Daily (Cat & 2 Snakes)

- **Cat:** Feed morning and evening, check fresh water, scoop/clean the litter tray daily, and brush regularly.
- **2 Snakes:** Daily check that enclosure temperatures and humidity look right, refresh water daily, spot-clean if needed, and keep an eye on shedding.

> [!important] Parent-Only: Our Dog's Addison's Medical Care
> Our dog has Addison's disease, which needs strict medical care. **Only Mum or Dad** give him morning steroid tablets, handle his monthly vet injections, or give any medicine (common dog painkillers like Metacam or Rimadyl are unsafe).
>
> **If you ever notice vomiting, diarrhoea, extreme tiredness, or collapse, tell Mum or Dad straight away.**

### 4. Weekly Rotating Jobs (Week A / B / C — Rotates Every Monday)

The three main weekly jobs and our shared pet care rotate every **Monday** so nobody gets stuck with the same job for long. Check the fridge chart if you aren't sure which week we are on.

#### Weekly Chores Rotation

| Week | Rae | Bessie | Pearl |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |:--- |
| **Week A** | Kitchen & Fridge | Bathrooms & Hall/Stairs | Bins, Front Room & Car |
| **Week B** | Bins, Front Room & Car | Kitchen & Fridge | Bathrooms & Hall/Stairs |
| **Week C** | Bathrooms & Hall/Stairs | Bins, Front Room & Car | Kitchen & Fridge |

#### Shared Pet Rota (Cat & 2 Snakes)

| Week | Cat (Morning/Evening Feed, Water & Litter) | 2 Snakes (Daily Temp/Humidity, Water & Spot-Clean) | Pet Helper (Cat Brush & Pet Area Tidy) |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |:--- |
| **Week A** | Rae | Bessie | Pearl |
| **Week B** | Pearl | Rae | Bessie |
| **Week C** | Bessie | Pearl | Rae |

*(Snake feeding every 1–2 weeks is checked on the family calendar with Mum or Dad.)*

> **Adjust Together:** Would you prefer to rotate pet care weekly on Mondays like this, or swap daily? Let's pick what feels easiest to remember.

#### What Each Weekly Job Covers

- **Kitchen & Fridge:**
  - Steam and wipe the inside of the microwave.
  - Scrub the hob and wipe the tiles behind it.
  - Wipe cupboard doors and handles.
  - Vacuum kitchen edges and mop the kitchen floor.
  - **Weekly quick fridge expiry check** (before the weekly shop): check use-by dates, bin out-of-date items, and wipe any sticky jars or spills.
- **Bathrooms & Hall/Stairs:**
  - Scrub toilet bowls; wipe seats, lids, and flush handles.
  - Scrub bath and shower, clean basins and taps, wipe mirrors, and mop both bathroom floors.
  - Restock loo roll and swap in fresh hand towels.
  - Empty bathroom bins into the main kitchen/utility collection.
  - **Weekly hall, stairs & landing vacuum** (including a quick wipe of handrails/banisters).
- **Bins, Front Room & Car:**
  - **Sunday evening bin reset:** Collect indoor bin bags (bathrooms, bedrooms, office, kitchen) into the outdoor **Wheelie Bins**, put fresh liners in all indoor bins, and wheel the outdoor bins out for Monday collection.
  - **Front Room:** Dust surfaces/skirting, vacuum floor (including under coffee table and sofa edges), and wipe TV screen (microfibre cloth) and glass/mirrors.
  - **Car quick clear:** Take out loose items and rubbish, shake mats outside, vacuum seats and footwells, and wipe inside glass plus the steering wheel and gear stick.
- **Everyone's Own Bedroom (Weekly):** Change your bedding, dust your desk and shelves, vacuum your carpet, and empty your bedroom bin into the main collection.

### 5. How Our Daily & Weekly Check-Ins Work

- **Daily Check-In:** **6:00 pm daily** (a calm 10-minute check together — see [[How to Run the Daily Chore Check-In]]) `[Confirm or adjust at our family meeting]`
- **Weekly Reset & Family Review:** **Sunday 5:00 pm** (weekly jobs finished and checked + a friendly 10-minute family chat: wins of the week, next week's rotation, and anything to tweak — see [[How to Run the Weekly Family Meeting]]) `[Confirm or adjust at our family meeting]`
- **One Calm Reminder:** Mum or Dad give **one calm reminder about 30 minutes beforehand**—no nagging or repeated prompts.

#### If a Job Gets Missed (Our 4-Step Positive Reset)

Forgetting happens. Here is how we fix it calmly together ([[How to Run the Four-Step Non-Punitive Chore Reset]]):

1. **Calm check:** Pause and look at the checklist together—no shouting and no blame. (If anyone is in Red Brain, calm down first.)
2. **Starter 2-minute step:** Pick one tiny first step together (*"Start with rubbish, or start with clothes?"*) to get moving.
3. **Tag-team if stuck:** Ask Mum, Dad, or a sibling for a quick tag-team assist or handover if you are genuinely stuck or overwhelmed.
4. **Tweak the system on Sunday:** If the same job keeps tripping us up, bring it to our Sunday family review and improve the checklist or timing together.

> **Adjust Together:** Do **6:00 pm daily** and **Sunday 5:00 pm** fit around school clubs, dinner, and activities—or should we shift them?
