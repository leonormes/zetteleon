## English Language

The GCSE English Language course is designed to develop students' ability to read a wide range of texts with understanding, to write effectively for different purposes and audiences, and to use grammar, spelling, and punctuation accurately.

- Subtopics:
   - Reading (Fiction and Non-fiction): Pupils will study high-quality texts from the 19th, 20th, and 21st centuries. They learn to identify and interpret explicit and implicit information, analyse how writers use language and structure to create effects and influence the reader, and compare writers' ideas and perspectives across different texts.
   - Writing (Creative and Transactional): Students will develop their skills in creative writing, producing descriptions and narratives. They will also learn to write for specific purposes and audiences, such as writing to argue, persuade, or advise.
   - Spoken Language: Pupils will be assessed on their ability to present information and ideas clearly, respond to questions and feedback, and use Standard English effectively in formal settings. This is a separate endorsement and does not contribute to the final GCSE grade.
- Outcomes: Bessie will learn to be a critical reader of diverse texts and a confident writer who can adapt her style for various tasks. She will be able to articulate her ideas verbally with precision and clarity.
