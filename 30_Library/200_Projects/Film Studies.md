## Film Studies

This creative and analytical subject explores film as a major art form of the 20th and 21st centuries.

- Subtopics:
   - US Film: Comparative study of two US films from different periods, exploring genre, narrative, and context.
   - US Independent Film: Analysis of a contemporary US independent film.
   - Global Cinema: Study of one global English-language film and one non-English-language film, focusing on narrative, representation, and style.
   - Contemporary UK Film: Detailed study of a recent British film, analysing its aesthetic qualities.
   - Production: A practical component where students produce a screenplay for a short film, a shooting script, or a film sequence, accompanied by an evaluative analysis.
- Outcomes: Bessie will learn to analyse and deconstruct films, understanding the significance of cinematography, mise-en-scène, editing, and sound. She will also develop practical skills in filmmaking or screenwriting.
