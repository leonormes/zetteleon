## Bang & Olufsen Beoplay H7

Premium closed-back, over-ear Bluetooth headphones in black leather. The H7 supports both wireless listening and passive listening through a 3.5 mm cable, but does not provide active noise cancellation.

### Technical Details

| Area | Detail |
|---|---|
| Drivers | 40 mm electro-dynamic |
| Frequency response | 20 Hz–22 kHz |
| Wireless | Bluetooth 4.1 with aptX Low Latency and AAC |
| Battery | Removable 770 mAh lithium-ion |
| Rated playback | Up to 20 hours over Bluetooth |
| Charge time | Approximately 2.5 hours over Micro-USB |
| Wired mode | Detachable 1.2 m 3.5 mm cable; can operate without battery power |
| Controls | Aluminium touch interface on right ear cup |
| Microphone | Built-in omnidirectional electret microphone |
| Materials | Aluminium, leather, fabric and polymer |
| Dimensions | 197 × 225 × 40 mm |
| Weight | 280 g |
