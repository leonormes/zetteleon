## Mathematics

The GCSE Mathematics curriculum builds on KS3, aiming to develop fluent knowledge, skills, and understanding of mathematical methods and concepts.

- Subtopics:
   - Number: Working with integers, fractions, decimals, and percentages, including standard form and estimation.
   - Algebra: Manipulating algebraic expressions, solving linear and quadratic equations, working with graphs of functions, and understanding sequences.
   - Ratio, Proportion, and Rates of Change: Solving problems involving ratio and proportion, and understanding growth and decay.
   - Geometry and Measures: Applying properties of shapes, using Pythagoras' theorem and trigonometry, and calculating area and volume.
   - Statistics & Probability: Representing and analysing data, interpreting charts and graphs, and calculating probabilities.
- Outcomes: Bessie will be able to apply mathematical knowledge to solve problems, reason mathematically, interpret and communicate mathematical information in a variety of forms.
