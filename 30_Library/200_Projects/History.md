## History

GCSE History encourages students to understand the past, both of Britain and the wider world, and to develop skills of critical thinking and analysis.

- Subtopics: The course is typically structured with four modules:
   - Thematic Study: Traces a theme over a long period, such as 'Medicine in Britain, c1250–present'.
   - Period Study: Focuses on a specific period of international history, such as 'Conflict and Tension, 1918-1939' or 'Russia, 1894-1945'.
   - British Depth Study: An in-depth study of a period of British history, often including a study of a historic site (e.g., 'Elizabethan England, c1568–1603').
   - Modern Depth Study: Focuses on a major 20th-century event or period, such as 'Germany, 1890-1945: Democracy and Dictatorship'.
- Outcomes: She will learn how to analyse historical sources, understand different interpretations of the past, and construct well-supported arguments. She will gain a deep understanding of how past events have shaped the modern world.
