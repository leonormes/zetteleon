## Art

GCSE Art and Design allows students to explore and develop their creativity using a wide range of materials and techniques.

- Subtopics:
   - Component 1 (Portfolio): This is a coursework component where students develop projects based on a theme or a starting point. They will produce a portfolio of work demonstrating their skills in drawing, painting, printmaking, sculpture, and other media. This includes research into the work of other artists and the development of their own ideas.
   - Component 2 (Externally Set Assignment): Students respond to a theme set by the exam board. They will have a preparatory period to research and develop ideas in a sketchbook, culminating in a final piece produced under timed, exam conditions (typically 10 hours).
- Outcomes: Bessie will develop her practical artistic skills and the ability to think creatively and independently. She will learn to analyse art, document her creative process, and produce a personal and meaningful body of work.
