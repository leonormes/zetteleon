## A Personalised Educational Pathway for Bessie Ormes: A Comprehensive GCSE Support Plan

### Introduction: Building on Strengths for a Successful GCSE Journey

This report outlines a comprehensive educational pathway for Bessie Ormes as she transitions into her GCSE studies. It is designed to be a collaborative and supportive blueprint for Bessie, her family, and her school. Central to this plan is the recognition of Bessie's unique and powerful combination of talents: she is inquisitive, caring, thoughtful, and highly creative. Her passions for Art, History, Film, and her love for horses are not merely extracurricular interests; they are significant assets that can be harnessed to fuel her academic journey and build a profound sense of achievement.

The purpose of this document is to provide a clear, structured overview of Bessie's chosen GCSE subjects and to offer a suite of tailored strategies that are directly informed by her learning profile, which includes ADHD, dyslexia-related needs, and anxiety. The approach is holistic, weaving together academic guidance with practical techniques for maintaining focus, managing workload, and building confidence. By understanding the curriculum's demands and aligning them with Bessie's strengths, this plan aims to create an empowering and positive learning experience. It seeks to demystify the GCSE process, reduce anxiety, and pave a clear path towards Bessie's aspiration of becoming a special effects make-up artist, ensuring her education is not only successful but also deeply meaningful and personally rewarding.

## Conclusion and Recommended Next Steps: Implementing Bessie's Success Plan

This report has detailed a comprehensive, personalised pathway for Bessie's GCSE studies. The recurring themes are clear: the need to break down tasks into manageable chunks, the critical role of technology in adapting resources, the power of multi-sensory and active learning, the importance of connecting learning to her personal interests, and the implementation of structured routines to manage anxiety. The overarching goal is to create an environment where Bessie feels empowered, confident, and successful.

To translate this plan into effective action, the following collaborative steps are recommended:

- Step 1: The Collaborative Team Meeting: The immediate priority is for Bessie and her parents to request a meeting with the school's Special Educational Needs Coordinator (SENCO). This report should be shared with the SENCO and relevant Heads of Department (e.g., Art, English, Maths) in advance of the meeting. The SENCO's role is to coordinate support across the school, liaise with teaching staff, and ensure parents are closely involved in the decision-making process, transforming the parent-school relationship into a supportive partnership.29
- Step 2: Formalising Accommodations and Access Arrangements: The meeting should be used to formally agree upon and document the specific in-class accommodations and official exam access arrangements that Bessie is entitled to. Based on this report, these must include:
   - Guaranteed use of a laptop for all extended writing tasks in class, homework, and internal/external examinations.
   - A school-wide commitment to providing printed resources (handouts, worksheets, presentation slides) on blue paper to eliminate the need for copying from the board.
   - Confirmation of her eligibility for 25% Extra Time in all timed examinations, a standard arrangement for students with her profile.
   - An agreement on strategies for managing anxiety, such as pre-arranged access to a quiet space if she feels overwhelmed.2
- Step 3: Empowering Bessie with a "Learning Passport": Following the meeting, the SENCO can work with Bessie to create a student-friendly, one-page summary of her key learning strategies and accommodations. This "Learning Passport" is a simple but powerful tool that she can share with each of her subject teachers. It empowers her to take ownership of her learning and advocate for her own needs in a confident and structured way, fostering independence.31
- Step 4: Establishing a Communication and Review Cycle: It is vital to recognise that this plan is the beginning of a dynamic process. The team should agree on a regular schedule for check-ins—perhaps a brief meeting every half-term involving Bessie, her parents, and the SENCO or another designated mentor. This creates a consistent feedback loop to monitor which strategies are working, adjust those that are not, celebrate successes, and proactively address any new challenges or anxieties that may arise.31

Ultimately, Bessie's journey through her GCSEs should be one that not only leads to academic qualifications but also reinforces her self-belief and passion for learning. Her unique way of seeing and interacting with the world is a profound strength. With a dedicated support team working in collaboration and implementing these tailored strategies, she is exceptionally well-equipped to thrive in her studies and progress confidently toward her future aspirations.
