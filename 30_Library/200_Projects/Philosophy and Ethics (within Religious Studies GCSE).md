## Philosophy and Ethics (Within Religious Studies GCSE)

This subject encourages students to explore ultimate questions about life, morality, and belief, considering different religious and non-religious viewpoints.

- Subtopics: This is usually taken as part of a Religious Studies GCSE.
   - The Study of Religions: In-depth study of the beliefs, teachings, and practices of two major world religions (e.g., Christianity and Islam).
   - Thematic Studies (Philosophy and Ethics):
      - Relationships and Families: Exploring religious teachings on the purpose of families, marriage, divorce, and sexuality.
      - Religion and Life: Examining religious perspectives on the origins of the universe, the sanctity of life, abortion, and euthanasia.
      - The Existence of God and Revelation: Studying philosophical arguments for and against the existence of God.
      - Religion, Peace and Conflict: Investigating religious views on war, violence, terrorism, and pacifism.
      - Religion, Crime and Punishment: Exploring attitudes towards law, justice, suffering, and the death penalty.
- Outcomes: She will develop her ability to think critically about complex ethical and philosophical issues, understand diverse perspectives, and construct reasoned arguments.
