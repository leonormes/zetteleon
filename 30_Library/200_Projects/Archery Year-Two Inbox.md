## Archery Year-Two Inbox

A parking list for coaching ideas, YouTube cues and technique detail that are not part of the year-one plan in [[Archery Year-One Coaching Framework (Months 5-12)]]. Writing an idea here is permission not to act on it yet.

### Rule

- Capture the idea in one line with its source. Do not try it.
- Review this list once, in May, when the outdoor transition block ends.
- Ideas that survive a coach conversation move into the step notes. The rest stay here.

### Already Shelved

- Back-tension anatomy: which muscle, scapular angles. See [[Archery Shot Process - Loading]].
- Clicker timing and reaction training. See [[Archery Shot Process - Expansion]], [[Clicker Reaction Time]].
- Active extensor release. See [[Forearm Release Strategy]].
- Micro-tuning: walk-back, plunger tension, tiller, group tuning at distance.
- Arrow experiments: point weight, FOC, fletching.
- Stabiliser balance experiments.
- Draw weight above 36 lb.
- Visualisation scripts and biofeedback. See the biofeedback drill in [[Archery Shot Process - Anchor]].
- Anatomy resource deep-dives. See [[Digital Archery Anatomy Resources]].
- Alternative coach views (KSL, NTS and others) in the step notes. See [[Archery Shot Process - Open Questions]].
- Refine-level flow markers: rhythm blend, tight raise-to-release timing, impassive demeanour, post-release kinaesthetic check, breath-hum test. See [[Archery Learning Sequence - Build the Whole Process First]].
