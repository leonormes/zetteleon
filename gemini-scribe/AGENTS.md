# AGENTS.md

This file provides context about this Obsidian vault for AI agents.

## Vault Overview

This vault is a sophisticated Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) system and digital garden, bridging deep theoretical research in mathematics and type theory with practical software engineering and operational protocols. It serves as a comprehensive 'Second Brain' that integrates technical expertise, philosophical inquiry, and structured ADHD management strategies into a unified system.

## Organization

The vault utilizes a modular structure that blends Zettelkasten principles with functional hierarchies:

- **Capturing & Meta**: `00_Inbox` handles new inputs (including Readwise), while `10_System` stores the vault's logic, such as AI prompts and templates.
- **Temporal & Thinking**: `01_journals` tracks daily progress, and `20_Thinking/21_Workbench` hosts active 'HEAD' notes for ongoing cognitive work and LLM-assisted drafting.
- **The Library (Core)**: This is the primary knowledge base, featuring:
  - `100_zettelkasten`: Over 900 atomic, declarative notes with full-sentence titles.
  - `200_projects`: Categorised into Infrastructure, Development, Maths, and Personal.
  - `MoC`: Maps of Content acting as central hubs for complex topics.
  - `ops` and `SoT`: Definitive protocols and 'Sources of Truth' for technical and personal workflows.

Notes are densely interconnected through bidirectional links and MOCs, facilitating a 'bottom-up' knowledge synthesis where atomic ideas are aggregated into higher-level frameworks.

## User Preferences

The User employs a highly systematic and intellectual approach, preferring declarative, full-sentence note titles that capture core insights (e.g., 'A Goals-First Mentality Restricts Happiness'). There is a strong emphasis on establishing 'Sources of Truth' (SoT) and repeatable 'Operations' (ops), indicating a preference for precision, consistency, and protocol-driven workflows.

Responses should be technical, concise, and provided in British English (en-GB). The User values high-density information and expects the agent to respect existing organisational structures, particularly the distinction between active 'Thinking' (found in the Workbench) and archived 'Library' knowledge.

## Custom Instructions

- Before providing technical or procedural advice, search `30_Library/ops/` or `30_Library/SoT/` to ensure alignment with existing protocols.
- When suggesting or creating new notes, use descriptive, declarative full-sentence titles (e.g., 'Master Micro-Actions & Starter Tasks').
- Use existing templates in `10_System/templates/` for any structural notes (e.g., daily notes or project headers).
- Refer to 'MOCs' (Maps of Content) to provide context or overviews for broad topics.
- Maintain the distinction between active workbench files in `20_Thinking/21_Workbench` and vetted knowledge in the `Library`.
- Support the 'Research-to-Action' protocol by focusing on actionable outcomes from theoretical research or documentation.
