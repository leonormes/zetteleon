## AGENTS.md

This file provides context about this Obsidian vault for AI agents.

### Vault Overview

This vault is a high-fidelity 'thinking engine' and personal knowledge management system that bridges technical infrastructure engineering (Kubernetes, Helm, Cloud Architecture) with cognitive optimization and architectural theory. It serves as a living laboratory for AI-human collaboration, containing a sophisticated library of 30+ specialized AI agent prompts and structured 'Systems of Thought' (SoT) to manage complex mental workflows and ADHD.

The system is designed to transform rapid capture and forensic analysis into permanent, synthesized knowledge, with a specific focus on the 'FitFile' infrastructure ecosystem, Rust development, and the mitigation of cognitive load through 'Context Engineering.'

### Organization

The vault utilizes a rigorous numbered folder structure to manage the lifecycle of information from ingestion to archival:

- **00_Inbox**: A landing zone for new data, forensic analyses (e.g., Helm Chart forensics), and Readwise syncs.
- **01_journals/Dailies**: Temporal logs capturing daily progress and activity.
- **10_Actions/11_Projects**: Active, high-priority work including development (Rust, CUE) and personal projects (Bessie Room Renovation).
- **10_System**: The 'Vault OS' containing `prompts` for AI agents, `templates` (PRODOS, Learning Projects), and `fileClasses` for metadata.
- **20_Thinking/21_Workbench**: A scratchpad for active synthesis; notes prefixed with `HEAD -` denote current mental focus or work-in-progress.
- **30_Library**: The primary knowledge base, subdivided into `100_zettelkasten` (atomic concepts), `200_projects` (deep technical/historical context, including Infrastructure and FitFile deployments), `MoC` (Maps of Content), and `SoT` (Systems of Thought/Protocols).
- **99_Archive**: Processed items, with a significant portion organized in `Processed_By_Agent`, indicating high levels of AI-assisted maintenance.

Linking is dense and structural, using MoCs as topical hubs and SoT files as authoritative procedural manuals.
