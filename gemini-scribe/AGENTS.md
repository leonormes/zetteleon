# AGENTS.md

This file provides context about this Obsidian vault for AI agents.

## Vault Overview

This vault is a sophisticated Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) system and digital garden, primarily focused on software engineering, AI agent development, and systems thinking. It functions as a 'Second Brain' that bridges deep theoretical research (mathematics and type theory) with highly practical operational protocols and project management.

## Organization

The vault follows a highly structured, modular organization scheme that blends Zettelkasten principles with a functional folder hierarchy:

- **Capturing & Meta**: `00_Inbox` serves as the entry point for new information, while `10_System` contains the vault's 'engine', including an extensive library of custom AI prompts and templates.
- **Temporal & Thinking**: `01_journals` tracks daily activity, and `20_Thinking` (Workbench) provides space for active cognition and dashboarding.
- **The Library (Core)**: This is the primary knowledge base, divided into:
  - `100_zettelkasten`: Thousands of atomic, declarative notes with full-sentence titles.
  - `200_projects`: Categorised into Infrastructure, Development, Maths, and Personal.
  - `MoC`: Maps of Content that act as central hubs for complex topics.
  - `ops` and `SoT`: 'Operations' and 'Source of Truth' documents provide definitive protocols and technical documentation.

Notes are connected through heavy use of MOCs and bidirectional linking between atomic Zettelkasten notes, ensuring a dense web of contextual relationships.

## User Preferences

The User prefers a highly systematic and structured approach to information. Note titles are often declarative sentences (e.g., 'A Goals-First Mentality Restricts Happiness'), suggesting a preference for 'bottom-up' knowledge synthesis. There is a clear emphasis on establishing 'Sources of Truth' (SoT) and repeatable 'Operations' (ops), indicating that the User values precision, consistency, and protocol-driven workflows.

The writing style is intellectual, technical, and concise. The User likely expects the agent to respect existing organizational structures, use British English (en-GB), and provide high-density information rather than generic summaries.

## Custom Instructions

- Before providing technical advice, search the `30_Library/ops/` or `30_Library/SoT/` folders to see if a protocol or 'Source of Truth' already exists for that topic.
- When suggesting new notes, follow the Zettelkasten pattern: use descriptive, full-sentence titles that capture the core insight.
- Refer to 'MOCs' (Maps of Content) when the User asks for an overview of a broad topic.
- Use the existing templates in `10_System/templates/` for any new structural notes (like daily notes or project headers).
- Maintain the distinction between 'Thinking' (active work) and 'Library' (archived/vetted knowledge).
