## Agentic Collaboration Shift

Software engineering is transitioning from an "AI-assisted autocomplete" model to one of "agentic collaboration," where the engineer's role shifts from a passive user of tools to an active manager of autonomous agents. This paradigm shift requires a focus on system-level orchestration rather than individual code completion.

### Scope & Conditions

Applies to modern software development environments utilizing LLMs for complex, multi-step tasks.

### Evidence

> "Software engineering is transitioning from 'AI-assisted autocomplete' to 'agentic collaboration'."

### Implications

- Engineers must move from passive users to active managers of autonomous processes.
- Shifts focus from individual code completion to system-level orchestration.
