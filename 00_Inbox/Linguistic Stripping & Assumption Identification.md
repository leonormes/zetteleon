title: Linguistic Stripping & Assumption Identification
---

1. Linguistic Stripping & Assumption Identification: * _Action:_ Strip away rhetorical noise, jargon, and attitude indicators to isolate the raw declarative statements.
    - _Enhancement:_ Actively identify the author's _unstated assumptions_. Often, the logical starting point of a text is entirely implicit. Bring these underlying premises to the surface before moving to the next filter.
    - Phase 1.1—Linguistic Stripping
        - Risk: in some genres, the rhetoric _is_ the content (political speeches, literary criticism, advertising). Stripping is lossy, not neutral. Add a qualifier: this step is valid for _argumentative prose_, invalid for rhetoric-as-object-of-study.
        - Good move: surfacing unstated assumptions. Consider adding an explicit steelman step before judgement—construct the strongest version of the author's case before you test it. Protects you from dismissing ideas you didn't fully engage.
