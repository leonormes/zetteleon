The video, titled **"The Highest Levels of Thinking Explained (it's not actually thinking)"** from the channel Seeking Satsang, presents a conceptual framework that describes a progression from basic self-centered cognition to a state of pure, non-dual awareness.

The model is structured around six stages, with the transition from the fourth to the fifth level marking the shift from **Thinking** (cognition) to **Awareness** (meta-cognition/being).

Here is a breakdown of the framework, focusing on the underlying logic of each stage:

### Framework: The Six Levels of Cognitive & Self-Awareness

The first four levels focus on an individual's engagement with their mind and the minds of others.

| Level | Conceptual Focus | Mechanism & Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1st Person Thinking** | **The Object** | One sees others as objects or obstacles without understanding that they possess independent thoughts or reasons for their actions. Judgements are based solely on face-value perception \[[00:42](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8UL-iATA1Nc&t=42)\]. |
| **2nd Person Thinking** | **The Other Thinker** | One recognises that another person has thoughts different from their own. However, this recognition lacks further extrapolation, meaning it does not lead to empathy, compromise, or a shift in one's own position \[[02:23](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8UL-iATA1Nc&t=143)\]. |
| **3rd Person Thinking** | **Mutual Perspective** | One can think about *the other person's thoughts* regarding oneself. This capacity for mutual perspective allows for an understanding of the other's motivations ("why they want what they want"), which serves as the fundamental basis for **empathy** and **compromise** \[[03:48](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8UL-iATA1Nc&t=228)\]. |
| **4th Person Thinking** | **Self-Reflection & Choice** | One can think about one's *own* thoughts and perspective in relation to the whole world of possibilities. This level enables one to choose an identity or belief system with greater clarity. It is the **doorway to higher awareness** but often leads to overthinking and analysis paralysis \[[05:50](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8UL-iATA1Nc&t=350)\]. |

The subsequent levels transcend thinking and move into a state of non-identification.

| Level | Conceptual Focus | Mechanism & Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **5th Person Awareness** | **The Observer** | The realisation that **"I am not my mind."** This is the beginning of meta-cognition, where one steps out of the mind to become the observer of one's own thoughts, emotions, and sensations. It is a state of observation and awareness, not active thinking, allowing one to choose not to identify with or give energy to arising thoughts \[[08:32](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8UL-iATA1Nc&t=512)\]. |
| **6th Person Awareness** | **The 'I' / Pure Beingness** | It is the ultimate awareness or "beingness" (the "I") that is behind, includes, and encompasses all forms, including the observer, the mind, and the body. At this stage, thinking is considered counterproductive. It is a state of complete, peaceful non-identification, where all experience is viewed as arising and dancing within this singular, universal awareness \[[11:04](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8UL-iATA1Nc&t=664)\]. |
