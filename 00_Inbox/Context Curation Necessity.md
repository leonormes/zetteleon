## Context Curation Necessity

Engineers must deliberately curate, isolate, and compress information provided to LLMs to compensate for the model's inherent lack of business judgment and technical context. Effective "context engineering" ensures that the AI remains focused on relevant logic and avoids confusion caused by informational noise.

### Scope & Conditions

Critical for interaction with agentic LLMs where context window management directly impacts reasoning quality.

### Evidence

> "The primary lever for success... is context engineering: the deliberate curation, isolation, and compression of information provided to the Large Language Model."

### Implications

- Prevents model confusion by removing irrelevant noise.
- Improves the precision of generated solutions by focusing on specific technical constraints.
