## Claim-Titled Notes Invite Inferential Links That Topical Notes Lack

A topical note ("What is X?") holds implicit, uncommitted propositions: retrievable elsewhere, inert in an argument. A claim-titled note commits to one contestable proposition, and that commitment raises the question of what supports, contradicts or presupposes it, which lowers the cost of typed, inferential links.
