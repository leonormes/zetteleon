## Core Thesis

Our brains evolved mental shortcuts, known as heuristics, that help us solve simple problems quickly but consistently lead to cognitive biases when applied to complex modern issues. The scientific method—particularly through rigorous experimental design and community peer review—serves as a systematic tool designed explicitly to counteract these innate biological biases and build durable, objective knowledge.

## Missing Context

- **The 'GI Joe Fallacy':** The video suggests that knowing about biases helps you fight them. However, cognitive science demonstrates that simply being aware of a bias does very little to prevent you from falling for it in the moment.
- **Systemic scientific bias:** The video presents scientific methodologies and peer review as highly effective filters for individual bias. It omits the systemic issues within modern science—such as publication bias (the tendency to only publish positive results), funding pressures, and the ongoing replication crisis in fields like psychology and medicine—which demonstrate that communal science still struggles deeply with collective bias.
