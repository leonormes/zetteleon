## Photons Not Experiencing Time Shows Human Time Perception Is One Limited Viewpoint

That photons do not experience time shows the human perspective is one specific, limited viewpoint. The underlying reality that mathematics seeks to describe may therefore be timeless.

### Scope & Conditions

A rhetorical inference in the source, and the hedge may be timeless is part of the claim. The physics of photons is covered in the linked relativity notes.

### Evidence

> "The fact that photons don't experience time simply reinforces that our human perspective is not the only one; it's a specific, limited viewpoint."
> (Gemini session, section Is Mathematics Invented or Discovered)

### Implications

- Human experience of time should not be assumed to be the only valid frame.
