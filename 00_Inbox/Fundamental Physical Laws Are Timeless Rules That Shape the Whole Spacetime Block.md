## Fundamental Physical Laws Are Timeless Rules That Shape the Whole Spacetime Block

On the block universe view, the fundamental laws of physics are timeless rules that fix the overall shape and structure of the spacetime block. The Higgs mechanism, for example, does not happen in time but describes relationships within the block.

### Scope & Conditions

An interpretation within the block universe model. It does not deny that the patterns the laws govern are made over time.

### Evidence

> "The fundamental laws of physics are not processes that happen in time; they are the timeless rules that dictate the overall shape and structure of the entire spacetime block."
> (Gemini session, section A Timeless View of Causality)

### Implications

- The objection that interaction with the Higgs field needs time and cause and effect is answered by redescribing it as a fixed relationship.
