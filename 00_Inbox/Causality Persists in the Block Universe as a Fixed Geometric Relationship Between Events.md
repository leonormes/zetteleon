## Causality Persists in the Block Universe as a Fixed Geometric Relationship Between Events

In the block universe model, cause and effect are permanent geometric relationships between an earlier and a later event in four-dimensional spacetime. They do not unfold in real time, and repetition is simply a repeating shape along the time axis.

### Scope & Conditions

Holds within the block universe (eternalist) model only. It does not say that model is correct.

### Evidence

> "The pattern of causality is a permanent, geometric feature of spacetime, not something that unfolds in real-time."
> (Gemini session, section Physics, Time, and the Block Universe)

### Implications

- Causal order and temporal patterns survive even if the flow of time does not.
