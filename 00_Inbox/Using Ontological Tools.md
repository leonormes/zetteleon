## Using Ontological Tools

   There are also practical tools and techniques from ontology design that can assist in building and organizing your knowledge system:

- Ontology Languages: If you want to take a more formal approach, you could explore tools like OWL (Web Ontology Language) to formally represent the relationships between concepts in your knowledge system.

- Knowledge Graphs: Tools like Neo4j or RDF databases are often used for representing and querying ontologies in a structured way. You could use these for more sophisticated analyses of your knowledge graph, especially if you're dealing with large amounts of information.
