# 15a-maxwell's_demon

Maxwell's demon is a thought experiment that appears to disprove the second law of thermodynamics. It was proposed by the physicist James Clerk Maxwell in 1867. The demon is an imaginary creature that can detect and react to the motions of individual molecules, apparently violating the second law of thermodynamics. However, it is now understood that the demon requires energy to perform its function, and thus it does not actually violate the laws of thermodynamics.
