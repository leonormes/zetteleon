- Evidence: "elite archers do not simply relax their flexors; they actively contract the extensor digitorum... synchronized perfectly with the audible snap of the clicker, allows the string to slip from the fingers with minimal interference"

### Atom 4: Formaster Shot Trainer Isolation
- Kind: procedure
- Statement: The Formaster or Astra Shot Trainer uses an elbow sling connected to the bowstring to bypass the hands and forearms, forcing the archer to draw and hold the bow weight exclusively using back and shoulder muscles.
- Scope & Conditions: Used as a training aid to build back tension endurance or diagnose release collapse.
- Evidence: "By drawing the bow with the elbow sling, the load is completely bypassed around the hands, wrists, and forearms. This forces the archer to draw and hold the bow weight using exclusively their back and shoulder girdle."
- Implications:
  - Allows for over-weight holds to safely induce muscle fatigue in the back without risking tendon damage in the fingers.
  - Executing a live shot with the trainer provides immediate physical shock feedback if the archer "collapses" (loses back tension) upon release.
