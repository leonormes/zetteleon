### 2\. The Argumentation Theorist: The Claim and the Warrant

Stephen Toulmin explicitly rejects the sterile, mathematical view of the formal logicians. He argues that propositions in the wild are not just true/false abstractions; they are living components of an argument.

- **The Rule:** Toulmin renames propositions as **Claims**. A claim cannot stand alone; its validity depends on the *Data* provided and the unstated *Warrant* that bridges the data to the claim *(Toulmin, The Uses of Argument, Ch. 3)*.

- **The Lens:** *Warrant* and *Assumption Set*. Toulmin teaches us to look at a proposition not just for its truth value, but for the buried rules (warrants) that allow the author to assert it.
