## Core Thesis

Philosophy matters because it addresses fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, and morality that the physical sciences cannot answer. By applying "common-sense" ethical intuitionism—starting with concrete, uncontroversial moral examples rather than abstract, dogmatic theories—individuals can systematically resolve complex ethical and societal problems while avoiding the absurd conclusions often generated by rigid frameworks like utilitarianism or strict egalitarianism.
