## 🧊 SOP: Cold Storage Sanitisation (Fridge)

**Objective:** To maintain a food-safe environment, prevent cross-contamination, and optimise thermal efficiency.

### 🧪 Required Kit

- **Cleaning Agent:** Anti-bacterial surface spray (e.g., Dettol or Milton) or a solution of warm water and bicarbonate of soda.
- **Tools:** 2x Microfibre cloths (one damp for cleaning, one dry for polishing), 1x old toothbrush (for seals/corners).
- **Safety:** Ensure hands are washed before and after. Do not use bleach near food surfaces.

---

### 📥 Phase 1: The Extraction (Top-Down Logic)

*Work shelf by shelf to prevent food temperature from rising too high.*

1. **Clear Shelf:** Move items from the top shelf to the kitchen worktop.
2. **Audit:** * Check **'Use By'** dates (Mandatory disposal if passed).
	- Check **'Best Before'** dates (Quality check; notify parents if stale).
	- Wipe the base of any sticky jars (jam, condiments) before they go back in.

### 🧼 Phase 2: The Sanitisation

1. **Spray:** Apply the anti-bacterial spray directly to the glass shelf or plastic bin.
2. **Agitate:** Use the damp cloth to remove spills. Use the toothbrush for the "Gully" (the drainage hole at the back) and the rubber door seals.
3. **Dry:** Use the dry microfibre cloth to buff the glass. *Residual moisture causes frost build-up.*

### 📤 Phase 3: The Restock (Organisation Logic)

*Follow the UK Food Standards Agency (FSA) shelving logic:*

- **Top/Middle Shelves:** Ready-to-eat foods (dairy, deli meats, leftovers).
- **Bottom Shelf:** Raw meat/fish (must be in sealed containers to prevent drips).
- **Drawers (Crispers):** Fruit, vegetables, and salad (keep separate from raw meat).
- **Door Bins:** Items with natural preservatives (juices, condiments, butter).
