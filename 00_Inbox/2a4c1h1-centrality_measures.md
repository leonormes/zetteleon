# Centrality Measures

Centrality measures are a vital tool for understanding networks, which can be represented as graphs.
These algorithms use graph theory to calculate the importance of any given node within the network. They help cut through noisy data, revealing the most important parts of the network that need attention. There are various centrality measures, each with its own definition of "importance"

This concept is useful for analyzing knowledge systems like your Zettelkasten. In your context, you can compute centrality measures to find which notes (nodes) are the most connected, most influential, or most essential for linking different ideas.
