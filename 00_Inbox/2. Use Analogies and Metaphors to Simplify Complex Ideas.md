## 2\. **Use Analogies and Metaphors to Simplify Complex Ideas**

Metaphors can help highlight the flaws in delaying security without directly challenging anyone's authority.

- *Example:* "Security in a medical records system is like the foundation of a building. It's not just a feature you can add later; it's something that supports the entire structure. Retrofitting a foundation after the building is up can be time-consuming, expensive, and sometimes impossible."
