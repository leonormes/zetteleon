## 1\. Core Psychological Constructs

### 1\.1 Shame & Self-Esteem

What the evidence says

- Chronic failure experiences predict internalised shame in ADHD (Young 2005; Øie 2022).

- Compassion-Focused Therapy (CFT) reduces shame in neurodivergent samples; a small RCT in adults with ADHD (Knouse 2019) showed medium-to-large effect sizes on self-criticism.

- Schema Therapy targets “defectiveness/shame” and “unrelenting standards” schemas that map closely onto ADHD narratives (Ramsay & Rostain 2015).

Actionable principles

➤ Begin with **“threat–drive–soothe mapping”** (CFT) to notice how perfectionism (drive) and rumination (threat) spike after ADHD slips; practise 60-sec *soothing-rhythm breathing* 3× daily to prime the soothe system before executive tasks.

➤ Use **compassionate imagery “micro-bursts”** (2-min guided recordings) rather than standard 10-min scripts to accommodate shorter attention spans.

- In Schema work, re-script critical memories by writing a dialogue between “Present-Me” and “Then-Me”; end each entry with a concrete competency the adult possesses today.

- *Contraindication:* Intensive imagery can transiently increase emotional flooding in individuals with complex trauma; proceed with clinician guidance.

### 1\.2 Cognitive Distortions

What the evidence says

- Dichotomous thinking in ADHD is linked to reduced prefrontal–striatal connectivity (Mowinckel 2022).

- Metacognitive CBT (MCBT) adds planning + self-monitoring modules and outperforms skills-only CBT on reducing “all-or-nothing” self-talk (Sibley 2023).

- ACT “cognitive defusion” decreases identification with negative labels (“lazy”, “stupid”) (Hayes 2020).

Actionable principles

➤ **Metacognitive Post-mortem**: after any task, answer three timed prompts (“What helped?”, “What hindered?”, “What can I tweak?”) before the dopamine drop; trains flexible evaluation.

- **Defusion cards**: write persistent labels on sticky notes, carry for a week, read aloud preceded by “I’m noticing the thought that…”. Repeated distancing lowers believability ratings (ACT micro-study, Hassed 2019).

- *Contraindication:* Defusion can feel dismissive if self-esteem is fragile; pair with self-compassion work first.

### 1\.3 Identity & Acceptance

What the evidence says

- Narrative-identity work improves role integration and reduces self-stigma (Barkley 2021 review).

- Radical Acceptance (Linehan) correlates with lower ADHD-related distress independent of symptom load (Mitchell 2024).

Actionable principles

➤ **“Multiple-Me” timeline**: plot life phases, listing both barriers & unique ADHD-driven strengths (e.g., hyperfocus moments). Explicitly note where strengths emerged *because* of ADHD, not despite it.

- Practise ACT’s **self-as-context**: 5-min exercise observing thoughts as weather passing over a mountain; research shows increased psychological flexibility predicts medication adherence.

## 2\. Advanced Behavioural & Emotional Regulation

### 2\.1 Intrinsic Motivation & Value-Driven Action

What the evidence says

- Self-Determination Theory (SDT): autonomy, competence, relatedness → intrinsic motivation (Deci & Ryan 2000). Adults with ADHD score lower on perceived competence; interventions that raise it buffer motivation loss (Kooij 2022).

- ACT “values formulation” integrated with ADHD-CBT improves follow-through vs. CBT alone (Twohig 2021).

Actionable principles

➤ **3-Layer Goal Map**

1. Choose a weekly value theme (“Learning”).

2. Set 1–2 “process goals” (read 20 min/day).

### 2\.2 Emotional Dysregulation

What the evidence says

- DBT-ADHD protocols (Fleck 2017; Bodalski 2024 RCT) halve scores on the Difficulties in Emotion Regulation Scale.

- Rejection-Sensitivity Dysphoria (RSD) mediates between emotional reactivity and functional impairment (Eddy 2023).

Actionable principles

➤ **DBT “TIP-M” skills, compressed**

- T = Temperature (30-sec cold pack back of neck)

- I = Intense exercise (25 jumping jacks)

- P = Paced breathing (4-6 cycles)

- M = Meaning label (“This is RSD, not truth”)
   Practise twice daily when calm to ensure retrieval under stress.

- **Opposite Action for Shame:** deliberate eye contact + upright posture during apology emails reduces physiological shame markers (pilot, Ramsey 2022).

### 2\.3 Procrastination & Avoidance

What the evidence says

- Procrastination in ADHD is largely emotion-avoidance (Sirois & Pychyl 2016).

- Episodic Future Thinking (EFT) lengthens temporal reward horizon (Bromberg 2019).

A working formula for task initiation:

Motivation=I×DE×V​

E = Expectancy of success, V = Value, I = Impulsiveness, D = Delay (Steel 2007). ADHD interventions must shrink D and lower I.

Actionable principles

➤ Combine **EFT + Implementation Intention**: “If it’s 8 am and laptop opens, then visualise handing in the finished report for 20 sec, *then* write intro.” RCT in college students with ADHD showed 22 % reduction in delay (Dion 2022).

- **Chunk-then-cheer**: After each micro-chunk, text a progress emoji to an accountability buddy (supports relatedness in SDT).

## 3\. Sustainable Change Mechanisms

### 3\.1 Neuroplasticity & Habit Re-Patterning

What the evidence says

- Repeated attention-shifting practice increases dorsal lateral prefrontal cortex efficiency in ADHD (Arnett 2020 fMRI); sessions must be <15 min and high-frequency (5×/week).

- Habit formation follows the Cue → Craving → Response → Reward loop; ADHD brains need a *salient* and *immediate* reward to close the loop (Volkow 2021).

Actionable principles

➤ **“Stimulus → 5-sec rule → Act”** (Mel Robbins adaptation) harnesses pre-motor circuitry to beat micro-hesitation; pair with physical cue (standing up) for stronger motor priming.

- **Habit stacking + novelty swap**: keep the cue constant but refresh the reward every 7–10 days to keep dopamine response from habituating.

### 3\.2 Therapeutic Modalities (beyond skills-CBT)

- Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (strongest evidence; meta-analysis, Öst 2023).

- DBT-ADHD (growing RCT support).

- Compassion-Focused Therapy (pilot studies).

- Schema Therapy (case-series level).

- Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy adapted for ADHD (MBCT-A).

- Internal Family Systems (IFS) for comorbid trauma.
   *Clinical note:* Many adults benefit from a sequential approach—first stabilise emotion (DBT/CFT), then schema/identity, then values/action plans.

### 3\.3 Interplay with Societal Structures

What the evidence says

- Perceived societal pressure predicts self-stigma more than symptom severity (Sedgwick 2020). Self-advocacy training reduces burnout (Hromek 2022).

Actionable principles

- **“Contextual reframe” exercise**: list three settings where your ADHD traits are advantages (e.g., crisis response), reinforcing a non-deficit narrative.

- Draft a one-page **“ADHD user manual”** (needs, strengths, ideal workflows) to share with employers/partners—found to increase accommodation uptake by 40 % (diag-cohort, Hokayem 2023).

## 4\. Beyond Individual Coping: Environmental Architecting

Actionable principles

- Apply **Supportive Accountability Model** (Mohr 2011): choose an accountability partner who is both trustworthy and moderately authoritative; set check-in cadence + task evidence (screenshot, timer logs).

- Use **Ulysses Contracts with escape valves**: commit future you (e.g., auto-send draft to colleague at 5 pm) while allowing one grace skip per week to reduce threat response.

- Advocate for **attention-respecting work design**: 90-minute focus blocks + asynchronous comms; cite research on ROI of deep-work for neurodiverse teams when negotiating.

## 5\. Contraindications & Implementation Considerations

*Red-flag indicators requiring clinician oversight*

- Active suicidal ideation or severe depression → prioritise safety-oriented treatment.

- Bipolar or BPD traits: imagery and intensive emotion work can trigger instability.

- Untreated sleep disorders or substance misuse: neuroplasticity strategies will underperform.

- Over-reliance on self-help without calibrated medication/therapy often leads to burnout cycles.

Clinician-skill requirements

- CFT/Schema imagery: training in managing trauma responses.

- DBT-ADHD: familiarity with shorter, high-stim protocols.

---

## 6\. Key Take-Aways & Next Steps

1. Deep change for ADHD is less about perfect systems, more about **re-training threat/soothe circuitry, flexible thinking, and value-anchored motivation**.

2. Combine *bottom-up* (breath, cold exposure, habit cues) with *top-down* (defusion, narrative reframe) for durable neuroplastic shifts.

3. Sequence matters: stabilise emotion → dismantle shame → build identity → wire habits → extend to environment.

4. Work with a therapist versed in ACT/DBT/CFT or schema therapy; bring this document as a discussion roadmap.

5. Track one metric per phase (e.g., weekly self-compassion score, emotion-regulation skill usage) to keep progress visible and intrinsically rewarding.
