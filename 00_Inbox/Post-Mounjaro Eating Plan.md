Implementation Framework
------------------------

### 1. The "Non-Negotiable" 4 PM Snack

The evening overeating usually starts because blood sugar drops and hunger
hormones rise around 5-6 PM. By consuming 20-30g of protein at 4 PM, you arrive
at dinner in a state of "controlled appetite" rather than "starvation."

### 2. The Fibre First Rule

At every meal, eat your vegetables first, then your protein, and your
carbohydrates last. This slows gastric emptying—mimicking some of the effects
of Mounjaro—and flattens the glucose response.

### 3. Hydration and Electrolytes

Coming off GLP-1 medications can shift fluid balance. Drink 500ml of water with
a pinch of sea salt or an electrolyte tablet mid-afternoon to ensure "thirst"
isn't being misread by the brain as "hunger."

### 4. Cognitive Distinctions

 -  Physical Hunger: Felt in the stomach, develops slowly. (Action: Eat protein).
 -  Hedonic Hunger: Felt in the mouth/mind, sudden urge for specific textures
    (crunchy/sweet). (Action: 10-minute delay or herbal tea).
