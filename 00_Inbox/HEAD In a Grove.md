- The Unreliability of Memory: Memory is not a literal recording of events. Neuroscientific and psychological factors, such as "egocentric bias," subconsciously reshape visual information and memories to cast the individual in a positive light. - Subjectivity Over Singular Truth: The Rashomon effect undermines the concept of a singular, objective truth It typically occurs in situations where there is no independent evidence to verify the facts, coupled with external pressure to identify a definitive conclusion.
