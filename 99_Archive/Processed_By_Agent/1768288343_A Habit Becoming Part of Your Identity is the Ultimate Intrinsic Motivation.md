## A Habit Becoming Part of Your Identity is the Ultimate Intrinsic Motivation

Summary: The most powerful form of intrinsic motivation occurs when a habit becomes fully integrated into your identity.

Details: This represents a mental shift from "I'm the type of person who _wants_ this" to "I'm the type of person who _is_ this." When your behavior and identity are aligned, you are no longer forcing change; you are simply acting like the person you believe yourself to be. The goal is not just to run a marathon, but to become a runner.
