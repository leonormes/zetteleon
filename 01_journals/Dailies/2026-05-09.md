## One thing worth flagging

The fourth tag (your implicit methodological claim) is the most interesting and the most fragile. The Turing case and the knot-theory case *feel* like the same move, but they may not be:

- Turing was reducing a **process** (what a calculator does) to its minimal mechanism.
- Early knot theorists were reducing an **object** (a tangled loop) to its minimal invariants.

Process-essence and object-essence might be different epistemic operations wearing the same coat. Worth a separate note.
