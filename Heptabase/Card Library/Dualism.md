# **Dualism**

This view, famously advocated by philosophers like [René Descartes](https://app.heptabase.com/c16a6d60-49a6-4aec-9d1a-6161cbbe31a8/card/83324a63-2afc-4306-b46f-23e5cf741fb7), posits that thoughts exist as part of a separate, non-physical realm of mind or consciousness. According to this perspective, thoughts have a distinct ontological status from physical objects
