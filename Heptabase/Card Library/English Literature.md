#### **English Literature**

This course fosters a love of reading and encourages students to engage deeply and critically with a wide range of literary texts.

- **Subtopics:**

   - **Shakespeare:** Study of a complete play by William Shakespeare (e.g., *Macbeth*, *Romeo and Juliet*).

   - **19th-Century Novel:** In-depth study of a classic novel (e.g., *A Christmas Carol* by Charles Dickens, *The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde* by Robert Louis Stevenson).

   - **Modern Prose or Drama:** Study of a post-1914 fiction or drama text (e.g., *An Inspector Calls* by J.B. Priestley).

   - **Poetry:** Analysis of a collection of poems, often linked by a theme (e.g., Love and Relationships, or Power and Conflict). This includes comparing poems and analysing unseen poetry.

- **Outcomes:** She will learn to analyse how language, form, and structure are used by writers to create meaning and effect. She will develop the ability to form independent critical judgments and support her interpretations with textual evidence.
