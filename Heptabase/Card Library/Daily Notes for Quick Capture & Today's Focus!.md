1. **Daily Notes for Quick Capture & Today's Focus:**

   - Use Obsidian's Daily Notes feature (Core plugin). This is your primary inbox for fleeting thoughts, ideas, quick to-dos, and a log of what you're working on or learning *today*.

   - **Crucial Habit:** At the end of the day, or when you have a moment, process items from your Daily Note. This might mean:

      - Moving a task to the relevant Project Hub's "Next Actions."

      - Linking a learning point to a Project Hub or a concept note.

      - Deleting it if it's no longer relevant.
