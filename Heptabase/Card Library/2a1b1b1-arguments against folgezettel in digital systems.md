## 2a1b1b1-arguments Against Folgezettel in Digital Systems

## Arguments Against Folgezettel in Digital Systems

Reduced Importance of Physical Sequence

The ability to create direct links between notes diminishes the significance of the physical order inherent in folgezettel. Links offer a more flexible and direct way to connect ideas, rendering the positional value of folgezettel less crucial.

Redundancy with Keyword Registers

Luhmann's keyword register, used to access relevant entry points in his physical Zettelkasten, already addresses the need for thematic organization that folgezettel partially fulfil. In a digital Zettelkasten, comprehensive search and linking capabilities make both folgezettel and even keyword registers less critical.

Imposition of Unnecessary Hierarchy

While folgezettel aim to represent a sequence of thought, they can inadvertently create a hierarchical structure that might not reflect the complex relationships between ideas. This imposed hierarchy can hinder the organic exploration and connection of ideas, a core strength of the Zettelkasten method.
