Core Philosophy for Your Obsidian Setup:

- Simplicity First: We'll use core Obsidian features as much as possible. Fewer plugins mean less to manage.
- Embrace Imperfection & Iteration: Your plans will change as you learn. Your Obsidian notes are there to capture and reflect that evolving understanding, not to be rigid documents.
- Focus on the _Next Action_: This is crucial for managing executive function differences. Knowing the very next small step can make a huge difference.
- Linking is Your Superpower: Obsidian's strength is in connecting thoughts. Use it liberally.
