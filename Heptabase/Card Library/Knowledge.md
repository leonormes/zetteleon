## Knowledge

Definition: Knowledge refers to the collection of information, skills, and experiences that a person has acquired. In PKM, knowledge includes both explicit knowledge (easily articulated, documented) and tacit knowledge (personal insights and experiences).

Usage: This term is widely accepted in various fields, including education, cognitive science, and information science.
