His reading notes were brief, focusing on key ideas and connections. He didn't underline sentences or write comments in the margins. Instead, he captured the essence of the idea on a separate piece of paper and stored it in his bibliographic slip-box.

Luhmann emphasized that taking notes wasn't about rote memorization but about identifying and capturing relevant information for future use.
