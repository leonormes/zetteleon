## Directed and Weighted Graphs

Here's how mathematicians would approach this and how you can apply it to your note-taking:

1. Directed and Weighted Graphs

- Directed: Since the relationship between notes can have a direction (e.g., "Note A supports the idea in Note B"), you're dealing with a directed graph where edges have arrows.
- Weighted: To represent the different types of links, you'd use a weighted graph, where each edge is assigned a numerical value (weight) signifying its importance or type.

1. Representing Link Types (Edge Weights)

You could assign weights to your edges like this:

- Sequential: 1
- Supportive: 2
- Contradictory: 3
- New Idea Synthesis: 4

You can customize these weights and add more types as needed.
