**Lit: The Clinical Facts and "Associative Hyperdrive"**

- The LLM correctly identifies the clinical presentation of Adult ADHD combined type.

- The DIVA scores of 9/9 for inattention and 7/9 for hyperactivity/impulsivity directly correlate with the "hyperactivity-of-thought" described.

- In the literature, the ADHD mind is specifically described as having "ideas firing around like kernels in a popcorn machine: ideas coming rat-a-tat fast, and on no discernable schedule".

- The LLM's term "associative hyperdrive" is an apt description for what the experts call having "the power of a Ferrari engine but with bicycle-strength brakes".
