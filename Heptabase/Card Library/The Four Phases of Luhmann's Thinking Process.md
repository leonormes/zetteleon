## The Four Phases of Luhmann's Thinking Process

Luhmann's thinking process consisted of four distinct phases. These phases, while separate, are interconnected and build upon each other. They are all supported by Luhmann's Zettelkasten method, which allows for the externalization, organization, and development of ideas:

[He took notes on anything that caught his attention. His notes weren't mere excerpts or summaries.md](He%20took%20notes%20on%20anything%20that%20caught%20his%20attention.%20His%20notes%20weren't%20mere%20excerpts%20or%20summaries.md)

[His reading notes were brief, focusing on key ideas and connections. He didn't underline sentence.md](His%20reading%20notes%20were%20brief,%20focusing%20on%20key%20ideas%20and%20connections.%20He%20didn't%20underline%20sentence.md)
