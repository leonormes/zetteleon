## Mount the Candle on a Wall

Consider a classic example: When given a candle, matches, and a box of thumbtacks to mount the candle on a wall, many people fail to see that emptying the box creates a platform. Their prior probability distribution for "box functions" is so heavily weighted toward "container" that it inhibits updates toward "platform."
