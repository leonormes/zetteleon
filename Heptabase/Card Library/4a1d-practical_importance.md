# Practical Importance

Data Compression
Entropy helps determine the theoretical limits for compressing data. Higher entropy means data is harder to compress, while lower entropy indicates redundant data that can be compressed efficiently

Error Detection and Correction
In communication systems, understanding entropy helps in designing error-correcting codes, ensuring that even with noise (uncertainty), messages can be accurately transmitted.

In essence, entropy measures the average surprise or uncertainty of information, and information itself is about reducing that uncertainty. The more uncertain or random a situation is, the more information we gain when we observe the actual outcome.
