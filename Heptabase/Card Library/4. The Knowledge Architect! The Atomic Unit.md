## 4\. The Knowledge Architect: The Atomic Unit

Your notes on personal knowledge management operationalise the proposition into a physical (or digital) artefact.

- The Rule: A proposition is an "Atomic Note." It must be strictly context-free, indivisible, and contain exactly one idea. If it contains two ideas, it is a compound that must be cleaved _(Knowledge System Cleaving and Synthesis Protocols, Core Principles)_. Sönke Ahrens echoes this, insisting that an idea must be written in a way that allows it to be understood completely outside the context in which it was originally found _(Ahrens, How to Take Smart Notes, Ch. 11)_.
- The Lens: _Atomicity_ and _Self-containment_.
