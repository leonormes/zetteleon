## 5\. The Post-Structuralist Disruption: The Statement

Michel Foucault throws a spanner in the works. He explicitly contrasts his concept of the "statement" (_énoncé_) against the logician's "proposition."

- The Rule: For Foucault, a proposition is a grammatical or logical construct. A _statement_, however, is an event tied to conditions of existence, power, and discourse. A proposition can be logically sound but functionally meaningless if it falls outside the accepted discourse of its time _(Foucault, Archaeology of Knowledge, Part III)_.
- The Lens: _Rhetorical residue_ and _Scope_. Foucault forces us to ask: what historical or discursive rules make this proposition permissible to utter in the first place?
