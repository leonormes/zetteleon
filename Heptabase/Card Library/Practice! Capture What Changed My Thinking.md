## Practice: Capture What Changed My Thinking

This is the single most valuable operational rule in the note. It solves the book-note problem directly. You do not need to summarise a networking book; you need to note the three claims that surprised you and the two procedures you will actually reuse.

This aligns with the Rozenblit & Keil mechanism from the IOED card: summarising produces the illusion of understanding. Capturing _delta_—what changed—forces actual cognitive work.
