## 2a4d1-don't_pre-judge_ideas

## Don't Pre-judge Ideas

The key here is to not pre judge. Don't dismiss things or treat them as lesser objects. Put the same level of detail and preparation into them. If they do become valuable later you don't want to have to work on them again.
