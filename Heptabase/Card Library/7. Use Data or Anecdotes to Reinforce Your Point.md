## 7\. Use Data or Anecdotes to Reinforce Your Point

Without making it personal, share examples of companies that either succeeded due to early security measures or suffered because they delayed them.

By focusing on shared goals, framing the discussion in terms of the product's long-term success, and offering practical steps, you can assert your point without coming across as combative.

Addressing this sensitive issue in an open forum requires tact, diplomacy, and a focus on the long-term benefits for the company. Here's an approach that balances respect for experience with the critical need for security:

"I appreciate the wealth of experience we have on our team, which has undoubtedly contributed to our success thus far. As we look to the future and our potential for growth, I believe it's crucial we consider how our early decisions will impact our long-term success and scalability.

In the realm of medical records software, security isn't just a feature—it's the foundation of our entire product and business model. Unlike general software products, we're handling highly sensitive personal health information protected by stringent regulations like HIPAA\[1\]. This places unique obligations on us from day one.
