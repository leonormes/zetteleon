## A Zettelkasten Is Many Things

Despite his significant output, Luhmann wrote very little about his zettelkasten. While the bits and pieces that can be gleaned from interviews and his essay, "Communicating with Slip Boxes," are valuable and telling, firsthand accounts of Luhmann's practices and reasonings are few. So, while there may appear to be a great deal to debate regarding why Luhmann did what he did, there are a number of understandings that have proven sound.
