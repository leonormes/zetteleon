## Ontology as a Structured Framework

 In ontology, concepts and categories are formally defined, with clear relationships between them. This can be applied to your notes by creating a hierarchy or taxonomy of concepts that are related to each other through well-defined relationships. It helps ensure that each idea or note has a precise context within your system.

   How to Apply It:

- Define key concepts and categories in your knowledge domain.
- Ensure that each note (node) belongs to a category or represents a concept that fits into a larger structure.
- Use metadata, tags, or folders to organize your notes according to these categories.

    Example: If you are studying both graph theory and cognition, you could categorize your notes under:

- "Mathematics > Graph Theory > Weighted Graphs"
- "Philosophy > Cognition > Conceptual Models"

    This way, your knowledge system reflects a structured, hierarchical ontology where each note has its place within a well-defined context.
