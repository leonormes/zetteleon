---

Knowledge is power: Recognizing biases is crucial for change.

Identify biases: Recognizing specific biases is the beginning of change.

Trivialization of goals: Saying "Ah, it's not that important" is a common bias.

Planning fallacy: Saying "There's lots of time yet, so I'd prefer to do tomorrow" is a common bias.

Intransitive preferences: Procrastination and delaying tasks are also biases.

Flags for change: Recognizing these biases can be "flags" for change.
