## 7\.11 Load Balancing in Private EKS

7\.11 Load Balancing in Private EKS

### Key Points

- Private EKS clusters rely on internal load balancers to route traffic between pods and other resources within the VPC.
- ENIs ensure that load balancer traffic stays within the private VPC network.
