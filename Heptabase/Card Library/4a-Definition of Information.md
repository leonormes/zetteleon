title: 4a-Definition of Information
---

Information, in a general sense, refers to data or knowledge that is meaningful and useful to someone.

It is the content that can be communicated, stored, processed, or transmitted in various forms such as text, numbers, images, or signals.

Information is not just raw data, but rather data that has been organized, structured, or processed to have meaning or value in a particular context.

Information theory is a mathematical framework developed to quantify the communication, transmission, and processing of information. It was pioneered by [4a3-claude_shannon.md](4a3-claude_shannon.md) in the 1940s in his landmark paper "A Mathematical Theory of Communication", where he defined fundamental concepts such as entropy, channel capacity, and noise.
