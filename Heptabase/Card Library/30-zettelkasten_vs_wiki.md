## 30-zettelkasten_vs_wiki

Purpose:

- Wiki: A wiki aims to organize and retrieve information efficiently. It's like a personal encyclopedia.
- Zettelkasten: A Zettelkasten is designed for thinking, connecting ideas, and generating new knowledge. It's a tool for deepening understanding and fostering creativity.
   Structure:

- Wiki: Typically hierarchical or categorical. You might have a page on "Gardening" with subpages for "Vegetables," "Flowers," etc.
- Zettelkasten: A network of interconnected notes. Links are based on conceptual relationships, not just hierarchical categories. This allows for non-linear exploration of ideas.

Content:

- Wiki: Focuses on comprehensive coverage of a topic. Notes tend to be longer and more explanatory.
- Zettelkasten: Favors atomic notes—small, focused notes containing a single idea. This promotes modularity and allows for diverse combinations.
   Linking:

- Wiki: Links primarily serve as navigational aids.
- Zettelkasten: Links are the core. They represent the connections between ideas, facilitating the emergence of new insights.

The fundamental difference lies in the purpose and process
