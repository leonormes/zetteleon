# Engage in Regular Reviews

Schedule weekly or monthly reviews of your Zettelkasten to: Revisit and Refresh: Read through a selection of notes, even those not directly related to current projects. This can spark new connections or remind you of forgotten ideas. Identify Patterns and Gaps: Look for recurring themes, contradictions, or areas where your understanding is still developing. This can guide further research and note-taking. Why it Matters: Regular reviews help maintain the dynamism of your Zettelkasten and prevent it from becoming a stagnant archive.
