## Ontological Evolution

   Ontologies are not static; they evolve as new concepts are discovered and relationships are redefined. Similarly, your knowledge system should remain flexible and open to evolving structures.

   How to Apply It:

- Periodically review and refactor your Zettelkasten to ensure that your ontology remains relevant and well-structured as your understanding deepens.
- Be open to creating new categories, redefining relationships, and merging or splitting notes as your knowledge grows.

    Example: You might start with a category for "Technology," but as you gather more notes, you might realize that "Artificial Intelligence" deserves its own category. You can then refactor your notes, placing related notes under this new, more specific category.
