## Emphasis on Permanent Notes

- The Zettelkasten method distinguishes between fleeting notes, literature notes, and permanent notes. Fleeting notes are temporary notes that you take while reading or brainstorming. Literature notes are summaries or paraphrases of sources you've read. Permanent notes are your own original thoughts and ideas, synthesized from your fleeting and literature notes. The goal of the Zettelkasten method is to create a critical mass of permanent notes that you can use to generate new ideas and insights.
