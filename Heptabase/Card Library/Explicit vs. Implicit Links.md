## Explicit vs. Implicit Links

Implicit Connections: Not all links are explicit; some ideas are connected implicitly (by topic overlap, similarity in examples, etc.). You might assign lower magnitude or information content to implicit links unless they’re formalized by creating an explicit note-to-note link.

Linking of Similar Tags: Use tags or keywords to identify indirect connections. The number of shared tags between two notes might indicate an implicit link, with its magnitude growing based on the tag overlap.
