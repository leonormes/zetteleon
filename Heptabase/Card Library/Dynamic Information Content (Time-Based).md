## Dynamic Information Content (Time-Based)

Relevance Over Time: The significance or information content of a link may change over time. For instance, as you add new ideas, some links may become more relevant or less novel. Revisit links periodically to update their "information content" rating.

Decay of Novelty: As an idea becomes more familiar, its information content might decrease. You could develop a metric that tracks how often a link is referenced, with novelty gradually “decaying” as it becomes routine.
