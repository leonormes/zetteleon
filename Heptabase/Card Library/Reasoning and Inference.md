## Reasoning and Inference

   In ontology, there are often inference rules that allow new knowledge to be generated based on the existing structure of concepts and relationships. In your PKM, you can apply similar reasoning by looking for emergent insights from the structure of your notes.

   How to Apply It:

- As you build your knowledge system, look for patterns or connections that suggest new ideas.

- For example, if two concepts are related by a common category, there may be a third idea that emerges from their combination, which you can capture as a new note.

    Example: If "Graph Theory" is linked to both "Social Networks" and "Epidemiology," you might infer that there’s a new area of exploration at the intersection of these ideas (e.g., "Graphs in Epidemiological Modeling"), leading to the creation of a new note.
