## The Folgezettel Debate

The sources discuss a debate regarding the use of **Folgezettel**, or note sequences, in digital Zettelkasten systems.

**Arguments against Folgezettel in a digital Zettelkasten:**

- **Reduced Importance Due to Links:** Some argue that Folgezettel are less important in a digital Zettelkasten because of the ability to create direct links between notes. In a physical Zettelkasten, Folgezettel were used to create a sequence of ideas because direct links were not possible. However, in a digital Zettelkasten, you can link directly to any note, regardless of its position in the system. Therefore, the value of a note's position in a sequence is reduced.

- **Collector's Fallacy:** The sources warn that relying on Folgezettel in a digital Zettelkasten can lead to "collector's fallacy" - the tendency to collect information without actually processing or using it. If you are using Folgezettel as a crutch to avoid making meaningful connections between notes, you may be falling into this trap.

**Arguments for Folgezettel in a digital Zettelkasten:**

- **Not Primarily for Physical Limitations:** Some argue that Luhmann's use of Folgezettel was not solely due to the physical limitations of paper notes. Instead, they argue that Folgezettel can be a useful technique for developing ideas in a digital Zettelkasten, even if you are not limited by space.

- **Meaningful Relationships:** Proponents of Folgezettel in digital Zettelkastens argue that they create specific, meaningful relationships between notes that are not captured by simple links. They argue that Folgezettel represent a "relationship of relationships", which is central to Luhmann's working principle of "Relationierung von Relationen".

**The Role of Keywords and Structure Notes:**

The sources also discuss the use of keywords and structure notes as alternatives to, or in conjunction with, Folgezettel. Some argue that keywords can be used to create thematic entry points into the Zettelkasten, similar to how a register functions. Others suggest using structure notes as a way to create a hierarchical or sequential structure within the Zettelkasten.

**Ultimately, the decision of whether or not to use Folgezettel in a digital Zettelkasten is a matter of personal preference.**

Experiment with different approaches and see what works best for you. The key is to focus on creating meaningful connections between your notes, regardless of the specific techniques you use.
