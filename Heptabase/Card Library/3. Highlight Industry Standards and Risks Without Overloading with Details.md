## 3\. **Highlight Industry Standards and Risks Without Overloading with Details**

Frame security as a business enabler, not a blocker.

- *Example:* "Industry trends show that organisations that handle sensitive data, like healthcare startups, often face scrutiny much earlier in their lifecycle. A single breach could jeopardise growth, credibility, and future funding. By integrating basic security measures now, we save ourselves from potential crises later."
