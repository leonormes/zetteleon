## Lit: Resistance at the Transition point

The brain dump in your last-but-one message is the clearest window into your executive function struggle. Look at what you said:

> _"I feel resistance to creating a new note to break out from what I am currently thinking. I want to capture my thought and carry on with the flow."_

This is task-switching cost made conscious. For ADHD brains, the cost of switching contexts mid-flow is disproportionately high because:

- Hyperfocus is fragile. You earned the flow state, and the brain correctly recognises the new-note action as threatening it.
- Working memory is taxed. Holding "where I was" whilst creating a new note is expensive.
- The decision-tree explodes. As I noted earlier, creating a new note triggers "what type? what title? what links?"—each decision is activation energy, and the sum is prohibitive.

Neurotypical brains have a lower switching cost and do not feel this as viscerally. You are not weak-willed here. You are correctly perceiving a cost that is higher for you than for others.
