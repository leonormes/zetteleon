## Do Thoughts Exist?

From an ontological perspective, the answer depends on what we mean by "existence" and the specific framework being used:

[In dualism, thoughts exist as non-material entities..md](In%20dualism,%20thoughts%20exist%20as%20non-material%20entities..md)

[In physicalism, thoughts exist as brain processes, with no independent or separate existence..md](In%20physicalism,%20thoughts%20exist%20as%20brain%20processes,%20with%20no%20independent%20or%20separate%20existence..md)

[In idealism, thoughts may be more real than the physical world..md](In%20idealism,%20thoughts%20may%20be%20more%20real%20than%20the%20physical%20world..md)

[In phenomenology, thoughts exist as part of lived experience in consciousness..md](In%20phenomenology,%20thoughts%20exist%20as%20part%20of%20lived%20experience%20in%20consciousness..md)

[In Platonism, thoughts may exist as abstract objects..md](In%20Platonism,%20thoughts%20may%20exist%20as%20abstract%20objects..md)

[In process philosophy, thoughts exist as temporal processes or events..md](In%20process%20philosophy,%20thoughts%20exist%20as%20temporal%20processes%20or%20events..md)
