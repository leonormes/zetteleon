## Lit: The Thing You Were Really Asking

Your brain dump kept circling back to "what is the end goal?" I think the real question underneath was:

> "How do I know this isn't all pointless?"

That is the RSD-adjacent fear talking, and it deserves an honest answer. The end goal is not a complete knowledge map. The end goal is:

1. You think more clearly today than yesterday because you forced the clarity of the claim-card format.
2. You recover your thinking six months from now instead of re-deriving it from scratch.
3. You find the gaps because the system makes them visible (open threads, missing falsifiers, undefended claims).
4. You build conviction about what you believe, so you can act on it—at work, in writing, in decisions.

If the system is giving you those four things, it is working. If it is not, something is wrong. Those are the metrics. Not card count. Not graph density. Not comprehensiveness.
