## 6\. Propose Actionable, Low-Effort Steps

Offer practical solutions that don't feel overwhelming.

- _Example:_ "We don't need to over-engineer security at this stage, but implementing foundational best practices—like secure coding guidelines and basic access controls—can position us for smoother growth."
