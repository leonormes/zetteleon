**Key Idea:** In process-oriented philosophies, like those of [Alfred North Whitehead](https://app.heptabase.com/c16a6d60-49a6-4aec-9d1a-6161cbbe31a8/card/14ef7af8-49dd-420a-ae11-d76cabfbf854), reality is composed of **events and processes** rather than static objects. Thoughts would be considered **dynamic occurrences** in the stream of consciousness or brain activity.
