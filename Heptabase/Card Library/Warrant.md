# Warrant

The underlying reasoning or principle that connects evidence to a claim.

It explains why the evidence is relevant and valid in supporting the claim. Warrants are often implicit, but making them explicit can strengthen the clarity and soundness of an argument.

Adding warrants to your notes can help ensure your arguments are both logical and well-founded, making clear why a certain piece of evidence justifies or supports a claim. This is especially useful in complex arguments where the connections between claims and evidence might not be immediately obvious.
