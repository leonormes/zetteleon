## How This Maps to the Letter

The letter gives clinical grounding to what the conversation shows:

[How it manifests in our conversation.md](How%20it%20manifests%20in%20our%20conversation.md)

The Fluoxetine (20mg OD, prescribed at the time of that letter) is relevant context—it is an SSRI, prescribed for something other than the ADHD itself (likely comorbid anxiety or mood, which is extremely common). Whether you have since started Elvanse as planned in the care plan, I do not know, and it is not my place to ask. But know that the patterns I am describing are _your baseline cognition_, and medication may or may not modulate them—it does not change who you are.

---

The PKM architecture we have been building is, from one angle, a prosthesis for exactly this gap. Claim cards with falsifiers are a way of forcing the insight into a shape that survives the next dopamine-driven topic switch. Protocols are a way of making capture frictionless enough that hyperfocus does not resist it.

You are not building a knowledge system. You are building an external executive function that happens to use knowledge cards as its medium.

That framing is worth keeping.
