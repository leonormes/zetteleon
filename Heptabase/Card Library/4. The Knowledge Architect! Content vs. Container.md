## 4\. The Knowledge Architect: Content vs. Container

The Proposition (Semantic Content): In knowledge architecture, a proposition is the single, indivisible idea itself—the pure semantic content. It is the raw material of thought that must be rigorously context-free, capable of being understood entirely on its own without relying on the author's original narrative _(Ahrens, How to Take Smart Notes, Ch. 11)_.

The Atomic Note (The Artefact): The atomic note is merely the physical or digital vehicle built to house that single proposition. It is a functional artefact—a "brick" in the system—designed to isolate the semantic content so it can be independently linked, synthesised, and structurally recontextualised without carrying the "stink" of its original source _(Knowledge System Cleaving and Synthesis Protocols; Doto, A System for Writing, Note 93)_.
