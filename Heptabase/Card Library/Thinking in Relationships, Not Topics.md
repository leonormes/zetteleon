# Thinking in Relationships, Not Topics

- Wikis often rely on hierarchical structures and top-down organisation. You create pages on specific topics and link them based on their predetermined relationships. This mimics traditional note-taking methods, which can limit creative exploration.

- Zettelkasten, on the other hand, thrives on bottom-up growth. Links are made based on your unique understanding of how ideas connect, allowing for unexpected and insightful relationships to emerge.
