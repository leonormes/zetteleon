## ADHD-Specific Strategies

- Quick Capture: Use Obsidian's mobile app or a quick capture tool (like Drafts) to jot down ideas immediately. Process them into your Project Hub later.
- Focus & Momentum: The "Next Action" is your focus. One small step at a time. Pomodoro Technique can help.
- Overwhelm Avoidance: If overwhelmed, zoom in on the "Next Action." If _that's_ too much, break it down further. The goal is forward motion, not perfection.
- Embrace Imperfection: Your plans _will_ change. That's okay. The Project Hub is a living document.
