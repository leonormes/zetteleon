### How to describe your cognitive issues clearly

To communicate these issues to a professional or to yourself, you can use these more rigorous terms:

- **Executive Transition Friction:** The high "energy cost" of moving from the act of writing (one mental state) to the act of categorising (a different state).

- **Dopaminergic System-Seeking:** A compulsive need to find novelty in *how* you work because the work itself has become "peripersonal" and boring.

- **DMN-Induced Perfectionism:** When your "Default Mode Network" interrupts your "Task Positive" work with ruminative loops about whether your system is "correct".

- **The "Now vs. Not Now" Constraint:** As an ADHD brain, you only recognize two times. If a note isn't finished *now*, it feels like it belongs in the infinite, scary "not now," leading to the urge to hold everything centrally where you can see it.

**The protocol you are looking for isn't a better system—it is a way to "shut down the Demon" by denying it your attention during the work phase**. You must treat the "urge to redo the system" as a **biological itch** rather than a logical requirement.
