## ADHD-Adapted Goal Setting

While general goal-setting advice is common, its application to value-driven pursuits is key. Strategies such as making goals Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound (SMART), breaking them down into smaller, manageable steps, creating written strategies, and incorporating accountability can all be applied to goals that stem from identified personal values. This structured approach helps bridge the gap between abstract values and concrete daily actions.
