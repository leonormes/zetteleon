## Do Note Rely on Pre-defined Categories

Instead of strictly avoiding categories, do not prioritize them. Allow categories to emerge naturally from the connections between ideas, fostering organic growth and unexpected connections without stifling creativity.

This approach emphasizes flexibility and adaptability in organizing information, allowing for categories to form organically as a byproduct of linking related ideas.
