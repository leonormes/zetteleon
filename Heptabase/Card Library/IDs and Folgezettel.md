# IDs and Folgezettel

Folgezettel are sequences of notes that build upon each other. They are related in some obvious way.

The most important aspect of a Zettelkasten is the connections between the notes.
We need to be able to reference individual ideas in a cloud of many, potentially 10s of thousands.

I am not sure about the IDs on the notes. I believe what I am reading, but as I am not using them it seems academic.

We do the sequence IDs to help make sense of the notes. You can see connections in the filenames and understand the thinking when you created them.
