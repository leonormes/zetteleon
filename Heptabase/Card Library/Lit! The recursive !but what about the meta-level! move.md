### Lit: The recursive "but what about the meta-level?" move

You did this repeatedly:

- Validated JTB → asked about IOED → then asked whether IOED is a claim or concept → then asked how to represent typed links → then asked about granularity → then asked about end-state

- Each question went *up a level* from the previous one.

This is characteristic ADHD cognition: **difficulty stopping at the level of analysis that answers the immediate question**. The brain keeps asking "yes but what is the *real* question underneath?" This is a genuine strength (it is why you spotted that Gemini was using mathematical vocabulary loosely) *and* a source of paralysis (you cannot ground until you have hit the meta-ceiling).

**Relevant quote from your brain dump:** *"I don't have an end game… that is endless. Each node off I could do the same thing."*

This is the recursion experienced from the inside. The infinite regress is not a philosophical problem you stumbled on — it is the shape of your attention pattern made visible.
