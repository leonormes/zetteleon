## Lit: The Knowledge Architect: Content vs. Container

- The Proposition (Semantic Content): The single, indivisible idea itself—the pure semantic content. It is the raw material of thought that must be rigorously context-free, capable of being understood entirely on its own without relying on the author's original narrative _(Ahrens, How to Take Smart Notes, Ch. 11)_.
- The Atomic Note (The Artefact): The physical or digital vehicle built to house that single proposition. It is a functional artefact designed to isolate the semantic content so it can be independently linked and synthesised _(Knowledge System Cleaving and Synthesis Protocols; Doto, A System for Writing, Note 93)_.
