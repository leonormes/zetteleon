## Focus on Atomicity

Each Zettel note should focus on a single idea or piece of information. This makes the notes more modular and reusable. In practice, this means breaking down large amounts of information into smaller, more manageable chunks. For example, when taking notes on a book, you might create a separate Zettel for each key concept, argument, or piece of evidence.
