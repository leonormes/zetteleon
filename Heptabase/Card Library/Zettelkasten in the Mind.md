# Zettelkasten in the Mind

The essence of the Zettelkasten system resides in the mental processes of the individual using it. The true value of the system lies in how it supports and enhances the cognitive functions of the user.

Without a brain, the Zettelkasten does not exist. The system’s utility and existence are entangled with the active engagement of the user's mind. Without the cognitive functions of thinking, interpreting, and making connections, the Zettelkasten is just a collection of inert information. It requires the dynamic and active participation of a brain for it to come to life.

The Zettelkasten is in a state of potentiality until the thinker actively engages it. Just as Schrödinger's cat is simultaneously alive and dead until observed, the Zettelkasten's value and organization are undefined until the user interacts with it, thinks about it, and brings it into a functional state through cognitive engagement.
