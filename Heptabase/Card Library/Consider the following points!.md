Consider the following points:

1. Regulatory Compliance: Medical data breaches can result in severe penalties, including fines up to $1.5 million per violation category per year\[2\]. Even as a small startup, we're not exempt from these regulations.
2. Customer Trust: In healthcare, data security is often a primary factor in purchasing decisions. Building security from the ground up demonstrates our commitment to protecting patient data, giving us a competitive edge\[3\].
3. Scalability Challenges: Retrofitting security into an existing system is significantly more complex and costly than building it in from the start. It often requires extensive refactoring, which can introduce new bugs and delay feature development\[4\].
4. Investor Confidence: As we grow, potential investors will scrutinise our security practices. A strong security foundation can be a key differentiator in attracting funding\[5\].
5. Reputation Management: A single data breach can irreparably damage our reputation in the healthcare industry, where trust is paramount\[6\].
