## Linking for Thinking

Consider categorizing links based on how they aid your thinking. For instance, a link could represent different types of relationships: explanatory, contradictory, sequential, or associative. This way, links aren't just for navigation; they embody different facets of your thought process, helping you explore your ideas from multiple perspectives.
