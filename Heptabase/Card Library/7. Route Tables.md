## 7\. Route Tables

- Definition: Route tables define the routing rules for subnets within a VPC. They determine where traffic to specific IP ranges should be sent.
- Role: Route tables are critical in ensuring traffic directed at an ENI's IP reaches the correct subnet and the ENI.
