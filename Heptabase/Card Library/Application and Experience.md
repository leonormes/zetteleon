## Application and Experience

Knowledge emerges when a person can:

- Apply the information in practical situations

- Use the information to solve problems or make decisions

- Gain insights through experience in using the information

As individuals use information in real-world contexts, they develop a more nuanced and actionable understanding\[1\]\[2\].

- **Application:** Knowledge is truly valuable when it can be applied to solve problems, make decisions, or create something new.
