## Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

CBT is instrumental in identifying and restructuring the unhelpful thinking patterns—such as catastrophic predictions about failure or harsh self-judgments related to productivity—that fuel shame, guilt, and avoidance. By replacing these with more balanced, realistic, and compassionate mindsets, individuals can reduce the emotional barriers to task engagement. The CBT for ADHD-Inattentive Presentation (CADDI) protocol, for instance, specifically targets procrastination and passivity through a combination of behavioral activation strategies and organizational skills training, alongside mindfulness.
