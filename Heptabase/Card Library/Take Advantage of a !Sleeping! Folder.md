## Take Advantage of a "Sleeping" Folder

If you capture lots of fleeting notes, there will inevitably be those that repeatedly get passed over when you attempt to process your inbox (3.3:2). These ideas may not speak to others already stored in your zettelkasten; they may not be related to any piece of writing you're actively working on; or maybe the idea just isn't as interesting as it was when you first captured it. Any notes that seem hard to process, but are still relevant to your thinking, should be moved to a "Sleeping" folder.

As with your inbox, your sleeping folder can either be a physical compartment or digital folder. I recommend checking in on sleeping notes every few months.
