**Subjectivity**: Unlike physical objects, thoughts are inherently subjective experiences, making their objective existence difficult to verify\[2\].

**Causality**: Thoughts seem to have causal powers (e.g., influencing behavior), which some argue necessitates granting them some form of existence\[3\].

**Intentionality**: Thoughts are often about something, possessing intentionality. This characteristic has led some philosophers to argue for a distinct ontological category for mental phenomena\[1\].
