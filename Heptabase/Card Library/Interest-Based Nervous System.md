## Interest-Based Nervous System

## Interest-Based Nervous System

Some individuals with ADHD have what's called an "interest-based nervous system." This means they're primarily driven by interest and passion, rather than external rewards or obligations.

If the "doing" feels like a chore or obligation, it becomes less appealing.
