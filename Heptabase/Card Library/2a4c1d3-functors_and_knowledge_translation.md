# Functors and Knowledge Translation

Functors in category theory can be seen as mappings between different knowledge domains or perspectives. In the context of PKM:

- Functors can represent ways of translating ideas between different areas of your knowledge graph

- They allow you to create analogies and draw connections between seemingly unrelated concepts, potentially leading to new insights
