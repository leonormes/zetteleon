# Link Strength

To add another dimension to your analysis, consider incorporating link strength.
This could represent the degree of connection between notes:

Weak: A tentative or exploratory connection.
Medium: A clear and relevant connection.
Strong: A fundamental or defining connection.

You can combine this with link types: Link Text

Use a strength parameter on a scale of 0 to 1 to indicate how strongly a note is connected to the target note. For instance:
