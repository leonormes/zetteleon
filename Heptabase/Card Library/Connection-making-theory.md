## Connection-making-theory

Connection-making serves multiple cognitive functions in knowledge development:

1. Pattern Recognition

   - Linking ideas reveals underlying patterns
   - Enables recognition of broader principles across domains

2. Knowledge Integration

   - New connections reshape existing understanding
   - Creates deeper comprehension through context

3. Insight Generation

   - Novel connections often trigger breakthrough moments
   - Cross-pollination between previously separate ideas
