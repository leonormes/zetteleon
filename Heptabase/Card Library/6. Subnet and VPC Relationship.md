### 6\. **Subnet and VPC Relationship**

- **Definition**: A VPC is divided into multiple **subnets**, each with its own CIDR block. The subnet is a smaller segment of the VPC’s IP range.

- **Role**: Subnets define IP addressing for ENIs and other resources in the VPC.
