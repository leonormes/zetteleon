## Ontological Commitment

   Ontologies require a clear commitment to how concepts are structured and how relationships are defined. By committing to an ontology in your Zettelkasten, you decide in advance how different types of notes and connections will be represented, ensuring consistency.

   How to Apply It:

- Decide on a consistent framework for how you will categorize and relate notes.
- Ensure that every time you create a new note or link, it fits into this ontological structure.
- This commitment prevents your knowledge system from becoming fragmented or disorganized, as it ensures that every note contributes to a coherent whole.

    Example: If you decide that "is-a" and "part-of" are the primary relationships you'll use to organize notes, every new note and link must adhere to this framework. This helps maintain consistency across your knowledge base.
