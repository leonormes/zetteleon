# sorites_paradox_knowledge_management

The Sorites Paradox relates to knowledge management and the "collector's fallacy":

- Knowledge acquisition isn't simply about accumulating discrete facts.

- True understanding involves integrating and connecting ideas.

- Learning is a gradual process of accumulation and integration, similar to the gradual transition along the spectrum of a vague concept.

This perspective emphasizes the importance of connecting and synthesizing information rather than merely collecting it.
