## 4\. Traffic Handling by ENI

- Definition: An ENI handles network traffic directed to its IP address. Traffic passes through the VPC's internal networking and arrives at the ENI.
- Direction: ENI forwards incoming traffic to the attached resource (e.g., EC2 instance) and handles outgoing traffic from the resource.
