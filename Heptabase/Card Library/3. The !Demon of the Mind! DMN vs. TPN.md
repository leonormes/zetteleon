## 3\. The "Demon of the Mind": DMN vs. TPN

Your difficulty in "breaking out" into a new note while writing is a classic DMN/TPN dysregulation common in ADHD.

- TPN (Task Positive Network): This is the "Angel" that keeps you focused on the task at hand (writing your current thought).
- DMN (Default Mode Network): This is the "Demon" or "chatterbox" that handles imagination and ruminating on the past or future.
- The ADHD Battle: In a neurotypical brain, when the TPN is on, the DMN turns off. In your brain, the DMN is muscling in while you are trying to work. It interrupts your flow with perfectionist thoughts: _"Is this note in the right folder? Is this atomic enough?"_.
- The Result: You feel "resistance" because your brain is trying to do two contradictory things at once: focus on the content (TPN) and judge the system (DMN).
