**3\. Explain the "Technical Debt" of Security**

- Use the analogy of technical debt to illustrate the cost of delaying security: "Think of security like the foundation of a house. Trying to add a strong foundation after the house is built is far more complex and expensive than building it right from the start. Retrofitting security into a mature codebase can lead to significant delays, vulnerabilities, and even rework, potentially hindering our growth and reputation."
