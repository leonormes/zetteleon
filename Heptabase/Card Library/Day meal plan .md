# Day meal plan 

Hydration: Drink plenty of water throughout the day (aim for at least 2-3 litres). This is crucial, especially when on Mounjaro. Herbal teas and sugar-free squash are also good options.

 \* Variety: It's important to eat a wide variety of foods over the week to ensure you're getting a broad spectrum of nutrients. Don't eat the exact same thing every day.

## Sample Meal Plan for a Day

This plan is an estimate and actual calorie and macronutrient content will vary based on specific ingredients and brands used. It aims for a lower calorie intake suitable for weight loss, while being nutrient-rich.

### Morning (e.g., 8:00 - 9:00 AM)

Breakfast: Protein-Packed Scramble

   \* 2 large eggs, scrambled with a splash of unsweetened almond milk or water.

   \* Add 75g of chopped spinach and 50g of chopped bell peppers (any colour) towards the end of cooking.

   \* Serve with 1 slice of wholemeal toast (look for high fibre options).

   \* Why: Eggs provide high-quality protein. Vegetables add fibre, vitamins (like A and C), and minerals. Wholemeal toast offers complex carbohydrates and fibre for sustained energy.

Mid-Morning Snack (e.g., 11:00 AM)

 \* Snack: Greek Yoghurt with Berries

   \* 150g plain, unsweetened Greek yoghurt (high in protein).

   \* 75g mixed berries (e.g., blueberries, raspberries, strawberries – fresh or frozen).

   \* Optional: A sprinkle of chia seeds (1 teaspoon) for extra fibre and omega-3s.

   \* Why: Greek yoghurt is an excellent source of protein and calcium. Berries are packed with antioxidants and fibre.

Lunchtime (e.g., 1:30 - 2:30 PM)

 \* Lunch: Chicken or Chickpea Salad Power Bowl

   \* 100g grilled chicken breast, diced (or 150g canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained, for a vegetarian option).

   \* Large handful of mixed salad leaves (e.g., rocket, romaine, watercress).

   \* 50g cucumber, sliced.

   \* 50g cherry tomatoes, halved.

   \* 25g quinoa, cooked (provides complete protein and fibre).

   \* Dressing: 1 tablespoon of olive oil and lemon juice dressing (or a light vinaigrette).

   \* Why: Lean protein for satiety. A variety of colourful vegetables for vitamins, minerals, and fibre. Quinoa adds complex carbohydrates and additional protein.

Mid-Afternoon Snack (e.g., 4:30 - 5:00 PM)

 \* Snack: Handful of Almonds and an Apple

   \* About 15-20 almonds (unsalted).

   \* 1 small apple.

   \* Why: Almonds provide healthy fats, protein, and fibre. An apple offers fibre and vitamins. This combination can help manage hunger pangs.

Evening (e.g., 7:00 - 8:00 PM)

 \* Dinner: Baked Salmon with Roasted Vegetables

   \* 120g salmon fillet, baked or grilled.

   \* 150-200g mixed non-starchy vegetables, roasted (e.g., broccoli florets, cauliflower florets, courgette, asparagus spears) with a teaspoon of olive oil, herbs (like dill or parsley), and a squeeze of lemon.

   \* Optional: 50g sweet potato, baked (for some complex carbohydrates).

   \* Why: Salmon is rich in omega-3 fatty acids and protein. Roasted vegetables are a great way to get fibre and a wide range of nutrients. Sweet potato provides sustained energy and vitamins.

Before Bed (Optional - if needed and at least 1-2 hours before sleeping)

 \* Light Snack/Drink:

   \* A cup of herbal tea (e.g., camomile, peppermint).

   \* Or, if genuinely hungry, a small portion (100g) of cottage cheese.

   \* Why: Herbal tea can be soothing. Cottage cheese offers slow-releasing protein if you need something before bed.

Key Nutrients This Plan Focuses On:

 \* Protein: Essential for maintaining muscle mass during weight loss, and helps with satiety. Sources: eggs, Greek yoghurt, chicken, chickpeas, salmon, almonds, quinoa, cottage cheese.

 \* Fibre: Aids digestion, promotes fullness, and helps regulate blood sugar. Sources: vegetables, fruits, wholemeal bread, quinoa, chia seeds, almonds.

 \* Healthy Fats: Important for hormone production, vitamin absorption, and overall health. Sources: olive oil, salmon, almonds, chia seeds.

 \* Vitamins and Minerals: A wide variety obtained from the variety of fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and whole grains.
