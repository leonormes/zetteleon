## A Proposition is a Declarative Claim that Bears a Truth Value

A proposition is a declarative claim that bears a truth value—it can be true or false.

- ✅ "B-trees outperform hash indexes for range queries"—proposition.
- ❌ "B-trees"—a concept/topic, not a proposition.
- ❌ "How do B-trees handle splits?"—a question, not a proposition.
- ❌ "Index that column"—an imperative, not a proposition.

Crucially, a proposition is distinct from the sentence expressing it. _"It is raining"_ and _"Il pleut"_ express the same proposition. The proposition is the _content_, not the _wording_.

\## Sub-types (each demands a different falsifier)

\- Empirical | Analytic | Normative | Evaluative | Predictive | Counterfactual | Definitional
