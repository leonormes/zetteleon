### **4\. Phenomenology**

- **View:** Thoughts are experiences that exist in consciousness.

- **Key Idea:** Phenomenologists like Edmund Husserl focus on how things appear in human consciousness. Thoughts, from this perspective, are **intentional acts of consciousness** (directed at objects or concepts) and are as real as other aspects of subjective experience.

- **Implication:** Thoughts exist as conscious experiences, but their mode of existence is different from physical objects. They exist within the **first-person perspective** of experience.
