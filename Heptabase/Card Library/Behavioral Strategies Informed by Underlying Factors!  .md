## Behavioral Strategies Informed by Underlying Factors

- _Behavioral Activation (BA):_ As discussed previously, BA encourages engagement in small, manageable, often value-aligned activities to build momentum, generate positive reinforcement, and counter the inertia of avoidance. This is particularly relevant for breaking the passivity associated with ADHD paralysis.
- _Systematic Task Breakdown:_ Dividing large or overwhelming tasks into very small, concrete steps reduces the perceived threat and increases the likelihood of initiation.
- _Strategic Goal and Deadline Setting:_ Establishing realistic goals and, where appropriate, external deadlines can provide structure and a sense of urgency.
- _Environmental Management:_ Proactively managing distractions and creating a workspace conducive to focus can reduce the cognitive load and friction associated with starting tasks.
- _Leveraging Social Support:_ Using techniques like body doubling (working alongside another person, either physically or virtually) can provide external accountability and reduce feelings of isolation during challenging tasks.
- _Gamification:_ Applying game-like elements (e.g., points, rewards, challenges) to mundane tasks can increase engagement and motivation for some individuals with ADHD.
