### Behavioral Strategies Informed by Underlying Factors:  

- *Behavioral Activation (BA):* As discussed previously, BA encourages engagement in small, manageable, often value-aligned activities to build momentum, generate positive reinforcement, and counter the inertia of avoidance. This is particularly relevant for breaking the passivity associated with ADHD paralysis.  

- *Systematic Task Breakdown:* Dividing large or overwhelming tasks into very small, concrete steps reduces the perceived threat and increases the likelihood of initiation. 

- *Strategic Goal and Deadline Setting:* Establishing realistic goals and, where appropriate, external deadlines can provide structure and a sense of urgency.

- *Environmental Management:* Proactively managing distractions and creating a workspace conducive to focus can reduce the cognitive load and friction associated with starting tasks.

- *Leveraging Social Support:* Using techniques like body doubling (working alongside another person, either physically or virtually) can provide external accountability and reduce feelings of isolation during challenging tasks.

- *Gamification:* Applying game-like elements (e.g., points, rewards, challenges) to mundane tasks can increase engagement and motivation for some individuals with ADHD.
