## 2. The Scarcity Loop of System Building

The act of re-designing your Zettelkasten has accidentally become a Scarcity Loop, a three-part system that drives near-compulsive repetition.

- Opportunity: A blank slate or a new app represents an opportunity to finally "solve" your thinking.
- Unpredictable Reward: You don't know if this new version will be the one that "clicks," and this uncertainty makes the search more exciting than the actual work.
- Quick Repeatability: It is very easy to delete a file or restart a protocol definition. The faster you can repeat a behaviour, the more likely you are to do it.
- The Result: You are essentially "gambling" on your productivity system, playing the "slot machine" of protocol design instead of actually writing notes.
