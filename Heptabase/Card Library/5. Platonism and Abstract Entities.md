## 5\. Platonism and Abstract Entities

- View: Thoughts may be part of a realm of abstract entities, like numbers or universals.
- Key Idea: In a Platonic view, some philosophers consider thoughts as abstract objects—non-physical entities that exist independently of the mind. These might be compared to mathematical objects or ideas in a higher realm of reality.
- Implication: Thoughts have existence, but not in a physical or mental sense; instead, they belong to the same category as concepts, ideas, or propositions, existing in a realm of abstract forms.
