### **1\. Dualism (e.g., Cartesian Dualism)**

**View:** Thoughts exist as distinct entities in the mind, separate from the physical body.

**Key Idea:** [René Descartes](https://app.heptabase.com/c16a6d60-49a6-4aec-9d1a-6161cbbe31a8/card/83324a63-2afc-4306-b46f-23e5cf741fb7), the most famous proponent of dualism, argued that mental phenomena (thoughts, emotions, consciousness) are fundamentally different from physical things. Thoughts exist in a non-physical realm of **mind** or **soul**.

**Implication:** Thoughts are not material objects, but they are real in the sense that they are part of the mind’s substance.
