## II. Addressing Core Psychological Constructs: Rebuilding the Inner Landscape

The journey towards profound transformation for adults with ADHD necessitates a dedicated focus on reshaping core psychological constructs that have often been negatively impacted by years of navigating challenges associated with the condition. These constructs, including shame, self-esteem, cognitive patterns, and identity, form the inner landscape upon which new, more adaptive ways of being can be built.
