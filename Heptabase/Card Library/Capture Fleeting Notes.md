# Capture Fleeting Notes

The Routine: Always carry a note-taking tool (notebook, digital app, even scraps of paper) to jot down ideas, insights, or questions as they arise. Don't censor yourself or worry about structure at this stage; simply capture the thought before it fades.
Why it Matters: Our short-term memory is fleeting. By capturing these fleeting notes, you create a record of potentially valuable ideas that can be revisited and developed later.
Example: During a lecture, you might quickly jot down a key concept the speaker emphasizes or a question that pops into your mind.
