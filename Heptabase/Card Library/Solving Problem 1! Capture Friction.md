## Solving Problem 1: Capture Friction

### The Capture Protocol

You need **one inbox**. Not six. Not "the right note." One place where any thought goes, regardless of what it is. In Heptabase, this is simply:

- A single card titled `Inbox` (or `Daily Journal` — Heptabase's Journal feature works well for this).

- When a thought arrives mid-flow, you open this card, type the thought, close it. **Done in 10 seconds.**

- No tagging. No linking. No trying to decide if it is a Claim or a Concept. That is processing, and you are not processing now.

The Journal feature in Heptabase is actually built for this — each day is a new card, so you get automatic chronological organisation without thinking.

**The rule:** in capture mode, the only question you ask is "did I write it down?" Yes → carry on. That is it.

### Why This Eliminates Resistance

Because the decision tree collapses. Currently when a thought arrives, your brain asks:

- Does this deserve a new note?

- What should the title be?

- Where does it fit?

- Is this a Claim or a Concept?

- Should I link it now?

Every one of those questions is activation energy. The Capture Protocol replaces all of them with one question: *open the inbox, type, close*. No choices. No structure. **The resistance you feel in Obsidian is the sum of those decisions. Remove the decisions, remove the resistance.**
