## 4\. Appeal To Strategic Thinking and Scalability

Position early security measures as forward-thinking.

- _Example:_ "Our team is small now, and this is exactly why it's the right time to embed security into our practices. As we grow, retrofitting these measures becomes significantly harder, slowing us down when we need to scale rapidly."
