# 4\.1 Definition Of Information

# Definition Of Information

Information, in a general sense, refers to data or knowledge that is meaningful and useful to someone.

It is the content that can be communicated, stored, processed, or transmitted in various forms such as text, numbers, images, or signals. Information is not just raw data, but rather data that has been organized, structured, or processed to have meaning or value in a particular context.

In different fields, the concept of information can take on slightly different meanings:

- [4\.1a In everyday language. Information refers to facts or knowledge communicated or received concerning a particular subject](4.1a%20In%20everyday%20language.%20Information%20refers%20to%20facts%20or%20knowledge%20communicated%20or%20received%20concerning%20a%20particular%20subject)

- [4\.1b In computing Information refers to data that has been processed and is ready for use or interpretation](4.1b%20In%20computing%20Information%20refers%20to%20data%20that%20has%20been%20processed%20and%20is%20ready%20for%20use%20or%20interpretation).

- [4\.1c In science and engineering. Information may refer to signals, patterns, or data that can be interpreted by machines or humans](4.1c%20In%20science%20and%20engineering.%20Information%20may%20refer%20to%20signals,%20patterns,%20or%20data%20that%20can%20be%20interpreted%20by%20machines%20or%20humans).
