## Network of Interests

In a Zettelkasten, the structure of links itself should reflect your unique path through your interests. Instead of organizing around fixed categories, your Zettelkasten can evolve as new insights connect previously separate ideas. Over time, this allows your PKM to adapt to your interests and help uncover personal connections and themes that a static wiki structure might obscure.
