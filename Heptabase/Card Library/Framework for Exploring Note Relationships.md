# Framework for Exploring Note Relationships

Utilise the "Idea Compass" to systematically explore potential connections:

N: What broader concept or category does this note belong to?

S: What are the sub-components or specific examples of this idea?

E: What is this idea similar to? Are there analogies or parallel concepts?

W: What is this idea different from? Are there contrasts or opposing viewpoints?

Consider additional relationship-focused questions:

- What does this idea enhance?

- What does it make obsolete?

- What does it retrieve from my memory?

- What does it reverse or challenge?

- Where is this idea relevant? (Consider time, place, context)

What are the strengths and weaknesses of this idea?

Don't force every connection. Only create links that genuinely resonate with your current thinking and research interests.

As you add new notes, consider how they relate to existing ideas within your Zettelkasten.
