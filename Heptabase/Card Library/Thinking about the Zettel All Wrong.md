# Thinking about the Zettel All Wrong

I wasn't giving the links enough attention. I was taking a word or a term, and using the back linking feature in obsidian to find all occurances of that term and just linking. Most of the time not having any idea of the context. This was an instance of [process deviation](../Inbox/6-process_deviation.md).

It is satisfying to link lots. In a subtle way, like the satisfaction from the variable reward of scrolling through a feed. Hard to notice and challenge.

And so I thought about abstracting the idea. This inevitably leads to the greatest of all abstracters, mathematics. At first I thought graph theory.
