1. The "Next Action" is Your Lifeline:

   - Before you stop working on something, _always_ define the very next action, no matter how small. "Open the file" is a valid next action if you're stuck.
   - When you feel overwhelmed or unsure where to start, look _only_ at your "Next Actions" for a project.
   - Prefixing with `NEXT` can help you quickly scan for it.
