1. **The "Next Action" is Your Lifeline:**

   - Before you stop working on something, *always* define the very next action, no matter how small. "Open the file" is a valid next action if you're stuck.

   - When you feel overwhelmed or unsure where to start, look *only* at your "Next Actions" for a project.

   - Prefixing with `NEXT` can help you quickly scan for it.
