### **2\. Physicalism / Materialism**

- **View:** Thoughts are physical processes, arising from the activity of the brain.

- **Key Idea:** In a materialist ontology, everything that exists is physical, and mental phenomena like thoughts are reducible to brain states or neural activity.

- **Implication:** Thoughts do not exist as independent entities; they are manifestations of physical interactions in the brain, often understood in terms of electrical signals or chemical reactions.
