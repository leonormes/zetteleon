## Focus on Emergence

- Regularly browse seemingly unrelated notes
- Create "connection notes" whose sole purpose is to articulate novel relationships
- Use your frontmatter to track insight chains:

```yaml
---
insight_chain:
  origin: "note-id-1"
  led_to: ["note-id-2", "note-id-3"]
  breakthrough: "note-id-4"
---
```
