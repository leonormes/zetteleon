## Teacher Training Needs to Help Bessie Respond Appropriately

Bessie's profile suggests she may be experiencing an internal "tug of war" if she has co-occurring AuDHD, where traits can seem contradictory and lead to significant internal chaos and exhaustion \[8, 9\].

A crucial concept for teachers to understand is "masking" or "camouflaging," where neurodivergent individuals suppress natural traits to appear neurotypical \[10, 11\]. This effort demands considerable mental and emotional resources, leading to profound exhaustion \[10, 11\]. Bessie's "two different people" phenomenon (familiar at home, struggles at school) is a common sign of masking and can result in an "after-school collapse" where distressed behavior, meltdowns, or shutdowns manifest when she feels safe enough to unmask \[10, 12-14\].
