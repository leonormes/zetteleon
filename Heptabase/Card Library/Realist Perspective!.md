Realist Perspective:

- Realists argue that thoughts have some form of objective existence, independent of our perception of them.
- Platonic realism might suggest that thoughts exist as abstract entities or forms in a realm beyond the physical world.
- Materialists could argue that thoughts are ultimately reducible to brain states and processes, and therefore exist as physical phenomena.
