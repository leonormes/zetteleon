### 2\. **ENI and Subnets**

- **Definition**: An ENI is always associated with a **subnet** within a VPC. The subnet defines the IP range that the ENI can use.

- **Role of Subnet**: The subnet is part of the **VPC’s IP space** and provides the IP address for the ENI.
