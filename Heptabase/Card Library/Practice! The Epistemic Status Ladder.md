### Practice: The Epistemic Status Ladder

The note's template has a `Confidence: low / medium / high` field. This is too coarse. A mature belief-revision system typically uses something like:

- **Speculation** — I just thought of this

- **Hypothesis** — I can defend it, but haven't tested it

- **Working belief** — I act on it but hold it loosely

- **Settled view** — I have argued through objections and hold it firmly

- **Public commitment** — I would defend this in writing

Granularity matters. "Medium confidence" is the PKM equivalent of "it's complicated" — it feels honest but dodges commitment.
