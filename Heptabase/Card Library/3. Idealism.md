### **3\. Idealism**

- **View:** Thoughts are more fundamental than material reality.

- **Key Idea:** Philosophical idealists, such as George Berkeley, assert that reality is fundamentally mental or conceptual. The external world is secondary to our thoughts and perceptions.

- **Implication:** In this view, thoughts are not just "things" that exist; they may be the very foundation of all reality. The material world may even depend on thoughts for its existence.
