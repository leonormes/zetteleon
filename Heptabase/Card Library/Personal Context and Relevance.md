## Personal Context and Relevance

Information becomes knowledge when it is:

- Relevant to the individual's needs or goals
- Connected to personal experiences or existing knowledge
- Meaningful within the person's specific context or field of expertise

As information becomes personally relevant, it is more likely to be retained and utilized as knowledge\[1\]\[2\].

- Context and Relevance: Information gains value when it is relevant to a specific situation, problem, or goal. Raw data without context is just noise.
