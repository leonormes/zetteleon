**Key Considerations:**

- **Data and Evidence:** Come prepared with data points, statistics on security breaches, and examples of companies that have faced challenges due to neglecting early security.

- **Tone and Body Language:** Maintain a calm, respectful, and professional demeanor. Avoid accusatory or confrontational language.

- **Focus on Solutions:** Rather than dwelling on the problem, emphasize proactive solutions and a collaborative approach to security implementation.

- **Pick your battles:** If the manager remains adamant, consider escalating the issue to a higher authority or seeking advice from other senior team members who might be more receptive to your concerns.
