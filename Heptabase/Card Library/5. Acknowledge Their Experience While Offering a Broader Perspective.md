## 5\. Acknowledge Their Experience While Offering a Broader Perspective

Show respect for their expertise but contextualise the uniqueness of the current scenario.

- _Example:_ "Your experience in fast-growing startups is invaluable, and I'd love to hear more about how you've balanced priorities in the past. With this product, though, the nature of medical data introduces unique risks that many startups in less sensitive domains don't face."
