# BC_LINK_HDR_UK

BC|Link is a software component developed by BC Platforms that plays a crucial role in connecting data sources to HDR UK's Cohort Discovery tool. This technology enables secure and privacy-compliant data sharing between healthcare organizations and researchers.

## BC|Link And HDR UK

BC|Link serves as a critical bridge between individual data sources and the Cohort Discovery tool on the HDR UK Innovation Gateway. Here's how it functions in relation to HDR UK:

1. Data Connection: BC|Link is installed within the infrastructure of organizations hosting health data. It allows these organizations to connect their datasets to the Cohort Discovery tool without moving the data from its original location.

2. Federated Queries: The software enables federated queries across standardized data using the Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership (OMOP) Common Data Model. This means researchers can perform searches across multiple datasets while the data remains under the control of each hosting organization.

3. Privacy and Security: BC|Link ensures that only aggregate count data can be viewed on the Cohort Discovery platform, maintaining patient privacy and adhering to data protection regulations like GDPR.

4. Scalability: The use of BC|Link has allowed HDR UK to expand the Cohort Discovery tool beyond its initial focus on COVID-19 data. It's now being used to connect datasets from various disease areas and research initiatives.

## Impact on Research

The implementation of BC|Link in HDR UK's data infrastructure has significantly enhanced the research capabilities:

- It has enabled the connection of over 16 different COVID-19 data sources to the Cohort Discovery tool, allowing queries on de-identified data from more than 5.2 million patient records across the UK.

- The technology is supporting HDR UK's efforts to develop federated research projects, going beyond just data discovery.

- It's being utilized in specific research initiatives, such as the Alleviate Pain Data Hub, which is working towards enhancing the Cohort Discovery tool to perform federated Genomic Data Analysis.

By leveraging BC|Link, HDR UK has created a more efficient and secure way for researchers to access and analyze health data, ultimately accelerating the pace of medical research and improving patient outcomes.

BC|Link and the Cohort Discovery tool are two distinct components that work together within the HDR UK data infrastructure, but they serve different purposes and operate in different locations.

## BC|Link

BC|Link is a software component developed by BC Platforms that operates within the data partners' infrastructure:

- Location: Installed behind the firewall of each data partner.

- Function: Executes queries on the local datasets and returns aggregated results.

- Data Access: Has direct access to the pseudo-anonymized version of the data that the data partner wishes to make discoverable.

- Control: Fully controlled by the data partner, with no external access allowed.

## Cohort Discovery Tool

The Cohort Discovery tool, on the other hand, is the user-facing interface:

- Location: Hosted on the HDR UK Innovation Gateway.

- Function: Allows researchers to create and submit cohort queries across multiple datasets.

- User Interaction: Provides a drag-and-drop interface for researchers to define their cohort criteria.

- Results Display: Shows aggregate results from across different data sources.

## Key Differences

1. Operational Environment:

   - BC|Link operates within each data partner's secure environment.

   - Cohort Discovery tool is a centralized platform accessible to researchers.

2. Data Handling:

   - BC|Link interacts directly with local datasets.

   - Cohort Discovery tool only receives and displays aggregated results.

3. User Access:

   - BC|Link is not accessible to researchers.

   - Cohort Discovery tool is the interface researchers use to conduct searches.

4. Functionality:

   - BC|Link executes the actual queries on local data.

   - Cohort Discovery tool formulates queries and presents results to users.

In essence, BC|Link acts as the secure bridge between the local datasets and the centralized Cohort Discovery tool, enabling federated queries while maintaining data privacy and security.
