1. Embrace the "Learning as You Go" aspect:

   - When you learn something new (e.g., a specific Terraform command, a GitOps concept), create a new note for it (e.g., `[[terraform validate]]`, `[[FluxCD Image Automation]]`).
   - Link to this new note from your Project Hub's "Brainstorming" or "Organised Thoughts" section. This builds your personal knowledge base.
   - Your "Brainstorming" section will naturally capture your learning journey.
