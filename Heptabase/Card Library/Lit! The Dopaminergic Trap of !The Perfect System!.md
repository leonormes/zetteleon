## Lit: The Dopaminergic Trap of "The Perfect System"

Your brain is chasing a "correct" protocol because of Dopamine, which is the molecule of anticipation, not satisfaction.

- The Future is Glamorous: In your mind, the _next_ protocol exists in "extrapersonal space"—the realm of possibility and the future. Because it isn't real yet, it is perfect.
- The Present is Dull: The moment you begin to actually use a protocol, it moves into "peripersonal space"—the here and now. It becomes concrete, flawed, and familiar. At this point, your dopamine levels drop, and you experience a "letdown".
- The Result: Your brain identifies this letdown as the protocol being "wrong," so it immediately starts searching for a new "correct" one to get that dopamine surge back.
