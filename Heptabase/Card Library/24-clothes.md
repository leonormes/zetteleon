# Clothes

Taking the right measurements can help you find clothes that fit well without having to rely on oversized items. Here are the key measurements to take and compare to online sizing charts

1. Shoulder Width: Measure across your back from one shoulder seam to the other. This helps ensure a good fit in shirts, jackets, and coats, as many styles use shoulder width as a base. 1. 52cm - 20.47inch

2. Chest: Wrap the measuring tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it snug but not tight. This measurement is critical for shirts, jackets, and blazers. 1. 118cm - 46.45 inch

3. Waist: Measure around the narrowest part of your waist, usually near the belly button. For pants, take a second measurement lower on your belly, where the waistband will sit, especially if you prefer a relaxed fit. 1. 128cm - 50.39 inches

4. Hips: Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your hips (typically around the top of your legs). This is especially useful for pants and can help if shirts are cut longer. 1. 116cm - 45.66

5. Length of Shirt: Measure from the top of your shoulder, near the base of your neck, down to where you want the shirt to end. Compare this to product lengths for a better idea of where the shirt will fall. 1. 69cm - 27.16

6. Sleeve Length: Measure from the shoulder seam down to your wrist. Many retailers list sleeve lengths for shirts, jackets, and coats. 1. 59cm - 23.22

7. Inseam (for pants): Measure from the crotch down to the desired length on the inner leg. This ensures that pants sit at the right length, whether you prefer full-length or cropped styles. 1. 68cm - 26.77
