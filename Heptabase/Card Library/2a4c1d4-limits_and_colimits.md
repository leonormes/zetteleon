# Limits and Colimits

These concepts can help you organize and synthesize information in your PKM:

- Limits represent the commonalities or intersections of ideas. In your notes, this could be used to find the core concepts that tie together multiple notes.

- Colimits represent the union or combination of ideas. This can be useful for synthesizing new concepts from existing notes.

Limits and colimits are fundamental tools in category theory, allowing for the abstraction and generalization of many important constructions from set theory and other mathematical domains.
