Lit: The "Double Edge": Scattering vs. Synthesis

- The LLM's advice to not suppress this trait, but rather to channel it, is the core thesis of modern ADHD management.
- The condition is characterised by paradoxical tendencies, such as "impulsive, wrongheaded decision making combined with inventive, out-of-the-blue problem solving".
- The literature emphasises a strength-based model, pointing out that "the flip side of impulsivity is creativity" and that "\[c\]reativity is impulsivity gone right".
- The LLM is correctly identifying that the very mechanism causing distractibility is the exact same mechanism enabling rapid, high-level synthesis of disparate ideas.
- It is a genuine cognitive advantage when harnessed, or as it is described in the clinical literature, a "bona fide superpower".
