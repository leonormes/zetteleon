### Lit: The question-as-thinking-aloud pattern

Many of your messages are not really questions asking for answers. They are you thinking with a high-bandwidth interlocutor. The brain dump is explicit about this, but it is true throughout:

> *"This paragraph has draft thinking in it but how do I extend it and solve it?"*

You are using the conversation as an external working memory. This is not a flaw — it is a known and effective ADHD accommodation. "Body doubling" and "rubber-duck thinking" exist precisely because ADHD brains can think better out loud than in silence. An LLM is an exceptionally good body double for this.

**But be careful:** the conversation *feels* like progress because ideas are being generated and validated. Generation is not the same as consolidation. The JTB card we discussed eight messages ago — is it actually in Heptabase yet? If not, the conversation has been partially substituting for the work rather than enabling it. That is the "talking about doing instead of doing" trap.
