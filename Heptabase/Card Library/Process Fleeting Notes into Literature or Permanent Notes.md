# Process Fleeting Notes into Literature or Permanent Notes

The Routine: Schedule dedicated time each day (perhaps at the end of the day or before starting new work) to review your fleeting notes. Decide whether to:
Discard: If the note is no longer relevant or interesting.
Transfer to Reference System: If it's a literature note containing bibliographic information or a summary of a source.
Develop into Permanent Note: If it's an idea you want to explore further, turn it into a permanent note in your Zettelkasten.
Why it Matters: This routine ensures you don't end up with a backlog of unprocessed notes. It also forces you to engage with your ideas and make deliberate choices about which ones to pursue.
