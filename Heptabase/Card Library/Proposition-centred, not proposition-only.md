Proposition-centred, not proposition-only

This is the most important paragraph in the whole note. It is the *exact* distinction I tried to make two exchanges ago when you asked about IOED: **not every useful note is a claim**. Concepts, practices, sources, and people all pull their weight differently.

The note correctly resists the temptation to say "everything is a proposition." That resistance is philosophically earned — the Ryle/Polanyi/Russell acknowledgement is doing real work. **Keep this.**
