## The Persistent Drive to Harness Complex Productivity Systems

The persistent drive to harness complex productivity systems like Getting Things Done (GTD) and knowledge management frameworks such as Zettelkasten is an often deeply frustrating experience for many individuals with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD).
