### Uncovering Root Causes through Deeper Exploration:  

- *Downward Arrow Technique:* This Socratic questioning method involves progressively asking "What does that mean?" or "If that were true, what would that mean about you?" in response to the client's statements about their avoidance. This helps to drill down from surface-level reasons for procrastination to uncover core underlying beliefs and emotions (e.g., a fear of cleaning the house might eventually reveal a core belief of "I am not worthy of a comfortable space" or "I will be judged harshly if I invite people over").55  

- *Imaginal Exposure:* This technique involves guiding the individual to vividly imagine themselves in the avoided situation (e.g., sitting down to start a dreaded report). By exploring the thoughts, feelings, and bodily sensations that arise in this imagined scenario, the therapist can help the client access and process the emotions that are being avoided in real life.55
