## Frontmatter Metadata as Thinking Tools

Metadata in your frontmatter can go beyond basic tags or keywords. Consider adding fields that define the context or reason for a note's creation, the question it addresses, or the core principles it touches on. This allows you to generate a rich, context-sensitive view of your notes that reflects your thought process and areas of curiosity.
