A mathematician uses deductive reasoning to justify his/her conclusions.

When you solve an equation for x you are using the information given by the equation to deduce what the value of x must be. Similarly, when mathematicians solve other kinds of mathematical problems, they always justify their conclusions with deductive reasoning

What's a conjecture?

A conjecture is basically an educated guess or a hunch in math. It's something we think might be true, but we haven't proven it for sure yet.

What's a theorem?

Once we prove a conjecture is definitely true, it becomes a theorem. Think of it like leveling up in a game - you've done the work to show it's solid.
