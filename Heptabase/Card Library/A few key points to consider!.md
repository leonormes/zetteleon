A few key points to consider:

1. Market Access: Healthcare organisations typically have strict security requirements for their vendors. By building security in from the start, we're actually expanding our total addressable market rather than limiting it.
2. Cost Efficiency: Research from IBM suggests that security fixes cost 6x more when implemented after deployment compared to during development. For a startup watching its runway, this represents a significant financial risk.
3. Regulatory Compliance: Unlike conventional software products, healthcare solutions face strict regulatory requirements under HIPAA. These aren't optional features we can add later - they're prerequisites for operating in the healthcare space.
4. Time to Market: While it might seem counterintuitive, implementing security foundations early actually accelerates our long-term velocity. We avoid the 'security debt' that would require a complete rebuild later, potentially during a crucial growth phase.
