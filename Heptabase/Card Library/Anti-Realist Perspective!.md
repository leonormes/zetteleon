Anti-Realist Perspective:

- Anti-realists or deflationists might argue that questions about the existence of thoughts are meaningless or lack objective answers.
- Conceptualists might say that thoughts exist only as mental constructs or concepts within individual minds.
- Nominalists could deny any independent existence of thoughts, considering them merely labels or names we assign to mental experiences.
