## 4\. **ENIs In High Availability and Fault Tolerance**

ENIs play a significant role in enhancing the **high availability** and **fault tolerance** of workloads in AWS:

- **Failover and Migration**: You can detach an ENI from one instance and attach it to another, which helps with failover scenarios. For instance, if an EC2 instance hosting an ENI fails, the ENI can be attached to another instance within the same AZ, minimizing downtime.

- **Network Interface Failover**: If an instance with multiple ENIs experiences an issue with one of its network interfaces, traffic can be rerouted through another ENI, ensuring continued operation.
