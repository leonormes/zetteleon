## 30b-structure_of_notes

## Structure of Notes

A wiki might have pages that are more comprehensive or encyclopedic. Zettelkasten notes are atomic by design, focused on single ideas that are individually understandable and interdependent. Each note should act as a piece of thinking, allowing you to combine, compare, and rearrange concepts more fluidly.

Zettelkasten:

- Atomic notes: Each note contains a single, well-defined idea
- Written in your own words, emphasizing personal understanding and insights
- Includes your own thoughts, questions, and reflections

Personal Wiki:

- Often contains longer, more comprehensive articles
- May include more direct quotes or summaries from sources
- Tends to be more objective and less personal

To maintain a Zettelkasten structure:

- Keep your notes concise and focused on a single idea
- Always rephrase information in your own words
- Include your personal insights, questions, and reflections in each note
