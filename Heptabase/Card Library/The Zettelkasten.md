## The Zettelkasten

At the center of the system is the zettelkasten---an object and a practice---a thing and a way to do things. The term comes from German, usually translated as "slip box" or "note box," less often, "card catalog," terms with which you may be familiar.

You may not, however, be familiar with German social scientist, [Niklas Luhmann](https://app.heptabase.com/c16a6d60-49a6-4aec-9d1a-6161cbbe31a8/card/85d69970-d0f2-4e28-a1f5-669be759e149), who through his ability to publish a surprisingly large number of articles and books (at last count, roughly five hundred publications), brought to light his unique approach to taking, making, connecting, and leveraging ideas.
