## Summary

Personal execution protocol designed to overcome task-initiation difficulty on large, ambiguous engineering projects. Treats human attention like a Lean software delivery pipeline — optimising for small batches, limiting WIP, and ensuring fast feedback loops. Created alongside the Natural Planning Model session for three summer engineering projects.

## Key Facts

- **Problem addressed:** Difficulty initiating complex, high-friction technical work despite having clear plans — [[raw/2026-05-23-pieces-natural-planning-model]] (Pieces: 49049282-6f30-48f6-8a68-c5cc68b79bb8)
- **Phase 1 — Pre-Flight (State Management):** Acknowledge overwhelm explicitly; use brief physical movement (brisk walk) as neurological reset — [[raw/2026-05-23-pieces-natural-planning-model]] (Pieces: 49049282-6f30-48f6-8a68-c5cc68b79bb8)
- **Phase 2 — Walking Skeleton Initiation:** Timebox to 15–25 minutes; execute only the single atomic next action; goal is overcoming inertia, not perfection — [[raw/2026-05-23-pieces-natural-planning-model]] (Pieces: 49049282-6f30-48f6-8a68-c5cc68b79bb8)
- **Phase 3 — The Unschedule:** Schedule breaks, meals, and leisure first; then schedule work around them; guaranteed endpoint removes dread — [[raw/2026-05-23-pieces-natural-planning-model]] (Pieces: 49049282-6f30-48f6-8a68-c5cc68b79bb8)
- **Phase 4 — Radical WIP Limits:** One major project at a time; creative neglect of others until active project reaches defined, testable milestone — [[raw/2026-05-23-pieces-natural-planning-model]] (Pieces: 49049282-6f30-48f6-8a68-c5cc68b79bb8)
- **Applicable projects:** K8s/OMOP stress testing, GitOps pipeline optimisation, Azure Entra IAM migration — [[raw/2026-05-23-pieces-natural-planning-model]] (Pieces: 49049282-6f30-48f6-8a68-c5cc68b79bb8)
