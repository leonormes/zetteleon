## Reps Log

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Specimen | Gemini (2026-07-07): Reform voters' beliefs explained via "soil/fertiliser/stress response" whilst their falsity was assumed, never demonstrated |
| Pattern | Bulverism / genetic fallacy—origin-tracing substituting for evaluation |
| Form test | "B arose from environment E, therefore B is false"—invalid; origin is independent of truth value |
| Not a fallacy when | The truth question is already settled independently and the causal story explains an error's _persistence_ (e.g. why astrology survives failed predictions) |
| Discrimination cue | Has anyone shown the claim false, or only explained why someone would believe it? |
